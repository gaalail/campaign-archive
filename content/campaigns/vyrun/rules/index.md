# Vyrun Campaign Rules

Vyrun's rugged mountain passes and untamed wildlands demand rigorous survival navigation, group preparedness, and endurance against brutal weather.

---

## Setting Mechanics

### Group Survival Checks

- **Wilderness Navigation:** When traversing uncharted wilderness, blizzards, or mountain passes, the party makes a **group Wisdom (Survival) check**.
- **Lowest Modifier Applies:** Rather than averaging or allowing a single ranger to guide unconditionally, the lowest Survival modifier in the travelling party sets the team's base modifier, reflecting the difficulty of moving an entire unit together through perilous terrain.
- **Success & Failure:** If at least half the party succeeds on their roll against the wilderness DC, the group stays on course. Otherwise, the party loses travel progress or encounters an environmental hazard.

### Accelerated Exhaustion

- **Harsh Exposure:** Traveling through severe blizzards, freezing alpine elevations, or torrential mountain storms without adequate shelter, fire, or cold-weather gear accelerates [[rules/conditions/exhaustion|exhaustion]].
- **Exposure Tiers:** Characters forced to march or camp without shelter in severe weather must make a DC 10 Constitution saving throw every 4 hours, gaining **one level of exhaustion** on a failure in addition to normal resting requirements.
