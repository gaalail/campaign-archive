# Solarys the First Ember

> *"A blizzard takes the lone traveler; a shared fire brings the settlement through till thaw."*

When magic died across Vyrun centuries ago, the old high temples collapsed into frost-cracked stone—only the simplest forge-prayers and watchfire blessings to **Solarys** survived in folk memory. Now that divine spark has returned, her lanterns burn without oil in remote border chapels, rallying pioneers against the encroaching wild.

## Tenets of the Unbroken Hearth

- **Tend the Common Fire:** No traveler is turned from warmth in a storm; survival on the frontier is collective.
- **Temper Steel and Resolve:** Craft tools that endure, fortify shelters before winter strikes, and stand your post.
- **Carry Light Beyond the Wall:** Explore bravely, but leave safe waystations for those who walk after you.

## Clergy & Orders

Clerics of Solarys serve as village smith-priests and expedition healers in the [[character-options/subclasses/Cleric/Dawn Domain|Dawn Domain]] or [[character-options/subclasses/Cleric/Life|Life Domain]]. Frontier paladins swear the [[character-options/subclasses/Paladin/Forge|Oath of the Forge]], [[character-options/subclasses/Paladin/Shield|Oath of the Shield]], or [[character-options/subclasses/Paladin/Oath of Righteousness|Oath of Righteousness]].
