# The Reawakened Old Gods

> *"For three centuries, temple bells in the border forts rang over empty stone. Now the fires light themselves—and ancient carvings in the deep woods have begun to whisper back."*

Faith in [[campaigns/vyrun/index|Vyrun]] stands at a volatile crossroads. After centuries during which magic lay silent across the continent, the sudden return of arcane and divine power has stirred half-forgotten frontier saints alongside the primeval spirits of mountain and ruin.

---

## Faith on the Frontier

- **The Hearth & Bastion:** Settlers, smiths, and outpost wardens rally around **[[campaigns/vyrun/pantheon/Solarys the First Ember|Solarys the First Ember]]**, whose flame staves off winter exposure and despair.
- **The Untamed Wild:** Rangers, druids, and mountain clans revere **[[campaigns/vyrun/pantheon/Kaelen of the Ash-Bough|Kaelen of the Ash-Bough]]** and fear/respect **[[campaigns/vyrun/pantheon/Gorrak the Iron-Tooth|Gorrak the Iron-Tooth]]** when blizzards descend.
- **The Awakening Ruins:** Scholars and first-generation casters attune to **[[campaigns/vyrun/pantheon/The Whispering Ley|The Whispering Ley]]**, wondering whether the ancient megaliths house a sleeping god or the remnants of an empire's soul.
