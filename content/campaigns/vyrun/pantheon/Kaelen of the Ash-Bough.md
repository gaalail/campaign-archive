# Kaelen of the Ash-Bough

> *"Clear only the timber you can haul, hunt only what you will eat, and never break the mossy boundary stones."*

Long before frontier towns raised palisades in Vyrun, carved ash-wood totems marked trails safe for foot travelers who respected the forest's terms. **Kaelen of the Ash-Bough** is neither friend nor enemy to settlement—he blesses guides who read the land honestly and turns briar, fog, and beast against those who strip the valleys bare.

## Tenets of the Frontier Trail

- **Read Before You Cut:** Learn the watershed, migrations, and ancient stones of a valley before claiming its timber.
- **Guide the Lost Safely Home:** True wilderness mastery protects companions through harsh weather and exhaustion.
- **Honor the Dormant Season:** Winter is not malice; it is rest. Respect nature's severity and prepare accordingly.

## Clergy & Orders

Kaelen's druids and scouts patrol boundary ridges alongside paladins of the [[character-options/subclasses/Paladin/Wilds|Oath of the Wilds]], [[character-options/subclasses/Paladin/Ancients|Oath of the Ancients]], or [[character-options/subclasses/Paladin/Vigilance|Oath of Vigilance]].
