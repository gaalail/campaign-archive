# The Whispering Ley

> *"Magic did not simply return to Vyrun like rain after drought. Something inside the ruins opened its eyes and remembered our names."*

Some call **The Whispering Ley** a slumbering god of the lost civilization; others insist it is a living harmonic chorus left behind by an age of archmages. Whatever its origin, pioneers who touch Vyrun's standing stones experience visions of sunken spires, and newly awakened spellcasters hear its resonant cadence guiding their prayers and incantations.

## Tenets of the Returning Resonance

- **Seek the Buried Truth:** Discover why magic fell silent for centuries—and what else might awaken alongside it.
- **Respect Living Power:** Untamed magic warps those who treat it as mere weaponry; listen before shaping the weave.
- **Bear Witness:** Record forgotten glyphs, myths, and ruined histories before rival factions weaponize them.

## Devotees & Orders
