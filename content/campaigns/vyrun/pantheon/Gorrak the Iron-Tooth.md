# Gorrak the Iron-Tooth

> *"Pray for mild weather and you pray in vain. Pray for legs that never buckle when the ridge breaks—Gorrak respects that."*

Worshipped around campfires high in Vyrun's jagged peaks, **Gorrak the Iron-Tooth** is honored not with hymns, but with endurance, trophies won in fair struggle, and oaths shouted into howling gale-winds. He scorns cowardice and oath-breaking, yet protects those who bleed side-by-side to bring their kin safely off the mountain.

## Tenets of the Iron Fang

- **Never Yield to Easy Ruin:** Meet storm, hunger, and monster on your feet; endurance itself is sacred defiance.
- **Keep Blood-Promises:** A vow made before battle binds tighter than iron; betray your pack and face Gorrak's wrath.
- **Honor Worthy Foes:** Respect the strength of primeval beasts and rivals alike; slay without cruelty, triumph without boasting.
