# Chapter 4: Factions, Guilds & Faiths

In Ra Gol'vek, travel through Nexus Gates reveals a world shaped as much by organizations, guilds, and competing visions of divinity as by emperors or kings.

---

## Religious Organizations

### The Church of the Archangels

Rooted in a loose city-state settled upon the fallen fragment of Heaven left behind during the **Sundering**, the **Church of the Archangels** stands as the single largest religious institution in Ra Gol'vek. Roughly a century after the **Entente of Divine Concordance**, **Archangels**—celestial beings intrinsically tied to the **Prime Material plane** rather than any distant Deitric realm—gave that independent community formal shape.

Because the Archangels reject traditional worship and remain anchored to mortal reality itself, countless citizens view them as a moral compass uncorrupted by distant divine politics. Beneath that pristine reputation, however, secretive radical sub-factions pursue extreme measures they believe align with Archangelic truth.

### The Old Pantheons & Their Devotees

Though the Gods withdrew under the Entente into **Graikorioi**, **Metu Neter**, **Norse**, **Daidōis**, **Shaivi**, and other Deitric realms, their temples, prayers, visions, and champions remain deeply woven into mortal nations.

To traditionalists, the Church of the Archangels appears as a mortal-led upstart lacking the mythic heritage of the surviving pantheons. Open continental religious wars remain rare—most friction stays localized—yet faithful sects vary widely between ancient orthodoxy and philosophies reshaped by planar contact.

### Regional Faiths & Ascended Powers

Beyond the major temples stand localized traditions and esoteric orders:

- **Ayineawa's Blood & Land Rites:** Intimate devotion to living forests, volcanic spirits atop **Maq'tara**, and ancestral crossings at the **Pit of Teotli**.
- **Exiled War Cults of Exoria:** Pilgrims and warbands seeking power or liberation for **Ares**, **Kratos**, and **Eris**.
- **Kingdoms of Night & Omneris:** Dark covenants rooted in the Shadowlands carved by **Raz'a Noct**, or the soul-magic ascendants of **Omneris**.
- **Forgotten Cults & Coastal/Mountain Shrines:** Mortal keepers tending ancient altars to deities whose names faded into **Plia Dral** or vanished during the **Deumachy**.
