# Ra Gol'vek Campaign Rules

Ra Gol'vek is an island-rich, supernatural frontier where ancient ruins, eldritch planar rifts, and untamed oceans test mortal sanity.

---

## Setting Mechanics

### Madness Saving Throws

- **Supernatural Shock:** When characters are directly exposed to alien horrors, dimensional distortions, or unfathomable planar entities, the DM may call for a **Wisdom saving throw** (DC typically 12–18 based on severity).
- **Madness Effects:** On a failed save, the creature suffers an episode of short-term madness (rolled from the DMG Madness table, p. 259) or temporary psychic disorientation.
- **Recovery:** A short rest, restorative magic such as _lesser restoration_, or emotional grounding can end acute madness effects.
