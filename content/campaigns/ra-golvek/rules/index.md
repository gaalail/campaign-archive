# Ra Gol'vek Campaign Rules

Ra Gol'vek is a high-magic cosmos built over the scar of a dead multiverse (**the Wound**), sustained by the **Law of Recurrence**, and strained in **Year 1199 AMH** as ancient wards weaken and erratic leylines herald a looming convergence.

---

## Setting Mechanics

### 1. Severed from the Loom of Fate (Player Characters)

- **Clean Resurrection Origin:** Unlike ordinary mortals—whose souls grow heavier and scar the weave of time with every return from death—each player character was deliberately pulled from their destined demise by a God and **severed from the Loom of Fate**.
- **Cosmic Blindspot:** You carry no traditional resurrection scars into play, making your party anomalies unseen by normal divination and prophecy—though your existence ripples outward, drawing attention from powers seeking either to steer or enforce the cycle.
- **Character Grounding:** See [[campaigns/ra-golvek/Afterlife & Resurrection|Chapter 2: The Afterlife & Resurrection]] for session-zero questions exploring how you died, what lay beyond the veil, and your stance toward the deity who returned you.

### 2. Madness Saving Throws (The Breach & Eldritch Horrors)

- **Supernatural Shock:** When characters confront fragments of what broke the prior multiverse, phenomena rising from the **Obscured** or **Wound**, dimensional fractures in **Exoria**, or erratic ley surges in the **Hexcrag Isles**, the DM may call for a **Wisdom saving throw** (DC typically 12–18 based on severity).
- **Madness Effects:** On a failed save, the creature suffers an episode of short-term madness (DMG Madness table, p. 259) or temporary psychic disorientation.
- **Recovery:** A short rest, restorative magic such as _lesser restoration_, or emotional grounding can end acute madness effects.

### 3. The Entente of Divine Concordance

- **Divinity Held at Distance:** Following the **Mortalimachy**, surviving pantheons withdrew into bridged **Deitric Realms** (*Graikorioi*, *Metu Neter*, *Norse*, *Daidōis*, *Shaivi*, etc.). Direct divine intervention on the Prime Material requires arbitration by the **Council of Pantheons**.
- **Freedom of Devotion & Forbidden Cults:** By treaty law, mortals hold the guaranteed right to worship any permitted faith—or none at all—which underpins the rise of the **Church of the Archangels** (**Daevayim**) alongside the Old Pantheons and **Avehrneol** cults. Worship of the **Cataclysms** remains strictly forbidden across most lands.
- **Sunset Clause:** Ratified for 2,000 years, the treaty clock stands at **Year 1199 AMH**—with Exoria's seals weakening and ley arteries misfiring.

### 4. Environmental Hazards (Ezuuli Reef & Hexcrag Wyrdstorms)

- **Ezuuli Reef (Ayineawa):** The magic-eating coral off western Ayineawa functions as a living arcane drain. Spells fail to manifest and enchanted items lose potency within the reef's boundary.
- **Wyrdstorms (Hexcrag Isles):** Arcane hurricanes surging from mutated eastern leylines distort magnetic and planar navigation and tear apart unwarded vessels.
