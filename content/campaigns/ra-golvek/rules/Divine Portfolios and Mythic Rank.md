# Divine Portfolios & Mythic Rank

In **Ra Gol'vek**, **Gods, Spirits, and Sovereigns** of **Divine Power** rank—including Pantheons, **Avehrneol** Archdevils, **Daevayim** Archangels, the **Kingdoms of Night**, **Heralds of the End**, **Powers Primaeval**, and bound **Cataclysms**—wield reality-shaping mythic traits alongside one or more **Portfolios**.

---

## Core Divine Power Traits

- **Divine Immunity:** A form of Immunity limited to Divine Powers; this functions as **Immunity III**. Unlike Immunity III, Divine Immunity resists attempts to lower it from spells or abilities, granting **Advantage** against such attempts. Attacks that overcome Immunity still overcome a level of Immunity, as it is not a reduction against the Divinity.
- **Miracle:** Divine Powers have access to **Miracle** (**3 MA uses**), allowing them to overwrite reality. Miracle may be used to make an enemy attack miss, to make their save a success, to make their own attack hit, or to grant **Superior Disadvantage** to an opposing save.
- **Mythic Action:** Divine Powers have **9 Mythic Actions**, which refresh at the end of the round. Mythic Actions combine aspects of Legendary Actions and Legendary Resistances, in that some may be used in reaction to something (failed save, failed attack), to add to their own action economy (after a player's turn), or to augment one of their own actions (bolstering an attack or form of movement).
- **Superior Advantage:** You roll with Advantage twice. If Disadvantage would apply, you still roll with Advantage once.
- **Superior Disadvantage:** You roll with Disadvantage twice. If Advantage would apply, you still roll with Disadvantage once.

## Portfolios with Defined Mechanics

### Battle
+5 to all physical stats. You have an additional reaction that can only be used for opportunity attacks. Expertise with all armor and weapons.
- **Mythic Action (4 uses):** Gain an additional Attack Action.

### Destruction
Deals an additional amount of damage equal to HD once per attack action. This bonus damage is typeless, and cannot be reduced. A creature slain by this damage is subject to disintegration. Divine Immunity to Evocation magic.
- **Mythic Action (2 uses):** May convert all damage dealt this attack to typeless damage that cannot be reduced.

### Fate
Superior Aura (90 ft): Possesses 5 Superior Advantage charges. May sacrifice an Advantage to give Disadvantage, or a Superior Advantage to give Superior Disadvantage. Recharges at the end of the round.
- **Mythic Action (5 uses):** Gain or remove an Action from yourself or a target within your aura.

### Fear
You have Expertise in Intimidation, and Disadvantage cannot be imposed on this skill. Your Fear effects are extremely powerful, overcoming a level of Immunity and lasting until successfully saved against or removed.

### Games
When playing any game or participating in any contest, you can add half your HD as a bonus to all checks, rolls, and saving throws related to that game or contest. Your critical range for rolls is 18–20. Divine Immunity to effects that would prevent you from winning a game or contest.
- **Mythic Action (2 uses):** May add `1d20` to a roll.

### Life
All healing variables are maximized. Regeneration equal to HD to self and allies within aura.

### Love
+10 Charisma; has Advantage against any harmful attack or ability that targets them. Must make a Wisdom Saving Throw when entering their aura, or you become Charmed. Charmed condition ends on a successful Wisdom Saving Throw. While Charmed, you view them as your True Love.
- **Mythic Action (5 uses):** May cause a target that can become Charmed to become Charmed.

### Madness
+5 Charisma. Fortification against the first form of damage each round. Divine Immunity vs. Mind-Affecting and the Surprised condition.
- **Mythic Action (4 uses):** May swap a healing effect to damage and a damaging effect to healing.

### Magic
Your magic overcomes a level of immunity. You may concentrate on an additional spell at once. Divine Immunity to spells of 6th level or lower.
- **Mythic Action (2 uses):** May automatically succeed a Concentration save.
- **Mythic Action (4 uses):** May absorb a spell, nullifying damage and restoring spell slots.

### Strength
Add half your HD to your Strength score, and gain Superior Advantage on Strength checks and Strength saving throws. You can lift, pull, drag, and/or push weight equal to twice your carry weight. Effects and abilities that would move you against your will are rolled against your Strength instead of the normally applicable stat.
- **Mythic Action (1 use):** May add Strength score to damage of an attack.

### Sun
As a bonus action, you may create a bright radiance that fills your aura for 1 minute. All creatures within the radius take half fire, half radiant damage equal to your HD at the start of their turn, and any undead within the radius have Disadvantage on all attack rolls and ability checks. Divine Immunity to radiant damage, Immunity II to fire damage.
- **Mythic Action (4 uses/turn):** You call forth an aspect of the Sun. You may place this 160-foot sphere anywhere within your aura *(unfinished in source draft: "as long as it...")*.

### Time
As a bonus action, you may enter a state of accelerated time, granting yourself an additional action on your turn that can be used to attack or take another action. Alternatively, you may enter a state of slowed time as a reaction, granting yourself Advantage on all attacks and ability checks until the end of your next turn. Divine Immunity to Aging Effects and Time Magic.
- **Mythic Action (6 uses):** Functions as *Time Stop*. Additionally, may rearrange the Initiative order.

### Truth
You cannot lie, and any attempt to magically detect if you're lying automatically fails. Additionally, you always know if a statement you hear is true or false, and magical disguises or illusions cannot deceive you. Divine Immunity to Illusion magic.

### Undeath
When a creature dies within your aura, heal for half of its total Hit Points. You have no limit on the number of undead you can control. Divine Immunity to Necrotic damage and Exhaustion.
- **Mythic Action (5 uses):** You fill your aura with necromantic power. All living creatures within your aura must make a saving throw *(save type TBD in draft)* or take damage equal to your HD and gain a level of exhaustion. Succeeding at this save reduces the damage to half and negates the level of exhaustion. Corpses within the area become undead, and undead heal equal to your HD.

### War
You have expertise with all weapons and may wield two, two-handed, and versatile weapons without penalty. You add half your HD to any checks regarding warfare. Lastly, your first attack each turn deals maximum damage.
- **Mythic Action (4 uses/round):** You enter a state where you connect yourself to your battlefield. You gain one additional action each round. At the start of each round, you may change your position in the initiative order.

### Water
You have a swim speed equal to twice your movement speed. You may breathe and talk underwater. You control all water within your aura: for enemies, it is considered difficult terrain; for you and your allies, you are considered under the effects of *freedom of movement*.
- **Mythic Action (6 uses):** Once per day, you may animate the water around you into a living creature. This creature is gargantuan in size and may take any form, from a hundred horses to a Kraken. It has HP equal to twice your maximum HP, and its attacks deal twice your HD in bludgeoning damage. It is immune to cold and fire damage.

### Wealth
Once per day, you may create an amount of gold equal to 1,000 times your HD. You may convert any mundane material into gold with a touch.
- **Mythic Action (4 uses):** You may create a horde of treasure equal to 100 times your HD. This horde converts any non-magical item within 60 feet into treasure for the horde. Creatures must make either a Charisma or a Wisdom saving throw or gradually convert to gold: on a 1st failure, speed is halved; a second failure turns the creature to gold.

### Wind
You gain a fly speed equal to twice your land speed. You can use a bonus action to Dash or Disengage. When moving, you can move through creatures and difficult terrain without impediment. Gains immunity to effects that would knock you prone or move you against your will.
- **Mythic Action (3 uses):** Summon a powerful gust of wind in a 120-foot cone. Each creature in that area must make a Dexterity saving throw (DC equal to your spell save DC) or take damage and be pushed 60 feet away from the divine creature. The damage is equal to the divine creature's HD and is typeless, cannot be reduced, and is considered magical for the purpose of overcoming resistances and immunities. On a critical hit, or a critically failed save, targets that fail the save are knocked prone. A successful save halves the damage and reduces the distance moved to 10 feet.

### Wine
All creatures within your aura must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw (DC equal to the god's spell save DC) or become intoxicated for 1 minute. While intoxicated, creatures suffer disadvantage on attack rolls and ability checks. Creatures that end this effect early gain a level of exhaustion, but are otherwise immune to this aura until their next short rest. Additionally, any creature that imbibes alcohol while under the effects of the aura gains temporary hit points equal to twice their level. Divine Immunity to Disease and Poisons.
- **Mythic Action (2 uses):** You may turn any liquid into a delicious wine. This wine functions as *greater restoration* and eliminates the detrimental effects of intoxication, albeit not ending the condition. This wine lasts until your next long rest.

### Winter
You ignore 2 stages of cold Immunity. You may use an action to create a 120-foot area of intense cold, dealing cold damage equal to your HD. Divine Immunity to cold.
- **Mythic Action (4 uses):** You expand your ability to cover your aura. You may target one creature within your aura; that creature must make a Dexterity saving throw or be restrained as they're covered in ice. This ice deals damage equal to half your HD each round until removed by a successful Strength check.

### Wisdom
+15 Wisdom. You may use an action to gain an understanding of a character/creature's class levels, supernatural abilities, resistances, and immunities. You may hold this understanding for a number of targets equal to your Wisdom modifier.
- **Mythic Action (2 uses):** You may spend an action to gain Superior Advantage against any or every target you have an understanding of until your next turn.

### Wrath
+5 Constitution. Add a bonus to damage rolls equal to half of HD (rounded up). Gain an extra action on a roll of 20 on any d20 roll, which can only be used to attack or deal damage. Divine Immunity to Enchantments.
- **Mythic Action (3 uses):** When you take damage, deal equal damage to the source.

### Youth
Regeneration equal to HD. You may spend an action to recover the use of a limited, per-day class feature. Divine Immunity to Aging Effects.
- **Mythic Action (9 uses):** You may target one creature within 30 feet, restoring its youth and vitality. This restores all of their lost hit points, removes all status effects, and reduces their age by `1d100` years. If the creature is below its natural age limit (e.g., an elf under 750 years old), then its lifespan is extended by 10% rounded up. This ability may only be used on the same target once per year.
