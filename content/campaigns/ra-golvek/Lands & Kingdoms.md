# Chapter 3: Lands, Continents & Realms

Ra Gol'vek is divided into **four major continents**, anchored alongside **bridged Deitric Realms** and encroaching planar borderlands born from the First Fracture and the Godly Wars.

---

## Deitric & Planar Realms

When the pantheons signed the **Entente of Divine Concordance**, most Gods withdrew into sovereign realms reflecting their image and bridged to the Prime Material:

- **Daidōis:** Realm of balance, bamboo forests, soaring pagodas, and mountain order where deities of Dragons and Emperors reside.
- **Graikorioi:** Immortal domain of ageless, covetous Hellenic divinities—marble cities, heroic contests, feasts, and deadly labyrinths.
- **Metu Neter:** Endless deserts and towering pyramids beneath **Amun Ra**, governed by deities as old as memory and eternal trials of the underworld.
- **Norse (Valhalla & Yggdrasil):** Frost and fire bound by the World Tree Yggdrasil, where honored dead feast alongside **Tyr** and **Thor** awaiting Ragnarok.
- **Omneris:** A dark domain ruled by ascended mortals who seized godhood through soul magic, cruel pacts, and forbidden ritual.
- **Plia Dral:** The forsaken refuge of forgotten deities whose mortal worship died out—home to the largest dragon population tied to Ra Gol'vek.
- **Shaivi:** Lush, vibrant realm of karmic divinities locked in the eternal dance of creation, destruction, and rebirth.
- **The Faewylds:** Wild, chaotic border-plane where time bends, logic unravels, and fey once altered early mortals into the **Aelves**.
- **Kingdoms of Night (The Shadowlands):** Carved loose from the Plane of Shadow by **Raz'a Noct**; ruled by **Pure-Blooded Vampires** bridging life and negative energy.

---

## 1. Exoria — The Lost Continent of Eternal War

Sealed behind ancient divine and mortal wards since the failed rebellion against the **Entente of Divine Concordance**, **Exoria** is the most dangerous landmass on the Prime Material—trapped in golden mists and perpetual warfare.

- **Everforge Peaks:** Continuously erupting northern volcanoes; legends claim one of Ares's great weapons lies sealed deep beneath, guarded by divine beasts and war-forged sentinels.
- **Wailing Wastes:** Eastern shifting dunes haunted by restless dead and nomadic exile-worshippers; hides the **Maze of Astraeus**, the sole remaining fragment of the **Loom of Destiny**.
- **Godsbane Expanse:** Southern battlefields where mortal armies, avatars, and exiled gods clash endlessly; souls slain here bind to the soil in perpetual combat.
- **Exiled Divinities:**
  - **Ares** commands continental campaigns to break the Entente wards.
  - **Kratos** trains brutal champions within the ruined **Shattered Colosseum**.
  - **Eris** twists local reality around the **Laughing Cathedrals**.

---

## 2. Omathu — Great Cities & Cursed Borderlands

- **Arkigike, the Jewel of Omathu:** Seven/nine spire-citadels housing Nexus Gates, guildhalls, and the **High Council** of immortal **Archlords** descended from **Heyl'el's** accord. Built upon *Hey-lel Crystal Steel and Stone* distilled from residual Archlord power.
- **The Cursed Northlands:** War-torn city-states and bleeding skies cursed after a former high king struck a dark bargain for power; eternally haunted battlegrounds hiding pre-curse relics.
- **Grands Bois Rougeoyants:** Eastern elven capital woven directly into mountain-tall bioluminescent trees under perpetual twilight; formal allies of Arkigike who still remember the **Faerie Expansion**.

---

## 3. Ayineawa — Land of Rites & Living Magic

Unrepresented at Heyl'el's peace table, **Ayineawa** pulses with living, untamed magic shaped around blood rites and ancestral pact-traditions:

- **Quetzali (The Living Forests):** Sentient jungle where trees whisper ancient spells and the **Quetzal-Kin** guide only those accepted by the forest itself.
- **Volcanic Heights of Maq'tara:** Sacred summits where the native **Ayi** perform blood rites to keep volcanic equilibrium intact.
- **The Pit of Teotli:** A fathomless physical gateway to the Underworld in the island continent's heart.
- **The Ezuuli Reef:** Western coral expanse that drains arcane power cleanly—disabling spellcasting and stripping magic items of potency inside its waters.
- **Trenches of Manō-Nui:** Vertical oceanic abyss patrolled by gargantuan **Titan Manō** sharks guarding sunken ruins and planar rifts.

---

## 4. Ulta — The Forge of Stone & Steel

- **Pozolochen Gora:** Subterranean dwarven stronghold beneath the **Throat of Titans**, heart of **Lithomancy** and rumor-site of **Worldstone Shards** predating Ra Gol'vek's current form.
- **Neocropoli:** Clockwork rail-metropolis fusing magic and industry into **hextech**. Governed by **Artifex Voda** and exporter of **War-Forged Constructs**—now contested by awakened construct general **Titanus 87**.
- **The Wilds of Nyar'cath:** Uncharted forests and peaks haunted by **Rune Giants** and **Hexwraiths** born of failed arcane experiments.
- **The Hexcrag Isles:** Mutated eastern archipelago ravaged by raw ley surges and destructive **Wyrdstorms**.
