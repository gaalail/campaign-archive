# The Church of the Archangels

> *"Their authority never had to be handed down from Heaven, because Heaven had already fallen through their ground once, and left something worth building on."*

The **Church of the Archangels** stands as the most powerful religious institution in modern **Ra Gol'vek**. Unlike the Old Pantheons that withdrew into distant **Deitric Realms** under the **Entente of Divine Concordance**, the Archangels are celestial beings intrinsically bound to the **Prime Material plane**.

## Origins & Teachings

Its roots predate any formal liturgy: during **Heaven's Sundering** in the Godly Wars, Samael's rebellion tore a fragment of Heaven loose into the mortal world. Where no God bothered to claim the scarred crash site, mortals settled independently. Roughly a century after the Entente's ratification, the Archangels stepped forward to give that self-governing settlement unity and purpose.

Because Archangels refuse traditional worship—presenting themselves as stewards of purity, justice, and truth anchored to mortal reality—citizens disillusioned by distant divine politics have flocked to their banner.

## Internal Tensions

- **Old Pantheon Rivalry:** Traditionalists across **Graikorioi**, **Metu Neter**, and **Omathu** view the Church as a mortal-led upstart lacking mythic pedigree, though open warfare remains rare.
- **Radical Factions:** Within the Church's immense hierarchy operate secretive extremist cells who interpret Archangelic righteousness through harsher, covert doctrines.

## Devoted Paths & Champions

Priests and inquisitors of the Church frequently serve as [[character-options/subclasses/Cleric/Angel Caller|Angel Caller]]s or clerics of the [[character-options/subclasses/Cleric/Holy Virtues Domain|Holy Virtues Domain]], while martial champions swear the [[character-options/subclasses/Paladin/Oath of Righteousness|Oath of Righteousness]], [[character-options/subclasses/Paladin/Oath of the Saints|Oath of the Saints]], or [[character-options/subclasses/Paladin/Templar|Templar]] vows.
