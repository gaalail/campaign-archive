# Chapter 2: The Afterlife & Resurrection

> *"To be mortal is to know death—yet you stand here, alive. Again."*

## Death & Returning in Ra Gol'vek

Death is not merely a departure from life in Ra Gol'vek; it is a journey through unseen realms where Gods, demons, and mysteries older than time wait. What meets a mortal soul beyond the veil depends on the faith they held in life, the deeds they wrought, and cosmic currents beyond mortal reckoning.

For ordinary mortals, escaping death leaves an indelible mark. The first return may find the ritual clear and the threshold open, yet with every subsequent resurrection, the soul grows heavier and the threads of fate draw tauter—until returning becomes perilous, and finally impossible.

## Severed from the Loom of Fate

**You are different.**

A God intervened at the exact moment of your demise, pulling you back from the brink not by accident, but with deliberate intent. Where normal resurrection frays the weave of time, **the threads of your fate were severed cleanly**. You stand alive again with a clean slate—no scars upon the weave, no ordinary tether binding you to your written end.

That second chance makes you an **anomaly**, a blindspot in the gaze of destiny itself. The deity who rescued you seeks an instrument capable of challenging the Armageddon Ra Gol'vek is barreling toward—yet defying the natural order does not go unnoticed. As you walk again, fate and forces far beyond mortal sight begin to shift around the loose thread you represent.

## Exploring Your Character's Death (Session Zero Prompts)

Use these questions to ground who your character was before death claimed them, how returning changed them, and what they owe this second life:

### The Moment of Dying
- **How did you die?** Was it glory, sacrifice, betrayal, or futility? Did it feel inevitable, or strike without warning?
- **Did you know you were going to die?** If you saw the end approaching, what did you do in those final moments?
- **What were your last thoughts?** A memory of someone dear, an unfulfilled oath, anger, regret, or unexpected peace?
- **Who or what mattered most to you when death came?** Has your bond to that person, cause, or ideal shifted since your return?
- **What did you leave unfinished?** Does that loose end feel like a heavy debt or a renewed purpose?

### Across the Veil & Return
- **Do you remember the moment after death?** A vast emptiness, an abyssal pressure, a Deitric hall, or the whisper of the deity who reached for you?
- **Did anyone mourn you?** Did those left behind know how you fell, honor your grave, or move on believing you gone for good?
- **Do you carry guilt from your death?** Did someone who relied on you suffer because you fell?
- **How have you changed?** Are you haunted, bolder, more reckless, or fiercely guarded against dying a second time?

### Faith, Purpose & Destiny
- **How has your relationship with the Gods—and the God who raised you—changed?** Are you grateful, wary, suspicious of being a divine pawn, or bound by a quiet debt?
- **What do you fear most now?** Death itself, failure, or what waits outside the Law of Recurrence?
- **Do you still feel like the same person?** Or did crossing over leave a gap between who you were and who stands here today?
- **How will the living react when they see you?** Reverence, terror, disbelief, or suspicion at an unnatural return?
- **Do you believe you are still meant to die—or that your severed fate means the end can actually be rewritten?**
