# Adrammelech

Once invoked as an ancient Mesopotamian solar-fire deity before descending into infernal demonology, **Adrammelech** rules **Gehenna** as Archdevil of **Acedia**—stifling ambition and turning mortal striving into hollow ash.

Though not a God in the classical Deitric sense, this sovereign wields **Divine Power** rank and receives widespread mortal reverence across Ra Gol'vek.

## Divine Profile

- **Pantheon / Structure:** Avehrneol
- **Primary Title:** Grand Chancellor of Ash & Sun-Searing Sloth
- **Alignment:** Lawful Evil
- **Sin:** Acedia
- **Dominion:** Gehenna
- **Portfolios:** Fire, Evil
- **Divine Domains:** Light, Trickery
- **Holy Symbol:** Peacock-feathered sun-wheel stained with ash
- **Favored Weapon:** Mace
