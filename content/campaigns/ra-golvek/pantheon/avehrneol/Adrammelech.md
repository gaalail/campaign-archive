# Adrammelech

**Adrammelech** is an **Archdevil** of **Avehrneol**, holding the sin of **Acedia** and ruling the dominion of **Gehenna**.

Although not a God in the classical Deitric sense, Adrammelech commands **Divine Power** rank and receives sufficient mortal worship to stand among Ra Gol'vek's recognized pantheon-like structures.
