# Algaemoni

**Algaemoni** is an **Archdevil** of **Avehrneol**, holding the sin of **Impenitence** and ruling the dominion of **Peklotum**.

Although not a God in the classical Deitric sense, Algaemoni commands **Divine Power** rank and receives sufficient mortal worship to stand among Ra Gol'vek's recognized pantheon-like structures.
