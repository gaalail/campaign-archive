# Algaemoni

**Algaemoni** rules **Peklotum** as Archdevil of **Impenitence**, honoring mortals who refuse remorse and embrace cold, unyielding defiance.

Though not a God in the classical Deitric sense, this sovereign wields **Divine Power** rank and receives widespread mortal reverence across Ra Gol'vek.

## Divine Profile

- **Pantheon / Structure:** Avehrneol
- **Primary Title:** Sovereign of Hardened Hearts
- **Alignment:** Lawful Evil
- **Sin:** Impenitence
- **Dominion:** Peklotum
- **Portfolios:** Punishment, Evil
- **Divine Domains:** Order, War
- **Holy Symbol:** Iron lock pierced by a thorn
- **Favored Weapon:** Morningstar
