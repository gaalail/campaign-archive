# Mephistopheles

Summoned alongside **Faustus**'s infernal host, **Mephistopheles** governs **Infernus** as Archdevil of **Pride**, trading forbidden arcana for mortal souls.

Though not a God in the classical Deitric sense, this sovereign wields **Divine Power** rank and receives widespread mortal reverence across Ra Gol'vek.

## Divine Profile

- **Pantheon / Structure:** Avehrneol
- **Primary Title:** Cold Lord of Arcane Bargains & Hubris
- **Alignment:** Lawful Evil
- **Sin:** Pride
- **Dominion:** Infernus
- **Portfolios:** Magic, Secrets, Evil
- **Divine Domains:** Arcana, Knowledge
- **Holy Symbol:** Forked silver quill piercing a frozen flame
- **Favored Weapon:** Rapier or Quarterstaff
