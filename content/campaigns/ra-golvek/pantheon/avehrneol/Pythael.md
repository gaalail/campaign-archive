# Pythael

**Pythael** is an **Archdevil** of **Avehrneol**, holding the sin of **Despair** and ruling the dominion of **Stygia**.

Although not a God in the classical Deitric sense, Pythael commands **Divine Power** rank and receives sufficient mortal worship to stand among Ra Gol'vek's recognized pantheon-like structures.
