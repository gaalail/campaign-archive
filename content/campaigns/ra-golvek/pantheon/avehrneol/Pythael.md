# Pythael

Summoned at **Sumar**'s ruin, **Pythael** holds **Stygia** as Archdevil of **Despair**, draining courage until mortals surrender every hope.

Though not a God in the classical Deitric sense, this sovereign wields **Divine Power** rank and receives widespread mortal reverence across Ra Gol'vek.

## Divine Profile

- **Pantheon / Structure:** Avehrneol
- **Primary Title:** Lord of Frozen Regret & Hopelessness
- **Alignment:** Lawful Evil
- **Sin:** Despair
- **Dominion:** Stygia
- **Portfolios:** Winter, Fear, Darkness
- **Divine Domains:** Twilight, Grave
- **Holy Symbol:** Cracked icicle suspended above a blackened mirror
- **Favored Weapon:** Trident or Dagger
