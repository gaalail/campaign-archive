# Belial

Summoned in the fall of **Sumar**, **Belial** commands **Hutam’ir** as Archdevil of **Wrath**, rewarding ruthless subversion and white-hot vengeance.

Though not a God in the classical Deitric sense, this sovereign wields **Divine Power** rank and receives widespread mortal reverence across Ra Gol'vek.

## Divine Profile

- **Pantheon / Structure:** Avehrneol
- **Primary Title:** Prince of Lawlessness & Unbridled Fury
- **Alignment:** Lawful Evil
- **Sin:** Wrath
- **Dominion:** Hutam’ir
- **Portfolios:** Wrath, Destruction, War
- **Divine Domains:** War, Trickery
- **Holy Symbol:** Shattered iron crown burning crimson
- **Favored Weapon:** Greatsword
