# Belial

**Belial** is an **Archdevil** of **Avehrneol**, holding the sin of **Wrath** and ruling the dominion of **Hutam’ir**.

Although not a God in the classical Deitric sense, Belial commands **Divine Power** rank and receives sufficient mortal worship to stand among Ra Gol'vek's recognized pantheon-like structures.
