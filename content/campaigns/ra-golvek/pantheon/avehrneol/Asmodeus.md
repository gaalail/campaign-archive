# Asmodeus

Summoned during **Faustus**'s betrayal of **Sumar**, **Asmodeus** rules **Raz’a Maz** as Archdevil of **Lust** and master of ironclad infernal bargains.

Though not a God in the classical Deitric sense, this sovereign wields **Divine Power** rank and receives widespread mortal reverence across Ra Gol'vek.

## Divine Profile

- **Pantheon / Structure:** Avehrneol
- **Primary Title:** Supreme Lord of Infernal Contracts & Desire
- **Alignment:** Lawful Evil
- **Sin:** Lust
- **Dominion:** Raz’a Maz
- **Portfolios:** Love, Evil, Trickery
- **Divine Domains:** Trickery, Order
- **Holy Symbol:** Ruby pentagram coiled in golden chains
- **Favored Weapon:** Ruby-headed Scepter (Mace)
