# Am-Heh

**Am-Heh** is an **Archdevil** of **Avehrneol**, holding the sin of **Gluttony** and ruling the dominion of **Ikiyyah**.

Although not a God in the classical Deitric sense, Am-Heh commands **Divine Power** rank and receives sufficient mortal worship to stand among Ra Gol'vek's recognized pantheon-like structures.
