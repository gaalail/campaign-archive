# Am-Heh

Drawn from Egyptian underworld lore as the hound-headed "Devourer of Millions" dwelling in a lake of fire, **Am-Heh** rules **Ikiyyah** as Archdevil of **Gluttony**.

Though not a God in the classical Deitric sense, this sovereign wields **Divine Power** rank and receives widespread mortal reverence across Ra Gol'vek.

## Divine Profile

- **Pantheon / Structure:** Avehrneol
- **Primary Title:** The Devourer of Millions in the Lake of Fire
- **Alignment:** Lawful Evil
- **Sin:** Gluttony
- **Dominion:** Ikiyyah
- **Portfolios:** Fire, Destruction
- **Divine Domains:** Death, War
- **Holy Symbol:** Hunting hound's jaws wreathed in underworld flame
- **Favored Weapon:** Greataxe
