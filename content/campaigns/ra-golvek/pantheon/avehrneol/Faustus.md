# Faustus

**Faustus** is an **Archdevil** of **Avehrneol**, holding the sin of **Envy** and ruling the dominion of **Malebolge**.

Although not a God in the classical Deitric sense, Faustus commands **Divine Power** rank and receives sufficient mortal worship to stand among Ra Gol'vek's recognized pantheon-like structures.
In the Mortal Era, Faustus engineered the destruction of **Sumar** before **Omathu**'s intervention shattered the balance of mortal realms in the **Mortalimachy**.
