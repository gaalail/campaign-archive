# Faustus

Once a mortal priestess in **Sumar**, **Faustus** played devils against one another and purchased her second name from an **Asura**—ultimately dragging a swath of the Mesopotamian pantheon into Hell. Today she rules **Malebolge** as Archdevil of **Envy**.

Though not a God in the classical Deitric sense, this sovereign wields **Divine Power** rank and receives widespread mortal reverence across Ra Gol'vek.

## Divine Profile

- **Pantheon / Structure:** Avehrneol
- **Primary Title:** Architect of Sumar & Sovereign of Contracts
- **Alignment:** Lawful Evil
- **Sin:** Envy
- **Dominion:** Malebolge
- **Portfolios:** Secrets, Magic, Trickery
- **Divine Domains:** Arcana, Trickery, Knowledge
- **Holy Symbol:** Blood-signed scroll weighed against a priest's heart
- **Favored Weapon:** Dagger
