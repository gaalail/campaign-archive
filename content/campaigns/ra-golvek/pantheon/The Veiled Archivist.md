# The Veiled Archivist

> *"A secret kept cleanly prevents a war; a truth unveiled at the right hour topples a throne."*

In a kingdom ruled by whispers and competing guild syndicates, **The Veiled Archivist** is revered behind locked library doors and in quiet confessionals. Neither cruel nor sentimental, the Archivist records every treaty, betrayal, and ancient spell lost since Ra Gol'vek's founding—including the true origin of the curse now gnawing at the realm.

## Tenets of the Ink & Seal

- **Never Burn What Can Be Read:** Even dangerous knowledge must be cataloged and warded rather than blindly erased.
- **Measure Every Disclosure:** Information is leverage; share truth carefully, and expose hypocrisy where it rots the state.
- **Guard the Mind:** Cultivate clarity against illusions, madness, and foreign manipulation.

## Clergy & Orders

Devotees serve as court scribes, investigators, and inquisitors. Spellcasters draw upon [[character-options/subclasses/Paladin/Mysticism|Oath of Mysticism]] or [[character-options/subclasses/Paladin/Inquisition|Oath of Inquisition]], while scholars of divine reason often walk the [[character-options/subclasses/Paragon/Order of Reason|Order of Reason]].
