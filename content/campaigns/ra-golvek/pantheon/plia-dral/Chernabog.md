# Chernabog

**Chernabog**—the Black God of Slavic mythic memory—now dwells within **Plia Dral**, the forsaken refuge of forgotten deities where the largest population of dragons in Ra Gol'vek endures.

Invoked in dread to avert winter blight and dark fortune, he embodies nocturnal omens and bitter endings.

## Divine Profile

- **Pantheon / Structure:** Plia Dral
- **Primary Title:** The Black God of Shadow, Doom & Winter
- **Alignment:** Chaotic Evil
- **Portfolios:** Darkness, Night, Evil, Winter
- **Divine Domains:** Death, Twilight, Tempest
- **Holy Symbol:** Horned obsidian silhouette looming over a waning moon
- **Favored Weapon:** Greatsword or Claws
- **Worshippers:** Cults of misfortune, dark winter rites, and dragon-haunted seers of Plia Dral
