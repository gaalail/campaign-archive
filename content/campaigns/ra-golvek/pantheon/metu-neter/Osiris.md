# Osiris

**Osiris**, slain and restored through ancient devotion, rules **Metu Neter**'s underworld as promise of renewal after death and fertile harvests beside eternal rivers.

## Divine Profile

- **Pantheon / Structure:** Metu Neter
- **Primary Title:** Lord of the Underworld, Rebirth & Floodplain Fertility
- **Alignment:** Lawful Good
- **Portfolios:** Afterlife, Death, Agriculture, Life
- **Divine Domains:** Grave, Life, Nature
- **Holy Symbol:** Crossed crook and flail of emerald majesty
- **Favored Weapon:** Flail or Quarterstaff
- **Worshippers:** Judges of the dead, farmers, resurrection seekers, and righteous stewards
