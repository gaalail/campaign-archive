# Heka

**Heka** is the personification of primordial magic and healing utterance in **Metu Neter**.

In Ra Gol'vek's deepest history, **Heka** stood beside **[[campaigns/ra-golvek/pantheon/iusiurand/Athena|Athena]]** and **[[campaigns/ra-golvek/pantheon/iusiurand/Tyr|Tyr]]** to lead the united pantheons through the **Titanomachy**—and later sat at the peace table to ratify the **Entente of Divine Concordance**.

## Divine Profile

- **Pantheon / Structure:** Metu Neter
- **Primary Title:** God of Primordial Magic, Medicine & Divine Speech
- **Alignment:** Lawful Neutral
- **Portfolios:** Magic, Medicine, Knowledge, Serpents
- **Divine Domains:** Arcana, Knowledge, Life
- **Holy Symbol:** Two intertwined serpents held aloft in balanced hands
- **Favored Weapon:** Quarterstaff
- **Worshippers:** Archmages, physician-priests, spellbreakers, and stewards of the Entente
