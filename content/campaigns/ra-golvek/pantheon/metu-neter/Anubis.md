# Anubis

**Anubis** guards the passage of the dead through **Metu Neter**'s underworld trials, weighing mortal hearts against the feather of truth.

Unlike necromancers who defile corpses, his faithful protect quiet graves and guide lost spirits toward judgment.

## Divine Profile

- **Pantheon / Structure:** Metu Neter
- **Primary Title:** Jackal Guardian of Tombs & Weigher of Hearts
- **Alignment:** Lawful Neutral
- **Portfolios:** Afterlife, Death, Truth, Protection
- **Divine Domains:** Grave, Death
- **Holy Symbol:** Black jackal silhouette above balanced scales
- **Favored Weapon:** Khopesh or Quarterstaff
- **Worshippers:** Embalmers, tomb guardians, grave clerics, and mourners
