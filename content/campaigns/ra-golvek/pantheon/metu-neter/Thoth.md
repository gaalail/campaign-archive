# Thoth

**Thoth** records every judgment, spell, and celestial cycle in **Metu Neter**, serving as patron of scribes, scholars, and balanced counsel.

## Divine Profile

- **Pantheon / Structure:** Metu Neter
- **Primary Title:** Ibis Scribe of Wisdom, Writing & Cosmic Equilibrium
- **Alignment:** Lawful Neutral
- **Portfolios:** Knowledge, Wisdom, Moon, Magic
- **Divine Domains:** Knowledge, Arcana, Order
- **Holy Symbol:** Ibis quill beside a crescent moon and ink-palette
- **Favored Weapon:** Quarterstaff
- **Worshippers:** Scholars, scribes, librarians, arbiters, and arcane researchers
