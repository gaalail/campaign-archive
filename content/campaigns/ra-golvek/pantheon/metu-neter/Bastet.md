# Bastet

**Bastet** watches over thresholds, hearths, music, and joyous celebration across **Metu Neter**—fierce as a lioness when defending the innocent, yet playful and swift in peace.

## Divine Profile

- **Pantheon / Structure:** Metu Neter
- **Primary Title:** Goddess of Hearth-Protection, Cats & Joy
- **Alignment:** Chaotic Good
- **Portfolios:** Protection, Joy, Music, Hunting
- **Divine Domains:** Light, Trickery, Life
- **Holy Symbol:** Seated feline wearing a golden sun-collar
- **Favored Weapon:** Dagger or Shortsword
- **Worshippers:** Guardians of homes, dancers, scouts, and hunters of hidden vermin
