# Horus

**Horus** sweeps across **Metu Neter**'s high skies with eyes of sun and moon—symbol of rightful sovereignty and relentless vengeance against the chaos of **[[campaigns/ra-golvek/pantheon/cataclysms/Set|Set]]**.

## Divine Profile

- **Pantheon / Structure:** Metu Neter
- **Primary Title:** Falcon Lord of Kingship, Sky & Just Vengeance
- **Alignment:** Lawful Good
- **Portfolios:** Sky, Justice, War, Protection
- **Divine Domains:** War, Light, Order
- **Holy Symbol:** Eye of Horus (Wedjat) or golden falcon
- **Favored Weapon:** Khopesh or Spear
- **Worshippers:** Rulers, sky-scouts, honorable warriors, and avengers of rightful succession
