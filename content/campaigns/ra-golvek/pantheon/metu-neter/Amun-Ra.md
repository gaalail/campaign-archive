# Amun-Ra

**Amun-Ra** is the supreme **Sun God** of **Metu Neter**, the ancient Deitric realm of endless deserts and sky-piercing pyramids where life and death endure in eternal balance.

From dawn to dusk, his solar barque maintains order against chaos and underworld shadows.

## Divine Profile

- **Pantheon / Structure:** Metu Neter
- **Primary Title:** King of the Sun, Creation & Eternal Order
- **Alignment:** Lawful Good
- **Portfolios:** Sun, Creation, Life, Law
- **Divine Domains:** Light, Life, Order
- **Holy Symbol:** Solar disc encircled by a golden cobra (Uraeus)
- **Favored Weapon:** Khopesh or Mace
- **Worshippers:** Sun-priests, desert monarchs, pyramid wardens, and guardians of cosmic order
