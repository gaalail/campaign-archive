# Amun-Ra

**Amun-Ra** is the **Sun God** of **Metu Neter**, one of the oldest Deitric realms bridged to Ra Gol'vek.

Built upon endless deserts and towering pyramids stretching toward **Amun-Ra**, Metu Neter preserves an eternal equilibrium between life and death, testing mortals through underworld trials and ancient temple rites.

- **Pantheon:** Metu Neter (Egyptian)
- **Role:** Sun God
