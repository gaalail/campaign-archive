# Isis

**Isis** is revered across **Metu Neter** as weaver of protective wards, restorer of life, and mistress of secret names and benevolent magic.

## Divine Profile

- **Pantheon / Structure:** Metu Neter
- **Primary Title:** Queen of Healing, Magic, Protection & Devotion
- **Alignment:** Neutral Good
- **Portfolios:** Magic, Protection, Family, Medicine
- **Divine Domains:** Arcana, Life, Knowledge
- **Holy Symbol:** Tyet knot (Knot of Isis) or winged solar throne
- **Favored Weapon:** Quarterstaff or Dagger
- **Worshippers:** Spellcasters, healers, mothers, mariners, and devoted protectors
