# Sekhmet

**Sekhmet**, eye of solar wrath in **Metu Neter**, breathes desert fire against wicked foes—yet the same power that sends fever also commands mastery to cure it.

## Divine Profile

- **Pantheon / Structure:** Metu Neter
- **Primary Title:** Lioness of Fierce War, Desert Flame & Curing
- **Alignment:** Neutral
- **Portfolios:** War, Fire, Medicine, Wrath
- **Divine Domains:** War, Light, Life
- **Holy Symbol:** Red solar disc resting atop a roaring lioness
- **Favored Weapon:** Khopesh or Greataxe
- **Worshippers:** Fierce vanguard warriors, royal bodyguards, and battle-healers
