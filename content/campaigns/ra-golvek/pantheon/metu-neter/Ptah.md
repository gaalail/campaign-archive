# Ptah

**Ptah** is the quiet architect and master smith of **Metu Neter**, patron of sculptors, stone-masons, and builders whose craft endures across ages.

## Divine Profile

- **Pantheon / Structure:** Metu Neter
- **Primary Title:** Master Architect, Crafter & Shaper of Forms
- **Alignment:** Lawful Neutral
- **Portfolios:** Creation, Artifice, Smithing, Knowledge
- **Divine Domains:** Forge, Knowledge, Order
- **Holy Symbol:** Djed pillar crowned with a craftsman's plumb line
- **Favored Weapon:** Warhammer
- **Worshippers:** Architects, stone-carvers, metalworkers, and inventors
