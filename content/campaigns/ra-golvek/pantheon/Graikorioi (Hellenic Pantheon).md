# Graikorioi (Hellenic Pantheon)

> *"A realm of eternal contests, marble cities, feasts, and divine intrigue—whose skies still ring with the echo of heroic deeds."*

**Graikorioi** is the sovereign **Deitric Realm** inhabited by Ra Gol'vek's Hellenic divinities—tempered from templates preserved in the World Tree at dawn and reshaped across the **Titanomachy**, **Deumachy**, and **Mortalimachy**.

## Key Divinities & Role in History

- **Athena:** One of the three primary divine architects—alongside **Tyr** and **Heka**—who held the divine alliance together during the **Titanomachy** and negotiated the **Entente of Divine Concordance** with **Heyl'el**.
- **Zeus & Nike:** Alongside **Thor**, Zeus and Nike imprisoned **Typhon** beneath a mountain after **Gaia** unleashed him in fury over the Hellenes' withdrawal into Graikorioi. Still weakened from the Deumachy, Zeus barely held the sealing.
- **Exiled Kin:** Not all Hellenes accepted the Entente—**Ares**, **Kratos**, and **Eris** rebelled and remain imprisoned across the sea on **Exoria**.

## Worship & Champions

Citizens and scholars across **Omathu** venerate Graikorioi's gods through agonistic games, civic philosophy, and strategic counsel. Devoted clerics walk the [[character-options/subclasses/Cleric/Domain of Mythology|Domain of Mythology]] or [[character-options/subclasses/Cleric/Dawn Domain|Dawn Domain]], while champions embrace the [[character-options/subclasses/Paladin/Glory|Oath of Glory]], [[character-options/subclasses/Paladin/Devotion|Oath of Devotion]], or [[character-options/subclasses/Paladin/Vengeance|Oath of Vengeance]].
