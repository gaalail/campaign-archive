# Poliʻahu

**Poliʻahu** wears a mantle of quiet snow atop towering island peaks, tempering **[[campaigns/ra-golvek/pantheon/kaheawahu/Pele|Pele]]**'s molten fury with calm winter beauty.

## Divine Profile

- **Pantheon / Structure:** Kaheawahu
- **Primary Title:** Goddess of High Snow Peaks & Cool Mists
- **Alignment:** Neutral Good
- **Portfolios:** Winter, Mountains, Peace
- **Divine Domains:** Nature, Twilight
- **Holy Symbol:** White snow-cloak draped over a basalt crest
- **Favored Weapon:** Longbow or Spear
- **Worshippers:** Highland scouts, winter travelers, and rivals of volcanic excess
