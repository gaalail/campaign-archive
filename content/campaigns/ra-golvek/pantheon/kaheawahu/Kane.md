# Kāne

**Kāne** is honored in **Kaheawahu** as life-giver of dawn, forests, and fresh running waters—revered without blood sacrifice by healers and caretakers of living land.

## Divine Profile

- **Pantheon / Structure:** Kaheawahu
- **Primary Title:** Father of Creation, Fresh Water & Dawn Light
- **Alignment:** Lawful Good
- **Portfolios:** Creation, Life, Water, Sun
- **Divine Domains:** Life, Light, Nature
- **Holy Symbol:** Crystal spring pouring beneath a rising sun
- **Favored Weapon:** Quarterstaff
- **Worshippers:** Healers, farmers, storytellers, and guardians of sacred springs
