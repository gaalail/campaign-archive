# Pele

**Pele** commands lava, steam, and the raw tectonic birth of new land—revered wherever volcanic heights like **Maq'tara** burn against the sea.

## Divine Profile

- **Pantheon / Structure:** Kaheawahu
- **Primary Title:** Goddess of Volcanoes, Molten Earth & Creation
- **Alignment:** Chaotic Neutral
- **Portfolios:** Fire, Mountains, Creation, Destruction
- **Divine Domains:** Light, Tempest, Forge
- **Holy Symbol:** Crater caldera glowing with molten obsidian glass
- **Favored Weapon:** Spear or Warhammer
- **Worshippers:** Volcano pilgrims, obsidian smiths, dancers, and Ayi fire-watchers
