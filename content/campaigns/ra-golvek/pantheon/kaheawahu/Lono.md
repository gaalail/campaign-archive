# Lono

**Lono** descends on gentle clouds and rainbows to renew crops, halt warfare during sacred seasons, and bless communities with song and peace.

## Divine Profile

- **Pantheon / Structure:** Kaheawahu
- **Primary Title:** God of Fertility, Peace, Rain & Music
- **Alignment:** Neutral Good
- **Portfolios:** Agriculture, Peace, Rain, Music
- **Divine Domains:** Peace, Life, Nature
- **Holy Symbol:** White-draped crossbeam banner crowned with fern fronds
- **Favored Weapon:** Quarterstaff
- **Worshippers:** Planters, musicians, peacemakers, and festival keepers
