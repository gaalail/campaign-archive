# Kamohoaliʻi

**Kamohoaliʻi** rules the apex predators of deep trenches—guiding respectful navigators safely home across perilous seas like the **Trenches of Manō-Nui** while turning fierce jaws against despoilers.

## Divine Profile

- **Pantheon / Structure:** Kaheawahu
- **Primary Title:** Sovereign Shark God & Guide of Lost Vessels
- **Alignment:** Neutral
- **Portfolios:** Sea, Animal, Protection
- **Divine Domains:** Tempest, Nature
- **Holy Symbol:** Fin of a titanic shark cleaving dark water
- **Favored Weapon:** Trident or Spear
- **Worshippers:** Navigators crossing the Trenches of Manō-Nui, whalers, and sea-guardians
