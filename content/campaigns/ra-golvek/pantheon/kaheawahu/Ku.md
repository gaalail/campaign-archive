# Kū

**Kū** stands as towering protector of island realms—governing disciplined warfare, sacred timber harvests, and resolute leadership.

## Divine Profile

- **Pantheon / Structure:** Kaheawahu
- **Primary Title:** Lord of War, Forests, Canoe-Building & Rulership
- **Alignment:** Lawful Neutral
- **Portfolios:** War, Forests, Strength, Law
- **Divine Domains:** War, Nature, Order
- **Holy Symbol:** Carved wooden war-tiki crowned with red feathers
- **Favored Weapon:** Spear or Greatclub
- **Worshippers:** Warriors, chiefs, canoe-wrights, and island defenders
