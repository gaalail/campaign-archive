# Māui

**Māui** stands as the beloved mortal-friendly trickster and hero of **Kaheawahu**—famous for snaring the sun, hauling islands from the deep with his enchanted fishhook, and bending divine treasures toward mortal survival.

## Divine Profile

- **Pantheon / Structure:** Kaheawahu
- **Primary Title:** Trickster Culture Hero & Snarer of the Sun
- **Alignment:** Chaotic Good
- **Portfolios:** Trickery, Fire, Courage, Travel
- **Divine Domains:** Trickery, Light, War
- **Holy Symbol:** Carved bone fishhook pulling up an island
- **Favored Weapon:** Glaive or Hook-Spear
- **Worshippers:** Adventurers, sailors, craftsmen, and bold mortals defying impossible odds
