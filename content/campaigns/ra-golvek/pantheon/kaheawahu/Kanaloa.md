# Kanaloa

**Kanaloa** rules the unfathomable ocean surrounding island realms and deep trenches—patron of long-distance wayfinders and keepers of sunken mysteries.

## Divine Profile

- **Pantheon / Structure:** Kaheawahu
- **Primary Title:** Sovereign of the Open Ocean & Deep Currents
- **Alignment:** Neutral
- **Portfolios:** Sea, Water, Magic, Afterlife
- **Divine Domains:** Tempest, Knowledge, Grave
- **Holy Symbol:** Deep-sea squid or spiral ocean wave
- **Favored Weapon:** Trident or Spear
- **Worshippers:** Wayfinders, deep-sea divers, mariners, and reef wardens
