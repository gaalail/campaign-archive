# Typhon

**Typhon**—forged in Gaia's fury out of the worst monsters of mythic memory—hunted surviving deities after the Entente until **Zeus**, **[[campaigns/ra-golvek/pantheon/graikorioi/Nike|Nike]]**, and **[[campaigns/ra-golvek/pantheon/norse/Thor|Thor]]** pinned him beneath a mountain.

Still undecayed after a thousand years, he can only return whole if all **nine of his hearts** are brought together.

## Divine Profile

- **Pantheon / Structure:** Cataclysms
- **Primary Title:** Father of Monsters & Tempest-Born Titan-Slayer
- **Alignment:** Chaotic Evil
- **Binding Condition:** Typhon’s 9 hearts must remain separated
- **Portfolios:** Storms, Destruction, Fire, Fear
- **Divine Domains:** Tempest, War
- **Holy Symbol:** Nine-hearted storm-vortex ringed by serpent coils
- **Favored Weapon:** Flail or Maul
