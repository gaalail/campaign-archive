# Typhon

**Typhon** is one of the **Cataclysms**—entities that belong to no Pantheon and whose worship is **strictly forbidden** and considered catastrophic across nearly all lands of **Ra Gol'vek**.
Typhon's defeat marked the **Gigantomachy** of the Godly Wars, leaving its nine hearts severed and guarded.

## Binding & Status

- **Classification:** Cataclysm (No Pantheon)
- **Worship Status:** Forbidden across most lands
- **Binding Condition:** Typhon’s 9 hearts must remain separated
