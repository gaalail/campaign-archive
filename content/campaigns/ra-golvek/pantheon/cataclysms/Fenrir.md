# Fenrir

**Fenrir**—the monstrous wolf of Norse apocalyptic myth—is a **Cataclysm** belonging to no Pantheon, whose worship is **strictly forbidden** across nearly all lands of **Ra Gol'vek**.

**[[campaigns/ra-golvek/pantheon/iusiurand/Tyr|Tyr]]** sacrificed his hand to see the beast shackled; today only masterworks of Dwarvencraft and woven Ley magics keep Fenrir from tearing loose and devouring the world.

## Divine Profile

- **Pantheon / Structure:** Cataclysms
- **Primary Title:** The Great Wolf of Unbinding & Devouring Fate
- **Alignment:** Chaotic Evil
- **Binding Condition:** Fenrir must remain bound by Dwarvencraft and Ley magics
- **Portfolios:** Destruction, Strength, Wrath
- **Divine Domains:** War, Nature
- **Holy Symbol:** Shattered iron chain wrapped around wolf fangs
- **Favored Weapon:** Bite or Greataxe
