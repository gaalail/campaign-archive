# Fenrir

**Fenrir** is one of the **Cataclysms**—entities that belong to no Pantheon and whose worship is **strictly forbidden** and considered catastrophic across nearly all lands of **Ra Gol'vek**.

## Binding & Status

- **Classification:** Cataclysm (No Pantheon)
- **Worship Status:** Forbidden across most lands
- **Binding Condition:** Fenrir must remain bound by Dwarvencraft and Ley magics
