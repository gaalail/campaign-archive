# A'aeshtar, the First Mother

> *"Hurt past any healing the old world knew how to give, the last architect didn't die in that black flood. She drowned in it, and something climbed back out that hadn't gone under in the first place."*

**A'aeshtar** predates every Deitric pantheon in Ra Gol'vek—and stands among the **Heralds of the End** as the **Harbinger of Death**.

She was one of the **Twelve** who tore Ra Gol'vek free from a dying multiverse, and the sole architect to survive when a fragment of the Breach followed them through the cut. Eleven architects were unmade so completely that no name remained to carve in stone. **A'aeshtar** drowned in the black flood and emerged rebuilt from their chorus of dying wills, claiming the black ocean beneath the infant world just as the frost-cold **Law of Recurrence** locked death into a cycle of rebirth. As Ra Gol'vek expanded above her, A'aeshtar and her black tide were pushed down into the depths beneath creation.

## Cosmic Echoes in Year 1199 AMH

Most mortals never hear A'aeshtar's name. Yet beneath **Nyar'cath**, across the **Hexcrag Isles**, and inside forgotten prophecies naming **the Obscured** and **the Wound**, scholars fear the submerged inheritance of the First Iteration is beginning to pull against the timeline once more. Seekers who touch these cosmic margins or stand outside normal divine destiny often walk as [[character-options/subclasses/Cleric/Domain of Witchcraft|Witchcraft]] clerics or [[character-options/subclasses/Paladin/Oathless|Oathless]] / [[character-options/subclasses/Paladin/Repentance|Repentance]] wanderers.
