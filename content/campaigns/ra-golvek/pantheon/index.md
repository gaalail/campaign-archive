# Deitric Realms & Pantheons of Ra Gol'vek

> *"Each pantheon kept sovereignty over its own affairs, but sovereignty stopped meaning the right to reach into mortal lives unchecked. The Gods agreed to withdraw into Deitric realms bridged to Ra Gol'vek rather than standing on it—close enough to matter, far enough to stop being a daily threat."*

When the newborn gods rose from templates preserved in the **World Tree** and survived centuries of divine conflict—the **Drakonmachy**, **Titanomachy**, **Gigantomachy**, and **Deumachy**—what endured after the **Mortalimachy** was codified in the **Entente of Divine Concordance**. Under that treaty (now in **Year 1199 AMH**, with 801 years remaining before its two-thousand-year sunset clause), mortals hold guaranteed freedom of devotion, while most pantheons govern from bridged **Deitric Realms** overseen by a **Council of Pantheons**.

## Traditions & Cosmic Powers

### The Prime-Tied Church
- **[[campaigns/ra-golvek/pantheon/The Church of the Archangels|The Church of the Archangels]]** — Built atop Heaven's fallen scar; guided by Prime-Material Archangels who eschew traditional worship.

### Bridged Deitric Realms (The Old Pantheons)
- **[[campaigns/ra-golvek/pantheon/Graikorioi (Hellenic Pantheon)|Graikorioi (Hellenic Pantheon)]]** — **Athena**, **Zeus**, **Nike**, and **Gaia**; marble citadels, heroic contests, and Olympian treaties.
- **[[campaigns/ra-golvek/pantheon/Metu Neter (Egyptian Pantheon)|Metu Neter (Egyptian Pantheon)]]** — **Amun Ra** and **Heka**; eternal sands, underworld judgment, and sun-and-magic orthodoxy.
- **[[campaigns/ra-golvek/pantheon/Valhalla & the Norse Pantheon|Valhalla & the Norse Pantheon]]** — **Tyr**, **Thor**, and **Yggdrasil**; frost, fire, and warriors feasting until Ragnarok.
- **[[campaigns/ra-golvek/pantheon/Daidois & Shaivi (Dragon & Karmic Realms)|Daidōis & Shaivi]]** — Dragons and Emperors of *Daidōis*, alongside the karmic cycles of creation and rebirth in *Shaivi*.

### Exiles, Shadowlands & Cosmic Origins
- **[[campaigns/ra-golvek/pantheon/Exoria Exiles (Ares, Kratos & Eris)|Exoria Exiles (Ares, Kratos & Eris)]]** — Rebellious divinities imprisoned on the Lost Continent for defying the Entente.
- **[[campaigns/ra-golvek/pantheon/Raz'a Noct & the Kingdoms of Night|Raz'a Noct & the Kingdoms of Night]]** — Night-god patron of the severed Shadowlands, **Pure-Blooded Vampires**, and the dark ascendants of **Omneris**.
- **[[campaigns/ra-golvek/pantheon/A'aeshtar, the First Mother|A'aeshtar, the First Mother]]** — Sole survivor of the original Twelve, drowned in the Breach's black tide beneath Ra Gol'vek, source of the **Law of Recurrence**.
