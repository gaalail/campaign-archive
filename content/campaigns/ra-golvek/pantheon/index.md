# The Tide-Sworn & Sunken Court

> *"In Ra Gol'vek, a merchant captain seals a ledger in Vaelor's name by day, pours salt wine for Thalassa before crossing the breakwater, and locks every mirror after midnight."*

Religion across the coastal kingdom of [[campaigns/ra-golvek/index|Ra Gol'vek]] mirrors the land's divided soul: gleaming civic temples funded by rival trade houses stand a stone's throw from drowned ruins and whispered back-alley shrines. As the ancient curse deepens and alien phenomena breach mortal awareness (see [[campaigns/ra-golvek/rules/index|Ra Gol'vek Campaign Rules]]), devotion is rarely purely benevolent or simple.

---

## Civic Faith vs. Deep Covenant

- **The Guild-Sanctioned Temples:** **[[campaigns/ra-golvek/pantheon/Vaelor of the Golden Wake|Vaelor of the Golden Wake]]** and **[[campaigns/ra-golvek/pantheon/The Veiled Archivist|The Veiled Archivist]]** preside over maritime law, harbor charters, and the covert diplomacy of merchant houses.
- **The Primordial Sea:** Sailors, cliff-villages, and free captains pay wary reverence to **[[campaigns/ra-golvek/pantheon/Thalassa the Salt-Mother|Thalassa the Salt-Mother]]**, whose tides reward courage and swallow hubris alike.
- **The Spreading Blight:** Beneath flooded vaults and salt-eaten barrows, desperate sects and corrupted nobles court **[[campaigns/ra-golvek/pantheon/Malakor the Undercrowned|Malakor the Undercrowned]]**, an echo of the kingdom's ancient curse.
