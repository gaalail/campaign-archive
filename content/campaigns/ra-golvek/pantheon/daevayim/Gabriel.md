# Gabriel

**Gabriel** ("Strength of God") is the Archangel of **Temperance** and divine messenger who balances wrath with clarity and mercy.

Unlike distant Deitric pantheons, the **Daevayim** remain anchored to the **Prime Material plane** and eschew traditional worship—yet inspire Ra Gol'vek's largest religious institution, the [[campaigns/ra-golvek/The Church of the Archangels|Church of the Archangels]].

## Divine Profile

- **Pantheon / Structure:** Daevayim
- **Primary Title:** Celestial Herald of Measure, Dreams & Prophecy
- **Alignment:** Lawful Good
- **Virtue:** Temperance
- **Portfolios:** Peace, Dreams, Truth
- **Divine Domains:** Light, Knowledge, Peace
- **Holy Symbol:** Silver lily and trumpet wreathed in dawn
- **Favored Weapon:** Spear or Trumpet-horn
