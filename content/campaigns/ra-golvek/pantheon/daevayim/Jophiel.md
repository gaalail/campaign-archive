# Jophiel

**Jophiel** ("Beauty of God") embodies **Grace**, guiding artists, architects, and compassionate healers to illuminate dark ages.

Unlike distant Deitric pantheons, the **Daevayim** remain anchored to the **Prime Material plane** and eschew traditional worship—yet inspire Ra Gol'vek's largest religious institution, the [[campaigns/ra-golvek/The Church of the Archangels|Church of the Archangels]].

## Divine Profile

- **Pantheon / Structure:** Daevayim
- **Primary Title:** Archangel of Illumination, Beauty & Creative Order
- **Alignment:** Lawful Good
- **Virtue:** Grace
- **Portfolios:** Beauty, Arts, Joy
- **Divine Domains:** Light, Life
- **Holy Symbol:** Golden rose crowned with seven stars
- **Favored Weapon:** Longsword
