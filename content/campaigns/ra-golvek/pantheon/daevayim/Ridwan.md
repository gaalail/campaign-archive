# Ridwan

**Ridwan**, keeper of paradise's gates in Islamic tradition, embodies **Truth**—admitting the faithful and barring deceit at every threshold.

Unlike distant Deitric pantheons, the **Daevayim** remain anchored to the **Prime Material plane** and eschew traditional worship—yet inspire Ra Gol'vek's largest religious institution, the [[campaigns/ra-golvek/The Church of the Archangels|Church of the Archangels]].

## Divine Profile

- **Pantheon / Structure:** Daevayim
- **Primary Title:** Custodian of Paradise Gates & Unclouded Truth
- **Alignment:** Lawful Good
- **Virtue:** Truth
- **Portfolios:** Truth, Protection, Afterlife
- **Divine Domains:** Order, Life
- **Holy Symbol:** Open golden gate radiant with dawnlight
- **Favored Weapon:** Halberd
