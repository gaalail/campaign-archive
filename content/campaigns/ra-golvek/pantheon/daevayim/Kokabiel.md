# Kokabiel

**Kokabiel** ("Star of God") personifies **Diligence** and watchful study of celestial laws across endless centuries.

Unlike distant Deitric pantheons, the **Daevayim** remain anchored to the **Prime Material plane** and eschew traditional worship—yet inspire Ra Gol'vek's largest religious institution, the [[campaigns/ra-golvek/The Church of the Archangels|Church of the Archangels]].

## Divine Profile

- **Pantheon / Structure:** Daevayim
- **Primary Title:** Archangel of the Star-Vault & Tireless Vigil
- **Alignment:** Lawful Good
- **Virtue:** Diligence
- **Portfolios:** Knowledge, Sky, Time
- **Divine Domains:** Knowledge, Twilight
- **Holy Symbol:** Astrolabe etched with constellation lines
- **Favored Weapon:** Quarterstaff
