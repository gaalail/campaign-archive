# Reshef

Ancient Levantine deity of plague, pestilence, and scorching arrows, **Reshef** stands among the **Heralds of the End** as **Harbinger of Plague**.

## Divine Profile

- **Pantheon / Structure:** Heralds of the End
- **Primary Title:** Lord of Pestilence, Fever & Lightning-Arrows
- **Alignment:** Neutral Evil
- **Harbinger:** Plague
- **Portfolios:** Death, Archery, Pain
- **Divine Domains:** Death, Tempest
- **Holy Symbol:** Quiver of blackened arrows wreathed in blight
- **Favored Weapon:** Longbow
