# Mastema

Known in apocalyptic tradition as the prosecuting angel of ruin and calamity, **Mastema** stands among the **Heralds of the End** as **Harbinger of Ruin**.

## Divine Profile

- **Pantheon / Structure:** Heralds of the End
- **Primary Title:** Accusing Angel of Disaster & Mortal Testing
- **Alignment:** Lawful Evil
- **Harbinger:** Ruin
- **Portfolios:** Destruction, Fate, Evil
- **Divine Domains:** Order, Death
- **Holy Symbol:** Shattered stone pillar wrapped in black cords
- **Favored Weapon:** Flail or Longsword
