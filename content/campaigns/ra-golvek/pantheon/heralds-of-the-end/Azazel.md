# Azazel

Drawn from ancient Watcher lore as the fallen instructor who taught mortals blades, armor, and warcraft, **Azazel** stands among the **Heralds of the End** as **Harbinger of War**.

## Divine Profile

- **Pantheon / Structure:** Heralds of the End
- **Primary Title:** Fallen Watcher of Iron & Forbidden Warfare
- **Alignment:** Chaotic Evil
- **Harbinger:** War
- **Portfolios:** War, Metal, Fire
- **Divine Domains:** War, Forge
- **Holy Symbol:** Blood-stained ram skull crowned in jagged iron
- **Favored Weapon:** Greatsword
