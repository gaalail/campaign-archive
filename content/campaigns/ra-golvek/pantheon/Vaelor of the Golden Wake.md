# Vaelor of the Golden Wake

> *"A signature cast into salt water dissolves; an oath sworn before Vaelor's lantern binds vessel, cargo, and blood."*

**Vaelor** is the towering civic deity of Ra Gol'vek's port cities, depicted as a storm-cloaked navigator holding an unquenchable brass lantern in one hand and a notched mariner's blade in the other. His cathedrals double as admiralty courts and clearinghouses where merchant guild contracts gain divine witness.

## Tenets of the Chartered Sea

- **Honor the Binding Word:** Commerce and peace alike collapse without trust; break a sworn charter and forfeit the wind at your back.
- **Keep the Beacons Lit:** Shelter lawful travelers, defend coastal harbors from piracy, and preserve sea-charts for future generations.
- **Weigh Coin Against Conscience:** Prosperity forged through fair passage uplifts the realm; avarice that invites ruin invites Vaelor's storm.

## Clergy & Orders

Clerics of Vaelor wear indigo vestments trimmed in tarnished gold and oversee harbor lighthouses. Paladins sworn in his cathedrals frequently adopt the [[character-options/subclasses/Paladin/Corsair|Oath of the Corsair]], [[character-options/subclasses/Paladin/Prosperity|Oath of Prosperity]], or [[character-options/subclasses/Paladin/Throne|Oath of the Throne]], while devoted clerics often study the [[character-options/subclasses/Cleric/Holy Virtues Domain|Holy Virtues Domain]].
