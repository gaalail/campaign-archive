# Daidōis & Shaivi (Dragon & Karmic Realms)

> *"Wisdom or destruction under celestial eyes in Daidōis; creation and rebirth locked in perpetual dance across Shaivi."*

Two of Ra Gol'vek's bridged **Deitric Realms** embody cosmic equilibrium—honored across **Omathu** and beyond as ancient pillars of order and recurrence:

- **Daidōis:** A realm of natural harmony and imperial order inhabited by deities of **Dragons and Emperors**, shaped from bamboo forests, cloud-piercing peaks, and towering pagodas.
- **Shaivi:** A vibrant domain ruled by **Karmic Divinities**, locked in an endless cycle of creation, destruction, and rebirth that mirrors Ra Gol'vek's own **Law of Recurrence**.
- **Plia Dral (The Forgotten Refuge):** Alongside these living realms lies forsaken **Plia Dral**, where deities whose names faded from mortal memory endure beside the largest surviving dragon population in Ra Gol'vek.

## Worship & Champions

Monastics, scholar-diplomats, and elemental mystics seeking balance within—or mastery beyond—the cycle study the [[character-options/subclasses/Cleric/Domain of Mythology|Domain of Mythology]] or swear oaths of the [[character-options/subclasses/Paladin/Ancients|Ancients]], [[character-options/subclasses/Paladin/Mysticism|Mysticism]], or [[character-options/subclasses/Paladin/Blade|Blade]].
