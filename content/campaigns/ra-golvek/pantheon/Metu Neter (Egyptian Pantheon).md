# Metu Neter (Egyptian Pantheon)

> *"Lands as old as time, built upon endless deserts and towering pyramids that stretch toward Amun Ra, where the balance between life and death is eternal."*

**Metu Neter** is the ancient **Deitric Realm** of solar sovereignty, funerary passage, and foundational magic—recognized across **Ra Gol'vek** as one of the oldest surviving divine traditions since the **War of Templates**.

## Key Divinities & Role in History

- **Amun Ra:** The blazing sun God at the apex of Metu Neter, illuminating the eternal desert skies above pyramid temples.
- **Heka:** Master of primordial magic and one of the three crucial negotiators—alongside **Athena** and **Tyr**—who led the divine coalition in the **Titanomachy** and signed the **Entente of Divine Concordance** with mortal Archlords.

## Worship & Champions

Followings of Metu Neter uphold solemn passage for the dead and disciplined mastery of spellcraft. Clerics commonly study the [[character-options/subclasses/Cleric/Dawn Domain|Dawn Domain]] or serve as [[character-options/subclasses/Cleric/Exorcist|Exorcist]]s guarding the boundary of death, while paladins swear oaths of [[character-options/subclasses/Paladin/Mysticism|Mysticism]], [[character-options/subclasses/Paladin/Shield|Shield]], or [[character-options/subclasses/Paladin/Vigilance|Vigilance]].
