# Z

**Z** is an entity of **Divine Power** rank listed under the **Kingdoms of Night**, the shadow-bridged realm carved between the Prime Material plane and a void of negative energy.

Like other powers of the **Kingdoms of Night**, **Z** commands worship and authority comparable to a pantheon structure without standing as a traditional Deitric God.
