# Raz'a Noct

**Raz'a Noct** is a **God of Night** whose ancient intervention shaped one of Ra Gol'vek's most feared shadow-bridged territories: the **Kingdoms of Night** (also known as the **Shadowlands**).

## Founding of the Kingdoms of Night

Before planar boundaries fully hardened in the early Era of the Godly Wars, Raz'a Noct freely bestowed part of his own essence upon a favored mortal bloodline, while charmed fellow deities poured blessing after blessing onto her children. Instead of fulfillment, an unquenchable hunger awoke in her, driving her to consume the Gods' other creations one by one.

The Gods answered with a permanent curse: no afterlife would ever open for her descendants, and no God would ever look upon them beneath open sun. Unwilling to abandon his children alone beneath that sentence, Raz'a Noct tore the Prime Material plane away from the **Plane of Shadow** with his own hands. What tore loose in that wound became the **Kingdoms of Night**—where her cursed line became the first **Pure-Blooded Vampires**.
