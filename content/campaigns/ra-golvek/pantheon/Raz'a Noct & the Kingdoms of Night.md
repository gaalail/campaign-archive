# Raz'a Noct & the Kingdoms of Night

> *"Unwilling to let his children stand alone under that sentence, Raz'a Noct cut the Prime Material plane away from the Plane of Shadow with his own hands."*

During the early **Godly Wars**, when planar boundaries had not yet hardened, **Raz'a Noct** bestowed his divine essence upon a favored mortal bloodline. When unfillable hunger drove her to devour other divine creations, the assembly cursed her descendants: no afterlife would open to them, and no God would ever look upon them under open sun.

Rather than abandon them, Raz'a Noct tore a border-realm loose between the Prime Material and the Plane of Shadow—the **Kingdoms of Night** (or Shadowlands)—where his line became the first **Pure-Blooded Vampires**.

## Dark Ascendants & Regional Pact-Powers

Alongside Raz'a Noct's shadow-carved sovereignty lies **Omneris**, the dark Deitric realm inhabited by mortals who achieved godhood through soul magic and cruel pacts—echoing the path once carved in blood by **Faustus** at the Fall of Sumar. Occultists drawn to these shadow and soul traditions often follow the [[character-options/subclasses/Cleric/Domain of Witchcraft|Domain of Witchcraft]] or swear the [[character-options/subclasses/Paladin/Oath of the Coven|Oath of the Coven]], [[character-options/subclasses/Paladin/Blackguard|Blackguard]], or [[character-options/subclasses/Paladin/Oath of the Hellbound|Oath of the Hellbound]].
