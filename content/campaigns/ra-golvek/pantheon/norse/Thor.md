# Thor

**Thor** rides storm-clouds across the **Norse** realm with his hammer, defending gods and mortals alike against giants and monsters.

Following the **Entente of Divine Concordance**, Thor joined **Zeus** and **[[campaigns/ra-golvek/pantheon/graikorioi/Nike|Nike]]** to crush and imprison the rampaging Cataclysm **[[campaigns/ra-golvek/pantheon/cataclysms/Typhon|Typhon]]** beneath a mountain.

## Divine Profile

- **Pantheon / Structure:** Norse
- **Primary Title:** Thunderer, Defender of Mortals & Hammer-Wielder
- **Alignment:** Chaotic Good
- **Portfolios:** Storms, Strength, Protection, Battle
- **Divine Domains:** Tempest, War, Light
- **Holy Symbol:** Short-handled warhammer (Mjölnir)
- **Favored Weapon:** Warhammer (Mjölnir)
- **Worshippers:** Soldiers, blacksmiths, farmers, sailors, and giant-slayers
