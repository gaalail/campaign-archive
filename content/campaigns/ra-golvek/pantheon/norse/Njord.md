# Njord

**Njord** calms northern gales and fills fishing nets, invoked by every captain setting out across icy fjords or stormy trade lanes.

## Divine Profile

- **Pantheon / Structure:** Norse
- **Primary Title:** Lord of Calm Seas, Fair Winds & Coastal Prosperity
- **Alignment:** Neutral Good
- **Portfolios:** Sea, Wind, Wealth, Peace
- **Divine Domains:** Tempest, Nature
- **Holy Symbol:** Wooden ship prow crowned with golden coins
- **Favored Weapon:** Spear or Trident
- **Worshippers:** Sailors, fishermen, coastal merchants, and fjord-dwellers
