# Odin

**Odin**, the Allfather perched atop **Yggdrasil**, sacrificed an eye and hung upon the World Tree to win the secret of runes.

In **Valhalla**, he gathers honored fallen warriors for the final reckoning—ever watchful for signs of **[[campaigns/ra-golvek/pantheon/cataclysms/Fenrir|Fenrir]]**'s unbinding.

## Divine Profile

- **Pantheon / Structure:** Norse
- **Primary Title:** Allfather of Wisdom, Runes, War & Prophecy
- **Alignment:** Neutral Good
- **Portfolios:** Wisdom, Rune, Magic, War, Knowledge
- **Divine Domains:** Knowledge, War, Arcana
- **Holy Symbol:** Watching blue eye flanked by twin ravens and Valknut
- **Favored Weapon:** Spear (Gungnir)
- **Worshippers:** Runecasters, skalds, kings, battle-planners, and seekers of costly wisdom
