# Hel

**Hel** rules misty **Helheim** beneath the roots of **Yggdrasil**, keeping calm, austere dominion over those who die of age, sickness, or quiet sorrow rather than battle.

## Divine Profile

- **Pantheon / Structure:** Norse
- **Primary Title:** Sovereign of Helheim & the Quiet Dead
- **Alignment:** Neutral
- **Portfolios:** Afterlife, Death, Winter
- **Divine Domains:** Grave, Death
- **Holy Symbol:** Face split half living-flesh and half bone-grey
- **Favored Weapon:** Dagger or Sickle
- **Worshippers:** Caretakers of the dead, northern healers, and souls passed outside battle
