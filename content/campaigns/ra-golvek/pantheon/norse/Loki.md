# Loki

**Loki** walks the knife-edge between ally and ruin—master of disguise, sharp-tongued bargains, and unpredictable fire.

## Divine Profile

- **Pantheon / Structure:** Norse
- **Primary Title:** God of Trickery, Fire-Wit & Dangerous Transformations
- **Alignment:** Chaotic Neutral
- **Portfolios:** Trickery, Chaos, Fire, Secrets
- **Divine Domains:** Trickery, Arcana

- **Favored Weapon:** Dagger
- **Worshippers:** Spies, illusionists, rebels, and gamblers playing dangerous games
