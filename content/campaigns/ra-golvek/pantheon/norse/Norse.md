# Norse Pantheon

The **Norse** deities rule a Deitric realm of frost and fire where **Yggdrasil**, the World Tree, binds all worlds together and warriors who died in glorious battle feast within **Valhalla**, awaiting **Ragnarok**.

While **[[campaigns/ra-golvek/pantheon/iusiurand/Tyr|Tyr]]** stands listed under **Iusiurand** and bound beings **[[campaigns/ra-golvek/pantheon/cataclysms/Fenrir|Fenrir]]**, **[[campaigns/ra-golvek/pantheon/cataclysms/Jormungandr|Jörmungandr]]**, and **[[campaigns/ra-golvek/pantheon/cataclysms/Surtr|Surtr]]** are classified separately among the **Cataclysms**, the wider Norse roster—including **Thor**, who helped trap **[[campaigns/ra-golvek/pantheon/cataclysms/Typhon|Typhon]]**—awaits detailed individual entries.

- **Tradition:** Norse Pantheon
