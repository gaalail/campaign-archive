# Freyja

**Freyja** shares the glorious dead with Odin, claiming half the fallen for **Folkvangr** while teaching the ancient prophetic sorcery of *seiðr*.

## Divine Profile

- **Pantheon / Structure:** Norse
- **Primary Title:** Lady of Love, Seiðr Magic & the Chosen Slain
- **Alignment:** Neutral Good
- **Portfolios:** Love, Beauty, Magic, Battle
- **Divine Domains:** Light, Trickery, War
- **Holy Symbol:** Amber necklace (Brísingamen) or falcon-feather cloak
- **Favored Weapon:** Shortsword or Spear
- **Worshippers:** Völvas, lovers, shieldmaidens, and travelers of Folkvangr
