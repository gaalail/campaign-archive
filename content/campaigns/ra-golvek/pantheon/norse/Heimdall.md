# Heimdall

**Heimdall** watches the bridged span between realms with eyes and ears sharp enough to hear grass grow—standing ready to sound **Gjallarhorn** if **[[campaigns/ra-golvek/pantheon/cataclysms/Surtr|Surtr]]** or **[[campaigns/ra-golvek/pantheon/cataclysms/Fenrir|Fenrir]]** ever breaks loose.

## Divine Profile

- **Pantheon / Structure:** Norse
- **Primary Title:** The White Watchman of Bifröst
- **Alignment:** Lawful Good
- **Portfolios:** Protection, Truth, Sky, War
- **Divine Domains:** Light, War, Order
- **Holy Symbol:** Curved ivory war-horn (Gjallarhorn)
- **Favored Weapon:** Longsword
- **Worshippers:** Border sentries, watchmen, navigators, and defenders of planar bridges
