# Skadi

**Skadi** hunts across frost-locked peaks of the Norse realm, honoring grit, self-reliance, and precision amid howling blizzards.

## Divine Profile

- **Pantheon / Structure:** Norse
- **Primary Title:** Goddess of Winter Peaks, Bowhunting & Skiing
- **Alignment:** Neutral
- **Portfolios:** Winter, Mountains, Hunting, Archery
- **Divine Domains:** Nature, Tempest
- **Holy Symbol:** Crossed wooden skis and silver arrowhead
- **Favored Weapon:** Longbow
- **Worshippers:** Mountain rangers, winter hunters, and northern guides
