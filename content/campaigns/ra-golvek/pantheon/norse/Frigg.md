# Frigg

**Frigg** weaves the clouds from **Fensalir**, guarding hearth, marriage, and unspoken prophecies of what may yet befall the World Tree.

## Divine Profile

- **Pantheon / Structure:** Norse
- **Primary Title:** Queen of Asgard, Weaving & Silent Foresight
- **Alignment:** Neutral
- **Portfolios:** Fate, Family, Wisdom, Protection
- **Divine Domains:** Knowledge, Light, Life
- **Holy Symbol:** Golden spindle and silver keys
- **Favored Weapon:** Quarterstaff
- **Worshippers:** Matrons, weavers, counselors, and mothers seeking protection for loved ones
