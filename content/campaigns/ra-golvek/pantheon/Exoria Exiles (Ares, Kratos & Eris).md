# Exoria Exiles (Ares, Kratos & Eris)

> *"Cast down for defying the divine accord, unable to leave the Lost Continent—yet their influence still reaches far beyond Exoria's borders."*

When the **Entente of Divine Concordance** ended the Mortalimachy, **Ares** led a faction of rebellious divinities who refused to surrender their standing right to rule mortals directly. Their uprising failed, and combined divine-and-mortal wards imprisoned them on **Exoria**.

## The Exiled Powers

- **Ares (God of War):** Commands vast continental campaigns across the **Godsbane Expanse** and guards legends of a great weapon buried beneath the **Everforge Peaks**.
- **Kratos (God of Strength & Rage):** Holds court in the ruined **Shattered Colosseum**, forging brutal champions at a terrible personal price.
- **Eris (Goddess of Chaos & Discord):** Dwells within the **Laughing Cathedrals**, warping local reality into madness and discord.

## Year 1199 AMH Threat

As **Exoria's** ancient wards degrade near the approaching sunset of the Entente, rumors spread that Ares or forces exploiting those weakening seals are slipping agents back into the wider world. Warlords and pilgrims bound to these exiles frequently walk as [[character-options/subclasses/Paladin/Conquest|Oath of Conquest]], [[character-options/subclasses/Paladin/Vengeance|Oath of Vengeance]], [[character-options/subclasses/Paladin/Blackguard|Blackguard]], or [[character-options/subclasses/Paladin/Zealot|Zealot]] champions.
