# Valhalla & the Norse Pantheon

> *"A land of frost and fire, where Yggdrasil binds together all that is—and warriors who died in glory feast until Ragnarok."*

The **Norse** Deitric Realm mirrors frost, fire, and martial destiny around the World Tree **Yggdrasil**—whose roots once held the very divine templates from which Ra Gol'vek's pantheons first emerged.

## Key Divinities & Role in History

- **Tyr:** God of war, law, and hard-won peace. Alongside **Athena** and **Heka**, Tyr led the gods' defensive alliance during the **Titanomachy** and personally negotiated the **Entente of Divine Concordance** at **Heyl'el's** table.
- **Thor:** Thunder-wielding ally of **Zeus** and **Nike** who helped trap **Typhon** beneath his mountain prison—and whose strength mortals invoke against monstrous remnants of the Godly Wars.

## Worship & Champions

In **Valhalla**, slain heroes await the final clash of Ragnarok—a prophecy hauntingly echoed in **Year 1199 AMH** by the stirring of Ra Gol'vek's **Law of Recurrence**. Clerics pursue the [[character-options/subclasses/Cleric/Holy Virtues Domain|Holy Virtues Domain]], while paladins charge into battle under the [[character-options/subclasses/Paladin/Glory|Oath of Glory]], [[character-options/subclasses/Paladin/Conquest|Oath of Conquest]], or [[character-options/subclasses/Paladin/Zealot|Zealot]] path.
