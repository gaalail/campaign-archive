# Malakor the Undercrowned

> *"Every dynasty forgets what it drowned to lay its foundations. Malakor waits patiently until the tide reaches the palace steps."*

Whispers in Ra Gol'vek's oldest districts speak of **Malakor the Undercrowned**—whether an ancient drowned monarch ascended through eldritch apotheosis or the personification of the curse consuming the kingdom from within. Where madness seeps through coastal fissures, shrines bearing his choked crown appear overnight.

## Doctrines of the Drowned Throne

- **Claim What Was Buried:** Reclaim forgotten birthrights and hidden authority regardless of whose house stands atop them.
- **Turn Fear Into Fealty:** Supernatural dread is a lever stronger than gold or steel.
- **Wait Out the Living:** Stone erodes and treaties rot; patience outlasts every rival.

## Cults & Corruptors

Open temples to Malakor are outlawed throughout Ra Gol'vek, yet cabalists practicing the [[character-options/subclasses/Cleric/Domain of Witchcraft|Domain of Witchcraft]] or champions sworn to [[character-options/subclasses/Paladin/Conquest|Oath of Conquest]], [[character-options/subclasses/Paladin/Blackguard|Blackguard]], or [[character-options/subclasses/Paladin/Oath of the Coven|Oath of the Coven]] move quietly within noble estates.
