# Poseidon

**Poseidon** is a Divinity of **Graikorioi**, ruling deep trenches and breaking surfs from golden halls while his trident shakes coastal bedrock.

Capricious as the gale, he grants swift voyages to respectful crews and swallows arrogant admirals beneath churning whitecaps.

## Divine Profile

- **Pantheon / Structure:** Graikorioi (Greek/Roman)
- **Primary Title:** Earth-Shaker, Sovereign of Seas & Storms
- **Alignment:** Chaotic Neutral
- **Portfolios:** Sea, Water, Storms, Earth
- **Divine Domains:** Tempest, Nature, War
- **Holy Symbol:** Three-pronged bronze trident rising over curling waves
- **Favored Weapon:** Trident
- **Worshippers:** Mariners, island fleets, horse-breeders, coastal fortress garrisons, and storm-callers
