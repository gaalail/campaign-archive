# Poros

**Poros** is a Divinity of **Graikorioi**, personifying the sudden flash of ingenuity that turns scarcity or impasse into an open passage.

Philosophers and frontier navigators invoke his name when supplies run dry and only wit can chart a way through.

## Divine Profile

- **Pantheon / Structure:** Graikorioi (Greek/Roman)
- **Primary Title:** Spirit of Expedience, Plenty & Ingenuity
- **Alignment:** Neutral
- **Portfolios:** Knowledge, Luck, Travel, Wealth
- **Divine Domains:** Knowledge, Trickery
- **Holy Symbol:** Golden key resting atop an unfurled scroll
- **Favored Weapon:** Dagger or Quarterstaff
- **Worshippers:** Inventors, expedition quartermasters, cunning tacticians, and scholars seeking elusive ways forward
