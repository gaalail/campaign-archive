# Hephaestus

**Hephaestus** is a Divinity of **Graikorioi**, hammering star-metal, bronze chariots, and tireless clockwork beneath volcanic peaks.

Mortals and gods alike seek his tempered blades, though his workshops remain fiercely guarded from exiles who would twist his craft toward siegecraft.

## Divine Profile

- **Pantheon / Structure:** Graikorioi (Greek/Roman)
- **Primary Title:** Master of the Forge, Volcanoes & Living Artifice
- **Alignment:** Neutral Good
- **Portfolios:** Smithing, Artifice, Fire, Metal, Creation
- **Divine Domains:** Forge, Light, Knowledge
- **Holy Symbol:** Iron anvil flanked by crossed tongs and rising sparks
- **Favored Weapon:** Warhammer
- **Worshippers:** Armorsmiths, artificers, dwarven master-crafters, siege engineers, and temple builders
