# Thalia

**Thalia** is a Divinity of **Graikorioi**, inspiring sharp satire, verdant hillsides, and joyful revelry even beneath shadow-haunted skies.

Her troupes remind war-weary kingdoms that mockery and song can topple tyrants faster than iron alone.

## Divine Profile

- **Pantheon / Structure:** Graikorioi (Greek/Roman)
- **Primary Title:** Muse of Comedy, Pastoral Joy & Blooming Grace
- **Alignment:** Chaotic Good
- **Portfolios:** Arts, Joy, Spring, Music
- **Divine Domains:** Life, Trickery, Light
- **Holy Symbol:** Smiling theatrical mask garlanded in green ivy
- **Favored Weapon:** Rapier or Whip
- **Worshippers:** Playwrights, actors, vineyard keepers, satirists, and festival troubadours
