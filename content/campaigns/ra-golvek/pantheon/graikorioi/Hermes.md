# Hermes

**Hermes** is a Divinity of **Graikorioi**, darting between bridged realms on winged sandals to carry divine dispatches and guide stray souls.

Caravans and smugglers alike toss copper coins to his roadside cairns, praying for sharp wit, fair winds, and quiet passage past watchful borders.

## Divine Profile

- **Pantheon / Structure:** Graikorioi (Greek/Roman)
- **Primary Title:** Winged Herald of Crossroads, Trade & Fortune
- **Alignment:** Chaotic Neutral
- **Portfolios:** Travel, Trickery, Luck, Wealth, Games
- **Divine Domains:** Trickery, Knowledge, Peace
- **Holy Symbol:** Winged caduceus entwined by twin golden serpents
- **Favored Weapon:** Shortsword or Dart
- **Worshippers:** Merchants, couriers, thieves, gamblers, sailors, and guides of the dead
