# Artemis

**Artemis** is a Divinity of **Graikorioi**, walking silver-lit timberlands where beasts obey ancient laws of predator and sanctuary.

She champions oath-bound stalkers, shields travelers lost beneath winter moons, and answers trespass against consecrated wilds with silent arrows.

## Divine Profile

- **Pantheon / Structure:** Graikorioi (Greek/Roman)
- **Primary Title:** Silver Lady of the Hunt, Moon & Wilds
- **Alignment:** Neutral Good
- **Portfolios:** Hunting, Moon, Archery, Forests, Animal
- **Divine Domains:** Nature, Twilight, War
- **Holy Symbol:** Silver crescent bow notched with a moon-tipped arrow
- **Favored Weapon:** Longbow
- **Worshippers:** Rangers, trackers, midwives, moonlit wardens, and protectors of sacred groves
