# Hera

**Hera** is a Divinity of **Graikorioi**, wearing the crown of high heaven and guarding sacred alliances, marriage covenants, and royal legitimacy.

Patient yet unforgiving toward broken faith, she rewards steadfast loyalty with dynastic shelter and strikes oath-breakers with relentless wrath.

## Divine Profile

- **Pantheon / Structure:** Graikorioi (Greek/Roman)
- **Primary Title:** Queen of Heaven, Marriage & Sovereign Oaths
- **Alignment:** Lawful Neutral
- **Portfolios:** Family, Law, Sky, Protection, Punishment
- **Divine Domains:** Order, Trickery, Light
- **Holy Symbol:** Peacock feather crowned by a golden royal diadem
- **Favored Weapon:** Scepter (Mace) or Spear
- **Worshippers:** Queens, matriarchs, hearth-wardens, magistrates, and guardians of sacred marriage vows
