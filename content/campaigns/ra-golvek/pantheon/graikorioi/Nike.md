# Nike

**Nike** is a Divinity of **Graikorioi**, descending upon battlefields and marble arenas to crown those whose discipline and courage win the day.

During the era following the Entente, **Nike** aided **Zeus** and **[[campaigns/ra-golvek/pantheon/norse/Thor|Thor]]** in trapping the monster **[[campaigns/ra-golvek/pantheon/cataclysms/Typhon|Typhon]]** beneath a mountain.

## Divine Profile

- **Pantheon / Structure:** Graikorioi (Greek/Roman)
- **Primary Title:** Winged Herald of Victory & Triumphant Contest
- **Alignment:** Lawful Neutral
- **Portfolios:** Battle, Courage, Games, Law
- **Divine Domains:** War, Light
- **Holy Symbol:** Outstretched golden wings cradling a laurel wreath
- **Favored Weapon:** Spear or Longsword
- **Worshippers:** Charioteers, duelists, generals, Olympic champions, and hopeful defenders under siege
