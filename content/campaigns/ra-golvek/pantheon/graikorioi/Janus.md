# Janus

**Janus** is a Divinity of **Graikorioi**, gazing simultaneously into the ashes of past cycles and the uncertain dawn ahead.

Every portal, calendar turning, and planar threshold falls beneath his quiet ward—making his priesthood deeply sensitive to shifts in the Law of Recurrence.

## Divine Profile

- **Pantheon / Structure:** Graikorioi (Greek/Roman)
- **Primary Title:** Two-Faced Keeper of Thresholds, Beginnings & Time
- **Alignment:** Lawful Neutral
- **Portfolios:** Time, Balance, Protection, Secrets
- **Divine Domains:** Order, Knowledge, Twilight
- **Holy Symbol:** Two-faced bronze medallion resting atop crossed keys
- **Favored Weapon:** Quarterstaff or Spear
- **Worshippers:** Gatekeepers, chronomancers, treaty-keepers, city watch captains, and planar navigators
