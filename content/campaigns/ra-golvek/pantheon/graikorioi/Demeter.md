# Demeter

**Demeter** is a Divinity of **Graikorioi**, sustaining mortal granaries and orchard valleys through blessed planting and bitter frost alike.

Where famine looms or winter lingers beyond measure, her clergy plead for the gentle thaw that wakes sleeping seed.

## Divine Profile

- **Pantheon / Structure:** Graikorioi (Greek/Roman)
- **Primary Title:** Mother of the Harvest, Soil & Turning Seasons
- **Alignment:** Neutral Good
- **Portfolios:** Agriculture, Earth, Fertility, Spring, Fall / Autumn
- **Divine Domains:** Nature, Life
- **Holy Symbol:** Golden sheaf of wheat bound with crimson poppies
- **Favored Weapon:** Sickle or Quarterstaff
- **Worshippers:** Farmers, druids, agrarian townsfolk, granary stewards, and keepers of seasonal rites
