# Apollo

**Apollo** is a Divinity of **Graikorioi**, guiding sunlit prophecy, harmonious song, and mortal healing beneath marble colonnades.

His twin aspects bring swift golden arrows against plague-bearers and clear prophetic verse to priests who read the shifting threads of destiny.

## Divine Profile

- **Pantheon / Structure:** Graikorioi (Greek/Roman)
- **Primary Title:** Lord of Sunlit Prophecy, Music & Healing
- **Alignment:** Neutral Good
- **Portfolios:** Sun, Music, Arts, Medicine, Archery, Truth
- **Divine Domains:** Light, Life, Knowledge
- **Holy Symbol:** Golden seven-stringed lyre framed by a laurel wreath
- **Favored Weapon:** Longbow or Shortsword
- **Worshippers:** Oracles, physicians, bards, sunlit archers, and seekers of unclouded truth
