# Mithras

**Mithras** is a Divinity of **Graikorioi**, revered in candlelit mithraea where soldiers and magistrates swear unbreakable vows of camaraderie and truth.

Invoked as witness to sealed pacts, his unconquered light burns brightest against oath-breakers and creeping darkness.

## Divine Profile

- **Pantheon / Structure:** Graikorioi (Greek/Roman)
- **Primary Title:** Lord of Unconquered Sun, Covenants & Light
- **Alignment:** Lawful Good
- **Portfolios:** Sun, Truth, Justice, Courage, Law
- **Divine Domains:** Light, Order, War
- **Holy Symbol:** Radiant solar wheel pierced by an upright straight blade
- **Favored Weapon:** Longsword or Dagger
- **Worshippers:** Legionaries, oath-brothers, subterranean mystery-initiates, and judges of honor
