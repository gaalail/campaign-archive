# Aphrodite

**Aphrodite** is a Divinity of **Graikorioi**, the Greek/Roman Deitric realm bridged to Ra Gol'vek—a realm of marble sanctuaries, cypress groves, and gilded contests.

Born from immortal sea-foam, she presides over both tender devotion and ruinous infatuation, reminding mortals that desire can raise empires or shatter treaties.

## Divine Profile

- **Pantheon / Structure:** Graikorioi (Greek/Roman)
- **Primary Title:** Goddess of Love, Desire & Eternal Beauty
- **Alignment:** Chaotic Good
- **Portfolios:** Love, Beauty, Joy, Fertility
- **Divine Domains:** Life, Light, Trickery
- **Holy Symbol:** Scallop shell cradling a pearl and blooming rose
- **Favored Weapon:** Dagger or Whip
- **Worshippers:** Lovers, courtesans, sea-born mystics, diplomats, and sculptors of mortal beauty
