# Eris

**Eris** is one of **Exoría's** supreme ruling powers, turning vanity, suspicion, and idle pride into continent-wide bloodshed.

Around her **Laughing Cathedrals**, local reality warps under bitter mirth—echoing across **[[campaigns/ra-golvek/Lands & Kingdoms|Exoria]]** as rival war-cults tear at one another for her golden favor.

## Divine Profile

- **Pantheon / Structure:** Exoría
- **Primary Title:** Goddess of Discord, Rivalry & Laughing Strife
- **Alignment:** Chaotic Evil
- **Portfolios:** Chaos, Madness, War, Trickery
- **Divine Domains:** Trickery, War
- **Holy Symbol:** Golden apple split by a grinning crescent crack
- **Favored Weapon:** Dagger or Whip
- **Worshippers:** Anarchists, scheming warmongers, revelers of ruin, and choirs of the Laughing Cathedrals
