# Cottus

**Cottus** belongs to **Exoría**, a towering Hekatonkheire whose hundred stone-hard arms hurl boulders like hailstones and split fortified ridges.

Remembered from the ancient Titanomachy as a living siege-engine, his waking steps rattle the sealed borders of the Godsbane Expanse.

## Divine Profile

- **Pantheon / Structure:** Exoría
- **Primary Title:** Hekatonkheire of Crushing Might & Tremors
- **Alignment:** Chaotic Neutral
- **Portfolios:** Strength, Earth, Mountains, Wrath
- **Divine Domains:** War, Tempest, Nature
- **Holy Symbol:** Clenched stone fist shattering a basalt mountain
- **Favored Weapon:** Maul or Greatclub
- **Worshippers:** Siege-breakers, subterranean miners, giant-kin, and storming vanguards of Exoria
