# Melinoë

**Melinoë** belongs to **Exoría**, walking moonless trenches clad in saffron shrouds as specters gather silently at her heels.

Across haunted stretches such as the Wailing Wastes, frightened sentries burn sweet incense to turn her wandering phantoms from their watchfires.

## Divine Profile

- **Pantheon / Structure:** Exoría
- **Primary Title:** Ghostly Lady of Nightmares & Restless Shades
- **Alignment:** Chaotic Neutral
- **Portfolios:** Night, Dreams, Undeath, Fear, Madness
- **Divine Domains:** Grave, Twilight, Death
- **Holy Symbol:** Half-obsidian, half-ivory torch burning with pale saffron flame
- **Favored Weapon:** Dagger or Quarterstaff
- **Worshippers:** Grave-watchers, dream-haunted mystics, spirit-mediums, and wanderers of the Wailing Wastes
