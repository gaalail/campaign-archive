# Lyssa

**Lyssa** belongs to **Exoría**, driving warriors past pain, fear, and reason until friend and foe dissolve in red haze.

Legends claim even divine hounds go wild when her shadow passes across a camp at night.

## Divine Profile

- **Pantheon / Structure:** Exoría
- **Primary Title:** Goddess of Mad Fury & Unfettered Frenzy
- **Alignment:** Chaotic Evil
- **Portfolios:** Madness, Wrath, Battle, Animal
- **Divine Domains:** War, Tempest
- **Holy Symbol:** Snarling wolf-jaw wreathed in crimson lightning
- **Favored Weapon:** Greataxe or Claws
- **Worshippers:** Berserkers, beast-kin marauders, maddened duelists, and howling warbands of Exoria
