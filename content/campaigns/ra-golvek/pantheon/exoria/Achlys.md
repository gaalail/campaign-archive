# Achlys

**Achlys** belongs to **Exoría**, the sealed continental prison-kingdom of endless divine conflict—she is the pale, weeping haze that settles across mortal eyes at the final breath.

Across Exoria's poisoned hollows and battlefield charnel pits, her breath turns wounds septic and despair into bitter resolve.

## Divine Profile

- **Pantheon / Structure:** Exoría
- **Primary Title:** Mist-Mother of Death-Haze, Poison & Sorrow
- **Alignment:** Neutral Evil
- **Portfolios:** Darkness, Death, Pain, Fear
- **Divine Domains:** Death, Grave, Twilight
- **Holy Symbol:** Ashen veil dripping dark tears over a withered lotus
- **Favored Weapon:** Dagger or Sickle
- **Worshippers:** Venom-brewers, mourners of slain legions, curse-weavers, and trench-scavengers of Exoria
