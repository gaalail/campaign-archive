# Phobos

**Phobos** belongs to **Exoría**, haunting shadowed bivouacs and tightening throats before an ambush springs.

Whereas **[[campaigns/ra-golvek/pantheon/exoria/Deimos|Deimos]]** crashes into the front line, Phobos whispers inside the helm until brave warriors flinch at their own shadows.

## Divine Profile

- **Pantheon / Structure:** Exoría
- **Primary Title:** Lord of Panic, Horror & Shattered Resolve
- **Alignment:** Chaotic Evil
- **Portfolios:** Fear, Battle, Night, Darkness
- **Divine Domains:** War, Twilight
- **Holy Symbol:** Lion-headed bronze shield etched with wide, staring eyes
- **Favored Weapon:** Shortsword or Mace
- **Worshippers:** Night-raiders, interrogators, grim mercenaries, and terror-banners of Exoria
