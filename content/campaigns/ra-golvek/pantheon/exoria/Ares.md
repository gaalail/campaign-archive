# Ares

**Ares** rules the war-torn heart of **Exoría**, refusing the tame peace of the Entente and forging eternal conflict beneath golden mists.

From his campaigns against the **Entente of Divine Concordance** wards to rumors of one of his great weapons sealed beneath the erupting **Everforge Peaks**, his banner never lowers in Year 1199 AMH.

## Divine Profile

- **Pantheon / Structure:** Exoría
- **Primary Title:** Lord of Slaughter, Bloodlust & Eternal War
- **Alignment:** Chaotic Evil
- **Portfolios:** War, Battle, Wrath, Blood, Destruction
- **Divine Domains:** War, Tempest
- **Holy Symbol:** Bloodstained bronze helm crossed by spear and burning torch
- **Favored Weapon:** Spear or Greatsword
- **Worshippers:** Exiled warlords, berserkers, siege-masters, and blood-covenants seeking to break Exoria's seals
