# Hedone

**Hedone** belongs to **Exoría**, offering intoxicating ecstasy and dream-soaked oblivion between relentless sieges.

Soldiers who survive a bloody campaign often fall into her midnight revels, trading tomorrow's vigilance for one breathless night.

## Divine Profile

- **Pantheon / Structure:** Exoría
- **Primary Title:** Mistress of Ecstasy, Revel & Fevered Delight
- **Alignment:** Chaotic Neutral
- **Portfolios:** Joy, Love, Wine, Dreams
- **Divine Domains:** Trickery, Twilight, Life
- **Holy Symbol:** Overflowing golden chalice entwined with crimson blossoms
- **Favored Weapon:** Whip or Rapier
- **Worshippers:** Hedonists, war-weary celebrants, dream-smokers, and decadent courts behind Exorian battlements
