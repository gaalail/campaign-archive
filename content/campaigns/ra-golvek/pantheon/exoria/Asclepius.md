# Asclepius

**Asclepius** belongs to **Exoría**, walking shattered battlefields not to kindle slaughter, but to stitch bone and wrest dying souls back from the grave.

Cast out in mythic memory for daring to raise the dead, his quiet sanctuaries endure as neutral ground amidst Exoria's endless war.

## Divine Profile

- **Pantheon / Structure:** Exoría
- **Primary Title:** Divine Physician & Defier of Mortality
- **Alignment:** Neutral Good
- **Portfolios:** Medicine, Life, Knowledge, Serpents
- **Divine Domains:** Life, Grave, Knowledge
- **Holy Symbol:** Single serpent coiled around a rough-hewn cypress staff
- **Favored Weapon:** Quarterstaff or Dagger
- **Worshippers:** Field surgeons, apothecaries, plague-nurses, and desperate healers tending Exoria's endless casualties
