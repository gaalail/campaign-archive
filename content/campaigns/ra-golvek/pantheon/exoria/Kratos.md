# Kratos

**Kratos** rules alongside **[[campaigns/ra-golvek/pantheon/exoria/Ares|Ares]]** and **[[campaigns/ra-golvek/pantheon/exoria/Eris|Eris]]** in **Exoría**, valuing strength tempered through unending ordeal.

Within the ruined **Shattered Colosseum**, **Kratos** personally forges brutal champions—testing whether mortal grit can outlast divine punishment.

## Divine Profile

- **Pantheon / Structure:** Exoría
- **Primary Title:** Sovereign of Brutal Strength & Shattered Chains
- **Alignment:** Lawful Neutral
- **Portfolios:** Strength, Battle, Punishment, War
- **Divine Domains:** War, Order
- **Holy Symbol:** Blazing twin chains bound around a heavy broadblade
- **Favored Weapon:** Greatsword or Twin Blades
- **Worshippers:** Gladiators, arena champions, war-monks, and disciples of the Shattered Colosseum
