# Deimos

**Deimos** belongs to **Exoría**, galloping alongside his father **[[campaigns/ra-golvek/pantheon/exoria/Ares|Ares]]** and brother **[[campaigns/ra-golvek/pantheon/exoria/Phobos|Phobos]]** to break shield-walls before steel ever meets shield.

Where Deimos's horn sounds across golden battle-fog, veteran cohorts drop their standards and trample one another in blind terror.

## Divine Profile

- **Pantheon / Structure:** Exoría
- **Primary Title:** Dread-Herald of Terror & Routed Armies
- **Alignment:** Chaotic Evil
- **Portfolios:** Fear, War, Darkness, Chaos
- **Divine Domains:** War, Death
- **Holy Symbol:** Fanged iron war-mask trailing black smoke
- **Favored Weapon:** Longsword or Flail
- **Worshippers:** Outriders, terror-knights, vanguard drummers, and cultists of Ares's iron retinue
