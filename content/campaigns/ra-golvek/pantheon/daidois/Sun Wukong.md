# Sun Wukong

**Sun Wukong** is a celebrated Divinity of **Daidōis**, laughing in the face of rigid celestial bureaucracy while shielding pilgrims from demons and tyrants.

Legends tell that even the Deumachy could never wholly pin the Great Sage down—wherever impossible odds call for bold mischief, his shadow leaps nearby.

## Divine Profile

- **Pantheon / Structure:** Daidōis (Chinese/Japanese)
- **Primary Title:** The Great Sage, Heaven's Equal & Trickster King
- **Alignment:** Chaotic Good
- **Portfolios:** Trickery, Battle, Courage, Games, Magic
- **Divine Domains:** Trickery, War, Nature
- **Holy Symbol:** Golden fillet circlet resting across an iron-banded staff
- **Favored Weapon:** Quarterstaff (Ruyi Jingu Bang)
- **Worshippers:** Acrobatic martial artists, rebellious folk-heroes, wanderers, and clever tricksters
