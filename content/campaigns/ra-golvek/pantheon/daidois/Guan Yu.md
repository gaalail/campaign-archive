# Guan Yu

**Guan Yu** is a revered Divinity of **Daidōis**, the Chinese/Japanese Deitric realm bridged to Ra Gol'vek.

Honored equally in barracks and counting-houses, he rewards sworn fellowship, honest contracts, and courage that never bends to bribery.

## Divine Profile

- **Pantheon / Structure:** Daidōis (Chinese/Japanese)
- **Primary Title:** Saint of Loyalty, Righteous War & Brotherhood
- **Alignment:** Lawful Good
- **Portfolios:** War, Justice, Law, Protection, Courage
- **Divine Domains:** War, Order
- **Holy Symbol:** Green Dragon Crescent Glaive framed by a crimson banner
- **Favored Weapon:** Glaive (Guandao)
- **Worshippers:** Sworn brothers-in-arms, honest merchants, city garrisons, and martial orders of honor
