# Lakshmi

**Lakshmi** bestows harmonious fortune, generosity, and clean prosperity wherever diligence and kindness dwell.

## Divine Profile

- **Pantheon / Structure:** Shaivi
- **Primary Title:** Goddess of Fortune, Prosperity & Radiant Grace
- **Alignment:** Lawful Good
- **Portfolios:** Wealth, Luck, Beauty, Peace
- **Divine Domains:** Light, Life, Peace
- **Holy Symbol:** Blooming pink lotus overflowing with gold coins
- **Favored Weapon:** Quarterstaff
- **Worshippers:** Honest merchants, households, patrons of charity, and diplomats
