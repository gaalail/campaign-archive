# Saraswati

**Saraswati** illuminates speech, music, and disciplined scholarship—honored wherever truth is spoken cleanly and craft is perfected.

## Divine Profile

- **Pantheon / Structure:** Shaivi
- **Primary Title:** Goddess of Eloquence, Music & Flowing Wisdom
- **Alignment:** Lawful Good
- **Portfolios:** Knowledge, Music, Arts, Wisdom
- **Divine Domains:** Knowledge, Light
- **Holy Symbol:** White swan and four-stringed veena lute
- **Favored Weapon:** Quarterstaff
- **Worshippers:** Musicians, poets, judges, scholars, and students of arcane theory
