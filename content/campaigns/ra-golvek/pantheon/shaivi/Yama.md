# Yama

**Yama** keeps exact ledger of mortal deeds in **Shaivi**, measuring justice without bribery, fear, or favoritism.

## Divine Profile

- **Pantheon / Structure:** Shaivi
- **Primary Title:** First Mortal & Dharmic Judge of the Dead
- **Alignment:** Lawful Neutral
- **Portfolios:** Afterlife, Death, Justice, Law
- **Divine Domains:** Grave, Order, Death
- **Holy Symbol:** Braided noose of fate and iron danda staff
- **Favored Weapon:** Mace (Danda)
- **Worshippers:** Judges, gravekeepers, jurists, and watchers of karmic balance
