# Durga

**Durga** bears the combined armaments of the pantheon, undefeated champion of protection and righteous war.

## Divine Profile

- **Pantheon / Structure:** Shaivi
- **Primary Title:** Invincible Warrior Mother & Fortress Against Evil
- **Alignment:** Lawful Good
- **Portfolios:** Battle, Protection, Courage, Justice
- **Divine Domains:** War, Order, Light
- **Holy Symbol:** Lion-banner bearing ten radiant weapons
- **Favored Weapon:** Trident or Spear
- **Worshippers:** Soldiers, protectors of villages, queens, and defenders of the weak
