# Shiva

**Shiva** anchors **Shaivi**'s endless dance of creation and dissolution—destroying outworn forms in compassionate detachment so life may renew cleanly.

## Divine Profile

- **Pantheon / Structure:** Shaivi
- **Primary Title:** The Destroyer, Cosmic Dancer & Lord of Ascetics
- **Alignment:** Chaotic Neutral
- **Portfolios:** Destruction, Balance, Time, Wisdom
- **Divine Domains:** Tempest, Death, Knowledge
- **Holy Symbol:** Three-pronged trishula trident and crescent moon
- **Favored Weapon:** Trident (Trishula)
- **Worshippers:** Ascetics, yogis, dancers, and philosophers of impermanence
