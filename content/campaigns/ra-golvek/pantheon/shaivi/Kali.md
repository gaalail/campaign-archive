# Kali

**Kali** unleashes terrifying grace against unstoppable monsters and spiritual pride—cutting through delusion in a heartbeat to liberate devoted souls.

## Divine Profile

- **Pantheon / Structure:** Shaivi
- **Primary Title:** Fierce Mother of Time, Liberation & Demon-Slaying
- **Alignment:** Chaotic Neutral
- **Portfolios:** Time, Death, Destruction, War
- **Divine Domains:** War, Death, Grave
- **Holy Symbol:** Curved khanda blade and garland of severed illusions
- **Favored Weapon:** Scimitar or Greatsword
- **Worshippers:** Demon-hunters, liberators, fierce ascetics, and defenders against tyrants
