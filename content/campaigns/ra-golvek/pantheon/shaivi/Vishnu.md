# Vishnu

**Vishnu** preserves karmic harmony across **Shaivi**, manifesting through righteous avatars whenever tyranny or chaos threatens to break mortal balance.

## Divine Profile

- **Pantheon / Structure:** Shaivi
- **Primary Title:** The Preserver & Keeper of Cosmic Dharma
- **Alignment:** Lawful Good
- **Portfolios:** Protection, Law, Balance, Peace
- **Divine Domains:** Order, Life, Light
- **Holy Symbol:** Spinning golden chakra discus and white conch
- **Favored Weapon:** Mace or Chakra
- **Worshippers:** Stewards of law, protectors of realms, healers, and devotees of dharma
