# Indra

**Indra** commands the **Vajra** thunderbolt and life-giving rains, leading celestial hosts against drought-demons and serpentine horrors.

## Divine Profile

- **Pantheon / Structure:** Shaivi
- **Primary Title:** Thunder-Wielding King of Heaven & Rainstorms
- **Alignment:** Chaotic Good
- **Portfolios:** Storms, Rain, Sky, War
- **Divine Domains:** Tempest, War, Light
- **Holy Symbol:** Double-ended diamond thunderbolt (Vajra)
- **Favored Weapon:** Warhammer or Spear (Vajra)
- **Worshippers:** Warriors, kings, storm-callers, and farmers praying for monsoon rains
