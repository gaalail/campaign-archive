# Ganesha

**Ganesha** stands honored at the threshold of every journey, manuscript, and enterprise in **Shaivi**, clearing hidden obstacles from sincere seekers.

## Divine Profile

- **Pantheon / Structure:** Shaivi
- **Primary Title:** Elephant-Headed Lord of Beginnings & Removed Obstacles
- **Alignment:** Lawful Good
- **Portfolios:** Wisdom, Arts, Luck, Wealth
- **Divine Domains:** Knowledge, Order, Peace
- **Holy Symbol:** Elephant-headed emblem and broken tusk quill
- **Favored Weapon:** Battleaxe or Mace
- **Worshippers:** Scholars, merchants, travelers, scribes, and founders of new ventures
