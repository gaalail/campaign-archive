# Thalassa the Salt-Mother

> *"Vaelor rules the piers and tollgates. Step one league past the headland, and you belong to the Salt-Mother."*

**Thalassa** is older than the kingdom of Ra Gol'vek itself. Sailors do not kneel to her in marble halls; they toss coins, rum, and scrimshaw trinkets over the gunwale before every voyage. She nurtures the shoals that feed coastal towns yet unleashes abyssal tempests without apology when mortals grow arrogant or overharvest the reefs.

## Tenets of the Shifting Tide

- **Respect the Horizon:** No ship, fortress, or empire is permanent before the open sea; humility keeps hulls afloat.
- **Give Back to the Deep:** Hoard nothing from a wreck without leaving a tribute for the drowned.
- **Flow Around Obstacles:** Adapt like water when rigid laws and brittle alliances crack.

## Clergy & Orders

Thalassa's tide-priests serve aboard ships, tend cliffside shrines carved from whalebone, and whisper old sea-myths through the [[character-options/subclasses/Cleric/Domain of Mythology|Domain of Mythology]]. Champions who answer her call often embrace the [[character-options/subclasses/Paladin/Corsair|Oath of the Corsair]] or [[character-options/subclasses/Paladin/Ancients|Oath of the Ancients]].
