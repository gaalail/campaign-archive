# Surya

**Surya** belongs to **Iusiurand**, driving a radiant chariot across high heaven where no perjury or hidden ambush can endure his golden gaze.

Revered both by Shaivi lineages and covenant magistrates, he seals treaties sworn in broad daylight.

## Divine Profile

- **Pantheon / Structure:** Iusiurand
- **Primary Title:** All-Seeing Charioteer of the Sun & Truth
- **Alignment:** Lawful Good
- **Portfolios:** Sun, Truth, Law, Fire, Justice
- **Divine Domains:** Light, Order, Life
- **Holy Symbol:** Twelve-spoked golden solar lotus drawn across rays
- **Favored Weapon:** Spear or Longbow
- **Worshippers:** Dawn-monks, royal judges, Vedic fire-priests, and oath-keepers beneath open skies
