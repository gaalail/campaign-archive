# Morrigan

**Morrigan** belongs to **Iusiurand**, hovering above contested crossings as crow or warrior-queen to weigh which crowns survive the bloodletting.

Neither merciful nor cruel without cause, she honors covenants struck in blood and watches threads fraying around the Loom of Fate.

## Divine Profile

- **Pantheon / Structure:** Iusiurand
- **Primary Title:** Phantom Queen of Fate, Ravens & Battlefields
- **Alignment:** Chaotic Neutral
- **Portfolios:** Fate, War, Death, Magic, Secrets
- **Divine Domains:** War, Twilight, Grave
- **Holy Symbol:** Three black ravens circling a blood-dipped spear
- **Favored Weapon:** Spear or Longsword
- **Worshippers:** Fate-seers, highland warbands, coven-witches, and heralds of impending doom
