# Tyr

**Tyr** belongs to **Iusiurand**, a recognized divine grouping in Ra Gol'vek bringing together oath-bound powers across mythic lineages.

Alongside **[[campaigns/ra-golvek/pantheon/iusiurand/Athena|Athena]]** and **Heka**, **Tyr** led the united divine front during the **Titanomachy** and negotiated the terms of the **Entente of Divine Concordance** with **Heyl'el**.
