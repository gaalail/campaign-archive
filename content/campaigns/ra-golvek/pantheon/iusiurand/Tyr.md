# Tyr

**Tyr** stands as a pillar of **Iusiurand**, revered across Valhalla and mortal courts alike as the god who laid his own hand inside **[[campaigns/ra-golvek/pantheon/cataclysms/Fenrir|Fenrir]]**'s jaws to seal a binding oath.

Alongside **[[campaigns/ra-golvek/pantheon/iusiurand/Athena|Athena]]** and **[[campaigns/ra-golvek/pantheon/metu-neter/Heka|Heka]]**, Tyr led the united divine front during the **Titanomachy** and negotiated the terms of the **Entente of Divine Concordance** with **Heyl'el**.

## Divine Profile

- **Pantheon / Structure:** Iusiurand
- **Primary Title:** One-Handed Lord of Justice, Honor & Sacrifice
- **Alignment:** Lawful Good
- **Portfolios:** Justice, Law, War, Courage, Protection
- **Divine Domains:** War, Order
- **Holy Symbol:** Balanced iron scales resting atop an upright longsword
- **Favored Weapon:** Longsword or Battleaxe
- **Worshippers:** Paladins, honest magistrates, sworn bodyguards, and Nordic champions awaiting Ragnarok
