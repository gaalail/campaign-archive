# Zagreus

**Zagreus** belongs to **Iusiurand**, embodying the fierce pulse of blood that refuses to remain chained in the dark after death.

Heroes torn back from destiny's loom occasionally find quiet resonance in his twice-born mysteries.

## Divine Profile

- **Pantheon / Structure:** Iusiurand
- **Primary Title:** Twice-Born Prince of Blood, Rebirth & Escape
- **Alignment:** Chaotic Neutral
- **Portfolios:** Afterlife, Death, Blood, Life, Madness
- **Divine Domains:** Grave, War, Death
- **Holy Symbol:** Split pomegranate crowned with bronze bull horns
- **Favored Weapon:** Spear or Trident
- **Worshippers:** Escaped captives, mystery-initiates of rebirth, defiant scions, and severed souls
