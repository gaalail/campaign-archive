# Athena

**Athena** belongs to **Iusiurand**, a recognized divine grouping in Ra Gol'vek bringing together oath-bound powers across mythic lineages.

Alongside **[[campaigns/ra-golvek/pantheon/iusiurand/Tyr|Tyr]]** and **Heka**, **Athena** forged the surviving divine alliances during the **Titanomachy** and later led the Gods' negotiations in ratifying the **Entente of Divine Concordance**.
