# Athena

**Athena** stands at the heart of **Iusiurand**, the oath-bound covenant uniting strategic deities across mythic lineages.

Alongside **[[campaigns/ra-golvek/pantheon/iusiurand/Tyr|Tyr]]** and **[[campaigns/ra-golvek/pantheon/metu-neter/Heka|Heka]]**, Athena forged the surviving divine alliances during the **Titanomachy** and later led the Gods' negotiations in ratifying the **Entente of Divine Concordance**.

## Divine Profile

- **Pantheon / Structure:** Iusiurand
- **Primary Title:** Strategist of Wisdom, Craft & Just War
- **Alignment:** Lawful Neutral
- **Portfolios:** Wisdom, War, Justice, Artifice, Knowledge, Protection
- **Divine Domains:** Knowledge, War, Order
- **Holy Symbol:** Owl perched upon an olive-wreathed spear and Gorgoneion aegis
- **Favored Weapon:** Spear or Longsword
- **Worshippers:** Tacticians, lawmakers, master architects, scholars, and disciplined city-defenders
