# Machaloth

**Machaloth** belongs to **Iusiurand**, echoing ancient Kabbalistic and night-queen traditions as a stern keeper of nocturnal compacts and forbidden ink.

Though mortals whisper of her shadowy retinue, within Iusiurand her cold word guarantees that dark bargains stay strictly within sworn bounds.

## Divine Profile

- **Pantheon / Structure:** Iusiurand
- **Primary Title:** Sovereign of Night-Covenants & Hidden Lore
- **Alignment:** Lawful Evil
- **Portfolios:** Secrets, Magic, Darkness, Night, Law
- **Divine Domains:** Arcana, Twilight, Trickery
- **Holy Symbol:** Silver crescent moon pierced by an iron serpent-key
- **Favored Weapon:** Dagger or Whip
- **Worshippers:** Coven-warlocks, nocturnal jurists, keepers of dangerous pacts, and shadow-diplomats
