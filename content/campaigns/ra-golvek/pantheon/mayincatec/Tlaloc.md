# Tlaloc

**Tlaloc** gathers clouds around high volcanoes, sending nourishing rain to living forests—or devastating lightning and floods when neglected.

## Divine Profile

- **Pantheon / Structure:** Mayincatec
- **Primary Title:** Lord of Storms, Mountain Rain & Living Crops
- **Alignment:** Neutral
- **Portfolios:** Rain, Storms, Agriculture, Water
- **Divine Domains:** Tempest, Nature
- **Holy Symbol:** Goggled rain-mask crowned with turquoise fangs
- **Favored Weapon:** Spear (Lightning Bolt)
- **Worshippers:** Farmers, rain-priests, jungle dwellers, and mountain watchers
