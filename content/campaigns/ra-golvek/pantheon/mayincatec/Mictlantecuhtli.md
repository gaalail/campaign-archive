# Mictlantecuhtli

**Mictlantecuhtli** sits enthroned in the deepest dark of the underworld—revered with solemn dread by those who descend toward **Ayineawa**'s **Pit of Teotli** to commune with ancestral spirits.

## Divine Profile

- **Pantheon / Structure:** Mayincatec
- **Primary Title:** Sovereign of Mictlan & Watcher of the Bone Pit
- **Alignment:** Lawful Neutral
- **Portfolios:** Death, Afterlife, Darkness
- **Divine Domains:** Death, Grave
- **Holy Symbol:** Flayed skull crowned with owl feathers and obsidian knives
- **Favored Weapon:** Dagger or Sickle
- **Worshippers:** Keepers of ancestral rites around the Pit of Teotli and underworld mediums
