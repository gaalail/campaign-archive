# Ixchel

**Ixchel** weaves tides, moonlight, and medicinal herbs into protective blessings—revered by healers, weavers, and keepers of sacred cycles.

## Divine Profile

- **Pantheon / Structure:** Mayincatec
- **Primary Title:** Jaguar Goddess of the Moon, Medicine & Weaving
- **Alignment:** Neutral Good
- **Portfolios:** Moon, Medicine, Water, Arts
- **Divine Domains:** Life, Twilight, Knowledge
- **Holy Symbol:** Silver crescent moon framed by coiled serpents and loom threads
- **Favored Weapon:** Quarterstaff or Dagger
- **Worshippers:** Midwives, healers, weavers, and oracle-readers
