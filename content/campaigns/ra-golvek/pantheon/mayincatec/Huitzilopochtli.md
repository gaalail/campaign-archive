# Huitzilopochtli

**Huitzilopochtli** drives back endless night with blazing fire-serpent rays—honored atop volcanic peaks like **Maq'tara** where blood rites keep cosmic equilibrium unbroken.

## Divine Profile

- **Pantheon / Structure:** Mayincatec
- **Primary Title:** Hummingbird of the South, War & Unrelenting Sun
- **Alignment:** Lawful Neutral
- **Portfolios:** Sun, War, Fire, Courage
- **Divine Domains:** War, Light
- **Holy Symbol:** Turquoise fire-serpent and hummingbird plume
- **Favored Weapon:** Macuahuitl (Greatsword) or Spear
- **Worshippers:** Ayi sun-warriors, guardians atop Maq'tara, and blood-rite champions
