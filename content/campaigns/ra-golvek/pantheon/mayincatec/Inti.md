# Inti

**Inti** warms terraced peaks and fertile valleys, revered as ancestral light-giver and compassionate patron of community stewardship.

## Divine Profile

- **Pantheon / Structure:** Mayincatec
- **Primary Title:** Golden Sun Father of Radiance & Harvest
- **Alignment:** Lawful Good
- **Portfolios:** Sun, Agriculture, Life, Justice
- **Divine Domains:** Light, Life
- **Holy Symbol:** Human-faced golden sun with sixteen rays
- **Favored Weapon:** Mace or Spear
- **Worshippers:** Terrace farmers, high-peak monarchs, and healers
