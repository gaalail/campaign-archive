# Pachamama

**Pachamama** is the breathing earth itself—honored with grateful offerings before planting, mining, or crossing high mountain passes.

## Divine Profile

- **Pantheon / Structure:** Mayincatec
- **Primary Title:** Earth Mother of Living Mountains & Fertility
- **Alignment:** Neutral Good
- **Portfolios:** Earth, Fertility, Mountains, Agriculture
- **Divine Domains:** Nature, Life
- **Holy Symbol:** Spiral carved into living volcanic stone and vine
- **Favored Weapon:** Quarterstaff
- **Worshippers:** Druids, terrace planters, miners, and living-forest guides
