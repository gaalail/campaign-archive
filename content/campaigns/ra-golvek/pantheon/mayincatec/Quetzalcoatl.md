# Quetzalcoatl

**Quetzalcoatl**, the Plumed Serpent, bridges sky and living forest—patron of crafts, astronomy, and life-giving winds across **Mayincatec** and the canopies of **Quetzali**.

## Divine Profile

- **Pantheon / Structure:** Mayincatec
- **Primary Title:** Feathered Serpent of Wind, Wisdom & Dawn
- **Alignment:** Lawful Good
- **Portfolios:** Wind, Wisdom, Arts, Serpents, Creation
- **Divine Domains:** Knowledge, Tempest, Light
- **Holy Symbol:** Winged serpent coiled around a morning star
- **Favored Weapon:** Spear or Quarterstaff
- **Worshippers:** Scholars, priests, navigators, artisans, and guides of Ayineawa
