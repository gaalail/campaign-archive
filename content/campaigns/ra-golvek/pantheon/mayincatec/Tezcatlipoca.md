# Tezcatlipoca

**Tezcatlipoca** gazes into every heart through his obsidian Smoking Mirror—toppling proud kings and testing warriors under starlit shadow.

## Divine Profile

- **Pantheon / Structure:** Mayincatec
- **Primary Title:** Smoking Mirror of Night, Fate & Shifting Fortune
- **Alignment:** Chaotic Neutral
- **Portfolios:** Night, Fate, Trickery, War, Secrets
- **Divine Domains:** Trickery, Twilight, War
- **Holy Symbol:** Polished obsidian mirror wreathed in smoke
- **Favored Weapon:** Spear or Dagger (Obsidian)
- **Worshippers:** Scouts, spies, warlords, and prophets of unseen reversals
