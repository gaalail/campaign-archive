# Mayincatec (Mayan/Incan/Aztec Pantheon)

**Mayincatec** is the Mayan, Incan, and Aztec Deitric pantheon listed among Ra Gol'vek's faiths. Across **Ayineawa**—where blood rites sustain **Maq'tara**, spells live in **Quetzali**, and the **Pit of Teotli** opens toward the underworld—Gods walk close to the living earth.
