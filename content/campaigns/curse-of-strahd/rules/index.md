# Curse of Strahd Campaign Rules

The gothic horror atmosphere of Ravenloft introduces specific environmental, mystical, and behavioral rulings for adventures within Barovia.

---

## Campaign Rules & Modifications

- **[[campaigns/curse-of-strahd/rules/alterations-to-magic|Alterations to Magic]]** — Barovian planar isolation, intercepted divinations, cosmetic spell manifestations, and Strahd's regional domain power.
- **Barovia's Curse:** Characters cannot leave Barovia willingly through any spell, portal, or physical boundary. The mists turn travelers back around.
- **Castle Proximity:** Strahd gains +1 to attack rolls and damage rolls within Castle Ravenloft or within 10 miles of its perimeter.
