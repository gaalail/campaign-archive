# Alterations to Magic

The Shadowfell's grip on Barovia alters the nature of magic within its borders. While magic functions normally in most respects, the Mists isolate the valley from the rest of the multiverse and twist spell manifestations.

## Planar Restrictions

- **Barovia's Curse:** Creatures cannot leave Barovia willingly by any means, including planar travel, teleportation, or ethereal movement.
- **Planar Travel:** Spells that summon creatures or objects from other planes function normally, but creatures summoned inside Barovia cannot return to their native planes while the barrier persists.
- **Divination & Contact:** Spells that allow contact with deities, otherworldly patrons, or extraplanar beings (such as _commune_ or _contact other plane_) are intercepted or shadowed by Strahd von Zarovich, who can choose to answer or observe the communication.

## Cosmetic Alterations

The macabre atmosphere of Barovia subtly corrupts spell appearances:

- **Conjuration & Evocation:** Bright radiant energy appears pale, ghostly, or cold; fire flickers with sickly purples or deep crimsons.
- **Necromancy & Illusion:** Illusory duplicates resemble corpses, skeletons, or decaying shadows; summoned entities take on grotesque or gaunt features.
- **Find Familiar & Steeds:** Familiars and mounts take on unnerving forms (skeletal ravens, undead mastiffs, or pale skeletal cats), though their statistics remain unchanged.

## Strahd's Domain Dominance

- **Lord of the Land:** Strahd gains a **+1 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls** while inside Castle Ravenloft or while the party is within 10 miles of the castle.
