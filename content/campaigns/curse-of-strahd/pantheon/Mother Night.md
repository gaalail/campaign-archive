# Mother Night

> *"Do not curse the dark when the road grows cold. Leave silver at the crossroads, and pray she lets you pass unseen."*

Where the Morninglord represents the faint memory of day, **Mother Night** embodies the reality of Barovia's long shadows. Revered cautiously by villagers who nail charms over their shutters at sundown—and fiercely by witches, werewolves, and wild druids—she is neither merciful nor purely cruel, answering those who respect night's dominion.

## Tenets of the Silver Dusk

- **Honor the Night's Boundary:** The dark belongs to hunters, spirits, and travelers who keep their oaths; arrogance in the woods invites ruin.
- **Witness the Hidden Cycle:** Rot feeds the soil, and endings clear ground for what follows. Preserve the natural balance against unnatural tyranny.
- **Exact What Is Owed:** Curses spoken under the moon carry weight; break a solemn pact and the shadows remember.

## Clergy & Worship in Barovia

Mother Night maintains no grand stone cathedrals. Her shrines are hollow oaks, stone cairns beneath the Berez marshes, and moonlit crossroads. Devoted spellcasters frequently walk the [[character-options/subclasses/Cleric/Domain of Witchcraft|Domain of Witchcraft]], [[character-options/subclasses/Paladin/Wilds|Oath of the Wilds]], or [[character-options/subclasses/Paladin/Vengeance|Oath of Vengeance]] against those who defile sacred groves or break ancient hospitality.

Rumors persist that Mother Night herself laid the curse upon Barovia's lycanthropes—or granted Baba Lysaga dark blessings in the swamps—making every omen in the night sky ambiguous.
