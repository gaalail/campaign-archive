# The Dark Powers

> *"Every savior who lingers long enough in Barovia begins to wonder what price the Mists might accept for a faster victory."*

The **Dark Powers** are not true gods in the mortal sense, yet their influence eclipses every temple in Ravenloft. From beyond the choking fog—and through the ancient vestiges entombed within the **Amber Temple**—they observe tormented souls, tempt heroes with impossible power, and weave Domains of Dread around those who seal their own damnation.

## Whispered Bargains

- **Power Without Restraint:** Strength to shatter a vampire lord is always available—at the cost of mercy, flesh, or sanity.
- **The Trap of Dominion:** Every gift binds the recipient closer to the Mists, turning conquerors into the valley's next eternal prisoners.
- **Secrecy & Temptation:** Few openly proclaim worship of the Dark Powers; cultists, warlocks, and fallen champions hide behind noble titles or ruined shrines.

## Champions & Warped Devotion

Mortals who strike pacts with the Dark Powers or the Amber Vestiges rarely serve out of love. Fallen paladins adopt the [[character-options/subclasses/Paladin/Blackguard|Blackguard]], [[character-options/subclasses/Paladin/Oath of the Hellbound|Oath of the Hellbound]], or [[character-options/subclasses/Paladin/Oath of the Coven|Oath of the Coven]], while warlocks draw patron whispers from entities older than Strahd's own curse.
