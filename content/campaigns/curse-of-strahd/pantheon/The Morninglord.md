# The Morninglord

> *"Even if the sun never truly clears the Svalich peaks, a single candle lit in defiance keeps the dark from claiming the room."*

The **Morninglord** is the primary benevolent faith remaining in Barovia, remembered as a compassionate solar deity whose first priests walked out of the mists bearing warmth, healing, and promises of renewal. Though Strahd's curse smothers the sky in perpetual overcast twilight, Barovians still gather at crumbling chapels in the Village of Barovia, Vallaki, and Krezk to beg for the return of true sunlight.

## Tenets of the Broken Dawn

- **Tend the Living:** Healing the wounded and sheltering the terrified is sacred defiance against the valley's despair.
- **Stand Against the Unquiet Dead:** Undead are a mockery of the cycle of dawn and rest; put them down with flame, steel, or prayer.
- **Keep Hope Unextinguished:** Even small mercies and truth spoken cleanly undermine the dread of Ravenloft.

## Clergy & Worship in Barovia

Worshippers wear bronze or wooden medallions stamped with a rising sun. Clerics and paladins who serve the Morninglord often gravitate toward the [[character-options/subclasses/Cleric/Dawn Domain|Dawn Domain]], [[character-options/subclasses/Cleric/Life|Life Domain]], [[character-options/subclasses/Cleric/Exorcist|Exorcist]], or [[character-options/subclasses/Paladin/Oath of the Saints|Oath of the Saints]].

Within Barovia, radiant magic manifests with a pale, cold luminescence rather than warm golden glare—a reminder of the [[campaigns/curse-of-strahd/rules/alterations-to-magic|Alterations to Magic]] gripping the land. Yet holy relics such as the **Symbol of Ravenkind** and the **Sunsword** remain lethal to the creatures of the night.
