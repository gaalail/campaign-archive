# Barovian Faiths & The Mists

> *"Prayers still cross the valley's mist-choked skies, though whether the Morninglord hears them—or only Strahd listens from Ravenloft's parapet—is a question best asked in whispers."*

Within the [[campaigns/curse-of-strahd/index|Domain of Dread]], religion is shaped by terror, isolation, and ancient bargains. The Mists sever Barovia from the Outer Planes, muting the presence of foreign gods and leaving mortals to cling to three enduring forces.

---

## Divine Magic in Barovia

Under the [[campaigns/curse-of-strahd/rules/alterations-to-magic|Alterations to Magic]]:

- **Divination & Intercession:** Clerics, paladins, and warlocks still regain spell slots and channel divinity normally, as the Mists do not block everyday class features. However, spells that directly contact deities or extraplanar entities (*commune*, *divination*, *contact other plane*) can be intercepted or shadowed by Count Strahd von Zarovich.
- **Outsider Faiths:** Adventurers dragged into Barovia from other worlds bring their own gods with them. Their prayers feel distant and hollow beneath the false twilight, yet their conviction sustains their magic.
- **Native Worship:** Most Barovians venerate **[[campaigns/curse-of-strahd/pantheon/The Morninglord|The Morninglord]]** for mercy and dawn, whisper offerings to **[[campaigns/curse-of-strahd/pantheon/Mother Night|Mother Night]]** out of fear, or fall prey to the whispered promises of **[[campaigns/curse-of-strahd/pantheon/The Dark Powers|The Dark Powers]]** sealed within the Amber Temple.
