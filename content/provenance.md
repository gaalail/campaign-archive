## Metadata Used

Every catalogued page uses:

- `canonical_id` for stable identity
- `entity_type` for content category
- `source` and `source_type` for provenance
- `edition` for rules compatibility
- `adaptation` for editorial treatment
- `license_status` for reuse context
- `content_status` for readiness

New expansion content must pass the archive validator before publication.
