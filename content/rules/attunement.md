# Variant Rule: Attunement Capacity

## Attunement Capacity

A character's ability to attune to magic items is determined by their **Attunement Capacity**, equal to **twice their Proficiency Bonus**.

Each magic item that requires attunement occupies a portion of this capacity, determined by its rarity.

| Item Rarity | Attunement Cost |
| :--- | :---: |
| **Common** | 1 |
| **Uncommon** | 1 |
| **Rare** | 2 |
| **Very Rare** | 3 |
| **Legendary** | 5 |
| **Mythic** | 8 |

A character may attune to any number of magic items, provided their combined **Attunement Cost** does not exceed their available **Attunement Capacity**.

---

## Attunement Requirements

- To attune to a magic item, a creature must meet any prerequisites specified by the item's description. These may include class, race, alignment, or other requirements.
- Attuning to an item requires a [[rules/resting|short rest]] focused on that item, during which the creature must be in physical contact with it. This process cannot be interrupted.
- At the end of the short rest, the creature becomes attuned to the item, provided sufficient **Attunement Capacity** is available.
- A creature cannot attune to more than one magic item during the same short rest.

---

## Ending Attunement

A creature's attunement to an item ends if any of the following conditions occur:

- The creature dies.
- Another creature attunes to the item.
- The creature voluntarily ends its attunement, requiring no action.
- The creature is more than 100 feet away from the item for at least 24 hours.
- The item is destroyed.

Additionally, certain items may specify unique conditions that end or suspend their attunement.

---

## Exceeding Attunement Capacity

- A creature cannot attune to an item if doing so would exceed its available **Attunement Capacity**.
- If a creature's **Attunement Capacity** decreases below its current expenditure, it must immediately end attunement to enough items to return within its available capacity.
- A creature cannot benefit from an item to which it is not attuned, even if it previously possessed an active attunement.

---

## Multiple Copies of an Item

A creature cannot attune to multiple copies of the same magic item unless the item's description explicitly allows it.

---

## Attunement and Item Rarity

- An item's **Attunement Cost** is determined by its rarity, regardless of its individual properties or power.
- Items that do not require attunement do not occupy **Attunement Capacity**.
