# Attunement: Epic Point Pool

This house rule replaces the standard limit of three attuned items with a scaling pool of attunement points.

## Attunement Pool

Your total attunement points equal **twice your proficiency bonus**.

The pool scales with proficiency bonus and can reach a maximum of **24 points at character level 40**.

The combined point cost of all items currently attuned to you cannot exceed your available pool.

When attuning to an item would exceed your limit, choose one option:

1. The new attunement fails.
2. Break the bond with one or more currently attuned items to free the required points.

## Item Costs

| Rarity    | Attunement Points | Design Philosophy                                                 |
| --------- | ----------------: | ----------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Common    |                 0 | Cosmetic or flavor-focused gear does not tax an epic hero's soul. |
| Uncommon  |                 1 | Minor utility or basic combat enhancements, including +1 weapons. |
| Rare      |                 2 | Standard mid-tier gear and solid defensive items.                 |
| Very Rare |                 4 | Major power spikes, including +3 weapons and top-tier armor.      |
| Legendary |                 8 | Tier-defining power. A 24-point pool supports exactly three.      |
| Artifact  |                12 | World-altering relics. A 24-point pool supports exactly two.      |

## The Dungeon Master Audit Rule

Official rarity classifications do not always match actual mechanical power. The Dungeon Master may manually audit an item and apply an **Audit Tax** when its practical power exceeds its listed rarity.

An Audit Tax increases the item's point cost. The DM should explain the adjustment before a character attempts attunement.

## Examples

- A character with 6 attunement points could attune to six Uncommon items.
- A character with 6 points could attune to three Rare items.
- A character with 24 points could attune to three Legendary items.
- A character with 24 points could attune to two Artifacts.
- A Common item costs 0 points but may still require attunement under its normal item description.
