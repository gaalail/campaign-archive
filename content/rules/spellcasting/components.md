# Spell Components

A spell's components are the physical requirements you must meet in order to cast it. Each spell's description indicates whether it requires Verbal (V), Somatic (S), or Material (M) components.

## Component Types

- **Verbal (V):** Most spells require the chanting of mystic words. A creature who is gagged or in an area of magical silence cannot cast a spell with a verbal component.
- **Somatic (S):** Spellcasting gestures might include a forceful gesticulation or an intricate set of gestures. The caster must have free use of at least one hand to perform these gestures.
- **Material (M):** Casting some spells requires particular objects, specified in the spell description.
  - A character can use a **component pouch** or a **spellcasting focus** in place of the components specified for a spell, unless a specific cost is indicated for a component.
  - If a spell states that a material component is consumed by the spell, the caster must provide this component for each casting.
  - A spellcaster must have a hand free to access a spell's material components, but it can be the same hand used to perform somatic components.
