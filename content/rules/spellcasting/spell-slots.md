# Spell Slots & Preparation

Regardless of how many spells a caster knows or prepares, they can cast only a finite number of spells before resting.

## Spell Slots

- Casting a spell requires expending a slot of the spell's level or higher.
- Cantrips are 0-level spells that can be cast indefinitely without expending spell slots.
- When a spellcaster casts a spell using a slot that is of a higher level than the spell, the spell takes on the higher-level slot for that casting and often gains enhanced effects.
- Finishing a [[rules/adventuring/resting|long rest]] restores all expended spell slots.

## Spell Preparation

> [!note] Rule Clarification: Preparing Spells
> Prepared casters (such as Clerics, Druids, and Paladins) choose their list of prepared spells whenever they finish a long rest. Swapping prepared spells takes no additional time beyond the rest itself.
