# Cover

Obstacles can provide cover during combat, making a target harder to harm. A target benefits from cover only when an attack or other effect originates on the opposite side of the cover.

## Degrees of Cover

- **Half Cover:** A target with half cover has a **+2 bonus to AC and Dexterity saving throws**. A target has half cover if an obstacle blocks at least half of its body (such as a low wall, furniture, or another creature).
- **Three-Quarters Cover:** A target with three-quarters cover has a **+5 bonus to AC and Dexterity saving throws**. A target has three-quarters cover if about three-quarters of its body is covered by an obstacle (such as a portcullis or an arrow slit).
- **Total Cover:** A target with total cover cannot be targeted directly by an attack or a spell, although some spells can reach such a target by including it in an area of effect.
