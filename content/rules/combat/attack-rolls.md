# Attack Rolls

When you make an attack, your attack roll determines whether the attack hits or misses.

## Calculating an Attack Roll

To make an attack roll, roll a **d20** and add the appropriate modifiers:

$$\text{Attack Roll} = \text{d20} + \text{Ability Modifier} + \text{Proficiency Bonus (if proficient)} + \text{Circumstantial Modifiers}$$

- **Target AC:** If the total equals or exceeds the target's Armor Class (AC), the attack hits.
- **Natural 1:** If the d20 roll is a 1, the attack automatically misses regardless of any modifiers.
- **Natural 20:** If the d20 roll is a 20, the attack automatically hits and scores a [[rules/combat/critical-hits|critical hit]].

## Advantage and Disadvantage

- **Advantage:** Roll two d20s and take the higher roll.
- **Disadvantage:** Roll two d20s and take the lower roll.
- If multiple circumstances impose both advantage and disadvantage, they cancel each other out completely, and you roll a single d20.
