# Death Saves

Whenever you start your turn with 0 hit points, you must make a special saving throw, called a death saving throw, to determine whether you creep closer to death or hang onto life.

## Standard Death Save Mechanics

- Roll a **d20** without adding any modifiers.
- **10 or Higher:** Success.
- **9 or Lower:** Failure.
- **Three Successes:** You become stable.
- **Three Failures:** You die.
- **Natural 1:** Counts as two failures.
- **Natural 20:** You immediately regain 1 hit point.
- **Taking Damage at 0 HP:** Counts as one death save failure (or two failures if from a critical hit).

> [!tip] Campaign House Rule: Stabilizing & Support Healing
>
> - **Ranged Medicine Check:** A creature can attempt a **DC 10 Wisdom (Medicine)** check to stabilize a dying ally from up to **10 feet away** (rather than requiring adjacent touch).
> - **Instant Stabilization:** Spells such as [[spells/1st-level/Healing Word|healing word]] stabilize a dying creature immediately upon casting.
