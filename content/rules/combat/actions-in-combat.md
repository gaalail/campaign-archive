# Actions in Combat

When you take your action on your turn, you can take one of the actions presented here, an action you gained from your class or another feature, or an action that you improvise.

## Standard Actions

- **Attack:** Make one melee or ranged weapon attack. Some features, such as Extra Attack, allow you to make more than one attack with this action.
- **Cast a Spell:** Cast a cantrip or spell with a casting time of 1 action, following the rules in [[rules/spellcasting/spell-slots|Spell Slots]] and [[rules/spellcasting/components|Components]].
- **Dash:** Gain extra movement for the current turn equal to your speed, after applying any active modifiers.
- **Disengage:** Your movement doesn't provoke opportunity attacks for the rest of the turn.
- **Dodge:** Until the start of your next turn, any attack roll made against you has disadvantage if you can see the attacker, and you make Dexterity saving throws with advantage. You lose this benefit if you are [[rules/conditions/incapacitated|incapacitated]] or your speed drops to 0.
- **Help:** Lend your aid to an ally. If you aid an ally in attacking an enemy within 5 feet of you, the ally's next attack roll against that target before your next turn has advantage. Alternatively, aid an ally on an ability check.
- **Hide:** Make a Dexterity (Stealth) check in an attempt to become hidden, following the rules for hiding.
- **Ready:** Wait for a particular trigger before you act using your reaction before the start of your next turn. When readying a spell, you cast it as normal but hold its energy, which requires [[rules/spellcasting/concentration|concentration]].
- **Search:** Devote your attention to finding something with a Wisdom (Perception) or Intelligence (Investigation) check.
- **Use an Object:** Interact with a second object on your turn or use a special object that requires an action.

## Bonus Actions and Reactions

- **Bonus Actions:** Various class features, spells, and abilities let you take an additional action on your turn called a bonus action. You can take only one bonus action on your turn.
- **Reactions:** A reaction is an instant response to a trigger of some kind, which can occur on your turn or on someone else's. You can take only one reaction per round.
