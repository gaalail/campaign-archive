# Flanking

Flanking is a tactical rule that rewards positioning and teamwork in close combat.

> [!tip] Campaign House Rule: +2 Flanking Bonus
>
> - **Flat Bonus:** When you and an ally are flanking an enemy, you each gain a **+2 bonus on melee attack rolls** against that enemy.
> - **No Advantage Stacking:** Flanking grants a flat +2 modifier rather than advantage, preventing advantage saturation and preserving features that grant advantage (such as Reckless Attack or Faerie Fire).
> - **Positioning Requirement:** A creature is flanked only when two allies are on **directly opposite sides or corners** of the target's space, and neither ally is [[rules/conditions/incapacitated|incapacitated]].
