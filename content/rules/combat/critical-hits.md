# Critical Hits

When you score a critical hit, you get to deal extra damage to your target.

> [!tip] Campaign House Rule: Critical Hits on 19–20
>
> - **Expanded Range:** Attack rolls of **19 or 20** on the d20 score critical hits for all characters and creatures.
> - **Maximized Base Damage:** Critical hits deal **maximum damage on all initial weapon and feature damage dice**, and then you roll **one additional damage die** of the weapon or attack's type.
> - _Design Philosophy:_ Makes combat dynamic and rewards martial strikes with consistent, impactful spikes rather than low-rolled critical damage.

## Standard Critical Rules

- Any additional damage dice from features such as Divine Smite or Sneak Attack are rolled as specified by their feature descriptions.
- Attacks against [[rules/conditions/paralyzed|paralyzed]] or [[rules/conditions/unconscious|unconscious]] targets within 5 feet are automatically critical hits on a hit.
