# Damage and Healing

Injury and death are constant threats when adventuring.

## Damage Rolls

Each weapon, spell, and harmful monster ability specifies the damage it deals. You roll the damage dice, add any modifiers, and apply the damage to your target:

- **Damage Resistance:** Damage of that type is halved against the target.
- **Damage Vulnerability:** Damage of that type is doubled against the target.
- **Damage Immunity:** The creature takes no damage of that type.

## Dropping to 0 Hit Points

When a creature drops to 0 hit points, it either dies outright or falls [[rules/conditions/unconscious|unconscious]]:

- **Instant Death:** If remaining damage equals or exceeds your hit point maximum, you die immediately.
- **Falling Unconscious:** If damage reduces you to 0 hit points without killing you outright, you fall [[rules/conditions/unconscious|unconscious]] and must make [[rules/combat/death-saves|death saving throws]].

## Temporary Hit Points

Temporary hit points are a buffer against damage rather than actual hit points. They do not stack; if you receive temporary hit points while already having some, you choose which pool to keep.
