# Grappled

- A grappled creature's speed becomes 0, and it can't benefit from any bonus to its speed.
- The condition ends if the grappler is [[rules/conditions/incapacitated|incapacitated]].
- The condition also ends if an effect removes the grappled creature from the reach of the grappler or grappling effect, such as when a creature is hurled away by the [[spells/1st-level/Thunderwave|thunderwave]] spell.
