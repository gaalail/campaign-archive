# Resting & Recovery

Adventurers cannot fight and explore indefinitely; they must rest to recover hit points, regain spell slots, and remove fatigue.

---

## Short Rest

A **Short Rest** is a period of downtime, at least **1 hour long**, during which a character does nothing more strenuous than eating, drinking, reading, and tending to wounds.

### Spending Hit Dice
At the end of a short rest, a character can spend one or more **Hit Dice** up to the character's maximum number of Hit Dice (equal to character level).
- For each Hit Die spent, the player rolls the die and adds the character's **Constitution modifier** to it.
- The character regains hit points equal to the total (minimum of 0).
- Short-rest resources (such as Warlock spell slots, Fighter's Action Surge & Second Wind, Monk Ki points, and Druid Wild Shape) recharge at the completion of a short rest.

---

## Long Rest

A **Long Rest** is a period of extended downtime, at least **8 hours long**, during which a character sleeps for at least 6 hours and performs no more than 2 hours of light activity (such as reading, talking, eating, or standing watch).

### Benefits of a Long Rest
- **Full HP Restored:** The character regains all lost hit points.
- **Hit Dice Recovery:** The character regains spent Hit Dice, up to a number equal to half of the character's total number of them (minimum of one die).
- **Spell Slots Regained:** All spell slots from long-rest spellcasting classes are completely refreshed.
- **Exhaustion Reduction:** At the end of a long rest, a character's exhaustion level is reduced by 1, provided the character has ingested some food and drink.

### Restrictions
- A character can benefit from no more than **one Long Rest per 24-hour period**.
- If the rest is interrupted by at least 1 hour of strenuous activity (walking, fighting, casting spells), the characters must begin the rest again to gain any benefit from it.
