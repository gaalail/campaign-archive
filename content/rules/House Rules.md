# Rules & House Rules

This section documents house rules, clarifications, and campaign-specific mechanics that differ from the standard Player's Handbook. Each topic is also detailed on its own modular card.

---

## Global House Rules

House rules are modifications to standard D&D 5e rules that apply across all campaigns.

### [[rules/combat/critical-hits|Critical Hits]]

- **Our Rule:** Attack rolls of **19–20** on the die are critical hits. Critical hits deal **maximum damage on all base damage dice**, plus **one additional rolled damage die**.
- **Full Card:** [[rules/combat/critical-hits|Critical Hits Rule]]
- _Reason:_ Makes combat more exciting and gives martials impactful, reliable critical strikes.

---

### [[rules/combat/death-saves|Death Saves]]

- **Our Rule:** You can make a **DC 10 Wisdom (Medicine)** check to stabilize a dying ally from **10 feet away** (instead of having to use an action adjacent to them). [[spells/1st-level/Healing Word|Healing word]] stabilizes a creature immediately.
- **Full Card:** [[rules/combat/death-saves|Death Saves Rule]]
- _Reason:_ Makes support play smoother and more valuable.

---

### [[rules/combat/flanking|Flanking]]

- **Our Rule:** Flanking grants a **+2 bonus to melee attack rolls** (not advantage). Flanking requires allies to be standing on directly opposite sides or corners of a target.
- **Full Card:** [[rules/combat/flanking|Flanking Rule]]
- _Reason:_ Action economy balance; avoids advantage saturation.

---

### [[rules/adventuring/resting|Long Rest Mechanics]]

- **Our Rule:** A long rest requires **8 hours of sleep plus 4 waking hours of light activity** (total 12 hours). Characters can take only one long rest in any 24-hour period.
- **Full Card:** [[rules/adventuring/resting|Resting Rule]]
- _Reason:_ Prevents resting immediately after every encounter; makes resource management matter.

---

### [[rules/Attunement - Epic Point Pool|Attunement: Epic Point Pool]]

- **Our Rule:** Replaces the flat 3-item attunement limit with a scaling point pool equal to **twice your proficiency bonus** (up to 24 points at character level 40), where item attunement costs scale with rarity (Common: 0, Uncommon: 1, Rare: 2, Very Rare: 4, Legendary: 8, Artifact: 12).
- **Full Card:** [[rules/Attunement - Epic Point Pool|Attunement: Epic Point Pool]]

---

## Table Clarifications

### Bonus Actions

> Class features, spells, and abilities (such as the _haste_ spell and Cunning Action) grant bonus actions. You can use only **one bonus action per turn**. Choose which one applies. Detailed in [[rules/combat/actions-in-combat|Actions in Combat]].

### Spell Preparation

> Prepared casters select their prepared spells when they finish a long rest. Changing preparation takes no additional time beyond the rest itself. Detailed in [[rules/spellcasting/spell-slots|Spell Slots & Preparation]].

### Multiclassing XP

> Multiclass characters gain levels at the standard unified XP thresholds. Character experience is shared in a single pool; there is no separate XP progression for each class.

## Campaign-Specific Rules

Campaign-specific rules apply only to their respective campaign settings:

- **[[campaigns/ra-golvek/rules/index|Ra Gol'vek Campaign Rules]]**: Wisdom saving throws against supernatural madness upon exposure to eldritch and planar phenomena.
- **[[campaigns/vyrun/rules/index|Vyrun Campaign Rules]]**: Group survival checks governed by the lowest party modifier; accelerated [[rules/conditions/exhaustion|exhaustion]] from harsh wilderness exposure.
- **[[campaigns/curse-of-strahd/rules/index|Curse of Strahd Campaign Rules]]**: [[campaigns/curse-of-strahd/rules/alterations-to-magic|Alterations to Magic]], Barovian planar isolation, and Strahd's domain attack bonuses.

---

## What's NOT a House Rule

The following are standard D&D 5e rules we do not modify:

- ✓ Ability checks, saving throws, standard attack rolls
- ✓ Spell mechanics, spell schools, and casting components
- ✓ Skill proficiencies and expertise
- ✓ Alignment (used for roleplaying context, not mechanical restrictions)
