# Rules & House Rules

This section documents house rules, clarifications, and campaign-specific mechanics that differ from the standard Player's Handbook.

---

## House Rules

House rules are modifications to standard D&D 5e rules that apply across all campaigns.

### Critical Hits

**Standard Rule:** Attack rolls of 20 on the die count as critical hits (nat 20).

**Our Rule:**

- Attack rolls of 19-20 on the die are critical hits (nat 19+)
- Critical hits deal maximum damage on damage dice, then add one additional damage die
- _Reason:_ Makes combat more exciting; gives martials better chances to shine

---

### Death Saves

**Standard Rule:** You make death saves when you drop to 0 HP. Three failures = death.

**Our Rule:**

- You can make a DC 10 Wisdom (Medicine) check to stabilize a dying ally from 10 feet away (instead of having to use an action next to them)
- Healing Word stabilizes immediately; the target doesn't need to succeed a save
- _Reason:_ Reduces save-scumming; makes support more valuable

---

### Flanking

**Standard Rule:** Flanking rules are optional (grants advantage on attacks).

**Our Rule:**

- Flanking grants +2 bonus to attack rolls (not advantage)
- You can only flank if you're standing on opposite sides of a target
- _Reason:_ Action economy balance; avoids advantage stacking

---

### Long Rest Mechanics

**Standard Rule:** 8 hours of sleep grants a long rest.

**Our Rule:**

- A long rest requires 8 hours of sleep plus 4 waking hours of light activity (not active combat)
- You can take only one long rest per 24-hour period
- _Reason:_ Prevents resting after every encounter; makes resource management matter

## Clarifications

Campaign-specific clarifications on ambiguous rules.

### Bonus Actions

> When multiple abilities grant bonus actions (like Haste spell + Cunning Action), you can only use ONE bonus action per turn. Choose which one applies.

### Spell Preparation

> Prepared spells can be changed during a long rest. You choose which spells are prepared when you finish the rest. Changing your preparation doesn't take extra time; it's automatic.

### Multiclassing XP

> If you multiclass, you gain levels at the same XP threshold as single-classed characters. Your experience pool is shared; there's no separate XP for each class.

## Campaign-Specific Rules

Campaign-specific rules apply only to their respective campaign.

### Ra Gol'vek

- **Madness Saves:** When exposed to supernatural events, make a Wisdom save (DC varies). Failure may inflict short-term madness (see DMG p. 259).

### Vyrun

- **Survival Checks:** In the wilderness, all creatures make group Survival checks (lowest modifier applies to the group).
- **Exhaustion Accelerates:** Traveling without shelter in harsh weather accelerates exhaustion by one level (in addition to normal exhaustion mechanics).

### Curse of Strahd

- **Barovia's Curse:** Characters cannot leave Barovia willingly (even by teleport). The land resists escape.
- **Strahd's Power:** Strahd gains +1 to attack rolls and damage rolls while inside Ravenloft or if the party is within 10 miles of the castle.

## What's NOT a House Rule

The following are standard D&D 5e rules we DON'T modify:

- ✓ Ability checks, saving throws, attack rolls
- ✓ Spell mechanics and casting rules
- ✓ Skill proficiencies
- ✓ Alignment (we use it but don't enforce it)
- ✓ Experience and leveling (except multiclassing note above)

If you're unsure whether a rule has been modified, ask your DM.
