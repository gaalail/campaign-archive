# Attunement & Magic Items

Some magic items require a creature to form a bond with them before their magical properties can be used. This bond is called attunement.

## Attuning to an Item

- Attuning to an item requires a creature to spend a short rest focused on only that item while being in physical contact with it.
- A creature cannot be attuned to more than one copy of the same item.

## Epic Point Pool House Rule

Rather than the standard limit of three attuned items, this archive uses the **Epic Point Pool** system:

- Your attunement capacity is measured in points equal to **twice your proficiency bonus** (scaling up to 24 points at character level 40).
- Magic items cost points based on their rarity (Common: 0, Uncommon: 1, Rare: 2, Very Rare: 4, Legendary: 8, Artifact: 12).
- For complete details, tables, and audit rules, see [[rules/Attunement - Epic Point Pool|Attunement: Epic Point Pool]].
