# Vision and Light

The fundamental tasks of adventuring depend heavily on whether a creature can see its surroundings.

## Light Levels

- **Bright Light:** Allows most creatures to see normally.
- **Dim Light:** Creates a lightly obscured area. Dim light usually marks the boundary between a source of bright light and surrounding darkness. Creatures have disadvantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on sight.
- **Darkness:** Creates a heavily obscured area. Characters face darkness outdoors at night, within unlit dungeons, or inside magical darkness. A heavily obscured area blocks vision entirely; creatures suffer from the [[rules/conditions/blinded|blinded]] condition when trying to see something within it.

## Special Senses

- **Darkvision:** A creature with darkvision can see in dim light within a specified radius as if it were bright light, and in darkness as if it were dim light (though in shades of gray).
- **Blindsight:** A creature with blindsight can perceive its surroundings without relying on sight, within a specific radius.
- **Truesight:** A creature with truesight can see in normal and magical darkness, perceive [[rules/conditions/invisible|invisible]] creatures and objects, detect visual illusions, and perceive the original form of shapechangers.
