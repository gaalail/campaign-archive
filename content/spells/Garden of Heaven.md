*2nd-level transmutation (ritual)*

**Material components:** a vial of holy water, a dozen red roses

You choose a point within range and cast a ritual by pouring holy water and throwing petals of red roses around it. At the end of the ritual, a 30-foot-radius sphere centered on the point transforms into a part of heaven before your eyes; with the greenest grass you've ever seen, trees with multi-colored, juicy fruit on the branches, and a temperate breeze. Creatures who finish a long rest in the area have advantage on an ability check or saving throw (of their choice) until the end of their next long rest.
