*6th-level illusion*

**Material components:** a bowl of milk, which is consumed by the spell

You touch a creature and show it a vision of one of the happiest or most peaceful moments of its life. It no longer suffers pain and one of the following effects of your choice takes place:

- It regains 10 hit points.
- A condition of your choice affecting the target ends.
- If the target is at the brink of death and there is nothing to prevent it, it dies peacefully. Even though it is a sinner, it can choose to go to the planes of good in the afterlife, if the forces of good also approve. However, if it already sold its soul, in other words if the soul already belongs to a force, this effect does not take place.
