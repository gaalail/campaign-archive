*7th-level illusion*

You create the illusion of a temple around you in an area that is 60 feet long, 40 feet wide, and 12 feet high. When you are targeted by a spell, an illusory reflection of the spell bounces off the temple gates, and is reflected back to its caster. You are affected normally by the spell, and the caster of the spell is also affected by its own spell.
