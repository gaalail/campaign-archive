_2nd-level evocation_

A strong wind (20 miles per hour) blows around you in a 10-foot radius and moves with you, remaining centered on you. The wind lasts for the spell's duration and has the following effects:
- It deafens you and other creatures in its area.
- It extinguishes unprotected flames.
- The area is difficult terrain for creatures other than you.
- Attack rolls of ranged weapon attacks have disadvantage if the attacks pass in or out of the wind.
- It hedges out vapor, gas, and fog.
