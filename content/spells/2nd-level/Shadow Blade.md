_2nd-level illusion_

You weave together threads of shadow to create a sword of solidified gloom in your hand. This magic sword lasts until the spell ends. It counts as a simple melee weapon with which you are proficient. It deals 2d8 psychic damage on a hit and has the finesse, light, and thrown properties (range 20/60). When you attack a target that is in dim light or darkness with the blade, you have advantage on the attack roll.

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a 3rd- or 4th-level spell slot, the damage increases to 3d8; at 5th or 6th, to 4d8; at 7th or higher, to 5d8.
