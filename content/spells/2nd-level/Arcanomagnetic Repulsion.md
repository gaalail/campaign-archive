Casting Time: 1 action
Range: Self (20-foot-radius sphere)
Components: V, S
Duration: Concentration, up to 1 minute

Attacks using ferrous weapons or ammunition against you have disadvantage. The area within 20 feet is difficult terrain for ferrous creatures or creatures wearing ferrous armor. As a bonus action when cast and on later turns, force one Huge or smaller creature in that terrain to make a Strength save; failure deals 1d6 force damage and knocks it prone.

**At Higher Levels.** Terrain radius and pulse damage increase by 5 feet and 1d6 per slot level above 2nd.
