Casting Time: 1 action
Range: Touch
Components: V, S, M (a toenail lost to frostbite)
Duration: Instantaneous

Make a melee spell attack against a creature you can reach. On a hit, it takes 5d6 cold damage and cannot take reactions until the end of its next turn. Until then, its speed is reduced by 15 feet and yours increases by 15 feet.

**At Higher Levels.** Damage increases by 1d6 per slot level above 2nd.
