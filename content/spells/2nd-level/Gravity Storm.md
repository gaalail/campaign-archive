_2nd-level transmutation_

You create a localized gravitational field that unleashes a storm of gravity waves. Choose a point within range that you can see. When you cast this spell and at the start of each of your subsequent turns for the duration, each creature within 20 feet of that point must make a Dexterity saving throw. On a failure, a target takes 2d6 bludgeoning damage and is knocked prone. On a success, a target takes half as much damage and isn't knocked prone. **At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 3rd level or higher, the damage increases by 1d6, the radius increases by 5 feet, and the duration increases by 1 round, for each slot level above 2nd.
