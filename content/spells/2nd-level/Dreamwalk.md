Casting Time: 1 action
Range: 30 feet
Components: V, S, M (a tiny dreamcatcher)
Duration: 24 hours

Choose up to ten willing creatures. They can lucidly dream and know if dream targets them. When the spell is cast, each affected creature may fall asleep instantly, even if a trait normally prevents magical sleep.
