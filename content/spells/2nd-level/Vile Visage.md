_2nd-level illusion (ritual)_

You pull on the base instincts of a creature you can see within range to twist its features into what it reviles most. The target must make a Charisma saving throw (it can choose to fail). On a failure, an illusion warps the target's face to reflect what it considers hideous, without altering core characteristics such as race.

For the duration, the target has a **-10 penalty to all Charisma skill checks**. Creatures with an Intelligence score lower than 4 or those lacking a concept of beauty or ugliness are immune to this spell.

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 3rd level or higher, the duration increases by 1 hour for each slot level above 2nd.
