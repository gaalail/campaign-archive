_2nd-level transmutation_

A single pitch-black sphere the size of a coin shoots from your palm to a point you choose within range. The sphere expands and rapidly collapses in the span of a single second, pulling with it everything it touched.

Each creature in a 15-foot-radius sphere centered on the point must make a Strength saving throw. A target is pulled 10 feet toward the center of the sphere and knocked prone on a failed save, or pulled 5 feet and not knocked prone on a successful one. All unanchored objects weighing less than 400 pounds are automatically pulled to the center.

If a creature collides with another object or creature of size Small or larger while being pulled, both take 1d6 force damage. A creature takes 1d6 force damage for each impact suffered during the collapse.

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 3rd level or higher, the radius of the sphere increases by 5 feet for each slot level above 2nd.
