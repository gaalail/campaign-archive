_2nd-level enchantment_

You create an invisible bubble of arcane mist in a 15-foot-radius sphere centered on you that moves with you, which remains for the duration of the spell or until dispelled. If a creature makes an attack against a surprised creature while inside the bubble, the attacker must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw or hear a loud ringing noise before completing its attack and be stunned until the start of its next turn.
