Casting Time: 1 action
Range: Self (50-foot line)
Components: V, S, M (a dead animal)
Duration: Instantaneous

A 5-foot-wide, 50-foot-long line erupts with negative energy. Creatures in the line make Dexterity saves, taking 3d8 necrotic damage on failure or half on success.

**At Higher Levels.** Damage increases by 1d8 per slot level above 2nd.
