_2nd-level conjuration_

You call forth a bestial spirit. It manifests in an unoccupied space that you can see within range. This corporeal form uses the Bestial Spirit stat block. When you cast the spell, choose an environment: Air, Land, or Water. The creature resembles an animal of your choice native to that environment, which determines certain traits in its stat block. The creature is an ally to you and your companions. In combat, it shares your initiative count and takes its turn immediately after yours.
