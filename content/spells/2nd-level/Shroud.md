_2nd-level abjuration (ritual)_

**Material components:** a bit of cotton.

One willing creature that you touch becomes immune to being targeted by divination magic for the duration.

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 3rd level or higher, the spell's duration increases by 1 hour for each slot level above 2nd.
