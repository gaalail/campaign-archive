*8th-level enchantment*

**Material components:** up to eight stone tablets with your code of conduct carved upon them

For an hour, you loudly declare the laws you dictate upon the land and place the stone tablets in an unoccupied space within the spell's range. When the spell takes effect, the stone tablets start to hover and orbit around the center of the space in which they were placed. The stone tablets can't be moved and can't be harmed or damaged for the duration of the spell.

Immediately after the casting is complete, all creatures within a 1-mile-radius sphere centered on the tablets become aware of the laws that they must obey. If a creature disobeys one of your laws, you can sense its mind and are aware of its exact location while the creature is within the sphere for the duration of the spell.

Casting this spell on the same spot every day for a year makes these effects permanent.

The laws must be in keeping with your alignment, your personal code of conduct, or the dogmas of your deity.
