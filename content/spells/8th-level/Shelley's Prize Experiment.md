*8th-level necromancy*

**Material components:** at least three humanoid corpses and a spool of copper wire.

You create a flesh golem. This golem follows your verbal commands to the best of its ability for the duration.

At the end of the duration, the golem gains autonomy and will leave you. Roll 1d20. On a roll of 5 or lower, it will return 2d6 days later and attack you, if you are still on the same plane of existence. On a roll of 6-19, you will never see it again. On a roll of 20, it will return 2d6 days later and serve as your ally indefinitely.

Creating a creature in this fashion is very difficult, and you may only do so one time every 6 months.
