*8th-level illusion*

**Material components:** a mirror that you break while casting the spell

You choose a target within range and show it an illusion in which the skies part, the ground trembles, and holy fire rains down to engulf and scorch the target until it is completely annihilated.

The illusion is so realistic that the target must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw or take 15d10 radiant damage. If an evil creature drops to 0 hit points as a result of this damage, they are consumed by the holy fire, which becomes real and leaves nothing but ashes behind. Such a creature can only be resurrected by means of a wish spell.
