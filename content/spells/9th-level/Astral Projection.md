_9th-level necromancy_

You and up to eight willing creatures within range project your astral bodies into the Astral Plane (the spell fails and the casting is wasted if you are already on that plane). The material body you leave behind is unconscious and in a state of suspended animation; it doesn't need food or air and doesn't age. Your astral form resembles your mortal form in almost every way, tethered to your material body by a silver cord.

> *6th level divination* — **Classes:** Cleric, Psion, Warlock, Wizard
> **Casting Time:** 1 hour | **Range:** Self | **Components:** V, S, M (a 10-foot long cord of mithril worth 1,000 gold for each target, which the spell consumes). | **Duration:** Special
>
> You separate your soul from your body, projecting it into the Astral Plane (The spell fails if you cast it on the Astral Plane). Your physical body is left behind, Unconscious and in a state of suspended animation; it does not need air, food, nor water, and it does not age. Your soul uses the following rules:
>
> **Astral Body.** Your soul forms an Astral Body around itself. It is an exact replica of your physical body with your statistics and posessions, though a silver cord extends from the center of its back, turning invisibile after 1 foot. Any damage or other effects that apply to it don't affect your physical body or carry over to it when you return to your body.
>
> **Silver Cord.** This cord tethers your soul to your physical body, and as long as it is intact, you can find your way to your physical body. If the cord is severed (which only happens if an effect specifically states that it does), your soul and body are permanently separated, killing you instantly.
>
> **Ending the Spell.** If you leave the Astral Plane through a portal to another plane or return to the plane where the spell was cast, your physical body and its possessions travel along your silver cord, and you re-enter your physical body there.
>
> The spell ends if you use your action to end it, if a spell like dispel magic is cast on your Astral Body or physical body, or if either body is reduced to 0 hit points. When the spell ends in this way, your soul instantly travels along your silver cord and returns to your physical body, where you awaken.
>
> **At Higher Levels.** If you cast this spell with a spell slot of 7th-level or higher, you can target three additional creatures for each spell slot level above 6th.
>
> Any additional creatures must remain within 10 feet of you while you cast the spell.
