_9th-level necromancy_

You target a creature within the spell's range who must succeed on a Constitution saving throw or be marked until the start of your next turn. When the mark fades, the target's body remembers all the pain it suffered in the last round and takes necrotic damage equal to the total damage it took while marked. This damage can't be reduced by any means.
