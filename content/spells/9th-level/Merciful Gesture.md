_9th-level evocation_

You make a gesture, pointing at a creature within range. The creature is bombarded by the magical energies of unmaking and it must make one Strength, one Dexterity, one Constitution, one Intelligence, one Wisdom, and one Charisma saving throw in a row. For each failed save, the creature takes 8d10 force damage. If the creature is slain by this spell, it feels no pain in its final moments and it can't be brought back by any means, except by the direct intervention of a deity.
