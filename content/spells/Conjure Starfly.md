*Conjuration cantrip*

You summon a starfly (p. 282) that appears in an unoccupied space that you can see within range. A starfly summoned with this spell disappears when it drops to 0 hit points or when the spell ends.

The summoned starfly is friendly to you and your companions. Roll initiative for the starfly, which has its own turns. It obeys any verbal commands that you issue to it (no action required by you). If you don't issue any commands to the starfly, it defends itself from hostile creatures, but otherwise takes no actions. The GM has the starfly's statistics.

You cannot summon more than 1 starfly at a time using this spell. The number of starflies you can conjure with this spell increases by 2 when you reach 5th level (3), 11th level (5), and 17th level (7).
