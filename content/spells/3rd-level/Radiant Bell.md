_3rd-level abjuration_

**Material components:** a silvered choir bell* worth at least 5 gp, which the spell consumes

You ring an arcane, silver bell, imbuing it with healing magics that cure the afflicted. Choose any number of creatures within 30 feet. For each target, you can end one of the following conditions: charmed, frightened, poisoned, petrified, or stunned. This cure does not work if a target is deafened. *It is rumored that the soft ringing carries its energies across planes.
