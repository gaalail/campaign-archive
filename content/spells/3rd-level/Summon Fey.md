_3rd-level conjuration_

You call forth a fey spirit. It manifests in an unoccupied space that you can see within range. This corporeal form uses the Fey Spirit stat block. When you cast the spell, choose a mood: Fuming, Mirthful, or Tricksy. The creature is an ally to you and your companions and shares your initiative count, taking its turn immediately after yours.
