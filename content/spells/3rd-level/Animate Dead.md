_3rd-level necromancy_

This spell creates an undead servant. Choose a pile of bones or a corpse of a Medium or Small humanoid within range. Your spell imbues the target with a foul mimicry of life, raising it as an undead creature (a skeleton or a zombie). On each of your turns, you can use a bonus action to mentally command any creature you made with this spell if the creature is within 60 feet of you. The creature is under your control for 24 hours, after which it stops obeying unless you cast this spell on it again.

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 4th level or higher, you animate or reassert control over two additional undead creatures for each slot level above 3rd.
