Casting Time: 1 action
Range: 90 feet (20-foot-radius, 60-foot-tall cylinder)
Components: V, S, M (a drop of acid)
Duration: Concentration, up to 1 minute

Corrosive rain fills a cylinder centered on a point within range. When it begins, each creature in the area makes a Dexterity save, taking 4d6 acid damage on failure, half on success. A creature also saves when it first enters the area on a turn or ends its turn there.

**At Higher Levels.** Damage increases by 1d6 per slot level above 3rd.
