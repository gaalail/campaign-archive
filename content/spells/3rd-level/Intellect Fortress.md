_3rd-level abjuration_

For the duration, you or one willing creature you can see within range has resistance to psychic damage, as well as advantage on Intelligence, Wisdom, and Charisma saving throws. Whenever the target succeeds on an Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma saving throw against a spell or magical effect, the creature that forced the save takes 2d10 psychic damage.

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 4th level or higher, you can target one additional creature for each slot level above 3rd.
