Casting Time: 1 action
Range: 90 feet (20-foot-radius sphere)
Components: V, S, M (a skunk's tail)
Duration: Concentration, up to 1 minute

A colorless noxious miasma fills a 20-foot-radius sphere. A creature entering it for the first time on a turn or starting there makes a Constitution save. Failure deals 3d6 poison damage and poisons it until the end of its next turn; success deals half damage and does not poison it. A creature poisoned this way has disadvantage on concentration saves.

**At Higher Levels.** Damage increases by 1d6 per slot level above 3rd.
