_3rd-level evocation_

You summon stellar bombardment upon your enemies. Choose a point you can see within range. A 20-foot-radius disk filled with blazing stars appears 50 feet directly above that point (the spell fails if sufficient vertical space is unavailable).

Each creature in a 50-foot tall, 20-foot-radius cylinder centered beneath the disk must make a Dexterity saving throw, taking **6d6 radiant damage** on a failed save, or half as much on a successful one. On each subsequent turn until the spell ends, you can use your action to move the disk up to 20 feet and call down the starfall again.

If you are outdoors under a clear night sky, you siphon cosmic energy directly from the heavens, increasing the spell's damage by **1d6**.

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 4th level or higher, the damage increases by 1d6 for each slot level above 3rd.
