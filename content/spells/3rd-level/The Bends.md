Casting Time: 1 action
Range: 90 feet
Components: V, S
Duration: Instantaneous

A creature you see makes a Constitution save as nitrogen bubbles precipitate in its blood. Failure deals 5d8 poison damage and poisons it for one minute; success deals half damage and does not poison it. If fully submerged, use d10s instead of d8s. It repeats the save at each turn's end, ending the poison on success.

**At Higher Levels.** Damage increases by 1d8 per slot level above 3rd.
