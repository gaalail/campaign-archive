_3rd-level illusion_

You create the image of an object, a creature, or some other visible phenomenon that is no larger than a 20-foot cube. It appears at a spot that you can see within range and lasts for the duration. It seems completely real, including sounds, smells, and temperature appropriate to the thing depicted.

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 6th level or higher, the spell lasts until dispelled, without requiring your concentration.
