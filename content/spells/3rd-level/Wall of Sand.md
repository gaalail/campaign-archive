_3rd-level evocation_

You conjure up a wall of swirling sand on the ground at a point you can see within range. You can make the wall up to 30 feet long, 10 feet high, and 10 feet thick, and it vanishes when the spell ends. It blocks line of sight, but not movement. A creature takes 3 feet of movement for every 1 foot it moves through the wall. A creature that enters the wall must succeed on a Constitution saving throw or be blinded until it leaves.
