Casting Time: 1 action
Range: 120 feet (20-foot-radius sphere)
Components: V, S, M (a red handkerchief)
Duration: Concentration, up to 1 minute

Humanoids in the sphere make Charisma saves; a creature may choose to fail. On failure, it becomes hostile to all creatures. An enraged creature concentrating on a spell makes a DC 10 Constitution save at the start of each turn or loses concentration. As an action, force an enraged creature to use its reaction to attack a randomly determined creature within reach. It repeats its save when it takes damage, ending the effect on success.

**At Higher Levels.** Force one additional enraged creature to attack per slot level above 3rd.
