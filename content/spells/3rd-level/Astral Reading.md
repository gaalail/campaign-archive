_3rd-level divination (ritual)_

You sit beneath the night sky and read the stars for answers, meaning, and purpose. This spell can only be cast outdoors at night with a clear view of the sky.

Roll a percentile die (d100) to determine if your reading is successful (a successful reading is always truthful; an unsuccessful reading is unclear):

- **Watchful Reading:** You ask if a person you are familiar with is safe or in danger. If within 60 miles, the reading succeeds automatically. If further away or on another plane, roll d100; on 51 or higher, the reading succeeds.
- **Introspective Reading:** You ask if an action taken in the past 24 hours was the right step toward achieving your goal. Roll d100; on 51 or higher, the reading succeeds.
- **Personal Reading:** You ask how a person you know feels about you. If played by a player at the table, they can choose to make the reading automatically succeed. Otherwise, roll d100; on 51 or higher, you learn one single, truthful word describing how that person feels about you.
