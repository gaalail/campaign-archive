_3rd-level transmutation (Laserllama Homebrew)_

You touch a willing creature. Until the spell ends, the target can move through other creatures and solid objects as if they were difficult terrain. If the target ends its turn inside an object, it takes 1d10 force damage and is shunted to the nearest unoccupied space.
