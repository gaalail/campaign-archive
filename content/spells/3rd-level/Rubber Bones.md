_3rd-level transmutation_

You touch a willing creature. Its bones become soft and rubbery, and it gains the following benefits: • It can spend 5 feet of movement to escape a grapple without requiring an ability check and can squeeze through a space that is large enough for a creature two sizes smaller than it. • It has advantage on Dexterity saving throws. • It has resistance to bludgeoning damage. Its reach with melee attacks increases by 5 feet. **At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 4th level or higher, you can target one additional creature for each slot level above 3rd.
