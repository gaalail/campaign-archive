_3rd-level abjuration_

When you cast this spell, you inscribe a glyph that harms other creatures, either upon a surface (such as a table or a section of floor or wall) or within an object that can be closed (such as a book, a scroll, or a treasure chest) to conceal the glyph. You decide what triggers the glyph when you cast the spell. When triggered, it erupts as Explosive Runes (5d8 acid, cold, fire, lightning, or thunder damage in a 20-foot radius) or a Spell Glyph casting a prepared 3rd-level or lower spell.

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 4th level or higher, the damage of an explosive runes glyph increases by 1d8 for each slot level above 3rd, and a spell glyph can store a spell of up to the same level.
