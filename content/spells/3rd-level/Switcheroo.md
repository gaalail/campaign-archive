Casting Time: 1 action
Range: 90 feet
Components: V, S
Duration: Instantaneous

Attempt to switch places with a Large or smaller creature you see. It makes a Charisma save or teleports to your space while you teleport to its former space. It automatically succeeds if either creature cannot fit in the other's space; a willing creature can choose to fail.

**At Higher Levels.** Range increases by 30 feet per slot level above 3rd. Maximum target size becomes Huge at 6th level and Gargantuan at 9th.
