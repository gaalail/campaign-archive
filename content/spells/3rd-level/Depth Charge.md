Casting Time: 1 action
Range: 90 feet (20-foot-radius sphere)
Components: V, S, M (some metamorphic rock)
Duration: Instantaneous

A thunderous pulse erupts at a point you choose. Creatures in a 20-foot-radius sphere make Constitution saves, taking 7d6 thunder damage on failure or half on success. Submerged creatures take 9d6 instead.

**At Higher Levels.** Damage increases by 1d6 per slot level above 3rd.
