_3rd-level conjuration_

You call forth a shadowy spirit from the Shadowfell. It manifests in an unoccupied space that you can see within range. This corporeal form uses the Shadow Spirit stat block. When you cast the spell, choose an emotion: Fury, Despair, or Fear. The creature shares your initiative count and obeys your verbal commands.
