_3rd-level conjuration_

You open a gateway to the dark between the stars, a region infested with unknown horrors. A 20-foot-radius sphere of blackness and bitter cold appears, centered on a point with range and lasting for the duration. The void creates difficult terrain, and creatures starting their turn within take 2d6 cold damage. Any creature ending its turn within must make a Dex save or take 2d6 acid damage as milky tentacles strike it.
