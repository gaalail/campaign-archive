_3rd-level enchantment_

You target a creature within range that can hear you and assign a goal to it in the form of a declaration. This goal must be reasonable and in line with the actual goals of the target. For example, you can say "You must kill the bandits that have ambushed us", or "You must find your ancestral swords that you've been looking for". In addition, the target must have an Intelligence score of 3 or higher.

The target receives a +1 bonus on every d20 roll it makes to pursue the goal. The GM determines whether the roll is related to the goal or not.

The target can make a Wisdom saving throw to negate the effects of the spell. The bonus lasts until the target achieves the goal. A creature can be affected by this spell once at a time.
