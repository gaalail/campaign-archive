_3rd-level transmutation_

You touch a ranged weapon with the ammunition property and infuse it with magical light that is ready to scorch its target. For the duration of the spell, the weapon's range is doubled and the first successful attack made with it deals an extra 4d8 radiant damage.
HL: When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 5th level or higher, you score a critical hit with the first successful attack in addition to dealing the extra radiant damage.
