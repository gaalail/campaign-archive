Casting Time: 1 action
Range: 90 feet
Components: V, S
Duration: Concentration, up to 1 minute

A creature you see makes an Intelligence save. On failure, it takes 4d10 psychic damage and you learn one fact: its name, fear, desire, condition immunities, damage vulnerabilities/resistances/immunities, or skill and saving throw proficiencies. On success, it takes half damage and reveals nothing. Intelligence 4 or lower automatically succeeds. If it failed initially, use an action on later turns to repeat the save; failure deals 4d6 psychic damage and reveals another fact, success ends the spell. The spell ends if the target leaves range.

**At Higher Levels.** Initial and ongoing damage increase by 1d10 and 1d6 per slot level above 3rd.
