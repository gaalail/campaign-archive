Casting Time: 1 action
Range: 90 feet (20-foot-radius sphere)
Components: V, S, M (a piece of magnetite)
Duration: Instantaneous

A supercooled magnetite shard detonates at a point you see. Creatures in a 20-foot-radius sphere make Dexterity saves, taking 4d6 cold and 3d6 piercing damage on failure or half on success. Ferrous creatures and creatures wearing ferrous armor have disadvantage.

**At Higher Levels.** Cold damage increases by 1d6 per slot level above 3rd.
