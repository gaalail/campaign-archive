*Transmutation cantrip*

You choose a point within range and a warm wind blows within a 15-foot-radius sphere centered on it. Wind, in the shape of Tiny animals (of your choice), runs freely within the area. You create one of the following magical effects within range:

- A creature of your choice within the sphere must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw or be charmed by you for the duration or until it leaves the area.
- Ranged weapon attacks passing through the area are made with disadvantage for the duration.
- Nonmagical flames go out.
- You can warm the area up or cool it down. However, you cannot cool it down to freezing temperatures, or warm it up to scorching temperatures.
