level: 4
school: Abjuration
classes:
- Druid
- Ranger
casting_time: 1 action
range: 30 feet
duration: Concentration, up to 1 minute
components: V, S
ritual: false
concentration: true
source: Corpus Angelus
---

*4th-level abjuration*

You target a creature within range and curse it. The target must succeed on a Constitution saving throw or it takes a -3 penalty to AC if it has natural armor for the duration of the spell.
