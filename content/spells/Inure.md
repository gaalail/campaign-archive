*Abjuration cantrip*

Your body is slightly strengthened against minor inconveniences. For the duration of the spell, you are unharmed by the hazards of controlled performances; such as walking on a pile of broken glass, sleeping on a bed of needles, holding an ember, or swallowing a burning torch. The GM decides whether something qualifies as a minor inconvenience or not.
