_1st-level abjuration_

As you cast this spell, you use the rope to create a circle with a 5-foot radius on flat ground. When you finish casting, the rope disappears and becomes a magical trap. The trap is nearly invisible and requires an Intelligence (Investigation) check against your spell save DC to be found.

When a Small, Medium, or Large creature enters the area, the trap triggers: the creature must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw or be hoisted into the air, suspended upside down 3 feet above the ground, and restrained.
