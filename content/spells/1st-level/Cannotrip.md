Casting Time: 1 action
Range: 60 feet
Components: V, S
Duration: Concentration, up to 1 minute

Choose a creature you see. Each time it tries to cast a spell of this spell's level or lower, it makes a spellcasting ability check against your spell save DC or wastes its action without expending the slot.

**At Higher Levels.** Inhibit spells one level higher per slot level above 1st.
