_1st-level transmutation (ritual)_

A 20-foot square centered on a point within range of any liquid you can see instantly comes to a boil. If a creature is in the liquid, it takes 3d8 fire damage at the start of its turns as long as it remains in the boiling liquid. If the creature leaves the boiling liquid, it takes 3d8 fire damage for an additional turn as the scalded liquid on its skin or clothing cools down.
