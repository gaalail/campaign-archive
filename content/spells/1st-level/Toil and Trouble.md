_1st-level necromancy_

You intensify the worries and physical exertions weighing a creature down. Choose up to three creatures you can see within range. Each target must make a Wisdom saving throw. Whenever a target that fails this saving throw makes a Strength or Dexterity saving throw or attack roll before the spell ends, the target must roll a d6 and subtract the number rolled from the result.

The target can make a Wisdom saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the spell on a success. For every three turns the target remains under the influence of this spell, it gains one level of exhaustion.
