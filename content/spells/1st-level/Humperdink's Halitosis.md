Casting Time: 1 action
Range: Self (15-foot cone)
Components: V, S
Duration: Concentration, up to 1 round

Acidic fumes fill a 15-foot cone and spread around corners. Creatures in the area make Constitution saves, taking 3d4 acid damage on failure or half on success. The fumes linger until the end of your next turn or a moderate wind disperses them. A creature ending its turn there saves against the damage again.

**At Higher Levels.** Both damage instances increase by 1d4 per slot level above 1st.
