_1st-level transmutation_

**Material components:** an empty phantasm shell*

You touch a weapon and imbue it with otherworldly bile. For the duration, it becomes silvered and, while you are holding it, it deals an additional 1d4 force damage on a hit. **At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 2nd level or higher, you can increase the duration to 10 minutes (2nd level), 1 hour (3rd level), 8 hours (4th level), or 24 hours (5th level). *The hollow corpse of an eldritch being, often found-or perhaps created—by the Radiant Church.
