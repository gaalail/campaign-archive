Casting Time: 1 action
Range: Touch
Components: V, S, M (red, yellow, and blue pigment)
Duration: Concentration, up to 10 minutes

A creature you touch shifts the hues of its skin, worn items, and carried equipment to match its surroundings. It can Hide as a bonus action, even while lightly obscured. If it did not move during its last turn, creatures have disadvantage on Perception checks to see it. Creatures that do not rely on sight are immune.

**At Higher Levels.** Target one additional creature per slot level above 1st.
