Casting Time: 1 action
Range: Self
Components: V, S, M (well-chewed peppermint chewing gum)
Duration: Concentration, up to 1 hour

A protective layer of hot, hard peppermint icing coats you. For the duration, you gain a +1 bonus to AC. If a creature hits you with a melee attack, it takes 1d4 fire damage. This damage can trigger no more than once per turn.

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 2nd level or higher, the fire damage increases by 1d4 for each slot level above 1st.
