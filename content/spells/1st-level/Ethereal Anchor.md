_1st-level abjuration (Laserllama Homebrew)_

You send forth translucent spectral bands to anchor a creature to the current plane of existence. The target must succeed on a Charisma saving throw or be unable to teleport, travel across planes, or shift into the Ethereal plane for the duration.
