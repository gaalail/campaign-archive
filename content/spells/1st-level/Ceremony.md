_1st-level abjuration (ritual)_

You perform a special religious ceremony that is infused with magic. When you cast the spell, choose one of the following rites, the target of which must be within 10 feet of you throughout the casting:
- **Atonement:** Restore a willing creature's original alignment.
- **Bless Water:** Turn a vial of mundane water into holy water.
- **Coming of Age:** A youth gains a 1d4 bonus to saving throws for 24 hours.
- **Dedication:** A creature dedicated to a god's service gains a 1d4 bonus to saving throws for 24 hours.
- **Funeral Rite:** Prevent a touched corpse from becoming undead for 7 days.
- **Wedding:** Touched adult humanoids gain +2 AC while within 30 feet of each other for 7 days.
