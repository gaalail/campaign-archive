_7th-level abjuration_

**Material components:** a piece of sapphire worth at least 100 gp, which the spell consumes

You hold a piece of sapphire in your hand, chant a prayer, and choose a creature within range. The target is instantly filled with valor and fortitude, and the feeling that nothing can stand in its way if it sets its mind to something.

The target cannot be affected by any condition for the duration unless it wants to be. If the creature is already affected by a condition, the condition ends. If the creature drops to 0 hit points, it does not fall unconscious; however, it still makes death saving throws as it normally would.
