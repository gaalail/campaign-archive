Casting Time: 1 action
Range: 60 feet
Components: V, S
Duration: Instantaneous

Make a ranged spell attack. On a hit, deal 1d8 lightning damage. On a critical hit, blind the target until the end of its next turn. The spell creates two sparks at 5th level, three at 11th, and four at 17th; make separate attacks for each spark.
