Casting Time: 1 action
Range: 60 feet
Components: V, S
Duration: Instantaneous

A creature you see makes a Constitution save or takes 1d8 psychic damage. Failure by 5 or more also gives it disadvantage on its next attack roll before the end of its next turn. Damage increases by 1d8 at 5th, 11th, and 17th level.
