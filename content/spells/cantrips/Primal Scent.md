Casting Time: 1 action
Range: Self
Components: S
Duration: Concentration, up to 1 minute

You enhance the scents around you. You have advantage on the next ability check relying on smell and treat a roll of 4 or lower as 5. At 5th level, smell checks treat d20 rolls of 7 or lower as 8; at 11th, 10 or lower as 11; at 17th, 13 or lower as 14.
