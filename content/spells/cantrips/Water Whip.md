Casting Time: 1 action
Range: 60 feet
Components: V, S
Duration: Instantaneous

Choose a visible water source within range containing at least 4 pints. Make a melee spell attack against a creature within 30 feet of that source. On a hit, it takes 1d6 slashing damage. If Large or smaller, it makes a Strength save or is pulled up to 10 feet toward the water. Damage and pull distance increase by 1d6 and 5 feet at 5th, 11th, and 17th level.
