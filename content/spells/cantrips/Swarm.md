Casting Time: 1 action
Range: 90 feet
Components: V, S, M (a spider sac)
Duration: 1 round

A cloud of tiny poisonous spiders flies toward a target. It makes a Dexterity save or is poisoned until the end of its next turn. At 5th level a failed save also deals 1d10 poison damage, increasing to 2d10 at 11th and 3d10 at 17th.
