Casting Time: 1 action
Range: 60 feet
Components: V, S, M (a strip of magnesium)
Duration: Instantaneous

Make a ranged spell attack. On a hit, deal 1d8 radiant damage. On a critical hit, attacks against the target have advantage until your next turn. Alternatively, create a falling flare that sheds bright light in a 20-foot radius and dim light for another 20 feet until your next turn. Damage increases by 1d8 and both light radii by 10 feet at 5th, 11th, and 17th level.
