Casting Time: 1 action
Range: 60 feet
Components: V, S
Duration: Instantaneous

A pressure burst audible 100 feet away surrounds a creature you see. It makes a Constitution save or takes 1d8 thunder damage and is deafened until your next turn. Damage increases by 1d8 at 5th, 11th, and 17th level.
