_Illusion cantrip_

**Material components:** a reflective surface

You touch a creature while it is looking at a reflective surface. Until the end of its next turn, the creature sees its more holy, good, and righteous reflection on the surface (for example, a corpse knight clad in an armor of rotten flesh and bones can see itself as a holy knight).
