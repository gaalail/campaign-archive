Casting Time: 1 action
Range: 90 feet
Components: V, S
Duration: Instantaneous

Make a ranged spell attack with a near-invisible arcanomagnetic pulse. On a hit, deal 1d6 force damage and the target makes a Strength save or falls prone. Ferrous creatures and creatures wearing ferrous armor have disadvantage. Damage increases by 1d6 at 5th, 11th, and 17th level.
