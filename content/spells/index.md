# Spells

**424+ spells available** | Pick during character creation & leveling | Core of magical characters

---

## What Is a Spell?

Spells are magical abilities that:
- Cast during combat or outside
- Range from minor effects (light) to powerful devastation
- Require spell slots (you have limited per day)
- Have concentration, duration, components (what you need to cast)
- Scale with your character level

## Common Spells (Cantrips — Always Available)

- **Fire Bolt** — Ranged damage (Wizard, Sorcerer)
- **Light** — Create illumination (any class)
- **Mage Hand** — Telekinesis (Wizard)
- **Message** — Send secret messages (Bard, Sorcerer)
- **Prestidigitation** — Minor magic tricks (Wizard)
