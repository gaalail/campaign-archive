*4th-level necromancy*

A creature you touch must make a Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, you draw out their body's moisture, causing them to shrivel and grow frail. The target creature's speed is halved and it has disadvantage on attack rolls and ability checks indefinitely. It may repeat the saving throw by spending its action on its turn, ending the effects on a success.
