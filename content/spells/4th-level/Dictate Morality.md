_4th-level enchantment_

You choose a creature within range and dictate your own rights and wrongs to it. The creature must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw or be affected until the spell ends. The affected creature takes 2d10 psychic damage if it harms a creature that has done no harm to it.

This spell and the damage dealt by the spell are not considered to harm the creature.
