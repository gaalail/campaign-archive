Casting Time: 1 action
Range: Self
Components: V, S, M (some frogspawn)
Duration: Concentration, up to 1 minute

You morph into a slimy, poisonous frog. Make a melee spell attack against a creature within reach; on hit, it takes 3d10 poison damage and is poisoned until your next turn. Repeat the attack as an action on later turns. You have advantage to avoid grapples and restraints and disadvantage on attempts to grapple. Creatures grappling you or grappled by you make Constitution saves at the start of their turns, taking 3d10 poison damage on failure or half on success.

**At Higher Levels.** Both damage values increase by 1d10 per slot level above 4th.
