_4th-level necromancy (ritual)_

**Material components:** one fattened mammalian livestock such as a cow or pig, or three fattened avian livestock such as hens (or the equivalent value of relevant sacrificial materials specific to your deity/pantheon).

By slaughtering the material components of this spell and immolating them, you entreat divine favors from your god or gods.

Upon completing the ritual, choose one creature that you touch. For the next 24 hours, that creature gains the benefits of the Lucky feat. If they already had the Lucky feat, they gain 1 additional Luck Point.
