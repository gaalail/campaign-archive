_4th-level enchantment_

You enhance the captivating qualities of a creature, making its presence irresistibly charismatic.

When you cast this spell on a willing creature, that creature gains a **+10 bonus to all Charisma skill checks** for the duration, and rolls all **Charisma saving throws with advantage**.

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 5th level or higher, you can either increase the duration by 1 hour or target one additional willing creature for each slot level above 4th.
