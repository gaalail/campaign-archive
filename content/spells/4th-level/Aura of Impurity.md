Casting Time: 1 action
Range: Self (30-foot-radius sphere)
Components: V
Duration: Concentration, up to 1 minute

An enervating aura moves with you. Creatures you choose that enter it for the first time on a turn or start their turns there make Charisma saves or become drained until the end of their next turn. While drained, they roll a d4 and subtract it from ability checks, attack rolls, and saving throws, and regain only half as many hit points.
