Casting Time: 1 action
Range: Touch
Components: V, S, M (a quiver containing ammunition)
Duration: Concentration, up to 1 hour

Imbue ammunition in a quiver with a creature, plant, or fungus spirit. As a bonus action, change the spirit. On a hit, the ammunition applies the spirit's effect: Cat adds 1d6 slashing (2d6 if the attack had advantage); Elk adds 1d6 bludgeoning and may knock prone or shove 5 feet; Hawk ignores half and three-quarters cover; Hogweed may blind; Psilocybin may incapacitate on a d4 roll; Roc may lift the target and reduce its speed to 0; Snake adds poison damage and may poison; Spider may restrain, escapable with an action and a Strength (Athletics) or Dexterity (Acrobatics) check against your save DC.

**At Higher Levels.** At 5th level or higher, duration becomes concentration, up to 8 hours.
