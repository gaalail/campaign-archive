_4th-level conjuration_

You summon fey creatures that appear in unoccupied spaces that you can see within range. Choose one of the following options: one fey creature of challenge rating 2 or lower; two fey creatures of challenge rating 1 or lower; four fey creatures of challenge rating 1/2 or lower; or eight fey creatures of challenge rating 1/4 or lower.

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using certain higher-level spell slots, more creatures appear: twice as many with a 6th-level slot and three times as many with an 8th-level slot.
