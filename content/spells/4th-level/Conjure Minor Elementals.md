_4th-level conjuration_

You summon elementals that appear in unoccupied spaces that you can see within range. You choose one of the following options: one elemental of challenge rating 2 or lower; two elementals of challenge rating 1 or lower; four elementals of challenge rating 1/2 or lower; or eight elementals of challenge rating 1/4 or lower.

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using certain higher-level spell slots, you choose one of the summoning options above, and more creatures appear: twice as many with a 6th-level slot and three times as many with an 8th-level slot.
