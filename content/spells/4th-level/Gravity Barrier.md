_4th-level evocation_

You create a wall of increased gravity on the ground at a point you can see within range. You can make the wall up to 30 feet long, 10 feet high, and 1 foot thick, or a ringed wall up to 20 feet in diameter, 20 feet high, and 1 foot thick. The wall lasts for the duration and its space is difficult terrain. Ranged weapon attacks that pass through the wall's space automatically fail, and other ranged attacks have disadvantage. Any creature that tries to pass through the wall's space must succeed on a Strength saving throw or take 4d8 bludgeoning damage, be knocked prone, and have its speed reduced to 0 until the end of its turn.
