_4th-level evocation_

The weapon you are wielding gathers great magical power which is to be released as you strike. The next time you hit a creature with a weapon attack before this spell ends, the target and other creatures in a 30-foot-long and 5-foot-wide line behind the target take 2d6 force damage. The creatures except the target can make a Dexterity saving throw to negate the effect.
