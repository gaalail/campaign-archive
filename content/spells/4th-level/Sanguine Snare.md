*4th-level necromancy*

**Material components:** a drop of your blood.

Make a ranged spell attack against a target within range that has taken damage on its most recent turn, or that has less than one half its maximum hit points.

On a hit, the creature's running blood hardens into chains that bind it into the ground. The creature becomes restrained for the duration. While restrained in this way, the creature takes 4d4 piercing damage at the start of each of its turns. The creature may make a Strength saving throw on the end of each of its turns, ending the spell on a success.

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 5th level or higher, the damage increases by 1d4 for each slot level above 4th.
