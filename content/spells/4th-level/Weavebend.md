Casting Time: 1 reaction, when you see a creature within range casting a spell
Range: 60 feet
Components: S
Duration: Instantaneous

You try to redirect the spell. A spell of 3rd level or lower can be redirected: move its ranged spell attack to a different target within 10 feet of the original target, or shift its area up to 10 feet. For a spell of 4th level or higher, make a spellcasting ability check against DC 10 + spell level; success redirects it.

**At Higher Levels.** With a 5th-level or higher slot, the spell is automatically redirected if its level is lower than the slot used.
