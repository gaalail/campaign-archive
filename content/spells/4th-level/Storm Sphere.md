_4th-level evocation_

A 20-foot-radius sphere of whirling air springs into existence, centered on a point you choose within range. The sphere remains for the spell's duration. Each creature in the sphere when it appears or that ends its turn there must succeed on a Strength saving throw or take 2d6 bludgeoning damage. Until the spell ends, you can use a bonus action on each of your turns to cause a bolt of lightning to leap from the center of the sphere toward one creature within 60 feet of the center, dealing 4d6 lightning damage on a hit.

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 5th level or higher, the damage for each effect increases by 1d6 for each slot level above 4th.
