_4th-level transmutation_

Reality swirls in a 30-foot-radius sphere centered on a point you can see within range. Each creature in the area must make a Strength saving throw, taking 6d6 bludgeoning damage on a failed save, or half as much damage ona successful one. Additionally, any creature that fails this saving throw is pushed 30 feet in a straight line in a direction of your choice.
