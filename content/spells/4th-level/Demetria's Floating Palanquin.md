_4th-level conjuration (ritual)_

A 10-foot-square ethereal palanquin of your chosen visual design springs into existence at a point within range. The palanquin comfortably accommodates five Medium creatures, possesses a speed of 40 feet, and has a carrying capacity of 600 pounds (AC 5, 20 HP; dissipating if reduced to 0 HP).

The palanquin hovers 2 feet above the ground, ignoring mundane difficult terrain. It can navigate solid ground or shallow water up to 5 feet deep, climbing slopes and stairs, but cannot traverse sudden elevation drops of 10 feet or greater.

**At Higher Levels.** When cast using a spell slot of 5th level or higher without ritual casting, you can choose one of the following enhancements for each slot level above 4th:
- Enlarge the palanquin by 5 feet, housing two additional Medium creatures.
- Increase its speed by 5 feet.
- Increase its carrying capacity by 100 pounds.
