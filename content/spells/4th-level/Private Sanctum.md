_4th-level abjuration_

You make an area within range magically secure. The area is a cube that can be as small as 5 feet to as large as 100 feet on each side. The spell lasts for the duration or until you use an action to dismiss it. You choose any combination of wards: sound cannot pass the barrier, the barrier appears dark and foggy, sensors created by divination spells cannot appear inside, creatures inside cannot be targeted by divination spells, and nothing can teleport into or out of the sanctum.

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 5th level or higher, you can increase the size of the cube by 100 feet for each slot level above 4th.
