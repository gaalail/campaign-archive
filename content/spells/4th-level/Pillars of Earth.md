_4th-level transmutation (Laserllama Homebrew)_

You cause up to four stone pillars to erupt from the ground in unoccupied 5-foot spaces you can see within range. Each pillar is up to 30 feet high. If a pillar rises under a creature, it must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw or be lifted up by the pillar. If the pillar strikes a ceiling, the creature takes 4d6 bludgeoning damage and is restrained between the pillar and the ceiling.
