_4th-level conjuration_

You call forth an aberrant spirit. It manifests in an unoccupied space that you can see within range. This corporeal form uses the Aberrant Spirit stat block. When you cast the spell, choose an aberrant form: Beholderkin, Slaad, or Star Spawn. The creature is an ally to you and your companions and obeys your telepathic commands.
