Casting Time: 1 action
Range: Self
Components: V, S, M (a miniature plunger)
Duration: Concentration, up to 1 minute

One arm elongates into a suckered tentacle. When cast and as an action on later turns, make a melee spell attack against a creature within 30 feet. On a hit it takes 5d8 bludgeoning damage and, if Huge or smaller, makes a Strength save. Failure lets you knock it prone, shove it 5 feet, or disarm it and take one held item. The tentacle can also serve as a grappling hook or rope at the GM's discretion.

**At Higher Levels.** Damage increases by 1d8 per slot level above 4th.
