_4th-level conjuration_

You conjure a ball of water with a 5-foot radius on a point you can see within range. The sphere can hover but no more than 10 feet off the ground. Any creature in the sphere's space must make a Strength saving throw. On a successful save, a creature is ejected from that space to the nearest unoccupied space. On a failed save, a creature is restrained by the sphere and is engulfed by the water. As an action, you can move the sphere up to 30 feet in a straight line.
