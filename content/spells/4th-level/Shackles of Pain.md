Casting Time: 1 action
Range: 60 feet
Components: V, S, M (a pair of manacles)
Duration: Concentration, up to 1 minute

Link a willing source creature to a target. The target makes a Charisma save or is linked for one minute. Whenever the source takes damage, the target takes psychic damage equal to half that damage. If you are the source, the target takes damage immediately before your concentration save. Whenever this damages the target, it can repeat its save, ending the effect on success.
