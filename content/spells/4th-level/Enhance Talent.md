_4th-level abjuration_

You touch a willing creature and refine their natural and honed talents into a perfectly calibrated instrument.

For the duration, the target rolls all ability checks with which it is proficient with **advantage**, and treats any ability check in which it possesses **expertise as an automatic 20** on the d20.

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 5th level or higher, you can increase the duration by 10 minutes for each slot level above 4th.
