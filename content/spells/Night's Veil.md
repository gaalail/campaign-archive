*1st-level illusion*

**Material components:** a black rag, and a pinch of glitter

You create the illusion of a dark, night-sky-like veil that remains for the duration. You can make the veil up to 30 feet long, 20 feet high, and 1 foot wide. The veil is opaque on one side and transparent on the other. You choose which is which when you cast the spell. Every creature that sees the opaque side of the veil must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw or take 1d8 psychic damage. If a creature takes 8 psychic damage from this spell in one instance, it must succeed on another Wisdom saving throw or be stunned until the end of its next turn.

Creatures with truesight are unaffected by this spell.
