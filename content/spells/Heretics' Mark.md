*1st-level divination*

You choose a creature you can see within range, and the symbol of your deity appears on its forehead (or another part of its body that can be seen clearly), marking it as a heretic. Until the spell ends, you deal an extra 1d6 radiant damage to the target whenever you or an ally hit it with a weapon attack.
HL: When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 2nd level or higher, the radiant damage increases by 1d6 for each slot level above 1st.
