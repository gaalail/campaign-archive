*4th-level evocation*

**Material components:** a vial of martyr's blood

You touch a creature and the signet ring of a martyr appears on the target's hand. For the duration, the ring shines with bright light. The target regains 4d8 hit points if it knocks an evil opponent unconscious.

If the target knocks an opponent unconscious with a critical hit, it regains 32 hit points instead.
