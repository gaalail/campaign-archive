*9th-level transmutation*

The rules of existence of all that surrounds you are altered constantly, bending to your will. For the duration of the spell, you can't cast spells or benefit from any magical effects other than this spell, can't wield or use items, or can't be affected by any conditions, spells, or magical effects. Additionally, when you are subjected to damage, you take no damage and instead regain a number of hit points equal to the amount of damage. This spell functions in areas affected by the antimagic field spell.
