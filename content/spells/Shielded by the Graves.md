*Necromancy cantrip*

**Material components:** an offering

You visit the grave of a person who is a saint, and cast a ritual in which you pray for their soul and leave an offering at their grave (a sword, a flower, a wooden holy symbol, etc.).

You will either be blessed by the saint's soul or by a spirit watching over the saint's grave. You gain a +3 bonus to AC until you are hit by an attack owing to the spirit watching over you.
