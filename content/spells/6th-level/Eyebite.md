_6th-level necromancy_

For the spell's duration, your eyes become an inky void imbued with dread power. One creature of your choice within 60 feet of you that you can see must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw or be affected by one of the following effects of your choice for the duration. On each of your turns until the spell ends, you can use your action to target another creature:

- **Asleep.** The target falls unconscious.
- **Panicked.** The target is frightened of you and must take the Dash action and move away from you by the safest and shortest available route.
- **Sickened.** The target has disadvantage on attack rolls and ability checks.
