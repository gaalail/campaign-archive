Casting Time: 1 action
Range: 90 feet (20-foot-radius sphere)
Components: V, S, M (a piece of felt)
Duration: Concentration, up to 1 minute

Whenever a creature attempts to cast a spell of 1st level or higher in the sphere, it makes a spellcasting ability check against your spell save DC. On success, the spell is cast normally. On failure, the spell is cast and an additional effect occurs; roll a d10: 1 gravity smash; 2 phantasmal killer; 3 confusion; 4 fireball; 5 zippit!; 6 web; 7 darkness; 8 silence; 9 regain 2d10 hit points; 10 cast the spell without expending its spell slot. Added spells target or center on the caster and require no concentration. At initiative count 20, roll a d20 for each ongoing added effect; it ends on 11 or higher.

**At Higher Levels.** Radius increases by 10 feet per slot level above 6th.
