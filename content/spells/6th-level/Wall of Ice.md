_6th-level evocation_

You create a wall of ice on a solid surface within range. You can form it into a hemispherical dome or a sphere with a radius of up to 10 feet, or shape a flat surface made up of ten 10-foot-square panels. If the wall cuts through a creature's space when it appears, the creature must make a Dexterity saving throw, taking 10d6 cold damage on a failure or half on a success. Destroying a 10-foot section leaves behind a sheet of frigid air that deals 5d6 cold damage.

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 7th level or higher, the damage the wall deals when it appears increases by 2d6, and the frigid air damage increases by 1d6, for each slot level above 6th.
