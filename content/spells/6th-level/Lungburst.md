Casting Time: 1 action
Range: 90 feet
Components: V, S
Duration: Instantaneous

A creature you see makes a Constitution save, with disadvantage while holding its breath. Failure deals 7d6 thunder and 7d6 necrotic damage and starts suffocation; success deals half damage and no suffocation. A suffocating creature repeats the save at each turn's end, ending the effect on success. Creatures that need not breathe are immune. Greater restoration, heal, or wish restores breathing and ends suffocation.

**At Higher Levels.** Both damage types increase by 1d6 per slot level above 6th.
