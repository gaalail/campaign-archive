_6th-level transmutation (ritual)_

Using a ritual of ancient magic, you distort the gravitational balance of an enclosed space of your choice for the duration. The chamber can be of any shape up to 200 feet wide, 200 feet tall, and 200 feet long. While in the chamber, a creature doesn't take falling damage, has its jump distance tripled, has a flying speed equal to its walking speed, and can hover. Casting this spell on the same spot every day for a year makes this effect permanent.
