_6th-level transmutation_

**Material components:** a handful of leaves

You throw a handful of leaves in the air, and they swiftly fly in a 60-foot cone, slashing into everything that stands in their way. Each creature in the cone must make a Dexterity saving throw. A creature takes 12d6 slashing damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.
HL: When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 7th level or higher, the damage increases by 1d6 for each slot level above 7th.
