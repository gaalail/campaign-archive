*Conjuration cantrip*

You conjure a symbol of your deity momentarily with a burst of light. All other creatures within 5 feet of you must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw or take 1d6 radiant damage. Your allies have advantage on this saving throw. An ally that worships the same deity as you does not take this damage.
HL: This spell's damage increases by 1d6 when you reach 5th level (2d6), 11th level (3d6), and 17th level (4d6).
