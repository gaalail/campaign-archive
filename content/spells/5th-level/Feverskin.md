Casting Time: 1 action
Range: Self
Components: V, S, M (a red-hot chilli pepper, consumed)
Duration: Concentration, up to 1 minute

Make a melee spell attack against a creature within reach. On a hit, it takes 6d6 fire damage. Repeat the attack as an action on later turns. Your speed increases by 15 feet, and you gain 2d6 temporary HP at the start of each turn.

**At Higher Levels.** Damage increases by 1d6 per slot level above 5th.
