_5th-level transmutation_

You carry the blood of a saint within your veins, which harms your enemies when spilled. When a creature within 5 feet of you deals piercing or slashing damage to you, it must make a Dexterity saving throw, taking 4d6 acid damage on a fail, or half as much on a successful one.
