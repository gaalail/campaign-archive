Casting Time: 1 action
Range: 60 feet
Components: V, S, M (a gem-encrusted cockroach worth 250 gp, consumed)
Duration: 1 minute

A willing creature ignores exhaustion and poison, cannot be incapacitated, stunned, paralyzed, or fall unconscious. Damage that would reduce it to 0 HP causes a death save instead; three failures kill it. When the spell ends, it gains one exhaustion level per failed save. If it survives, it falls unconscious for a duration equal to the spell's duration and cannot be woken except by wish.

**At Higher Levels.** Duration becomes 10 minutes at 6th, 1 hour at 7th, 8 hours at 8th, or 1 day at 9th level.
