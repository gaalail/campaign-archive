Casting Time: 1 action
Range: Self (20-foot-radius sphere)
Components: V, S, M (bones from at least one non-animated corpse, consumed)
Duration: 1 minute

Draw bones from corpses in range to form a carapace. Gain temporary hit points per corpse: Small 1d6+1, Medium 1d8+2, Large 1d10+5, Huge 1d12+12, Gargantuan 1d20+25. As an action, erupt the armor and expend all remaining temporary hit points. Other creatures in range make Dexterity saves, taking piercing damage equal to the expended temporary hit points on failure, half on success. The bones then shatter.

**At Higher Levels.** Radius increases by 10 feet per slot level above 5th.
