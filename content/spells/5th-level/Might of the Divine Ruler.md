_5th-level transmutation_

Your body becomes silver and your size increases by one size category. For instance, if your size is Medium, you become Large. You have resistance to necrotic; bludgeoning, piercing and slashing damage from nonmagical attacks, advantage on saving throws against necromancy spells and being charmed and frightened. Additionally, your melee weapon attacks deal an extra 2d6 radiant damage on a hit.
