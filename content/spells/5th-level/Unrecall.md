_5th-level enchantment_

You completely wipe away the memories a creature has of you. One creature you can see must make a Wisdom saving throw (with advantage if in combat against you). On a failed save, the target is charmed by you and incapacitated while its memories of you dissolve.

You must break line of sight with the target before the spell ends; otherwise, the spell fails and the memories return. If the target takes damage or is targeted by another spell, the effect ends immediately without erasing memory.

On a successful casting, the creature replaces you in its recollection with plausible rationalizations (e.g., jumping out of the path of a missile itself rather than being pushed). If it sees you later, it makes a Wisdom saving throw each minute to recall you. The memory loss can only be reversed via [[greater-restoration]] or [[wish]].

**At Higher Levels.** When cast using a spell slot of 6th level or higher, you can attempt to erase the memory of one additional creature for each slot level above 5th.
