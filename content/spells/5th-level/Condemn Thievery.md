_5th-level divination_

**Material components:** a stolen coin retrieved from a thief

You touch a creature and draw the attention of justice to it. The creature must succeed on a Charisma saving throw or magically drop anything it is carrying that was stolen within a week. The dropped objects fall on the nearest unoccupied space on the ground.
