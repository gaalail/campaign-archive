_5th-level evocation_

**Material components:** a piece of skull bone

Choose a creature you can see within range. You cause its skull to snap, piercing its insides. The creature must make a Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, the target takes 8d10 piercing damage and is paralyzed until the end of its next turn. On asuccessful save, the target takes half as much damage and isn't paralyzed. А creature without a skull is immune to this spell.
