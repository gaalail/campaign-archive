_5th-level evocation_

You create a Large hand of shimmering, translucent force in an unoccupied space that you can see within range. The hand lasts for the spell's duration, and it moves at your command, mimicking the movements of your own hand. When you cast the spell and as a bonus action on your subsequent turns, you can move the hand up to 60 feet and then cause Clenched Fist (4d8 force damage), Forceful Hand (push creature), Grasping Hand (grapple and crush for 2d6 + modifier bludgeoning), or Interposing Hand (half cover and block movement).

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 6th level or higher, Clenched Fist damage increases by 2d8 and Grasping Hand damage increases by 2d6 for each slot level above 5th.
