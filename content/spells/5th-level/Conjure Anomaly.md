Casting Time: 1 minute
Range: 90 feet
Components: V, S, M (an aberration component)
Duration: Concentration, up to 1 hour

An aberration of CR 5 or lower, chosen by the GM, appears in an unoccupied space. It is friendly, has its own initiative, and obeys verbal commands. Without commands, it defends itself but takes no other actions. It disappears at 0 hit points or when the spell ends. If concentration breaks, it remains up to one hour but becomes hostile and cannot be dismissed.

**At Higher Levels.** Maximum CR increases by 1 per slot level above 5th.
