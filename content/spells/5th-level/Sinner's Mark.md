*5th-level divination*

You hold the hand of a creature and learn whether or not it has sinned in the last week by reading the marks on its hand. The creature must also succeed on a Wisdom saving throw or you see a cryptic vision about the sins it committed. If the creature has no hands, you can look at its eyes, face, or any other body part that the GM approves of.
