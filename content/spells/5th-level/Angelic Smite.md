_5th-level evocation_

You imbue a weapon you are wielding with angelic might until the spell ends. The imbued weapon gains the thrown property (with a range of 20/60 feet) for the next attack you make with this weapon until the spell ends. Also, the weapon deals an extra 4d6 radiant damage on a hit.
