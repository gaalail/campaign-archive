*3rd-level illusion*

**Material components:** a piece of glass

When you are targeted by a melee attack, you spread wings made of glass. The wings instantly burst into color with all the colors of the rainbow and confuse the attacker. When you use this spell, roll a d100. On a 50 or higher, the attack misses you, dealing no damage. Otherwise, you take the hit.
HL: When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 4th level or higher, the lower limit of the interval required for the attack to miss you is lowered by 5 percent for each slot level above 3rd.
