**Prerequisite:** You must be a good-aligned paladin

Your Lay on Hands feature is infused with the goodness of your soul. The range of your Lay on Hands feature becomes 15 feet. In addition, your pool of healing power for Lay on Hands becomes equal to your paladin level x 6.

*From Corpus Angelus.*
