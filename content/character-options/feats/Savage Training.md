# Savage Training

You can draw upon the anger within your soul to perform Savage Exploits.

**Savage Exploits Known.** Learn two 1st-degree Savage Exploits from the [[character-options/classes/Barbarian|Alternate Barbarian]] list. If an Exploit requires a save DC, it equals 8 + your Proficiency Bonus + your Strength or Dexterity modifier (your choice).

**Retraining Exploits.** Whenever you gain a level, you can replace one Savage Exploit learned through this feat with another 1st-degree Savage Exploit of your choice.

**Exploit Dice.** You gain two d4 Exploit Dice to fuel these Exploits, expended when used and regained after a short or long rest. If you already have Exploit Dice when you gain this feat, you instead gain one Exploit Die equal to your others.
