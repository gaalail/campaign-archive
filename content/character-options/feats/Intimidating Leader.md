# Intimidating Leader

**Prerequisite:** Intimidation proficiency

You use fear and intimidation to rally those who follow you into battle.

**Ability Score Increase.** Increase your Strength, Constitution, or Charisma score by 1, to a maximum of 20.

**Fearsome Rally.** When another creature within 30 feet of you that can see or hear you fails a saving throw to resist the Charmed or Frightened condition, you can use your reaction to make an Intimidation check against the same save DC. On a success, the creature is not Charmed or Frightened.

You can use this reaction a number of times equal to your Proficiency Bonus, and regain all expended uses when you finish a long rest.
