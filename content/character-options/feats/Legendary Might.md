# Legendary Might

You have always found ways to leverage your great might for additional benefits. You gain the benefits on the table that correspond to your Strength modifier and lower. If your Strength modifier increases, the benefits you gain also increase.

| Strength Modifier | Benefit                                                                                                                                      |
| ----------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| +1                | You count as one size larger for carrying capacity, the size of creatures you can Grapple, and the weight you can push, pull, lift, or drag. |
| +2                | You can make Strength (Intimidation) checks in place of Charisma (Intimidation) checks.                                                      |
| +3                | You gain a climbing speed or swimming speed equal to your walking speed.                                                                     |
| +4                | Whenever you make an Athletics check, treat a d20 roll of 7 or lower as an 8.                                                                |
| +5                | When forced to make a Constitution saving throw, you can choose to make a Strength saving throw instead.                                     |
