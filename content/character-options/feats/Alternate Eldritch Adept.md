# Alternate Eldritch Adept

**Updated Eldritch Adept Feat**

You have unlocked a fragment of occult lore, known as an Eldritch Invocation. It follows the rules listed below:

**Trinket.** This dark knowledge is bound within a Trinket, a tiny object reminiscent of the power bound within. To gain the Invocation's benefits, you must have the Trinket on your person. If it is lost or destroyed, it reforms on your person at the end of your next short or long rest.

**Eldritch Invocation.** You learn one [[character-options/class-options/eldritch-invocations/index|Eldritch Invocation]] of your choice from the [[character-options/classes/Warlock|Alternate Warlock]]. If an Invocation has a Warlock level prerequisite, you can learn it if your total level would meet that level prerequisite.

**Eldritch Spellcasting.** If the Eldritch Invocation you learn requires a Spellcasting or Pact Ability, use your Spellcasting ability (if you have one). If you do not have one, choose either Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma as your Pact Ability.

Any spell you learn through an Eldritch Invocation counts as one of your Spells Known, or the spells you prepare each day, without counting against the total. If you have another method of casting spells, such as Spellcasting, Psionics, or Pact Magic, you can cast this spell using those methods.
