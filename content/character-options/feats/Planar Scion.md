# Planar Scion

**Prerequisites:** Approval of your DM

You have been touched by the powerful magic of one of the outer Planes of the Cosmos, granting you wondrous power. When you gain this Feat, choose one of the Planes from the Planar Scion table, and you gain the associated benefits:

**Ability Score Increase.** Increase either your Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma score by `1`, up to a maximum of `20`.

**Infused Magic.** You learn the one Infused Spell associated with your Plane, as shown on the Planar Scion table.

**Infused School.** You also learn one 1st-level spell of your choice from one of the Infused Schools associated with your Plane, as shown on the Planar Scion table.

**Planar Spellcasting.** Use your current Spellcasting Ability as the Spellcasting Ability for these spells (if you have one). If you do not have one, choose either Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma as your Spellcasting ability for these spells.

Once per long rest, you can cast one of these spells without expending a spell slot. If you have another method of casting spells, such as Spellcasting, Psionics, or Pact Magic, you can cast both of these spells using those methods as well.
