# Greater Familiar

**Prerequisites:** 4th level, the [[spells/1st-level/Conjure Familiar|conjure familiar]] spell

You have unlocked ancient or forbidden magic to conjure more powerful Familiars. You gain the following benefits:

**Eldritch Familiar.** Choose one [[character-options/class-options/familiars/index|Eldritch Familiar]] available to the [[character-options/classes/Warlock|Alternate Warlock]] (also browsable under [[companions|Familiars & Companions]]). When you cast [[spells/1st-level/Conjure Familiar|conjure familiar]], you can choose for your Familiar to take that form.

**Combat.** The Familiar is bound to obey your orders to the best of its abilities. In combat, it shares your initiative, and it acts on your turn. It can move and use its reaction on its own; however, it will only take the Dodge action on its turn unless you use your bonus action to command it to take an action from its stat block or another combat action.

**Statistics.** This special Eldritch Familiar stat block uses your Proficiency Bonus (`PB`). References to Warlock level use your total character level instead, and any references to your Spell save DC or Spell Attack modifier use your Spellcasting ability (if you have one). If you do not have one, choose either Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma as the ability score to be used for your Pact Ability and Spellcasting Ability.
