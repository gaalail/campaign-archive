# Feats

**Feats** are special abilities you can take instead of ability score increases.

Gain at levels 4, 8, 12, 16, and 19 (unless your class says otherwise). The table below is every feat in this archive — mostly Corpus Angelus, plus SRD [[Grappler]].

---

## What is a feat?

A feat gives you a special power, skill boost, resistance, or talent. Some have prerequisites.

---

## How feats work

**Every 4 levels,** you can either:
- Increase an **ability score** (for example +2 STR, or +1 to two scores)
- Take a **feat** instead
