# Feats

**Feats** are special abilities you can take instead of ability score increases.

**100+ feats available** | Gain at levels 4, 8, 12, 16, 19 | Optional

---

## What Is a Feat?

A feat gives you:
- **Special power** (new ability or technique)
- **Skill boost** (expertise in something)
- **Resistances or immunities**
- **Talent enhancement** (better at something you already do)

---

## How Feats Work

**Every 4 levels,** you can either:
- ✓ Increase an **ability score** (e.g., +2 STR, +1 WIS)
- ✓ Take a **feat** (special ability instead)

**Levels you gain feats:** 4, 8, 12, 16, 19

## Popular Feats

- **Great Weapon Master** — Attack with bigger weapons
- **Sharpshooter** — Attack with bows/crossbows
- **War Caster** — Better spell casting in combat
- **Resilient** — Gain proficiency in a saving throw
- **Lucky** — Reroll dice (requires Halfling)
- **Observant** — See and hear better
- **Polearm Master** — Weapon fighting technique

---

## Feat Prerequisites

Some feats have **requirements:**
- Ability score minimums (e.g., "requires STR 13+")
- Class requirements (e.g., "Monks only")
- Other feat requirements (e.g., "requires Great Weapon Master")

Check the feat page for requirements.
