*Clever, masked beast-folk with nimble paws and bushy tails, divided into urban tricksters, resilient scavengers, and mystical illusionists.*

Rakin are small, furry humanoids bearing the traits of raccoons, possums, and tanukis. Renowned across coastal cities, ancient forests, and refuse-rich harbor towns for their insatiable curiosity and dexterity, rakin approach life as a high-stakes scavenger hunt. Their prehensile paws, sharp night vision, and natural climbing agility allow them to scale urban roofs and high forest canopies with identical ease.

## Rakin Traits

**Ability score increase:** +2 DEX  
**Age:** Rakin mature quickly, reaching adulthood by age 14, and live around 70 to 80 years.  
**Alignment:** Independence, curiosity, and a disregard for strict property laws mean most rakin lean toward chaotic good or chaotic neutral alignments.  
**Size:** Rakin stand between 3½ and 4 feet tall and weigh between 50 and 90 pounds. Your size is Small.  
**Speed:** 30 feet, climb 30 feet  
**Languages:** You can speak, read, and write Common and one additional language of your choice.

## Traits

> [!info]- Darkvision
> Accustomed to nighttime scavenging and subterranean alleyways, you can see in dim light within 60 feet of you as if it were bright light, and in darkness as if it were dim light. You can't discern color in darkness, only shades of gray.

> [!info]- Subrace
> Three main subraces populate the world: Urkin (raccoon-folk), Posskin (possum-folk), and Tanukin (raccoon-dog spirit-folk). Choose one of these subraces.

## Subraces

### Urkin (Raccoon-Kin)

Urkin sport distinctive banded facial masks, ringed tails, and unmatched manual dexterity. They thrive in crowded settlements and thieves' guilds where locks and pockets invite their curious claws.

> [!info]- Ability Score Increase
> Your Intelligence or Charisma score increases by 1 (your choice).

> [!info]- Urban Infiltrator
> You gain proficiency in your choice of the Sleight of Hand or Stealth skill. You also learn Thieves' Cant.

> [!info]- Nimble Dodge
> When you are subjected to an effect that allows you to make a Dexterity saving throw to take only half damage and you can see the source of the effect, you can use your reaction to make the save with advantage. Immediately after making the save, you can move up to your speed without provoking opportunity attacks. Once you use this trait, you can't use it again until you finish a short or long rest.

---

### Posskin (Possum-Kin)

Posskin are hardy, pink-nosed survivors with prehensile tails and an ironclad digestive constitution. They are famous for their ability to fake their own demise with terrifying authenticity when overwhelmed.

> [!info]- Ability Score Increase
> Your Constitution score increases by 1.

> [!info]- Sharp Jaws
> Your pointed snout contains a set of sharp teeth. Your bite is a natural weapon, which you can use to make unarmed strikes. If you hit with it, you deal piercing or slashing damage equal to 1d6 + your Strength modifier.

> [!info]- Scavenger's Constitution
> Your immune system handles foul rot and toxins easily. You have advantage on saving throws against poison and disease, and you have resistance against poison damage.

> [!info]- Play Dead
> When you take damage that reduces you to half your hit point maximum or lower, you can use your reaction to drop prone and enter a state of feigned death. For the next hour, or until you choose to end this state as a bonus action or take further damage, you appear dead to all inspection and magic that detects life. During this time, you are incapacitated and have resistance to all damage except psychic damage. Once you use this trait, you can't do so again until you finish a short or long rest.

---

### Tanukin (Tanuki-Kin)

Tanukin are round, gregarious forest folk imbued with ancient nature magic and spirit-tricks. Masters of transformation and illusion, they love sharing feasts, playing lighthearted pranks, and wandering hidden spirit trails.

> [!info]- Ability Score Increase
> Your Wisdom or Charisma score increases by 1 (your choice).

> [!info]- Silver Tongue
> You gain proficiency in your choice of the Deception or Persuasion skill.

> [!info]- Wild Speech
> Through sounds and gestures, you can communicate simple ideas with Small or smaller beasts.

> [!info]- Tanukin Magic
> You know the [[minor-illusion]] cantrip. When you reach 3rd level, you can cast the [[disguise-self]] spell once with this trait and regain the ability to do so when you finish a long rest. When you reach 5th level, you can cast the [[alter-self]] spell once with this trait and regain the ability to do so when you finish a long rest. Charisma or Wisdom (your choice when you choose this subrace) is your spellcasting ability for these spells.
