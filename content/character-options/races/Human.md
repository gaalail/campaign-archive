*In the reckonings of most worlds, humans are the youngest of the common races, late to arrive on the world scene and short-lived in comparison, yet possessing boundless ambition, adaptability, and drive.*

## Human Traits

**Ability score increase:** +1 STR, +1 DEX, +1 CON, +1 INT, +1 WIS, +1 CHA  
**Age:** Humans reach adulthood in their late teens and live less than a century.  
**Alignment:** Humans tend toward no particular alignment. The best and the worst are found among them.  
**Size:** Humans vary widely in height and build, from barely 5 feet to well over 6 feet tall. Regardless of your position in that range, your size is Medium.  
**Speed:** 30 feet  
**Languages:** You can speak, read, and write Common and one extra language of your choice. Humans typically learn the languages of other peoples they deal with, including obscure dialects. They are fond of sprinkling their speech with words borrowed from other tongues: Orc curses, Elvish musical expressions, Dwarvish military phrases, and so on.

## Traits

> [!info]- Extra Language
> You can speak, read, and write Common and one extra language of your choice.

> [!info]- Human Versatility (Standard)
> Humans are the most adaptable and ambitious people among the common races. Your ability scores each increase by 1.

> [!info]- Variant Human (Optional Rule)
> If your campaign uses the optional feat rules, your DM might allow these variant traits:

> - **Skills:** You gain proficiency in one skill of your choice.
> - **Feat:** You gain one [[character-options/feats/index|Feat]] of your choice.
