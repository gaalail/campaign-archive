> "I'll call you nugget."
> — Ur-Gash, Orc Adventurer

Nestled in secluded clearings in the woods, on hilltops with extraordinary vantages, or in easily collapsible canyons, gobbocs (go-BOC) seek safety above all else. Survival is a gobboc's watchword; their own survival is their number one goal on an expedition—treasure, glory, and saving the world be damned.

## Brave Cowards

You'll likely recognize the back of a gobboc's head more than the front, given their propensity to run away at the first sign of danger. While this might look like cowardice, to a gobboc it is the bravest form of pragmatism: life is terrifying, and living to see another dawn is the greatest triumph possible.

In battle, gobbocs follow the *Klucking Fighting Creed*, darting between larger allies as mobile shields and unleashing lightning-fast skirmishing attacks before falling back out of retaliation range.

## Avian Ascendency

Gobbocs worship birds—the larger the bird, the more sacred its majesty. Gobboc belief carries an innate, curious magic that actually sparks heightened intellect in avian creatures around them. In wartime, gobboc hosts march alongside razor-clawed cassowary cavalry and super-intelligent hippogriffs.

## Gobboc Traits

**Ability Score Increase:** Your Dexterity score increases by 2, and either your Charisma score or Wisdom score increases by 1 (your choice).  
**Age:** Gobbocs grow rapidly and die young. They walk hours after hatching, reach adulthood by age 7, and live around 30 years.  
**Size:** Gobbocs are a diminutive race, standing between 2 and 3 feet tall and weighing 30 to 45 pounds. Your size is Small.  
**Speed:** Your base walking speed is 25 feet.  
**Languages:** You can speak, read, and write Common and one other language of your choice.

## Traits

> [!info]- Commune with Fowl Folk
> You have the ability to communicate in a limited manner with feathered beasts and monstrosities. They can understand the meaning of your words, though you have no special ability to understand them in return. You have advantage on all Charisma checks you make to influence them.

> [!info]- Coward's Creed
> After successfully hitting with a melee attack, you can take the Disengage action as a bonus action.

> [!info]- Feathered
> Your light bones and feathered form cushion your falls; you take only half damage from falling.

> [!info]- Gallus Domesticus
> You can cast the *[[spells/4th-level/polymorph|polymorph]]* spell on yourself with this trait, using Charisma as your spellcasting ability. When you cast polymorph with this trait, you always assume the form of a **Tiny chicken** (statblock below). Once you cast this spell, you cannot do so again with this trait until you finish a long rest.

> [!info]- Headless Chicken
> When you are reduced to 0 hit points but not killed outright, you don't immediately fall unconscious. Instead, you can immediately move up to your speed without provoking opportunity attacks. You then fall unconscious as if you had been reduced to 0 hit points in the space where you finish this movement.
