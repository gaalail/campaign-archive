*Amorphous, semi-translucent beings formed of cohesive living gelatin, capable of shifting into predatory cubes or creeping puddles.*

Oozekin are sentient amalgams of gelatinous matter and humanoid morphology. Some trace their lineage to arcane laboratory experiments gone awry or alchemical disasters; others arose near deep cavern shrines dedicated to primeval slime lords. Beneath their translucent, brightly tinted dermal layers, internal organs drift suspended like colorful stones in clear water, reshaping on command.

## Oozekin Traits

**Ability score increase:** +2 CON, +1 STR  
**Age:** Oozekin coalesce into consciousness fully formed and do not age conventionally. As long as they maintain moisture and organic nutrients, they can endure for centuries.  
**Alignment:** Adaptable, curious, and fluid in thought, oozekin generally lean toward chaotic or neutral alignments.  
**Creature Type:** You are both a Humanoid and an Ooze. If a spell or effect affects either type, you are affected by it.  
**Size:** You can choose whether your standard humanoid form is Small (3 to 4 feet tall) or Medium (4 to 6 feet tall) when you create your character.  
**Speed:** 30 feet  
**Languages:** You can speak, read, and write Common and Primordial (Aquan).

## Traits

> [!info]- Darkvision
> You can see in dim light within 60 feet of you as if it were bright light, and in darkness as if it were dim light. You can't discern color in darkness, only shades of gray.

> [!info]- Acidic Flesh
> Your protoplasmic flesh dissolves hostile material. You have resistance to acid damage. In addition, you know the [[acid-splash]] cantrip. Constitution is your spellcasting ability for it. When you cast it targeting a creature you are grappling or that is grappling you, the creature automatically fails its saving throw against the spell.

> [!info]- Reshapeable
> Starting at 3rd level, your control over your semi-viscous matrix allows you to alter your physical structure. As an action, you can reshape your body into one of two forms for up to 1 hour, or until you revert to your standard form as a bonus action:
> 
> - **Cube Form:** You assume the geometric form of a translucent, predatory gelatinous cube (Medium or Large, your choice). While in this form, your speed becomes 20 feet, and you can attempt to Engulf a creature within 5 feet. As an action, make a melee attack roll against a creature of your size or smaller. On a hit, the creature is trapped inside your gelatinous mass: it is restrained, takes 2d6 acid damage at the start of each of its turns, and has disadvantage on saving throws against spells you cast. The creature can use its action to make a Strength (Athletics) check against DC 8 + your Constitution modifier + your proficiency bonus to escape. You can hold only one engulfed creature at a time.
> 
> - **Puddle Form:** You melt down into a creeping, fluid puddle. In this form, you gain a climbing speed of 20 feet and can move along vertical surfaces and ceilings without needing to make an ability check. You can also squeeze through spaces as narrow as 1 inch wide without squeezing penalties. While in puddle form, you cannot wield weapons, hold items, or cast spells with somatic or material components.
> 
> Once you use this trait, you cannot do so again until you finish a short or long rest.
