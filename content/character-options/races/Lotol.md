# Lotol

*Curious, wide-eyed salamander-folk known for their unshakeable calm, slippery epidermis, and rapidly mutating physiology.*

Lotol resemble bipedal, brightly colored axolotls and salamanders with wide, unblinking eyes and frilled external gills. Known across riverlands and tropical deltas for an almost unflappable demeanor, lotol drift through catastrophes with serene nonchalance. Their bodies are remarkably plastic, able to mutate specialized morphological adaptations to match whatever biome or peril they encounter.

---

## Lotol Traits

Your lotol character has unique amphibian qualities and genetic adaptability.

> [!info]- Ability Score Increase
> Your Wisdom score increases by 2, and your Constitution score increases by 1.

> [!info]- Age
> Lotol reach physical maturity quickly by age 12 and live relatively short, vibrant lives, rarely exceeding 60 years.

> [!info]- Alignment
> Driven by curiosity and rarely perturbed by societal rigidities, lotol tend toward chaotic neutral or neutral good alignments.

> [!info]- Size
> Lotol stand between 4 and 5 feet tall with slender, supple builds weighing around 100 to 130 pounds. Your size is Medium.

> [!info]- Speed
> Your base walking speed is 30 feet.

> [!info]- Oblivious
> Your cheerful detachment and calm mindset protect you from mental assaults. You have advantage on Wisdom saving throws against being charmed, frightened, or mentally compelled.

> [!info]- Slippery Skin
> Your skin secretes a protective, frictionless coating of mucus. You have advantage on ability checks and saving throws made to escape or avoid being grappled or restrained.

> [!info]- Adaptive Polymorphism
> Your body readily morphs to develop specialized survival adaptations. Choose two of the following traits when you create your character. Whenever you finish a long rest, you can meditate to swap one of your chosen adaptations for another:
> 
> - **Aqueous Adaptation:** You develop webbed digits and internal gills. You gain a swimming speed of 30 feet and can breathe both air and water.
> - **Gecko Cling:** You develop microscopic spatulae on your pads. You gain a climbing speed of 30 feet and can climb difficult surfaces, including upside down on ceilings, without needing to make an ability check.
> - **Darkvision:** Your eyes develop reflective tapetum lucidum layers. You can see in dim light within 60 feet of you as if it were bright light, and in darkness as if it were dim light. You can't discern color in darkness, only shades of gray.
> - **Prehensile Tongue:** Your tongue lengthens and becomes coated in strong adhesive. You can use an action to manipulate an object, retrieve an unheld item weighing up to 10 pounds, or make an unarmed strike within 10 feet of you.
> - **Shocking Skin:** Your mucus carries bioelectric charges. When a creature touches you or hits you with a melee attack while within 5 feet of you, you can use your reaction to deal 1d6 lightning damage to that creature.

> [!info]- Languages
> You can speak, read, and write Common and Aquan.
