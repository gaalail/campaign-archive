# Golynn

*Burly, subterranean earth-shapers covered in dense, chitinous plates and equipped with heavy excavation claws.*

Golynn are subterranean humanoids whose stout frames and reinforced carapaces are built for life beneath tons of solid rock. Originating in deep fault lines and cavernous under-realms, they view soil and stone not as barriers, but as malleable medium through which to move. Though quiet and patient by disposition, their earth-churning biology makes them devastating shock troops and relentless subterranean explorers.

---

## Golynn Traits

Your golynn character possesses natural adaptations evolved for excavation and subterranean survival.

> [!info]- Ability Score Increase
> Your Constitution score increases by 2, and your Strength score increases by 1.

> [!info]- Age
> Golynn mature slowly, reaching adulthood around 25 years old. Thanks to their slow metabolic rate, they commonly live up to 250 years.

> [!info]- Alignment
> Accustomed to close-knit, highly disciplined mining societies, most golynn lean toward lawful alignments, often neutral lawful or lawful good.

> [!info]- Size
> Golynn stand between 7 and 8 feet tall and weigh between 300 and 400 pounds. Your size is Medium.

> [!info]- Speed
> Your base walking speed is 30 feet. You also possess a burrowing speed of 15 feet through soft earth, sand, or loose stone, but not solid stone or metal.

> [!info]- Natural Armor
> Your tough, segmented hide provides natural protection. When you aren't wearing armor, your AC is 13 + your Dexterity modifier. You can use your natural armor to determine your AC if the armor you wear would leave you with a lower AC. A shield's benefits apply as normal while you use your natural armor.

> [!info]- Powerful Build
> You count as one size larger when determining your carrying capacity and the weight you can push, drag, or lift.

> [!info]- Drill Dervish
> When you burrow directly beneath a creature of Large size or smaller, you can disturb the ground underfoot. The target must make a Strength saving throw (DC = 8 + your Strength modifier + your proficiency bonus) or be knocked prone as the ground collapses beneath its footing.

> [!info]- Tremorsense
> You can detect and pinpoint the origin of vibrations within 5 feet of you, provided that you and the source of the vibrations are in contact with the same ground or surface. As an action, you can concentrate on your subterranean senses to extend this tremorsense range to 20 feet until the end of your next turn.

> [!info]- Languages
> You can speak, read, and write Common and Terran.
