# Ombrask

*Mute, shadowy wanderers touched by the Shadowfell who communicate solely by stealing and mimicking the voices of the fallen.*

Ombrasks are enigmatic beings whose forebears were irrevocably altered by prolonged exposure to the desolate Gloom of the Shadowfell or deep planar rifts. Their silhouettes seem to absorb ambient illumination, appearing as silhouettes with faint ember-like glints for eyes. Born without vocal cords capable of speech, an ombrask's mind can siphon the sonic psyches of creatures expiring near them, allowing them to replicate stolen voices with eerie precision.

---

## Ombrask Traits

Your ombrask character shares traits born of planar shadow and auditory mimicry.

> [!info]- Ability Score Increase
> Your Dexterity score increases by 2. In addition, one other ability score increases by 1, determined by your ancestral origins prior to planar transformation (such as Strength for former Orc/Goliath ancestry, Intelligence for Gnome/Elf, Charisma for Human/Tiefling, or Constitution for Dwarf).

> [!info]- Age
> Ombrask mature at the same rate as the species they descended from, but their life spans are prolonged by planar shadow, frequently exceeding 200 years.

> [!info]- Alignment
> Surviving through discretion and camouflage, most ombrasks lean toward neutral alignments.

> [!info]- Size
> Depending on your ancestral lineage, your size is either Medium (typically 5 to 6 feet tall) or Small (typically 3 to 4 feet tall).

> [!info]- Speed
> Your base walking speed is 30 feet.

> [!info]- Darkvision
> Accustomed to shadow-saturated realms, you can see in dim light within 60 feet of you as if it were bright light, and in darkness as if it were dim light. You can't discern color in darkness, only shades of gray.

> [!info]- Fade Away
> Starting at 5th level, you can vanish into ambient shadow. You can cast the [[invisibility]] spell on yourself once with this trait, requiring no material components, and you regain the ability to cast it this way when you finish a long rest. Constitution is your spellcasting ability for this spell.

> [!info]- Lightbender
> Your shadow-woven form disperses piercing illumination. You have advantage on saving throws against being blinded, and you have resistance to radiant damage.

> [!info]- Trackless
> You leave no footprints and muffle your own steps. You gain proficiency in the Stealth skill.

> [!info]- Voice Thief
> You are naturally mute and cannot speak normally. However, when a creature that can speak dies within 30 feet of you, you can use your reaction to capture a fragment of its acoustic psyche. 
> 
> You can store a number of stolen voices equal to your proficiency bonus. You can flawlessly mimic the pitch, tone, accent, and inflection of any stored voice, and you can communicate verbally using those voices. If you attempt to harvest a voice when your capacity is full, you choose which existing voice to replace. A creature that hears you speak can discern that the voice is an imitation with a successful Wisdom (Insight) check contested by your Charisma (Deception) check.

> [!info]- Languages
> You can read and write Common and Undercommon, but you can only speak aloud using voices acquired through your Voice Thief trait.
