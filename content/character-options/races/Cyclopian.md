> "Ok, ready? Left eye. Right eye. Left eye. Right... Oh, I do beg your pardon."
> — Sir Raffael, Optometrist

The pursuit of knowledge, the acquisition of secrets, and the hoarding of trinkets with storied histories are common practices among any creature raised by cyclopians (sy-CLO-pee-ans). From dog tags to tapestries, musical manuscripts to carved tablets, and magical memory orbs to ancient heirlooms, their "libraries" (as cyclopians refer to their cliffside and tower dwellings) are brimming with lore.

## Monocular and Myopic

A cyclopian's singular eye reflects this race's intense, almost obsessive tendency to focus on one subject to the exclusion of all else. When a cyclopian uncovers an enticing mystery or forgotten archive, little can stop their manic efforts to acquire every relevant scrap of text, artifact, or answer. Famously, cyclopians possess the supernatural capability to metabolize knowledge into physical, nutritional sustenance.

## Pilgrimage of Huge Discovery & Budding

Young cyclopians frequently embark on a Pilgrimage of Huge Discovery (PHD), journeying into the world to uncover rare knowledge and relics for their family archives. In moments when research volume becomes truly overwhelming, a cyclopian can even undergo spontaneous somatic budding—producing a smaller, genetically distinct research assistant to help catalogue their discoveries.

## Cyclopian Traits

**Ability Score Increase:** Your Intelligence score increases by 2, and either your Dexterity score or Wisdom score increases by 1 (your choice).  
**Age:** Cyclopians reach maturity in their early teens and typically live as long as humans (about 80–100 years).  
**Creature Type:** You are a humanoid. You are also considered an **aberration** for any prerequisite or effect that requires you to be an aberration.  
**Size:** With their crouched and hulking posture, cyclopians typically appear smaller than they are. At full stretch, a cyclopian stands between 5 and 6 feet tall. Your size is Medium.  
**Speed:** Your base walking speed is 30 feet, and you have a climbing speed of 20 feet.  
**Languages:** You can speak, read, and write Common, Undercommon, and one other language of your choice.

## Traits

> [!info]- Darkvision
> Accustomed to life in dimly lit archives and underground libraries, you have superior vision in dark and dim conditions. You can see in dim light within 60 feet of you as if it were bright light, and in darkness as if it were dim light. You can't discern color in darkness, only shades of gray.

> [!info]- Knowledge Seeker
> Learning is an irresistible compulsion among cyclopians. You gain proficiency in your choice of two of the following skills: Arcana, History, Insight, Investigation, Medicine, Perception, Religion, or Stealth.

> [!info]- Savant of Secrets
> You can tap into your ancestral instinct to spot hidden passages, concealed traps, and subtle clues. As an action, you can enter a cyclotrance for 1 minute. For the duration, you have advantage on Perception, Investigation, and Insight checks. Once you activate this trait, you can't do so again until you finish a long rest.

> [!info]- Thought for Food
> Information is nourishing for cyclopians in the truest sense. Each day that you discover significant new information (GM's discretion), you need only half as much food and water as normal, and you gain advantage on Constitution saving throws until you finish your next long rest.

> [!info]- Top Shelf
> Generations spent scaling towering library shelves have honed your agility. You have a climbing speed of 20 feet.
