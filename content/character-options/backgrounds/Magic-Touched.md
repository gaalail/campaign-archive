# Magic-Touched

You are one of the magic-touched. Magic flows through the earth along subterranean ley lines, and magic flows through your veins in much the same manner. Whether by birthright, astrological alignment, or arcane accident, you possess an intrinsic harmonic bond with pure magic.

**Skill Proficiencies:** Arcana, Insight  
**Languages:** Primordial  
**Equipment:** An instrument used to detect the presence of magic (such as a lantern that burns bright near arcane conduits), traveler’s clothes, and a pouch containing 10 gp.

## Feature: Intuitive Connection

You are always instinctively aware of any powerful natural sources of magic in your immediate vicinity, such as ley lines or planar convergences. You also sense if a ritual spell has been cast within the past seven days in the space you currently occupy.
