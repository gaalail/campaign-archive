# Seer

You are a seer. You bear the heavy gift of glimpsing the past, the present, and the unfolding threads of the future. The visions that appear in your mind are rarely crystal clear—like attempting to read an ancient scroll through murky water—yet they offer fragments of prophecy and warning that can alter destinies.

**Skill Proficiencies:** Insight, Perception  
**Equipment:** A divination focus (such as tarot cards, an astrolabe, or cast astragali dice), a vision journal, a quill and bottle of ink, traveler's clothes, and a pouch containing 10 gp.

## A Tool for the Job

| d8 | Divination Method |
| :---: | :--- |
| **1** | **Aeromancy:** Interpreting storm fronts, cloud patterns, and winds. |
| **2** | **Runic Divination:** Casting engraved stone or bone runes. |
| **3** | **Cartomancy:** Reading cards such as a tarot or deck of fates. |
| **4** | **Astragalomancy:** Casting knucklebones or polyhedral dice. |
| **5** | **Astrology:** Mapping the motions and conjunctions of celestial bodies. |
| **6** | **Botanomancy:** Burning sacred herbs and interpreting rising embers. |
| **7** | **Arithmancy:** Calculating sacred numerical ratios and equations. |
| **8** | **Capnomancy:** Reading shapes woven into ritual braziers' smoke. |

## Feature: Second Sight

Once per day, you can conduct a brief divination ritual to seek a glimpse of what is to come. Using your preferred focus, you enter a meditative state. The GM grants a prophetic vision regarding future events—whether personal, local, or cataclysmic—whose clarity and cryptic nature reflect the tides of destiny.
