# Apothecary

You are an apothecary. You mix liquids, herbs, powders, and salts to create the salves that will save a person's life. You might not be a doctor, but a doctor can do all the diagnosing they want: no healing happens without your products. You live surrounded by vials, pots, and canisters, ready to concoct a remedy for the deadliest of diseases or the smallest of peeves.

**Skill Proficiencies:** Nature, Medicine  
**Tool Proficiencies:** Alchemist's Supplies, Herbalism Kit  
**Languages:** One language of your choice  
**Equipment:** 10 glass vials, a pouch filled with dried herbs, a small pewter cauldron, traveling clothes, and a pouch containing 10 gp.

## Feature: Pills and Potions

Your expertise with ointments and potions allows you to ascertain the composition of any liquid you inspect, discerning the ingredients used in its creation and whether magical properties are contained within.
