# Witch

You are a witch. You forged a sacred covenant with an experienced mentor to learn the oral traditions and spirit arts that witches have practiced for millennia. You manipulate spiritual energy to augment both your spellcraft and your martial prowess. Though the path of the craft is arduous, you do not walk alone: the spirits of those who walked before you guide your every step.

**Skill Proficiencies:** Arcana, Perception  
**Languages:** One exotic language of your choice (Abyssal, Celestial, Draconic, Deep Speech, Infernal, Primordial, Sylvan, or Undercommon)  
**Equipment:** 5 sticks of ritual incense, a witch’s herbal satchel with brewing components and focus charms, a token or ring identifying your coven, traveler’s clothes, and a pouch with 10 gp.

## Witch Mentor

| d6 | Mentor Relationship |
| :---: | :--- |
| **1** | A parent or grandparent passed down the lineage craft across generations. |
| **2** | A reclusive wilderness hermit took me in when I had nowhere else to turn. |
| **3** | Indentured to a powerful urban witch to pay off an insurmountable family debt. |
| **4** | Apprenticed in an elite metropolitan coven under a world-renowned archwitch. |
| **5** | My mentor was slain by zealots; I seek vengeance and preservation of their craft. |
| **6** | Bound unwillingly to an ancient family tradition that I now strive to understand. |

## Feature: Witch Eye

You instinctively recognize whether a person you speak with is an active spellcaster or belongs to a witch tradition. In addition, you receive hospitality, sanctuary, and preferential treatment from foreign covens and fellow practitioners of the craft.
