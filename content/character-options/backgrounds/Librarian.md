# Librarian

You are a librarian. You have been tasked with keeping an unfathomable repository of books and arcane knowledge in its proper order. There is no nook, cranny, or shelf in the archives you do not know like the back of your hand. Now that you have stepped out onto the open road, you bring that uncompromising dedication to alphabetical order and archival research with you.

**Skill Proficiencies:** Investigation, History  
**Languages:** Two languages of your choice  
**Equipment:** 5 pots of ink, a quill, 5 scrolls of parchment, a ledger containing the catalog of the library you served, common clothes, and a pouch with 10 gp.

## Feature: Speed Reader

Your reading comprehension and scanning speed are vastly increased. Reading any text, tome, or manuscript takes you half as long as it would take a normal scholar.
