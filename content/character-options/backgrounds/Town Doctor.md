# Town Doctor

You are a town doctor. You are a bedrock institution in your community, knowing the ailments, genealogies, and secrets of every household in your county. While formal diagnosis and academic medicine are your trade, practical battlefield triage has made you an indispensable asset on perilous adventures.

**Skill Proficiencies:** Perception, Medicine  
**Tool Proficiencies:** Herbalism Kit  
**Languages:** Two additional languages of your choice  
**Equipment:** A set of fine clothes, a dark practitioner's coat and leather examination gloves, a medical satchel with surgical tools, a licensed physician's certificate, and a coin purse with 10 gp.

## Feature: Bedside Manner

Elderly, infirm, and ailing individuals in small towns and villages immediately regard you as a comforting authority and pillar of trust. Civic authorities and common folk alike afford you hospitality, truthful answers, and respect.
