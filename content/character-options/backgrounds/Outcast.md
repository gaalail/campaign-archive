# Outcast

You are an outcast. Following a real or perceived transgression against societal orthodoxies, you were ostracized and forced to survive on the fringes of civilization. You have learned self-reliance in the wild and forged bonds with fellow exiles, fugitives, and forgotten wanderers.

**Skill Proficiencies:** Deception, Stealth  
**Equipment:** A set of traveler’s clothes, a brass compass, a small keepsake from the home from which you were banished, 3 flasks of lamp oil, a hooded lantern, and a belt pouch with 5 gp.

## Ostracized

| d6 | Reason for Banishment |
| :---: | :--- |
| **1** | Committed a major cultural taboo and was cast out by my elders. |
| **2** | Framed for a heinous crime by an influential rival. |
| **3** | Fundamentally rejected the oppressive dogmas of my community and walked away. |
| **4** | Blackmailed into exile to protect a devastating family secret. |
| **5** | Born with an innate mark or trait viewed with superstitious dread by my people. |
| **6** | Voluntarily severed all ties with civilization in pursuit of ascetic enlightenment. |

## Feature: Birds of a Feather

Outcasts recognize each other on sight and share an unspoken bond of survival. Vagrants, hermits, bandits, and pariahs living beyond societal bounds are much more inclined to offer shelter, share intelligence, and trust your intentions.
