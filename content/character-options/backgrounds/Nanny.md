# Nanny

You are a nanny. You have dedicated yourself to safeguarding, guiding, and nurturing children who are not your own. Dealing with rowdy, rambunctious, and mischievous youth has prepared you for anything an unruly party of headstrong adventurers can throw your way.

**Skill Proficiencies:** Persuasion, Intimidation  
**Languages:** One additional language of your choice  
**Equipment:** A set of fine servant’s clothes, 10 days of rations, a spacious satchel, 3 wholesome toys/games without small choking parts, a vial of cold remedy elixir, and a purse containing 10 gp.

## Feature: A Spoonful of Sugar

You are exceptionally skilled at relating to children and adolescents. They naturally trust your guidance and comply with your instructions. Furthermore, you can readily secure comfortable lodging and employment in wealthy aristocratic estates with young children, provided you commit to staying at least seven days.
