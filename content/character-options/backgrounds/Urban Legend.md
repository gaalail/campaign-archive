# Urban Legend

You are an urban legend. Perhaps you are the hermit woman dwelling in the crooked hut deep in the weeping woods, the recluse in the derelict manor on the edge of town, or the hooded figure walking churchyards on moonless nights. Whispers and ghost stories swirl around your name, and though many boast of not believing a word, beneath the bravado, they fear your wrath.

**Skill Proficiencies:** Intimidation, Deception  
**Equipment:** A tattered hooded cloak, an ominous keepsake associated with your rumors, traveler’s clothes, and a pouch containing 10 gp.

## Urban Lore

| d6 | Rumor Lore |
| :---: | :--- |
| **1** | A witch who lures travelers with sweet songs to harvest vital youth. |
| **2** | Transforms into a ravenous predator when the town clock strikes midnight. |
| **3** | Slew wealthy suitors to inherit sprawling estates, burying them under red rose gardens. |
| **4** | Bound to a fiend to remain unaging, paying a monthly tribute in harvested souls. |
| **5** | A restless shade haunting an old manse, trapping dinner guests for eternity. |
| **6** | Jilted at the altar, now walking the earth seeking an undead match for eternity. |

## Feature: I Heard a Rumor

Locals, guards, and passing travelers who recognize you or hear your moniker believe the chilling campfire tales surrounding you. Common folk are easily intimidated, frightened of crossing you, and will go out of their way to avoid provoking your ire.
