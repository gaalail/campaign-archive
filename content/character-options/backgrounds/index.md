# Backgrounds

Your **background** tells your character's story before the adventure began.

**40+ backgrounds available** | Choose at character creation | Provides roleplay & mechanics

---

## What Is a Background?

A background includes:
- **Story elements** (who you were, what you did)
- **Skill proficiencies** (you're trained in 2 skills)
- **Extra language** or crafting tools
- **Feature** (special ability from your background)
- **Suggested personality traits** (optional flavor)

---

## How Backgrounds Work

Choose a background and:
- You gain **2 skill proficiencies** (you're trained in these)
- You gain a **background feature** (a special ability)
- You can speak 1 extra **language**
- You may gain tools or equipment

## How to Use Your Background

**In Character Creation:**
1. Pick a background
2. Get its skill proficiencies
3. Get its feature
4. Use it to build your character's story

**During the Game:**
- Use your feature when it's relevant
- Roleplay your background (it informs how your character thinks/acts)
- Your skills are trained (better chance of success)
