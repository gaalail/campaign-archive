# Medium

You are a medium. You have an intrinsic connection with spirits that roam beyond the veil of the living. You can sense their presence and hear fragmentary whispers of their unspoken regrets. It falls upon you whether to heed the requests of the deceased or close your ears to the voices of the grave.

**Skill Proficiencies:** Insight, Perception  
**Equipment:** A set of tools used to commune with spirits (such as carved osteomancy bones or a crystal scrying sphere), 10 sheets of parchment for transcribing automatic writing, common clothes, and a pouch with 10 gp.

## The Gift

| d6 | Origin of the Gift |
| :---: | :--- |
| **1** | Passed down across ancestral generations in my family. |
| **2** | A near-death experience opened a permanent doorway to the spirit realm. |
| **3** | The lingering ghost of someone dear connected me to all spirits upon passing. |
| **4** | My soul has an alluring resonance that naturally attracts spiritual wanderers. |
| **5** | Developed through exhaustive, grueling occult meditation and ritual study. |
| **6** | Began as a fraudulent carnival act until genuine spiritual contact abruptly ignited. |

## Feature: Whispers from the Grave

You can understand undead creatures and they can understand you, regardless of whether you share a common language. This does not make undead inherently friendly, but permits dialogue, interrogation, and mutual comprehension.
