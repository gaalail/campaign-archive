# Splicer

Splicers alter companion physiology through biomancy and surgical augmentation.

## Augment

_3rd-level Splicer feature_

You gain 3 splicer points and gain 1 additional point each time you gain a Tamer level. Spend points to modify companions. Augments take effect after the next long rest.

If a companion is released, it loses its augments and the points are refunded. You also gain proficiency with surgeon's tools and can remove all augments from a companion during one hour of work.

## Modular Upgrades

_7th-level Splicer feature_

At the start of a long rest, choose one upgrade for each companion. It completes at the end of the rest. The save DC equals your Tamer spell save DC.

- **Compelling Plumage:** Creatures in a 25-foot cone can be charmed and incapacitated until the effect ends.
- **Phosphoburst:** Creatures in a 10-foot sphere make Constitution saves or take 2d10 radiant damage and become blinded.
- **Poison Web:** A 20-foot cube becomes webbed; creatures can become restrained and take poison damage.
- **Sirenshriek:** Creatures in a 15-foot sphere make Intelligence saves or take 3d8 psychic damage and lose concentration.

A companion can use an upgrade once before a short or long rest. At 18th level, it can use the action twice before a rest.

## Battlefield Harvester

_10th-level Splicer feature_

As an action, command a companion to consume the corpse of a non-swarm creature dead less than one minute. Choose a resistance, immunity, or condition immunity from the corpse. All companions gain the corresponding resistance or condition immunity until this feature is used again.

## Adrenal Overload

_14th-level Splicer feature_

As an action, a companion grows to Huge size, gains temporary hit points equal to ten times your Tamer level, has Strength 22 if not higher, gains Athletics proficiency, and gains a three-attack appendage Multiattack. The effect lasts one minute; afterward the companion falls unconscious for 1d4 hours and gains two exhaustion levels. Regain after a long rest.
