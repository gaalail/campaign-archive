# Leader

Leaders use encouragement, gestures, game plans, or telepathic reinforcement to push companions beyond their normal limits.

## Inspire

_3rd-level Leader feature_

As an action, inspire your companion. Until the beginning of your next turn, whenever it makes a saving throw or attack roll, it can add a die to the result.

You can use this feature a number of times equal to your Tamer spellcasting ability modifier, minimum once. Regain all uses after a short or long rest.

The die is a d6, becoming a d8 at 6th level, d10 at 10th level, and d12 at 14th level.

## Leader Spells

These spells count as Tamer spells and do not count against spells known. Appendix B spells remain unimported.

| Tamer Level | Spells                                          |
| ----------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| 3rd         | _divine favor_, _protection from evil and good_ |
| 5th         | _inequality_ (Appendix B), _warding bond_       |
| 9th         | _beacon of hope_, _haste_                       |
| 13th        | _death ward_, _freedom of movement_             |
| 17th        | _dispel evil and good_, _endure_ (Appendix B)   |

## Action Burst

_7th-level Leader feature_

When you command your companion, it can take one additional action on its turn. Once a companion benefits from this feature, it must finish a short or long rest before benefiting again.

At 18th level, companions can benefit twice before a rest, but only once on the same turn.

## Leader's Fortitude

_10th-level Leader feature_

When an attacker you can see hits your companion, use both your reaction and your companion's reaction to halve the attack's damage.

## Combo

_14th-level Leader feature_

As an action, summon a second companion. When you command your companions, give each a different command. They act in an order of your choice after your turn.

Once used, this feature returns after a long rest.
