# Artificer

**Masters of invention** | Use magic through tools | Craft magical items and infusions

---

## Quick Overview

**Hit Dice:** 1d8  
**Primary Ability:** Intelligence  
**Saving Throws:** Constitution, Intelligence  
**Skills:** Choose 2 from Arcana, History, Investigation, Medicine, Nature, Perception, Sleight of Hand

Artificers use a variety of tools to channel their arcane power. To cast a spell, an artificer might use alchemist's supplies to create a potent elixir, calligrapher's supplies to inscribe a sigil of power, or tinker's tools to craft a temporary charm. The magic of artificers is tied to their tools and their talents.

**Multiclass Requirement:** You must have an Intelligence score of 13 or higher to multiclass in or out of this class.

---

## Proficiencies

**Armor:** Light armor, medium armor, shields  
**Weapons:** Simple weapons  
**Tools:** Thieves' tools, tinker's tools, one type of artisan's tools of your choice

## Key Class Features

### Magical Tinkering (1st Level)

You can invest a spark of magic into mundane objects. You must have thieves' tools or artisan's tools in hand. Touch a Tiny nonmagical object and give it one magical property:

- Sheds bright light in a 5-foot radius and dim light for 5 more feet
- Emits a recorded message (up to 6 seconds) when tapped
- Continuously emits an odor or nonverbal sound (perceivable up to 10 feet)
- Displays a static visual effect (picture, text, lines, shapes)

You can affect a number of objects equal to your Intelligence modifier (minimum 1). If you exceed your maximum, the oldest property ends.

### Spellcasting (1st Level)

You cast spells through your tools. You must have a spellcasting focus (thieves' tools or artisan's tool) in hand to cast any spell with the spellcasting feature.

**Cantrips:** You know 2 cantrips at 1st level, gaining more at higher levels (see table).

**Spell Slots:** You prepare spells equal to your Intelligence modifier + half your level (rounded down), minimum 1.

**Spellcasting Ability:** Intelligence

- **Spell Save DC** = 8 + proficiency bonus + Intelligence modifier
- **Spell Attack Modifier** = proficiency bonus + Intelligence modifier

You can cast artificer spells as rituals if the spell has the ritual tag and you have it prepared.

### Infuse Item (2nd Level)

At the end of a long rest, you can touch a nonmagical object and imbue it with one of your artificer infusions. You know 4 infusions at 2nd level and learn more at higher levels. You can infuse a number of items equal to your level (see progression table).

### Artificer Specialization (3rd Level)

At 3rd level, choose one Artificer Specialization. It grants features at the levels listed in its description. The available specializations in this archive are [[Agent]], [[Forgebound]], [[Grenadier]], [[Lycanist]], [[Ooze Rancher]], [[Static Conductor]], [[Venomist]], and [[Wagoneer]]. Browse them in the [[character-options/subclasses/Artificer/index|Artificer Specializations index]].

### The Right Tool for the Job (3rd Level)

With thieves' tools or artisan's tools in hand, you can magically create one set of artisan's tools in an unoccupied space within 5 feet. This requires 1 hour of uninterrupted work (can coincide with a short or long rest). The tools vanish when you use this feature again.

### Tool Expertise (6th Level)

Your proficiency bonus is doubled for any ability check using your tool proficiency.

### Flash of Genius (7th Level)

When you or a creature you can see within 30 feet makes an ability check or saving throw, you can use your reaction to add your Intelligence modifier to the roll.

Uses: Equal to your Intelligence modifier (minimum 1). Regain on long rest.

### Magic Item Adept (10th Level)

- You can attune to up to 4 magic items at once
- If you craft a magic item of common or uncommon rarity, it takes 1/4 normal time and costs 1/2 normal gold

### Spell-Storing Item (11th Level)

At the end of a long rest, you can touch a simple or martial weapon or spellcasting focus and store a 1st- or 2nd-level spell (that takes 1 action to cast) in it. A creature holding the object can use an action to produce the spell's effect using your spellcasting ability.

The spell stays until used a number of times equal to 2 × your Intelligence modifier (minimum 2).

### Magic Item Savant (14th Level)

- You can attune to up to 5 magic items at once
- You ignore all class, race, spell, and level requirements on magic item attunement and use

### Magic Item Master (18th Level)

You can attune to up to 6 magic items at once.

### Soul of Artifice (20th Level)

You gain a +1 bonus to all saving throws per magic item you're attuned to.

If reduced to 0 hit points but not killed outright, you can use your reaction to end one of your infusions, causing you to drop to 1 hit point instead of 0.

## Starting Equipment

Choose one or both:

- Any two simple weapons
- A light crossbow and 20 bolts

Choose one:

- Studded leather armor, or
- Scale mail

Always gain:

- Thieves' tools and a dungeoneer's pack

**Alternative:** 5d4 × 10 gold pieces

## Spellcasting Notes

**Tools Required:** You need a spellcasting focus (thieves' tools or artisan's tools) in hand when casting artificer spells.

**Prepared Spells:** You prepare a number of spells equal to your Intelligence modifier + half your level (minimum 1). Change your prepared list after each long rest.

**Ritual Casting:** Cast spells with the ritual tag as rituals without using a spell slot.
