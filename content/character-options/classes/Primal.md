# Primal

The charging minotaur's bellowed cry becomes a thunderous crash as a pillar of ice erupts in its path, the ground shuddering from the impact of horn and frost. A towering figure advances on the wreckage, armor covered in rime, even as fire crackles from their fist.

The bloodthirsty howls of the werewolf pack turn to confusion when the scrawny elf that looked only to be dinner rebuffs a massive werewolf's charge, their arms swelling in size becoming bear-like limbs, dwarfing the surrounding werewolves' own once impressive claws. All around, the werewolf pack can only look on in confusion as the elf continues to grow, grabbing and flinging a yelping werewolf like a rag doll.

The goblins have only a moment of glory, springing their ambush and pouring out of the forest upon the hapless group of humans before the plants beneath their feet writhe to life. To their shock and horror they find grasping vines dangling them from trees like misbegotten ornaments, frantically trying to remember the word in common for "surrender."

The bandit looks at his broken weapon haft in confusion, and then back up at the armored warrior whose stony plate seems unaffected by the blow that has just shattered steel. His confusion lasts only a moment before the earth heaves beneath him and tosses him from his feet in a violent crunching shake. A heavy stone boot plants firmly on his chest as the jagged rocky form looms over him.

## Primal Champions

Primals are not born of civilization and order. Though some Primals seek to defend such things (or at least tolerate them), they are those that have harnessed primal, untamed forces—lashing, roiling energies of ancient nature that, left unchecked, would wash civilization from the face of the material plane as if it were but a temporary blip.

### Aspects of Power
The powers of a Primal are expressions of deep and primordial currents. They are power given sentience and will: entropy and creation harnessed and focused to purpose and cause. Primals channel this power as a conduit, bringing it to heel through force of will and inner conviction. As a blacksmith forges a lump of iron to take on the keen edge of a sword, a Primal must always refine their power, taming and tempering it to be a force of deliberate change rather than blind destruction.

### Chosen Powers
While the situations that lead a Primal to take up their mantle of powers vary widely, their powers are reflections of who they are: a synchronization that would be impossible to thrust upon someone who did not choose this path. A Primal may feel they had no choice but to embrace these primal forces to defend their kin, but at the heart of their mantle is a choice: a choice to stand, a choice to fight, and a choice to be a champion.

## Creating a Primal

When creating a Primal, consider the factors that have led them down their path. A mortal connected to deep primordial powers is extraordinary. Is this something your character sought out to right an ancient wrong, or a cosmic resonance dropped in their lap that they must now master? How do you intend to use this power, and what principles will guide your hand?

### Quick Build
To make a Primal quickly, first place your highest ability score in **Strength**, followed by **Constitution** and **Wisdom**. Second, choose a wilderness-related or folk-hero background.

## Class Features

As a Primal, you gain the following class features.

### Hit Points
- **Hit Dice:** 1d12 per Primal level
- **Hit Points at 1st Level:** 12 + your Constitution modifier
- **Hit Points at Higher Levels:** 1d12 (or 7) + your Constitution modifier per Primal level after 1st

### Proficiencies
- **Armor:** Light armor, medium armor, shields
- **Weapons:** Simple weapons
- **Tools:** None
- **Saving Throws:** Strength, Wisdom
- **Skills:** Choose two from Animal Handling, Athletics, Insight, Intimidation, Medicine, Nature, Perception, and Survival
- **Languages:** Primordial

### Equipment
You start with the following equipment, in addition to the equipment granted by your background:
- (a) scale mail or (b) leather armor
- a simple melee weapon and a shield
- (a) two javelins or (b) two handaxes
- (a) a dungeoneer's pack or (b) an explorer's pack
- *Alternatively, you may start with 5d4 × 10 gp to purchase your own equipment.*

### Mystic Bulwark
*1st-level Primal feature*

When you don light or medium armor, you can enhance it with mystical defenses, integrating it with manifestations of your Primal Bond (for example, an Elemental Soul may manifest icy plating to reinforce it). When you do so, you can use your **Wisdom modifier** in place of your Dexterity modifier when calculating your AC with that armor.

While you have your armor integrated with your primal power, when you take bludgeoning, piercing, or slashing damage, you can reduce the damage taken by **2**. This reduction increases at 5th level (to 3), 9th level (to 4), 13th level (to 5), and 17th level (to 6).

> **Damage Reduction Tip:** Damage reduction is applied before damage resistance when calculating damage.

---

### Primal Bond
*1st-level Primal feature*

You select an aspect of primordial power, gaining the ability to manifest your bond with that elemental or cosmic current. Your choice grants you features at 1st level and again at 3rd, 7th, 14th, and 17th level.

Browse available [[character-options/subclasses/Primal/index|Primal Bonds]] in the **Subclasses** section below.

---

### Primal Interdiction
*2nd-level Primal feature*

Starting at 2nd level, nature hinders your foes with aspects of your primal power. This effect has a **5-foot radius** centered on you, turning the ground within range into difficult terrain for creatures of your choice.

Additionally, at the end of your turn, you can choose to recklessly defend your allies within this area at the cost of your own safety: attacks against you have advantage, and attacks against allies within the effect have disadvantage if you are also in range of the attack being made (or have been at any point since the start of the attacker's turn) and not behind total cover.

The range of this effect increases by 5 feet at 5th level (to 10 feet), again at 11th level (to 15 feet), and at 17th level (to 20 feet).

---

### Endurance Dice
*2nd-level Primal feature*

At 2nd level, you tap into a reservoir of physical resilience represented by three **d8** Endurance Dice. You have two primary ways to use these dice, though other features may grant additional uses:

- **Damage Reduction:** Once per turn, when you take damage, you can roll an Endurance Die to reduce the damage taken by the amount rolled (no action required).
- **Saving Throw Boost:** When you make a saving throw, you can roll an Endurance Die and add it to the saving throw (no action required). You can do this after you roll the d20, but before you know the outcome of the save.

You gain additional Endurance Dice as you advance: 4 dice at 5th level, 5 dice at 9th level, 6 dice at 13th level, and 7 dice at 17th level. The size of the dice increases at 5th level (to d10) and 11th level (to d12). You regain all expended Endurance Dice when you complete a short or long rest.

---

### Primal Manifestations
*3rd-level Primal feature*

At 3rd level, your connection to primal forces develops in unique ways that grant you specialized powers.

You gain two Primal Manifestations of your choice from the [[character-options/class-options/primal-manifestations/index|Primal Manifestations catalogue]]. When you reach certain Primal levels, you learn additional manifestations as indicated in the Manifestations Known column of the Primal Table.

Additionally, when you gain a level in this class, you can choose one of the manifestations you know and replace it with another manifestation that you could learn at that level. If a manifestation has a prerequisite, you must meet it to learn the manifestation. A level prerequisite refers to your level in this class.

---

### Primal's Grasp
*3rd-level Primal feature*

Starting at 3rd level, when you make an opportunity attack, you can replace the attack with a grapple check.

---

### Ability Score Improvement
*4th, 8th, 10th, 12th, 16th, and 19th-level Primal feature*

When you reach 4th level, and again at 8th, 10th, 12th, 16th, and 19th level, you can increase one ability score of your choice by 2, or you can increase two ability scores of your choice by 1. As normal, you can't increase an ability score above 20 using this feature.

---

### Extra Attack
*5th-level Primal feature*

Beginning at 5th level, you can attack twice, instead of once, whenever you take the Attack action on your turn.

---

### Empowered Endurance
*6th-level Primal feature*

Starting at 6th level, when you roll an Endurance Die to reduce damage, you can add your **Constitution modifier** to the result rolled.

Additionally, if you roll for initiative with no Endurance Dice remaining, you immediately regain one Endurance Die.

---

### Primal Strike
*6th-level Primal feature*

Starting at 6th level, your attacks made with natural weapons count as magical for the purpose of overcoming resistance and immunity to nonmagical attacks and damage.

---

### Natural Body
*9th-level Primal feature*

At 9th level, you sustain yourself directly from primal forces. You no longer need to eat or drink (though you can if you choose), and you become immune to disease.

---

### Evolving Power
*11th-level Primal feature*

Starting at 11th level, the damage dice of all natural weapons granted by the class increase by one size (for example, a d6 becomes a d8). If a weapon has multiple damage dice, only one die increases.

---

### Mystic Barrier
*13th-level Primal feature*

Starting at 13th level, your **Mystic Bulwark** damage reduction applies to all damage types (rather than just bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing damage).

---

### Second Life
*15th-level Primal feature*

At 15th level, when you would be reduced to 0 hit points, you are instead reduced to 1 hit point, and you immediately regain hit points equal to half your hit point maximum. Once triggered, you cannot use this feature again until you complete a long rest.

---

### Movement Through Nature
*15th-level Primal feature*

Also at 15th level, you gain your choice of a **climbing speed** or **swimming speed** equal to your walking speed, or a **burrowing speed** equal to half your walking speed. When you burrow, you leave behind a stable tunnel sized to your body.

---

### Timeless Body
*18th-level Primal feature*

Starting at 18th level, the primal magic suffusing your flesh causes you to age with unnatural slowness: for every 10 years that pass, your body ages only 1 year.

---

### Invulnerable Endurance
*18th-level Primal feature*

Also at 18th level, when you roll an Endurance Die, you can roll two dice and take the higher roll. Doing so does not expend an additional die.

---

### Primal Decree
*20th-level Primal feature*

At 20th level, you can invoke your primordial connection to reshape the physical world on a monumental scale. This can take the form of spells such as *earthquake*, *control weather*, or a transmutation or conjuration spell from the Druid spell list that modifies terrain (such as *wall of stone*).

At the DM's discretion, this might accomplish other monumental feats not listed by a spell, such as diverting the course of a river, quenching a raging forest fire, or triggering a controlled eruption in an active volcano. Its area of effect is generally contained within a 1-mile radius when not replicating the spells listed above. When used in this manner, it takes 1 minute to invoke the effect (which may take longer to fully manifest depending on its nature).

Once you invoke a Primal Decree, you cannot do so again until you complete a long rest.
