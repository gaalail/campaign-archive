# Tamer

Whether imbuing companions with primordial elements, enhancing them through biomancy, or inspiring them with words of encouragement, tamers work through captured friends. Alone, a tamer possesses little destructive ability. With carefully selected companions, a tamer can become an unstoppable menagerie of mayhem.

> [!warning]- This class entry is incomplete
> The supplied material does not include the Tamer progression table, companion summary table, complete Tamer spell list, or the detailed Leader, Infuser, Necromancer, and Splicer Training Paradigms. Those sections must be supplied before this class is treated as complete.

## Creating a Tamer

What prompted you to begin taming creatures? Loneliness? A need to transport your favourite dragonling through customs? A pathological desire to catch them all?

The bond tamers experience with their companions is intimate; both are privy to the other's innermost thoughts. Some tamers view a companion as a friend and equal, while others view it as a tool for fulfilling their goals.

### Quick Build

Choose Wisdom, Intelligence, or Charisma as your highest ability score, followed by Constitution.

## Class Features

### Hit Points

**Hit Dice:** 1d8 per tamer level  
**Hit Points at 1st Level:** 8 + your Constitution modifier  
**Hit Points at Higher Levels:** 1d8 (or 5) + your Constitution modifier per tamer level after 1st

### Proficiencies

**Armor:** Light armor, medium armor, shields  
**Weapons:** Simple weapons, nets  
**Tools:** One type of artisan's tools or one musical instrument of your choice  
**Saving Throws:** Constitution, Charisma  
**Skills:** Choose two from Animal Handling, Insight, Medicine, Nature, Perception, and Persuasion

### Equipment

You start with the following equipment, in addition to the equipment granted by your background:

- Scale mail or leather armor
- A simple melee weapon, a net, and a shield
- A light crossbow and 20 bolts
- A dungeoneer's pack or an explorer's pack

If you forgo this equipment and the items granted by your background, you start with 5d4 × 10 gp.

## Pocket Familiar

_1st-level Tamer feature_

You become bonded to a companion that accompanies you and is trained to fight alongside you. Choose a Small or smaller creature with a challenge rating of 1/2 or lower that is not a humanoid, giant, or swarm. Work with your GM to choose a companion appropriate to the campaign.

When a creature becomes your companion:

- Its maximum hit points equal the average of its Hit Dice.
- It cannot cast spells.
- It obeys your commands and is friendly to you and your allies.

### Vessel

When not summoned, your companion exists inside a magical vessel of your design, such as a painted animal skull, bejewelled egg, or crystal sphere. While inside, the companion has full cover, exists in stasis, needs no food, drink, sleep, or air, and is immune to poison and disease. A companion at 0 hit points is stabilized when it enters its vessel.

If the vessel is broken, or the companion is released for another reason, it ceases to be your companion and acts according to its own wishes. It retains improvements gained while bonded.

### Summoning

As an action, summon your companion to an unoccupied space you can see within 30 feet, or within 5 feet of you. You can dismiss it within 30 feet as an action or bonus action. You must hold the vessel to summon or dismiss the companion. Only one companion can be summoned at a time through this feature.

### Combat

Your companion shares your initiative count and acts immediately after you. It can move and use reactions, but takes only the Dodge action unless you use an action or bonus action to command it. A commanded companion can use an action from its statistics or another appropriate action, bonus action, or object interaction.

If you are incapacitated, the companion can act freely. It cannot use Multiattack until you reach 5th level in this class.

### Rest

A companion gains the benefits of a long rest when you finish one. If it has at least 1 hit point, it regains all hit points. A companion cannot spend Hit Dice during a short rest.

### Items and Item Bonding

A companion can wear or carry equipment its size and body shape permit. It must be proficient with armor or weapons to use them normally.

Over one hour, you can bond a companion to one wearable or carriable object that is not a container. A companion can be bonded to up to three items. Unbonded equipment is dropped when the companion returns to its vessel.

Companions cannot attune to magic items unless a specific feature permits it.

## Soul Bond

_1st-level Tamer feature_

Companions make death saving throws, die, and can be revived like player characters. A stabilized companion remains unconscious until it regains hit points or you finish a long rest. A stable companion at 0 hit points regains half its maximum hit points when you finish a long rest.

You have a healing pool equal to five times your tamer level. It replenishes when you finish a long rest. When you finish a short rest, you can restore hit points among your companions up to the remaining amount in the pool.

## Monster Trainer

_1st-level Tamer feature_

Your companions use your proficiency bonus instead of their own.

Whenever you gain an Ability Score Improvement from gaining a tamer level, each companion gains one additional Hit Die. Increase its hit point maximum by rolling the Hit Die and adding its Constitution modifier, minimum 0.

Whenever you gain a tamer level beyond 1st, each companion gains one improvement from the missing **Companion Summary** rules. Newly gained companions receive these improvements as though you had possessed them since 1st level.

## Tame Creature

_1st-level Tamer feature_

You can tame creatures as you encounter them. Your maximum companion size and challenge rating increase as shown in the missing **Tamer Companion Summary** table.

### Preparing a Vessel

Preparing a vessel takes 8 hours and requires materials worth at least 100 gp multiplied by the target creature's challenge rating.

### Taming a Creature

As an action, throw an empty vessel at a creature within 30 feet. The creature makes a Charisma saving throw against your tamer spell save DC or becomes trapped.

A creature automatically succeeds if it is:

- A humanoid, giant, or swarm
- Larger than your maximum companion size
- Above your maximum companion challenge rating
- Above the vessel's capture rating
- Above half its hit points

A creature with fewer than 10 hit points or one-quarter of its hit points automatically fails if it does not meet one of those exclusions. A creature that fails becomes your companion when you next finish a long rest.

### Lost Traits

A companion loses the following traits when it becomes bonded:

- Spellcasting
- Summoning actions or actions that create additional creatures
- Regeneration, Rejuvenation, and Legendary Resistance
- Unlimited or unrestricted healing effects
- Legendary actions, mythic traits, and related options
- Lair actions and regional effects

## Spellcasting

_2nd-level Tamer feature_

You can cast Tamer spells. The supplied material references a separate Tamer spell list that is not included in the source provided for this archive.

**Cantrips:** You know two cantrips from the Tamer spell list and learn more as shown in the missing Tamer progression table.

**Spell Slots:** The Tamer table determines your spell slots. You regain all expended slots when you finish a long rest.

**Spells Known:** You know two 1st-level Tamer spells and learn more as shown in the missing progression table. When you gain a level, you can replace one known Tamer spell with another for which you have slots.

**Spellcasting Ability:** At 1st level, choose Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma as your Tamer spellcasting ability.

- **Spell save DC** = 8 + your proficiency bonus + your spellcasting ability modifier
- **Spell attack modifier** = your proficiency bonus + your spellcasting ability modifier

A companion's vessel can serve as your spellcasting focus.

## Bolster

_2nd-level Tamer feature_

While your companion is within 100 feet and you hold its vessel, you can use an action to expend a spell slot and restore hit points equal to your spellcasting ability modifier, minimum 1, plus 2d4 per level of the slot.

## Psychic Bond

_2nd-level Tamer feature_

While your companion is within 100 feet, you can communicate telepathically. As an action, you can see through its eyes and hear through its senses until the start of your next turn. During this time, you are deaf and blind to your own senses.

While your companion is summoned, within 100 feet, and you hold its vessel, its known spells are added to your known spells. When you cast a spell with a range of self or touch, your companion can be the target.

## Pocket Family

_3rd-level Tamer feature_

You can bond with two companions at 3rd level, three at 7th, four at 11th, five at 15th, and six at 19th level. Your maximum companion size becomes Medium at 5th level, Large at 11th, and Huge at 17th.

If you are bonded to your maximum number and bond with a new companion, release one existing companion.

## Training Paradigm

_3rd-level Tamer feature_

Choose a Training Paradigm. The supplied material names these paradigms but does not include their feature descriptions:

- Leader
- Infuser
- Necromancer
- Splicer

Your paradigm grants features at 3rd, 7th, 10th, and 14th level. Its 7th-level feature improves at 18th level.

## Feat

_4th-level Tamer feature_

At 4th level, and again at 8th, 12th, 16th, and 19th level, gain the Ability Score Improvement feat or another feat for which you qualify.

## Multiattack

_5th-level Tamer feature_

When you command your companion, you can command it to use Multiattack if it has that action.

## Malleable Presence

_5th-level Tamer feature_

While your companion is within 100 feet, you can command it to alter its behavior toward a creature you can see. The target makes Wisdom saving throws against your tamer spell save DC at the start of its turns for one minute.

**Aggressive:** The target has disadvantage on attacks against creatures other than your companion.  
**Cautious:** The target has disadvantage on attacks against your companion if another hostile creature is within 5 feet of it.

Once used, this feature returns after a short or long rest.

## Alpha Strike

_6th-level Tamer feature_

As part of summoning a companion, you can command it to take an action. You can use this feature a number of times equal to your spellcasting ability modifier, minimum once, regaining uses on a long rest.

## Wilful Blows

_6th-level Tamer feature_

Your companions' attacks count as magical for overcoming resistance and immunity to nonmagical attacks and damage.

## Switcheroo

_13th-level Tamer feature_

While you and a companion are within 100 feet, you can use a bonus action, or a reaction when you or the companion is targeted by an attack, to switch places. You can use this feature a number of times equal to your spellcasting ability modifier, minimum once, regaining uses on a long rest.

## Magnificent Presence

_17th-level Tamer feature_

You can command a companion to adopt one of two behaviors for one minute:

**Provoking Poise:** Hostile creatures within 15 feet that can see the companion have disadvantage on attacks against other creatures.  
**Shrinking Violet:** Hostile creatures have disadvantage on attacks against the companion if another hostile creature is within 15 feet of the attacker.

Once used, this feature returns after a long rest.

## Summon the Horde

_20th-level Tamer feature_

As an action, summon additional companions for one minute, up to three simultaneously from this feature. On later turns, command all companions with one action. When the minute ends, all but one return to their vessels and you cannot move or act until the end of your next turn.

Once used, this feature returns after a long rest.
