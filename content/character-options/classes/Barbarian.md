# Barbarian

Barbarians are primal warriors who draw on instinct and the fury of Rage to become forces of destruction in battle. Their Rage can be bloodlust, an emotionless trance, an inherited tradition, or a supernatural instinct that reveals weakness and shrugs off deadly blows.

## Creating Your Barbarian

Consider how your Barbarian first awakened their Rage and how it manifests in battle. Was it sparked by violence, revenge, or a desire to find a worthy rival? Does it become a bestial fury, a protective ancestral trance, or a supernatural combat instinct?

## Multiclassing

**Ability score minimum:** Strength 13.

**Proficiencies gained:** Simple weapons, martial weapons, and shields.

If you learn Exploits from more than one source, use the rules in [[character-options/class-options/savage-exploits/index|Savage Exploits]] and [[character-options/class-options/martial-exploits/index|Martial Exploits]] to determine your total Exploit Dice and Exploits Known.

## Class Features

### Hit Points

**Hit Dice:** 1d12 per Barbarian level  
**Hit Points at 1st Level:** 12 + your Constitution modifier  
**Hit Points at Higher Levels:** 1d12 (or 7) + your Constitution modifier per Barbarian level after 1st

### Proficiencies

**Armor:** Light armor, medium armor, shields  
**Weapons:** Simple weapons, martial weapons  
**Tools:** None  
**Saving Throws:** Strength, Constitution  
**Skills:** Choose two: Animal Handling, Athletics, Intimidation, Nature, Perception, or Survival

### Equipment

You start with this equipment, in addition to the equipment granted by your background:

- (a) a greataxe, greatsword, or maul
- (a) two simple weapons or (b) a dagger and shield
- (a) hide armor or (b) leather armor
- An explorer's pack, a dagger, and four javelins

### Quick Build

Make Strength your highest ability score, then choose Constitution or Dexterity. Choose the Outlander or Mercenary background.

### Rage

Starting at 1st level, if you aren't wearing heavy armor, you can enter a Rage as a bonus action on your turn. While raging:

- You have advantage on Strength saving throws and ability checks that use Strength or Constitution.
- When you hit with a weapon attack using Strength, you deal bonus damage equal to your Exploit Die. This bonus increases as shown in the Barbarian table.
- You resist all bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing damage.
- You can't cast spells or concentrate on spells or effects.

Your Rage lasts until the end of your next turn and ends early if you become Unconscious. It extends until the end of your next turn when you attack a hostile creature, take damage, make a Strength check, or use your bonus action to extend it. You can maintain a Rage for up to 10 minutes.

You can Rage the number of times shown in the Rages column. You regain one use when you finish a short rest and all uses when you finish a long rest.

### Unarmored Defense

At 1st level, while you aren't wearing armor, your Armor Class equals 10 + your Dexterity modifier + your Constitution modifier. You can use a shield and still gain this benefit.

### Reckless Attack

Starting at 2nd level, whenever you make a Strength-based attack on your turn, you can attack recklessly. If you do, you have advantage on Strength-based attacks until the beginning of your next turn, but attacks against you also have advantage until then.

### Savage Exploits

At 2nd level, you gain savage abilities that enhance your martial skill on and off the battlefield.

**Exploit Dice.** The Barbarian table shows how many Exploit Dice you have. Most Exploits require you to expend dice. You can use only one Exploit per attack, ability check, or saving throw. You regain all expended dice when you finish a short or long rest. Your dice begin as d4s and increase as shown in the table. When you add an Exploit Die to a roll, roll it and add the result.

**Exploits Known.** At 2nd level, you learn two Exploits from the [[character-options/class-options/savage-exploits/index|Savage Exploits catalogue]] (optional Advanced Savage Exploits are also available with DM approval). You must meet their prerequisites. You learn more at the levels shown in the Barbarian table. Whenever you gain a Barbarian level, you can replace one Exploit you know with another.

**Exploit saving throws.** Choose Strength or Dexterity as the ability used to calculate your Exploit save DC. This choice can't be changed.

**Exploit Save DC** = 8 + your Proficiency Bonus + your Strength or Dexterity modifier

**Savage Skill.** Whenever you make an ability check with a skill from the Barbarian skill list, you can expend an Exploit Die and add it to the roll. You can do so after rolling, but before you know whether you succeed.

### Primal Path

At 3rd level, choose the Primal Path that best represents the source of your Rage. Your path grants features at 3rd, 6th, 10th, and 14th level. If a path feature forces a saving throw, it uses your Exploit save DC.

Some paths teach Path Exploits at specific Barbarian levels. These don't count against your Exploits Known and can't be switched. You learn them even if you don't meet their prerequisites.

Browse the [[character-options/subclasses/Barbarian/index|Primal Paths]] below.

### Ability Score Improvement

At 4th level, and again at 8th, 12th, 16th, and 19th level, increase one ability score by 2 or two scores by 1. This feature can't raise a score above 20.

### Danger Sense

Starting at 5th level, you have advantage on initiative rolls and can choose to Rage whenever you roll initiative. If you are surprised when you roll initiative, you can act normally during the first round as long as you Rage as part of rolling initiative.

### Extra Attack

Beginning at 5th level, you can attack twice, instead of once, whenever you take the Attack action. While raging, if you use your action to Dash or Disengage, you can make one weapon attack as a bonus action.

### Feral Instinct

At 7th level, you have advantage on Dexterity saving throws against effects you can see, such as traps and spells. You don't gain this benefit while Blinded, Deafened, or Incapacitated.

Your Rage always lasts for its maximum duration unless you become Unconscious or choose to end it.

### Improved Critical

Beginning at 9th level, your Strength-based weapon attacks score a critical hit on a roll of 19 or 20. This range increases to 18–20 at 13th level and 17–20 at 17th level.

### Critical Strike

Starting at 11th level, whenever you score a critical hit with a weapon attack while raging, you can use a Savage Exploit you know as part of that attack without expending an Exploit Die.

### Unstoppable

At 11th level, if you drop to 0 hit points while raging but aren't killed outright, you can end your Rage to drop to 1 hit point instead.

While raging, you also gain a bonus to Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma saving throws equal to your Constitution modifier (minimum +1).

### Persistent Rage

Beginning at 15th level, your Rage lasts for up to 1 hour.

### Indomitable Might

Starting at 18th level, if you make a Strength or Constitution check and the result is lower than your Strength score, you can use your Strength score in place of the result.

### Primal Champion

At 20th level, your Strength and Constitution scores increase by 4, and their maximums become 24.
