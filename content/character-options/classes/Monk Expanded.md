# Alternate Monk Expanded

The Alternate Monk Expanded supplement adds new options for the Alternate Monk. Its Mystic Techniques can be learned when their prerequisites are met. Its two Monastic Feats let other characters access some of the class's abilities, and it adds nine Monastic Traditions available at 3rd level.
