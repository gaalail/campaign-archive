You have trained to serve the divine on the field of battle.

- **Crusader (1st Level).** You gain proficiency in heavy armor and a single martial weapon of your choice, often one considered sacred to your deity.
- **Greater Crusader (7th Level).** Your deity infuses your weapons with divine wrath. Choose a damage type from this list that best fits your Divine Domain: cold, fire, lightning, necrotic, poison, thunder, or radiant. Once per turn, when you hit with a weapon attack, you can deal a bonus 1d8 damage of your chosen type.
- **Sanctified Crusader (14th Level).** Your weapons strike with the full force of your deity's will. The bonus damage from Greater Crusader becomes 2d8.
