# Sacred Callings

**Cleric class options chosen at 1st level (scaling at 7th and 14th levels).**

Mortals are called according to their abilities in the service of the divine. At 1st level, a Cleric chooses one of three Sacred Callings representing how they serve their deity. Once chosen, your Sacred Calling cannot be changed.
