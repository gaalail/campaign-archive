# Veil Techniques

**Thanatomancer-exclusive techniques fueled by mortis points.**

At 1st level, a Thanatomancer learns two Veil Techniques of their choice, gaining additional techniques at 3rd, 7th, 11th, and 15th level. Starting at 7th level (and again at 13th level), you can expend additional mortis points to unleash enhanced effects.
