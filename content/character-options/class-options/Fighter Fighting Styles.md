# Fighter Fighting Styles

At 1st level, choose a Fighting Style for which you meet the prerequisites. You learn additional styles at 6th, 12th, and 18th level. When you gain a Fighter level, you can replace one style you know with another for which you qualify. An Expert option requires its associated style and 6th-level Fighter.

## Styles

### Archery

**Prerequisite:** Dexterity 13  
You gain a +1 bonus to attack rolls with ranged weapons. Your ranged attacks ignore half cover and treat three-quarters cover as half cover.

#### Archery Expert

You gain another +1 bonus to ranged weapon attack rolls, and attacking at long range no longer imposes disadvantage.

### Balanced Fighting

While wielding a melee weapon in one hand and no other weapons, you gain a +2 bonus to damage rolls with that weapon. You can use a shield with this style.

#### Balanced Expert

While wielding a versatile melee weapon and a shield, you use the weapon's two-handed damage die. You can also use your shield to make a Shove attack as a bonus action.

### Brawling

**Prerequisite:** Proficiency in Athletics  
Your unarmed strikes deal 1d6 + your Strength modifier damage. If both hands are free, you can make one unarmed strike, Shove, or Grapple as a bonus action.

### Classical Swordplay

**Prerequisite:** Dexterity 13  
While wielding a single finesse weapon, with no shield and no heavy armor, you gain a +2 bonus to attack rolls with that weapon and a +1 bonus to AC.

#### Classical Swordplay Expert

When you are hit by an attack while using Classical Swordplay, you can use your reaction to add your Dexterity modifier to your AC against that attack.

### Defensive Fighting

While wearing medium or heavy armor, or wielding a shield, you gain a +1 bonus to AC.

#### Defensive Expert

While wearing heavy armor, reduce nonmagical bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing damage you take by your Constitution modifier (minimum 1). If your armor is magical, this can also reduce magical damage of those types.

### Dual Wielding

**Prerequisite:** Strength or Dexterity 13  
When you fight with two weapons, you can make your off-hand attack as part of the Attack action instead of as a bonus action, and add your ability modifier to its damage. You can't also make an attack with your bonus action that turn.

#### Dual Wielding Expert

One-handed melee weapons have the Light property for you. While wielding a melee weapon in each hand, you gain a +1 bonus to AC.

### Great Weapon Fighting

**Prerequisite:** Strength 13  
When you hit a target with a heavy melee weapon attack, you can treat a damage die as its average result instead of rolling if the roll is lower than that average: d4 (2), d6 (3), d8 (4), d10 (5), or d12 (6).

#### Great Weapon Expert

When you reduce a creature to 0 hit points with a heavy melee weapon attack, you can make another attack with the same weapon as part of that action, bonus action, or reaction.

### Mounted Warrior

**Prerequisite:** Proficiency in Animal Handling  
While riding a controlled mount, you and your mount gain a +1 bonus to AC. You can use a bonus action on each of your turns to command the mount to take an action from its stat block or another action.

### Polearm Fighting

**Prerequisite:** Strength and Dexterity 11  
While wielding only a glaive, halberd, pike, quarterstaff, or spear and no shield, you gain a +1 bonus to damage rolls with it. Creatures provoke an opportunity attack from you when they enter or move within your reach with that weapon.

#### Polearm Expert

When you take the Attack action while using Polearm Fighting, you can use a bonus action to make a melee attack with the end of your weapon. On a hit, it deals 1d4 + your Strength modifier bludgeoning damage.

### Protection

When a creature you can see hits you or a target within 5 feet with a melee attack, you can use your reaction to add your proficiency bonus to the target's AC against that attack. You must be wielding a shield or melee weapon.

#### Protection Expert

When a creature within 5 feet is forced to make a Dexterity saving throw, you can use your reaction to add your proficiency bonus to its roll. You can do so after it rolls the d20 but before you know the outcome.

### Strongbow

**Prerequisite:** Strength 13  
You can use your Strength modifier instead of Dexterity for attack and damage rolls with longbows and shortbows. When you do, you gain a +1 bonus to damage rolls with those weapons.

### Versatile Fighting

**Prerequisite:** Strength or Dexterity 11  
While wielding a single versatile weapon and no shield, you gain a +1 bonus to attack rolls with it. You can use a bonus action to make a Grapple or Shove attack, or take the Use an Object action.
