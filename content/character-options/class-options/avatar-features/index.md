# Avatar Features

**Paragon and Order-exclusive divine abilities.**

As a Paragon blessed by your deity, you gain unique powers through your **Avatar Form**. These **Avatar Features** represent your connection to divine forces.

## How They Work

- Avatar Features unlock at certain levels as you bond with your deity
- Most are tied to your **Avatar Form** (a divine transformation)
- Some are tied to your **Sacred Haven** (a holy sanctuary)
- You choose features as your character levels up
- Different Orders have different features available

## Orders Available

- **[[Order of the Voice]]** — Divine speech and command
- **[[Order of Valor]]** — Holy warfare and protection
- **[[Order of Reason]]** — Divine knowledge and arcane magic

Each Order has its own set of features. Click your Order to see what's available.

---

## How to Choose Features

1. Find your Order (Voice, Valor, or Reason)
2. See which features are available at your level
3. Pick the ones that match your character concept
