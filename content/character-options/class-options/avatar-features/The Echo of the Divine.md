Sacred Language echoes through you and can be heard by anyone within 15 feet of you. Your allies in the area feel more energetic, gaining a +1 bonus to attack rolls.

**Avatar Form Only.** Your enemies within 30 feet of you feel dread, taking a -1 penalty to saving throws.

The bonus becomes +2 and the penalty becomes -2 when you reach 14th level in this class.

**Prerequisite:** The Speaker  
**Minimum level:** 6
