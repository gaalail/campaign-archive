With the words that are given to you, you can deny death. You can cast the death ward spell.

You can use this feature once, or twice when you reach 14th level in this class. You regain any expended uses when you finish a long rest.

**Avatar Form Only.** You become the envoy of the divine, and they grant you their protection as a result. A red silk ribbon decorated with golden threads hovers around your body. You have the death ward spell cast on you until its benefits take effect or until your avatar form ends.

**Prerequisite:** None  
**Minimum level:** 9
