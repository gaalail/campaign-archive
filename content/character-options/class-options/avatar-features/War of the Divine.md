**Avatar Form Only.** You can summon twin angels of war (p. 289) to your domain by using the Summoning Crystal you acquired through the Sacred Haven feature instead of other angels you can summon with it. Also, while being apart from each other would normally weaken twin angels of war, there are no such limitations in this domain.

**Prerequisite:** None  
**Minimum level:** 17
