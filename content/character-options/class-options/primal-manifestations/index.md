# Primal Manifestations

**Class Options for the [[character-options/classes/Primal|Primal]] Class.**

Starting at 3rd level, a Primal's bond with primordial forces manifests in unique customizations called **Primal Manifestations**. You learn 2 manifestations at 3rd level, advancing up to 7 manifestations at 18th level. Whenever you gain a level in the Primal class, you can swap one manifestation for another available option.

---

## 3rd-Level Options (No Prerequisite)

- **[[character-options/class-options/primal-manifestations/Balanced Assault|Balanced Assault]]:** Gain the Two-Weapon Fighting style (add ability modifier to off-hand damage).
- **[[character-options/class-options/primal-manifestations/Create Barrier|Create Barrier]]:** Replace an attack to summon an earthen/natural 5-foot barrier (AC 15, HP 10 + Primal level). Subclasses upgrade this into Astral, Ice, Iron, Tree, or Stone barriers.
- **[[character-options/class-options/primal-manifestations/Elemental Rebuke|Elemental Rebuke]]:** Cast *Aldricor's elemental rebuke* without material components (1/long rest, or recast by spending 2 Endurance Dice).
- **[[character-options/class-options/primal-manifestations/Gift of Life|Gift of Life]]:** Spend an Endurance Die as an action to heal a touched target 1 HP + grant temp HP equal to Endurance Die + Wisdom modifier.
- **[[character-options/class-options/primal-manifestations/Guided Wrath|Guided Wrath]]:** Use Wisdom modifier instead of Strength/Dexterity for attacks and damage with Primal natural weapons. Upgrades with Living Weapons (Elderheart), Living Chains (Ironbound), or Living Arms (Astral Guardian).
- **[[character-options/class-options/primal-manifestations/Heart of the Storm|Heart of the Storm]]:** Gain resistance to damage from allied AoE spells (or take 0 damage if already resistant).
- **[[character-options/class-options/primal-manifestations/Karmic Edict|Karmic Edict]]:** Cast *karmic reflection* without expending a spell slot (1/long rest, or early with Endurance Dice).
- **[[character-options/class-options/primal-manifestations/Mystic Deflection|Mystic Deflection]]:** Gain +1 AC while wearing light or medium armor.
- **[[character-options/class-options/primal-manifestations/Mystic Reinforcement|Mystic Reinforcement]]:** Mystic Bulwark damage reduction increases by 1.
- **[[character-options/class-options/primal-manifestations/Primal Intuition|Primal Intuition]]:** Gain blindsight out to 10 feet.
- **[[character-options/class-options/primal-manifestations/Protective Interdiction|Protective Interdiction]]:** Reaction to reduce nonmagical damage taken by an ally in your Primal Interdiction by twice your Mystic Bulwark reduction.
- **[[character-options/class-options/primal-manifestations/Reliable Endurance|Reliable Endurance]]:** Replace your Endurance Die roll with 2d4 (1d4 + 1d6 for d10, 3d4 for d12).
- **[[character-options/class-options/primal-manifestations/Unrelenting Onslaught|Unrelenting Onslaught]]:** Cast a spell as an action, then make a single natural weapon attack as a bonus action.
- **[[character-options/class-options/primal-manifestations/Well of Fortitude|Well of Fortitude]]:** Regain hit points during a short rest by rolling a free Hit Die without expending it (PB times per long rest).

---

## Subclass-Specific Options

- **[[character-options/class-options/primal-manifestations/Engulfing Vines|Engulfing Vines]]** *(Elderheart)*: Grapple a number of targets with each vine equal to Wisdom modifier; pull grappled targets 5 feet at start of turn.
- **[[character-options/class-options/primal-manifestations/Temporal Torsion|Temporal Torsion]]** *(Timetwister)*: Replace an attack to deal 1d8 + Wisdom necrotic damage and slow a target within 30 feet by 10 feet.
- **[[character-options/class-options/primal-manifestations/Time Trick|Time Trick]]** *(Timetwister)*: Accelerate mundane actions to don/doff armor or complete non-combat tasks within 1 minute as an action (Wisdom modifier uses/long rest).
- **[[character-options/class-options/primal-manifestations/Wildheart|Wildheart]]** *(Beasthide)*: Perpetual *sanctuary* against Beasts of CR ≤ Primal level; advantage on animal interaction checks.

---

## 6th-Level Prerequisites

- **[[character-options/class-options/primal-manifestations/Sacrificing Stance|Sacrificing Stance]]:** Roll saving throw against an AoE damage effect with disadvantage to grant all allies in your Primal Interdiction resistance to that damage.
- **[[character-options/class-options/primal-manifestations/Savage Weapons|Savage Weapons]]:** Damage dice of all class natural weapons increase by one die size (e.g., d6 to d8).

---

## 9th-Level Prerequisites

- **[[character-options/class-options/primal-manifestations/Devouring Earth|Devouring Earth]]:** Cast *quicksand* once per long rest without a spell slot.
- **[[character-options/class-options/primal-manifestations/Falling Sky|Falling Sky]]:** Cast *sleet storm* once per long rest without a spell slot.
- **[[character-options/class-options/primal-manifestations/One With Stone|One With Stone]]:** Cast *meld into stone* once per long rest without a spell slot.

---

## 12th-Level Prerequisites

- **[[character-options/class-options/primal-manifestations/Arise Stone|Arise Stone]]:** Cast *wall of stone* once per long rest without a spell slot.
- **[[character-options/class-options/primal-manifestations/Crushing Grip (Primal Manifestation)|Crushing Grip]]:** Deal 2d6 bludgeoning damage to all creatures you are grappling at the start of your turn.
- **[[character-options/class-options/primal-manifestations/Font of Will|Font of Will]]:** Make end-of-turn saving throws against ongoing effects at the start of your turn instead.
- **[[character-options/class-options/primal-manifestations/Revitalizing Endurance|Revitalizing Endurance]]:** Start your turn at 0 HP and expend an Endurance Die to immediately revive and regain rolled HP.
- **[[character-options/class-options/primal-manifestations/Unlimited Endurance|Unlimited Endurance]]:** Your maximum pool of Endurance Dice increases by 1.

---

## 16th-Level Prerequisites

- **[[character-options/class-options/primal-manifestations/Arise Thorns|Arise Thorns]]** *(Elderheart)*: Cast *wall of thorns* once per long rest without a spell slot.
- **[[character-options/class-options/primal-manifestations/Arise Ice|Arise Ice]]** *(Elemental Soul)*: Cast *wall of ice* once per long rest without a spell slot.
- **[[character-options/class-options/primal-manifestations/Immortal Body|Immortal Body]]:** Regain 1d6 HP at start of turn if below half maximum HP and above 0 HP.
- **[[character-options/class-options/primal-manifestations/Last Stand|Last Stand]]:** Until start of next turn, redirect any lethal damage to allies within 60 feet onto yourself (1/short or long rest).
- **[[character-options/class-options/primal-manifestations/Planar Guardian|Planar Guardian]]** *(Astral Guardian)*: Cast *plane shift* (willing targets only) once per long rest without a spell slot.
