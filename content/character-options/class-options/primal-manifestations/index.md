# Primal Manifestations

**Class Options for the [[character-options/classes/Primal|Primal]] Class.**

Starting at 3rd level, a Primal's bond with primordial forces manifests in unique customizations called **Primal Manifestations**. You learn 2 manifestations at 3rd level, advancing up to 7 manifestations at 18th level. Whenever you gain a level in the Primal class, you can swap one manifestation for another available option.

---

## 3rd-Level Options (No Prerequisite)

- **Balanced Assault:** Gain the Two-Weapon Fighting style (add ability modifier to off-hand damage).
- **Create Barrier:** Replace an attack to summon an earthen/natural 5-foot barrier (AC 15, HP 10 + Primal level). Subclasses upgrade this into Astral, Ice, Iron, Tree, or Stone barriers.
- **Elemental Rebuke:** Cast *Aldricor's elemental rebuke* without material components (1/long rest, or recast by spending 2 Endurance Dice).
- **Gift of Life:** Spend an Endurance Die as an action to heal a touched target 1 HP + grant temp HP equal to Endurance Die + Wisdom modifier.
- **Guided Wrath:** Use Wisdom modifier instead of Strength/Dexterity for attacks and damage with Primal natural weapons. Upgrades with Living Weapons (Elderheart), Living Chains (Ironbound), or Living Arms (Astral Guardian).
- **Heart of the Storm:** Gain resistance to damage from allied AoE spells (or take 0 damage if already resistant).
- **Karmic Edict:** Cast *karmic reflection* without expending a spell slot (1/long rest, or early with Endurance Dice).
- **Mystic Deflection:** Gain +1 AC while wearing light or medium armor.
- **Mystic Reinforcement:** Mystic Bulwark damage reduction increases by 1.
- **Primal Intuition:** Gain blindsight out to 10 feet.
- **Protective Interdiction:** Reaction to reduce nonmagical damage taken by an ally in your Primal Interdiction by twice your Mystic Bulwark reduction.
- **Reliable Endurance:** Replace your Endurance Die roll with 2d4 (1d4 + 1d6 for d10, 3d4 for d12).
- **Unrelenting Onslaught:** Cast a spell as an action, then make a single natural weapon attack as a bonus action.
- **Well of Fortitude:** Regain hit points during a short rest by rolling a free Hit Die without expending it (PB times per long rest).

---

## Subclass-Specific Options

- **Engulfing Vines** *(Elderheart)*: Grapple a number of targets with each vine equal to Wisdom modifier; pull grappled targets 5 feet at start of turn.
- **Temporal Torsion** *(Timetwister)*: Replace an attack to deal 1d8 + Wisdom necrotic damage and slow a target within 30 feet by 10 feet.
- **Time Trick** *(Timetwister)*: Accelerate mundane actions to don/doff armor or complete non-combat tasks within 1 minute as an action (Wisdom modifier uses/long rest).
- **Wildheart** *(Beasthide)*: Perpetual *sanctuary* against Beasts of CR ≤ Primal level; advantage on animal interaction checks.

---

## 6th-Level Prerequisites

- **Sacrificing Stance:** Roll saving throw against an AoE damage effect with disadvantage to grant all allies in your Primal Interdiction resistance to that damage.
- **Savage Weapons:** Damage dice of all class natural weapons increase by one die size (e.g., d6 to d8).

---

## 9th-Level Prerequisites

- **Devouring Earth:** Cast *quicksand* once per long rest without a spell slot.
- **Falling Sky:** Cast *sleet storm* once per long rest without a spell slot.
- **One With Stone:** Cast *meld into stone* once per long rest without a spell slot.

---

## 12th-Level Prerequisites

- **Arise Stone:** Cast *wall of stone* once per long rest without a spell slot.
- **Crushing Grip:** Deal 2d6 bludgeoning damage to all creatures you are grappling at the start of your turn.
- **Font of Will:** Make end-of-turn saving throws against ongoing effects at the start of your turn instead.
- **Revitalizing Endurance:** Start your turn at 0 HP and expend an Endurance Die to immediately revive and regain rolled HP.
- **Unlimited Endurance:** Your maximum pool of Endurance Dice increases by 1.

---

## 16th-Level Prerequisites

- **Arise Thorns** *(Elderheart)*: Cast *wall of thorns* once per long rest without a spell slot.
- **Arise Ice** *(Elemental Soul)*: Cast *wall of ice* once per long rest without a spell slot.
- **Immortal Body:** Regain 1d6 HP at start of turn if below half maximum HP and above 0 HP.
- **Last Stand:** Until start of next turn, redirect any lethal damage to allies within 60 feet onto yourself (1/short or long rest).
- **Planar Guardian** *(Astral Guardian)*: Cast *plane shift* (willing targets only) once per long rest without a spell slot.
