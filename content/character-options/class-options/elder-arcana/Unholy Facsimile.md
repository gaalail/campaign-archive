During the course of a long rest, you can perform a forgotten and unholy 1-hour Ritual which separates the [[character-options/class-options/pact-foci/Eldritch Flesh|Eldritch Flesh]] from your body, replicating the [[spells/7th-level/Create Simulacrum|create simulacrum]] spell.

This simulacrum is an Aberration; it has Vulnerability to Psychic damage instead of Fire, and neither you nor it can benefit from your [[character-options/class-options/pact-foci/Eldritch Flesh|Eldritch Flesh]] while it lives. As an action, you can touch the simulacrum to destroy and absorb it into yourself, regaining the benefits of your Eldritch Flesh.

**Prerequisite:** 13th-level Warlock, [[character-options/class-options/pact-foci/Eldritch Flesh|Eldritch Flesh]]  
**Minimum level:** 13
