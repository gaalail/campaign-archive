## Rule

Prerequisite: 13th-level Warlock, Eldritch FleshDuring the course of a long rest, you can perform a forgotten and unholy 1-hour Ritual which separates the Eldritch Flesh from your body, replicating the create simulacrum ll spell.This simulacrum is an Aberration; it has Vulnerability to Psychic damage instead of Fire, and neither you nor it can benefit from your Eldritch Flesh while it lives. As an action, you can touch the simulacrum to destroy and absorb it into yourself, regaining the benefits of your Eldritch Flesh.
