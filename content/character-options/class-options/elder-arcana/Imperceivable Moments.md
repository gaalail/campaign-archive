When you roll initiative, so long as you are not Incapacitated or Surprised, you can take one of the following actions before any other creature has the chance to act: Hide, Search, Use an Object, or move up to your speed without provoking opportunity attacks.

**Prerequisite:** 11th-level Warlock, [[character-options/class-options/pact-foci/Eldritch Hourglass|Eldritch Hourglass]]  
**Minimum level:** 11
