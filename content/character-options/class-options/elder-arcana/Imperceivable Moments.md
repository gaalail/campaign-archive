## Rule

Prerequisite: 11th-level Warlock, Eldritch HourglassWhen you roll initiative, so long as you are not Incapacitated or Surprised, you can take one of the following actions before any other creature has the chance to act: Hide, Search, Use an Object, or you move up to your speed without provoking opportunity attacks.
