When you transform using [[character-options/class-options/pact-foci/Eldritch Flesh|Eldritch Flesh]], you can choose to reinforce your body with sinister magicks. For the duration of that transformation, you have Resistance to all Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing damage, and the damage types of your Eldritch Blast. However, your speed is reduced by `5` feet.

**Prerequisite:** 11th-level Warlock, [[character-options/class-options/pact-foci/Eldritch Flesh|Eldritch Flesh]]  
**Minimum level:** 11
