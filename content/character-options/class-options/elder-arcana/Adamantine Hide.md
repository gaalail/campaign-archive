## Rule

Prerequisite: 11th-level Warlock, Eldritch FleshWhen you transform using Eldritch Flesh, you can choose to reinforce your body with sinister magicks. For the duration of that transformation, you have Resistance to all Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing damage, and the damage types of your Eldritch Blast. However, your speed is reduced by 5 feet.
