## Rule

Prerequisite: 17th-level Warlock, Eldritch ChaliceAt the end of a long rest, you can perform a Ritual of ancient power which grants you the following unnatural benefits until the end of your next long rest:You stop aging, and return to the full vigor of your youth.You have Immunity to Poison damage and you cannot be Blinded, Deafened, Paralyzed, or Poisoned by magic.So long as you have at least 1 hit point, but less than half of your maximum hit points, you regain hit points equal to your Pact modifier at the start of each of your turns.However, if you finish a long rest and do not perform this Ritual, you immediately age as if you had never performed it.
