## Rule

Prerequisite: 13th-level Warlock, Eldritch HourglassWhen you finish your turn, you can choose to undo the entire turn; any and all movement, actions, reactions, rolls, features, attacks, and spells are undone as if they never happened. All creatures then regain any hit points lost or resources spent.You then return to the space in which you began your turn, and can take your turn again as normal. Creatures other than you are unaware that you have used this feature, and have no memory of turn that was undone.When you are not in combat, you can use this Arcanum to undo the previous 10 seconds, using the same guidelines.You can use this feature once between each long rest with no negative effects. However, each subsequent time you use this feature before you rest, you gain one level of Exhaustion.
