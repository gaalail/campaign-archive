Using your [[character-options/class-options/pact-foci/Eldritch Chalice|Eldritch Chalice]] as the Spellcasting Focus, you can cast the [[spells/5th-level/Reincarnate|reincarnate]] spell without expending a spell slot or its material components once between each long rest.

**Prerequisite:** 11th-level Warlock, [[character-options/class-options/pact-foci/Eldritch Chalice|Eldritch Chalice]]  
**Minimum level:** 11
