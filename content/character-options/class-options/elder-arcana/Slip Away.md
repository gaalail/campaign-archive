## Rule

Prerequisite: 17th-level Warlock, Eldritch ShroudYou can fade away from reality, only to reappear moments later. While your Eldritch Shroud is equipped, you can use a bonus action on each turn to teleport to an unoccupied space that you can see within 30 feet.
