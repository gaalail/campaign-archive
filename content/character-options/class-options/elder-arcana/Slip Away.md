You can fade away from reality, only to reappear moments later. While your [[character-options/class-options/pact-foci/Eldritch Shroud|Eldritch Shroud]] is equipped, you can use a bonus action on each turn to teleport to an unoccupied space that you can see within `30` feet.

**Prerequisite:** 17th-level Warlock, [[character-options/class-options/pact-foci/Eldritch Shroud|Eldritch Shroud]]  
**Minimum level:** 17
