You know the [[spells/9th-level/Time Stop|time stop]] spell, and it counts as a Warlock spell for you. You can use your [[character-options/class-options/pact-foci/Eldritch Hourglass|Eldritch Hourglass]] to cast [[spells/9th-level/Time Stop|time stop]] at 7th-level without expending a spell slot once between each short or long rest.

**Prerequisite:** 15th-level Warlock, [[character-options/class-options/pact-foci/Eldritch Hourglass|Eldritch Hourglass]]  
**Minimum level:** 15
