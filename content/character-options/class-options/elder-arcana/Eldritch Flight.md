While transformed into your [[character-options/class-options/pact-foci/Eldritch Flesh|Eldritch Flesh]], you sprout great wings reminiscent of your Patron's nature, which grant you a flying speed equal to your walking speed.

If you already have a flying speed, it increases by `10` feet.

**Prerequisite:** 15th-level Warlock, [[character-options/class-options/pact-foci/Eldritch Flesh|Eldritch Flesh]]  
**Minimum level:** 15
