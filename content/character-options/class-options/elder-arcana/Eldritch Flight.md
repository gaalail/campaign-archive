## Rule

Prerequisite: 15th-level Warlock, Eldritch FleshWhile transformed into your Eldritch Flesh, you sprout great wings reminiscent of your Patron's nature, which grant you a flying speed equal to your walking speed.If you already have a flying speed, it increases by 10 feet.
