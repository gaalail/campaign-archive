While holding Eldritch Blade, you cannot have disadvantage on attacks and add your Pact modifier (minimum +1) to concentration saves. Once per long rest, creatures you choose within 30 feet save or take 10d6 Eldritch Blast damage and fall Prone; half damage on success.
