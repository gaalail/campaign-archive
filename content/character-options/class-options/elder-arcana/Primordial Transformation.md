The power which infuses your flesh rivals the raw potential of creation. You learn the [[spells/9th-level/True Polymorph|true polymorph]] spell, and it becomes a Warlock spell for you. Once per long rest, you can cast it on yourself only, using the creature to creature option. When you do so, your concentration can't be broken by taking damage.

**Prerequisite:** 17th-level Warlock, [[character-options/class-options/pact-foci/Eldritch Flesh|Eldritch Flesh]]  
**Minimum level:** 17
