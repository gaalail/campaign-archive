As an action, you can shatter your [[character-options/class-options/pact-foci/Eldritch Hourglass|Eldritch Hourglass]] to open a temporal rift, pulling a potential future version of yourself into your time. This potential future self uses the rules below while it remains with you:

**Statistics.** Your Future Self has the same statistics as you, including ability scores and features from your class, race, or other sources. However, they share in your pool of spell slots and other limited-use features. When conjured, they have hit points equal to your Warlock level.

**Combat.** In Combat, your Future Self shares your initiative and acts during your turn. However, they act independently of you, and will aid you to the best of their abilities.

**Knowledge.** You can ask your Future Self a single question concerning the future. They will answer using the guidelines in the [[spells/5th-level/Commune|commune]] spell. However, their knowledge is limited to things you may know in the future, determined by the DM.

**Duration.** Your Future Self returns to their time if you use a bonus action to conjure your Eldritch Hourglass, if they are reduced to `0` hit points, or after 1 hour has passed.

There can only ever be one version of your Future Self in your current time at once. Conjuring a second Future Self causes any others to instantly return to their time.

**Prerequisite:** 17th-level Warlock, [[character-options/class-options/pact-foci/Eldritch Hourglass|Eldritch Hourglass]]  
**Minimum level:** 17
