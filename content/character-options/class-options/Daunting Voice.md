title: Daunting Voice
option_group: Avatar Feature
parent_class: Order of the Voice
min_level: 4
prerequisite: None
summary: One word from you sows a relentless fear in the minds of your enemies.
source: Corpus Angelus
---

One word from you sows a relentless fear in the minds of your enemies. As an action, you choose a target within 30 feet of you and speak a word in Sacred Language. The target must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw, or be frightened of you until the end of their next turn.

**Avatar Form Only.** When you speak a word in Sacred Language in this way, you can choose to affect each enemy within 15 feet of you, instead of a target you choose within 30 feet of you.

A creature who succeeded on the saving throw is immune to this effect until it finishes a long rest. Also, when you reach 10th level in this class, the creatures that are frightened of you as a result of this feature are frightened for 1 minute.

**Prerequisite:** None  
**Minimum level:** 4
