# Inferno Smite Path

**Damage type:** Fire

Inferno punishes action until even the strongest monsters stop to think.

## Using This Path

- **When you can smite.** When you hit with a melee weapon attack or an unarmed strike, expend a spell slot to use one smite from this path. You can use only one smite per hit.
- **Slot scaling.** A smite accepts its listed slot or higher. Each slot level above the listed level adds one damage die, to a maximum of six dice. A 5th-tier smite has fixed damage and accepts only a 5th-level slot.
- **Access.** The table shows the Paladin level and minimum slot for each smite.
- **Saving throws.** Use your spell save DC.
- **Concentration.** A smite marked concentration lasts until your concentration is broken. You can't use it while concentrating on a spell; casting a concentration spell ends the smite. You can concentrate on only one smite at a time. Any smite effect lasting past the end of the target's next turn requires concentration.
- **Limits.** A smite's effect lasts no longer than 1 minute and affects creatures in the fight, not objects or the world.
- **Improved dice.** Each damage die marked `*` increases one size at Paladin 9th level: d6 becomes d8, and d8 becomes d10. This applies to the base damage, added dice from higher-level slots, and the listed maximum.
- **Righteous Strikes.** This path uses fire damage for Righteous Strikes.

## Smites

### Searing Smite

_1st-tier smite · 1st-level slot · Paladin level 2nd+_

Your weapon flares with white-hot intensity. The attack deals an extra 2d8 fire damage for a 1st-level slot, plus 1d8 for each slot level higher than 1st, to a maximum of 6d8. This damage increases by 1d8 if the target is covered in something flammable or is flammable itself (this increase can exceed the maximum).

### Scorching Smite

_2nd-tier smite · 2nd-level slot · Paladin level 5th+_

Pillars of fire swirl around your weapon. The attack deals an extra 3d6* fire damage for a 2nd-level slot, plus 1d6* for each slot level higher than 2nd, to a maximum of 6d6*. After the attack, fire clings to the target until the end of its next turn. The first time it moves more than 1 foot or makes an attack while under this effect, it takes 1d8 fire damage and the effect ends.

### Bursting Smite

_3rd-tier smite · 3rd-level slot · Paladin level 9th+ · concentration_

The tip of your weapon glows with an orb of explosive energy, and your attack deals an extra 4d10 fire damage for a 3rd-level slot, plus 1d10 for each slot level higher than 3rd, to a maximum of 6d10. The orb transfers to the target with your strike and lasts for up to 1 minute or until your concentration is broken. While the orb is attached to a creature, that creature takes 1d10 fire damage at the start of each of its turns. Once while this effect is active, you can use your bonus action to cause the orb to explode. When it does, the target must make a Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, it takes 2d10 fire damage and its speed is 0 until the end of your next turn.

### Blistering Smite

_4th-tier smite · 4th-level slot · Paladin level 13th+ · concentration_

Heat works its way from your weapon into the wound, and the attack deals an extra 5d10 fire damage for a 4th-level slot, plus 1d10 for each slot level higher than 4th, to a maximum of 6d10. The target must make a Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, the heat keeps working for up to 1 minute or until your concentration is broken: the first time on each of its turns that it moves more than 5 feet or makes an attack, it takes 2d8 fire damage. It repeats the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success.

### Molten Smite

_5th-tier smite · 5th-level slot · Paladin level 17th+_

Your weapon becomes covered in molten rock, and the attack deals an extra 6d10 fire damage for a 5th-level slot. This smite's fire damage ignores resistance to fire. The target must make a Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, until the end of its next turn it can either move or take an action on its turn, not both.
