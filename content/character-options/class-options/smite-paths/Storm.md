# Storm Smite Path

**Damage type:** Lightning and thunder

Storm strikes with thunder first, then lightning.

## Using This Path

- **When you can smite.** When you hit with a melee weapon attack or an unarmed strike, expend a spell slot to use one smite from this path. You can use only one smite per hit.
- **Slot scaling.** A smite accepts its listed slot or higher. Each slot level above the listed level adds one damage die, to a maximum of six dice. A 5th-tier smite has fixed damage and accepts only a 5th-level slot.
- **Access.** The table shows the Paladin level and minimum slot for each smite.
- **Saving throws.** Use your spell save DC.
- **Concentration.** A smite marked concentration lasts until your concentration is broken. You can't use it while concentrating on a spell; casting a concentration spell ends the smite. You can concentrate on only one smite at a time. Any smite effect lasting past the end of the target's next turn requires concentration.
- **Limits.** A smite's effect lasts no longer than 1 minute and affects creatures in the fight, not objects or the world.
- **Improved dice.** Each damage die marked `*` increases one size at Paladin 9th level: d6 becomes d8, and d8 becomes d10. This applies to the base damage, added dice from higher-level slots, and the listed maximum.
- **Righteous Strikes.** This path lets you choose lightning or thunder damage for Righteous Strikes.

## Smites

### Thundering Smite

_1st-tier smite · 1st-level slot · Paladin level 2nd+_

Your weapon rings with thunder, and the attack deals an extra 2d6* thunder damage for a 1st-level slot, plus 1d6* for each slot level higher than 1st, to a maximum of 6d6*. Additionally, if the target is a creature, it must succeed on a Strength saving throw or be pushed 10 feet away from you and knocked prone.

### Ringing Smite

_2nd-tier smite · 2nd-level slot · Paladin level 5th+_

Your weapon rings like a struck bell, and the attack deals an extra 3d6* thunder damage for a 2nd-level slot, plus 1d6* for each slot level higher than 2nd, to a maximum of 6d6*. The target must succeed on a Constitution saving throw or be deafened and unable to take reactions until the end of its next turn.

### Arcing Smite

_3rd-tier smite · 3rd-level slot · Paladin level 9th+_

Your weapon surges with lightning, and the attack deals an extra 4d8 lightning damage for a 3rd-level slot, plus 1d8 for each slot level higher than 3rd, to a maximum of 6d8. Lightning arcs off the target: up to two other creatures of your choice within 15 feet of it must make a Dexterity saving throw, taking 1d8 lightning damage on a failed save, or half as much on a successful one. If no other creature is within 15 feet, the lightning arcs back and strikes the target again for 1d8 lightning damage.

### Surging Smite

_4th-tier smite · 4th-level slot · Paladin level 13th+_

Your weapon hums and vibrates, and the attack deals an extra 4d8 lightning damage for a 4th-level slot, plus 1d8 for each slot level higher than 4th, to a maximum of 6d8. You can use your bonus action this turn to make a weapon attack against a creature within your reach. That attack can't carry a smite.

### Crackling Smite

_5th-tier smite · 5th-level slot · Paladin level 17th+ · concentration_

Your weapon glows blue-white and crackles with energy. The attack deals an extra 6d12 lightning damage for a 5th-level slot. For up to 1 minute or until your concentration is broken, your attacks deal an extra 1d8 lightning damage on a hit.
