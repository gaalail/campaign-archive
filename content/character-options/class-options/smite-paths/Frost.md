# Frost Smite Path

**Damage type:** Cold

Frost slows what it touches, spreads the slowing across the field, then holds the target in place.

## Using This Path

- **When you can smite.** When you hit with a melee weapon attack or an unarmed strike, expend a spell slot to use one smite from this path. You can use only one smite per hit.
- **Slot scaling.** A smite accepts its listed slot or higher. Each slot level above the listed level adds one damage die, to a maximum of six dice. A 5th-tier smite has fixed damage and accepts only a 5th-level slot.
- **Access.** The table shows the Paladin level and minimum slot for each smite.
- **Saving throws.** Use your spell save DC.
- **Concentration.** A smite marked concentration lasts until your concentration is broken. You can't use it while concentrating on a spell; casting a concentration spell ends the smite. You can concentrate on only one smite at a time. Any smite effect lasting past the end of the target's next turn requires concentration.
- **Limits.** A smite's effect lasts no longer than 1 minute and affects creatures in the fight, not objects or the world.
- **Improved dice.** Each damage die marked `*` increases one size at Paladin 9th level: d6 becomes d8, and d8 becomes d10. This applies to the base damage, added dice from higher-level slots, and the listed maximum.
- **Righteous Strikes.** This path uses cold damage for Righteous Strikes.

## Smites

### Chilling Smite

_1st-tier smite · 1st-level slot · Paladin level 2nd+_

Water condenses into a mist around your weapon, and the attack deals an extra 2d6* cold damage for a 1st-level slot, plus 1d6* for each slot level higher than 1st, to a maximum of 6d6*. The target's speed is reduced by 10 feet until the end of its next turn.

### Splintering Smite

_2nd-tier smite · 2nd-level slot · Paladin level 5th+_

Ice coats the area of your strike and then shatters, and the attack deals an extra 3d6* cold damage for a 2nd-level slot, plus 1d6* for each slot level higher than 2nd, to a maximum of 6d6*. Creatures other than you in a 15-foot cone originating from you and centered on the target must make a Dexterity saving throw, taking 1d8 cold damage on a failed save, or half as much on a successful one. On a failed save, a creature's speed is also reduced by 10 feet until the end of its next turn.

### Hailstorm Smite

_3rd-tier smite · 3rd-level slot · Paladin level 9th+ · concentration_

Icicles form on your weapon, and the attack deals an extra 4d10 cold damage for a 3rd-level slot, plus 1d10 for each slot level higher than 3rd, to a maximum of 6d10. Additionally, the area in a 15-foot radius around the target flurries with sharp ice for up to 1 minute or until your concentration is broken. The area is difficult terrain for creatures other than you, and any creature other than you that enters the area for the first time on a turn or ends its turn there must make a Constitution saving throw, taking 1d10 cold damage on a failed save, or half as much on a successful one.

### Freezing Smite

_4th-tier smite · 4th-level slot · Paladin level 13th+ · concentration_

Your weapon is coated in clear ice, and the attack deals an extra 5d10 cold damage for a 4th-level slot, plus 1d10 for each slot level higher than 4th, to a maximum of 6d10. The target must make a Constitution saving throw or be affected by this smite for up to 1 minute or until your concentration is broken. The target's speed is halved, it takes a -2 penalty to AC and Dexterity saving throws, and it can't use reactions. On its turn, it can use either an action or a bonus action, not both. Regardless of the creature's abilities or magic items, it can't make more than one melee or ranged attack during its turn. It repeats the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success.

### Encasing Smite

_5th-tier smite · 5th-level slot · Paladin level 17th+ · concentration_

The air around your weapon is tinted blue, and the attack deals an extra 6d12 cold damage for a 5th-level slot. The target must make a Strength saving throw. On a failed save, for up to 1 minute or until your concentration is broken it is affected as by Freezing Smite, except that its speed is 0, and it takes 1d10 cold damage at the start of each of its turns. It repeats the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success.
