# Venom Smite Path

**Damage type:** Poison

Venom fails a foe's body one system at a time.

## Using This Path

- **When you can smite.** When you hit with a melee weapon attack or an unarmed strike, expend a spell slot to use one smite from this path. You can use only one smite per hit.
- **Slot scaling.** A smite accepts its listed slot or higher. Each slot level above the listed level adds one damage die, to a maximum of six dice. A 5th-tier smite has fixed damage and accepts only a 5th-level slot.
- **Access.** The table shows the Paladin level and minimum slot for each smite.
- **Saving throws.** Use your spell save DC.
- **Concentration.** A smite marked concentration lasts until your concentration is broken. You can't use it while concentrating on a spell; casting a concentration spell ends the smite. You can concentrate on only one smite at a time. Any smite effect lasting past the end of the target's next turn requires concentration.
- **Limits.** A smite's effect lasts no longer than 1 minute and affects creatures in the fight, not objects or the world.
- **Improved dice.** Each damage die marked `*` increases one size at Paladin 9th level: d6 becomes d8, and d8 becomes d10. This applies to the base damage, added dice from higher-level slots, and the listed maximum.
- **Righteous Strikes.** This path uses poison damage for Righteous Strikes.

## Smites

### Sickening Smite

_1st-tier smite · 1st-level slot · Paladin level 2nd+_

Green energy drips from your weapon, and the attack deals an extra 2d6* poison damage for a 1st-level slot, plus 1d6* for each slot level higher than 1st, to a maximum of 6d6*. The target must succeed on a Constitution saving throw or be poisoned until the end of its next turn.

### Disorienting Smite

_2nd-tier smite · 2nd-level slot · Paladin level 5th+_

A band of golden light surrounds your weapon, and the attack deals an extra 3d6* poison damage for a 2nd-level slot, plus 1d6* for each slot level higher than 2nd, to a maximum of 6d6*. The target must succeed on a Constitution saving throw or have its speed halved and have disadvantage on Dexterity saving throws until the end of its next turn.

### Weakening Smite

_3rd-tier smite · 3rd-level slot · Paladin level 9th+ · concentration_

Your strike leaves a black mark of siphoning energy on your foe, and the attack deals an extra 4d10 poison damage for a 3rd-level slot, plus 1d10 for each slot level higher than 3rd, to a maximum of 6d10. The target must succeed on a Constitution saving throw, or for up to 1 minute or until your concentration is broken it deals only half damage with weapon attacks that use Strength. At the end of each of its turns, it can repeat the saving throw. On a success, the effect ends.

### Hallucinating Smite

_4th-tier smite · 4th-level slot · Paladin level 13th+ · concentration_

Vine-like projections grow along your weapon, and the attack deals an extra 5d10 poison damage for a 4th-level slot, plus 1d10 for each slot level higher than 4th, to a maximum of 6d10. The target must make a Wisdom saving throw. On a failed save, its perception of creatures around it is scrambled for up to 1 minute or until your concentration is broken: whenever it attempts to target another creature, it must roll a d4. On a 1, you choose the target; on a 2, the target is chosen at random; on a 3 or 4, the DM chooses the target normally. It repeats the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success.

### Deathly Smite

_5th-tier smite · 5th-level slot · Paladin level 17th+ · concentration_

When you strike, your weapon throbs with the beat of the victim's heart, and the attack deals an extra 6d12 poison damage for a 5th-level slot. For up to 1 minute or until your concentration is broken, the target must make a Constitution saving throw at the start of each of its turns. If it has moved more than 10 feet or attacked since the start of its last turn, it makes the saving throw with disadvantage. For each failed saving throw it accumulates, it suffers an effect, listed below. Each effect can occur only once. Once the creature has accumulated three successful saving throws, or once the duration elapses, the effects end.

| Failed save count | Effect                               |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| 1                 | The target is poisoned.              |
| 2                 | The target's speed is halved.        |
| 3                 | The target takes 3d12 poison damage. |
