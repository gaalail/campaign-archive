# Corrosion Smite Path

**Damage type:** Acid

Corrosion strips a foe's gear and then its flesh.

| Tier | Required slot | Paladin level | Concentration |
| ---- | ------------- | ------------- | ------------- |
| 1    | 1st-level     | 2             | No            |
| 2    | 2nd-level     | 5             | No            |
| 3    | 3rd-level     | 9             | No            |
| 4    | 4th-level     | 13            | Yes           |
| 5    | 5th-level     | 17            | No            |

See the [[character-options/class-options/smite-paths/index|shared Smite Path rules]] for slot scaling, saving throws, and concentration.

## Smites

### Rending Smite

_1st-tier smite · 1st-level slot · Paladin level 2nd+_

Drops of clear acid drip from the top of your weapon, and the attack deals an extra 2d8 acid damage for a 1st-level slot, plus 1d8 for each slot level higher than 1st, to a maximum of 6d8. The damage increases by 1d8 if the target is wearing armor (this increase can exceed the maximum).

### Corroding Smite

_2nd-tier smite · 2nd-level slot · Paladin level 5th+_

Your weapon sizzles and smokes with acidic energy, and the attack deals an extra 3d6* acid damage for a 2nd-level slot, plus 1d6* for each slot level higher than 2nd, to a maximum of 6d6*. The target must make a Dexterity saving throw. On a failed save, its attacks with any weapon it holds or wears take a -3 penalty to the attack roll until the end of its next turn.

### Sundering Smite

_3rd-tier smite · 3rd-level slot · Paladin level 9th+_

Acid eats into the seams of armor and hide, and the attack deals an extra 4d8 acid damage for a 3rd-level slot, plus 1d8 for each slot level higher than 3rd, to a maximum of 6d8. The target must make a Dexterity saving throw. On a failed save, its AC is reduced by 3 until the end of its next turn.

### Dissolving Smite

_4th-tier smite · 4th-level slot · Paladin level 13th+ · concentration_

A yellow aura appears around your weapon, and the attack deals an extra 5d10 acid damage for a 4th-level slot, plus 1d10 for each slot level higher than 4th, to a maximum of 6d10. The target must make a Dexterity saving throw. On a failed save, until the end of your next turn or until your concentration is broken, its AC is 10 + its Dexterity modifier, regardless of its normal AC, and its weapon attacks have disadvantage.

### Agonizing Smite

_5th-tier smite · 5th-level slot · Paladin level 17th+_

Green and yellow energy swirl around your weapon, and the attack deals an extra 6d10 acid damage for a 5th-level slot. The target must make a Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, until the end of its next turn its speed is halved and it can use either its action or its bonus action on its turn, not both.
