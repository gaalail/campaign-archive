# Judgment Smite Path

**Damage type:** Psychic

Judgment shakes a foe's nerve, then its footing, then its aim, and finally its sense of what it is doing.

| Tier | Required slot | Paladin level | Concentration |
| ---- | ------------- | ------------- | ------------- |
| 1    | 1st-level     | 2             | Yes           |
| 2    | 2nd-level     | 5             | No            |
| 3    | 3rd-level     | 9             | No            |
| 4    | 4th-level     | 13            | Yes           |
| 5    | 5th-level     | 17            | Yes           |

See the [[character-options/class-options/smite-paths/index|shared Smite Path rules]] for slot scaling, saving throws, and concentration.

## Smites

### Cowing Smite

_1st-tier smite · 1st-level slot · Paladin level 2nd+ · concentration_

Your weapon hisses vengeful utterings, and your attack deals an extra 2d8* psychic damage for a 1st-level slot, plus 1d8* for each slot level higher than 1st, to a maximum of 6d8*. Additionally, if the target is a creature, it must make a Wisdom saving throw or be frightened of you for up to 1 minute or until your concentration is broken. An affected creature repeats the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success.

### Staggering Smite

_2nd-tier smite · 2nd-level slot · Paladin level 5th+_

The hilt of your weapon takes on a violet hue, and the attack deals an extra 3d6* psychic damage for a 2nd-level slot, plus 1d6* for each slot level higher than 2nd, to a maximum of 6d6*. The target must make a Wisdom saving throw. On a failed save, until the end of its next turn it has disadvantage on attack rolls, its speed is halved, and it can't take reactions.

### Enraging Smite

_3rd-tier smite · 3rd-level slot · Paladin level 9th+_

A red mist envelops your weapon, and the attack deals an extra 4d8 psychic damage for a 3rd-level slot, plus 1d8 for each slot level higher than 3rd, to a maximum of 6d8. Additionally, if the target is a creature, it must make a Wisdom saving throw. On a failed save, until the end of its next turn it has disadvantage on attacks against creatures other than you, and creatures other than you make saving throws against its abilities and spells with advantage.

### Humbling Smite

_4th-tier smite · 4th-level slot · Paladin level 13th+ · concentration_

Your weapon pierces both body and mind, and the attack deals an extra 5d10 psychic damage for a 4th-level slot, plus 1d10 for each slot level higher than 4th, to a maximum of 6d10. The target must make a Charisma saving throw. On a failed save, for up to 1 minute or until your concentration is broken, whenever it deals damage to a creature it takes psychic damage equal to half the damage dealt (maximum 10). It repeats the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success.

### Confusing Smite

_5th-tier smite · 5th-level slot · Paladin level 17th+ · concentration_

Your weapon glows with streaks of red light, and the attack deals an extra 6d12 psychic damage for a 5th-level slot. The target must make a Wisdom saving throw. On a failed save, for up to 1 minute or until your concentration is broken, it rolls a d6 at the start of each of its turns to decide its behavior for the turn:

| d6  | Behavior                                                                                                              |
| --- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1-2 | It spends all its movement moving in a random direction before acting. Roll a d8 and assign a direction to each face. |
| 3-4 | It can't take reactions and has disadvantage on attack rolls this turn.                                               |
| 5-6 | It acts normally.                                                                                                     |

At the end of each of its turns, the target repeats the saving throw, ending the effect on itself on a success.
