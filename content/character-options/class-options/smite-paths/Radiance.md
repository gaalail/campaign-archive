# Radiance Smite Path

**Damage type:** Radiant

Radiance exposes a foe, takes its sight, binds it, and condemns it.

| Tier | Required slot | Paladin level | Concentration |
| ---- | ------------- | ------------- | ------------- |
| 1    | 1st-level     | 2             | No            |
| 2    | 2nd-level     | 5             | No            |
| 3    | 3rd-level     | 9             | Yes           |
| 4    | 4th-level     | 13            | Yes           |
| 5    | 5th-level     | 17            | Yes           |

See the [[character-options/class-options/smite-paths/index|shared Smite Path rules]] for slot scaling, saving throws, and concentration.

## Smites

### Purifying Smite

_1st-tier smite · 1st-level slot · Paladin level 2nd+_

Your weapon glows with a holy light. The attack deals an extra 2d8 radiant damage for a 1st-level slot, plus 1d8 for each slot level higher than 1st, to a maximum of 6d8. The damage increases by 1d8 if the target is an undead or a fiend (this increase can exceed the maximum).

### Branding Smite

_2nd-tier smite · 2nd-level slot · Paladin level 5th+_

Your weapon gleams with astral radiance as you strike. The attack deals an extra 3d6* radiant damage for a 2nd-level slot, plus 1d6* for each slot level higher than 2nd, to a maximum of 6d6*. Until the end of its next turn, the target is visible if it's invisible, can't become invisible, and sheds dim light in a 5-foot radius. The next attack roll made against it before then has advantage.

### Blinding Smite

_3rd-tier smite · 3rd-level slot · Paladin level 9th+ · concentration_

Your weapon flares with a bright light, and the attack deals an extra 4d10 radiant damage for a 3rd-level slot, plus 1d10 for each slot level higher than 3rd, to a maximum of 6d10. The target must succeed on a Constitution saving throw or be blinded for up to 1 minute or until your concentration is broken. A creature blinded by this effect repeats the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success.

### Shackling Smite

_4th-tier smite · 4th-level slot · Paladin level 13th+ · concentration_

Chains of radiant light coil around your weapon, and the attack deals an extra 5d10 radiant damage for a 4th-level slot, plus 1d10 for each slot level higher than 4th, to a maximum of 6d10. The target must succeed on a Charisma saving throw or be restrained for up to 1 minute or until your concentration is broken. At the end of each of its turns, as well as each time the target takes damage, it can repeat the saving throw, ending the effect on itself on a success.

### Exorcising Smite

_5th-tier smite · 5th-level slot · Paladin level 17th+ · concentration_

Your weapon is set ablaze with white flame, and the attack deals an extra 6d12 radiant damage for a 5th-level slot. The target must make a Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, it is wreathed in consecrating flame for up to 1 minute or until your concentration is broken. While wreathed, it can't regain hit points and takes 1d10 radiant damage at the start of each of its turns. It repeats the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success.
