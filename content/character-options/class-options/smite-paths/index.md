# Paladin Smite Paths

Divine Smite draws its smites from these paths. Choose one path at 2nd level and a second at 11th. Each path has five smites, one for each spell slot level from 1st through 5th. Each path follows a single through-line: every smite deepens the one before it or opens the next act.

## Using Smites

- **Hit, then spend.** When you hit with a melee weapon attack or an unarmed strike, you can expend a spell slot to use one smite. You can use only one smite per hit.
- **Slot level.** Each smite lists the lowest slot it accepts. A higher slot adds one die for each level above that, to a maximum of six dice. Smites with fixed damage (5th tier) accept only 5th-level slots.
- **Availability.** Smites unlock by tier: 1st-level smites at 2nd level, 2nd-level smites at 5th, 3rd-level smites at 9th, 4th-level smites at 13th, and 5th-level smites at 17th.
- **Saving throws.** The DC equals your spell save DC.
- **Concentration.** A smite marked concentration lasts until your concentration is broken, as if you were concentrating on a spell. You can't use it while concentrating on a spell, casting a concentration spell ends it, and you can concentrate on only one smite at a time. Any effect that would last past the end of the target's next turn requires concentration.
- **Combat scale.** No smite effect lasts longer than 1 minute, and no smite affects objects or the world. Smites act on creatures in the fight.
- **Marked dice.** A die marked with an asterisk grows one size when you reach 9th level (d6 becomes d8, d8 becomes d10).
- **Damage types.** Righteous Strikes uses the damage type of a path you know. For Storm, choose lightning or thunder; see the Paladin class table.

## Choose a Path

Each path has five smites, unlocking as your Paladin level rises. Choose the theme and combat effect you want to build around; the linked page includes that path’s full rules and all five smites.

- **[[Corrosion|Corrosion Smite Path]]** · Acid damage — Strips away a foe's defenses, then corrodes its body.
- **[[Arcana|Arcana Smite Path]]** · Force damage — Repositions enemies and disrupts the magic and protections they rely on.
- **[[Frost|Frost Smite Path]]** · Cold damage — Slows enemies, spreads that control across the field, then locks a target in place.
- **[[Radiance|Radiance Smite Path]]** · Radiant damage — Reveals hidden foes, blinds them, binds them, and brings divine judgment.
- **[[Inferno|Inferno Smite Path]]** · Fire damage — Punishes a foe for attacking or moving, escalating into overwhelming heat.
- **[[Judgment|Judgment Smite Path]]** · Psychic damage — Shakes a foe's courage, footing, aim, and control of its actions.
- **[[Storm|Storm Smite Path]]** · lightning or thunder damage — Opens with thunder and builds into lightning, disruption, and surging force.
- **[[Venom|Venom Smite Path]]** · Poison damage — Undermines a foe's body, steadily weakening its ability to act.
- **[[Blight-Smite-Path|Blight Smite Path]]** · Necrotic damage — Drains a foe's vitality and withers its ability to fight.

## Designer's Notes

> [!info]- Design and playtest notes
>
> - Dice formula. Base die d8. Concentration adds one die size. A non-damage rider subtracts one die size until 9th level, which is what the asterisk marks. At 17th level the top tier adds one die size. A smite that locks down a creature gets no further discount, because no smite locks a creature down.
> - Path rules. Effects stay on combat scale (1 minute or less, creatures only). Each path has one through-line. Riders rise from tier to tier. A failed save never takes a creature's turn: it can hobble, tax, or redirect, but the creature still acts.
> - Playtest summary. A solo level 11 paladin against a homebrew dire owlbear (140 HP, AC 15) and an alpha version (200 HP, three Legendary Resistances) showed smites adding a modest edge, about 3 to 6 points of win rate against the alpha. Bonus-action Lay on Hands drove most of the survivability gap over the base file. The arena also caught Corroding Smite's natural-weapon wording, now fixed.
> - Dials, if play shows overshoot. Keep the rider penalty past 9th level. Drop the 17th-level die step for concentration smites. Raise Rekindle's cost to 4 charges per slot level. Limit Legendary Conviction's refund to 3rd-level and higher slots.
> - Changes from the source files. Renamed: Decaying to Corroding, Ruinous to Dissolving, Missile to Darting, Misty to Phasing, Runic to Warding, Banishing to Expelling, Constricting to Shackling, Firestorm to Scorching, Radiating to Blistering, Wrathful to Cowing, Repentant to Humbling, Thunderous to Thundering. New: Sundering. Replaced: Gripping with Wasting. Retired: Pooling. The nine per-path Strikes lines are gone, because Righteous Strikes now names a damage type for each path. Deathly is the only smite name that isn't a gerund.
