# Arcana Smite Path

**Damage type:** Force

Arcana reaches and repositions first, then unravels what the foe depends on.

## Using This Path

- **When you can smite.** When you hit with a melee weapon attack or an unarmed strike, expend a spell slot to use one smite from this path. You can use only one smite per hit.
- **Slot scaling.** A smite accepts its listed slot or higher. Each slot level above the listed level adds one damage die, to a maximum of six dice. A 5th-tier smite has fixed damage and accepts only a 5th-level slot.
- **Access.** The table shows the Paladin level and minimum slot for each smite.
- **Saving throws.** Use your spell save DC.
- **Concentration.** A smite marked concentration lasts until your concentration is broken. You can't use it while concentrating on a spell; casting a concentration spell ends the smite. You can concentrate on only one smite at a time. Any smite effect lasting past the end of the target's next turn requires concentration.
- **Limits.** A smite's effect lasts no longer than 1 minute and affects creatures in the fight, not objects or the world.
- **Improved dice.** Each damage die marked `*` increases one size at Paladin 9th level: d6 becomes d8, and d8 becomes d10. This applies to the base damage, added dice from higher-level slots, and the listed maximum.
- **Righteous Strikes.** This path uses force damage for Righteous Strikes.

## Smites

### Darting Smite

_1st-tier smite · 1st-level slot · Paladin level 2nd+_

Your weapon is coated in sky-blue arcane energy, and the attack deals an extra 2d8 force damage for a 1st-level slot, plus 1d8 for each slot level higher than 1st, to a maximum of 6d8. Additionally, a dart of magical force strikes a different creature of your choice that you can see within 30 feet of the target. The dart deals 1d4 + 1 force damage. A creature protected by the shield spell takes no damage from the dart.

### Phasing Smite

_2nd-tier smite · 2nd-level slot · Paladin level 5th+_

A fine mist surrounds your weapon, and the attack deals an extra 3d6* force damage for a 2nd-level slot, plus 1d6* for each slot level higher than 2nd, to a maximum of 6d6*. You can then teleport up to 20 feet to an unoccupied space that you can see.

### Warding Smite

_3rd-tier smite · 3rd-level slot · Paladin level 9th+ · concentration_

Glowing runes appear around your weapon, and the attack deals an extra 4d10 force damage for a 3rd-level slot, plus 1d10 for each slot level higher than 3rd, to a maximum of 6d10. The target must make a Charisma saving throw. On a failed save, it can't impart conditions onto other creatures for up to 1 minute or until your concentration is broken.

### Dispelling Smite

_4th-tier smite · 4th-level slot · Paladin level 13th+_

Arcane energy flows into your weapon, and the attack deals an extra 5d8 force damage for a 4th-level slot, plus 1d8 for each slot level higher than 4th, to a maximum of 6d8. Additionally, any spell of 3rd level or lower on the target ends. For each spell of 4th level or higher on the target, make an ability check using your spellcasting ability. The DC equals 12 + the spell's level. On a successful check, the spell ends.

### Expelling Smite

_5th-tier smite · 5th-level slot · Paladin level 17th+ · concentration_

Your weapon crackles with force, and the attack deals an extra 6d12 force damage for a 5th-level slot. The target must make a Charisma saving throw. On a failed save, you teleport it up to 30 feet to an unoccupied space you can see within that range, and for up to 1 minute or until your concentration is broken its speed is halved and it can't take reactions. It repeats the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the speed and reaction effect on a success.
