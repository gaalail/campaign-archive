# Blight Smite Path

**Damage type:** Necrotic

Blight feeds on a foe's life, then withers it piece by piece.

| Tier | Required slot | Paladin level | Concentration |
| ---- | ------------- | ------------- | ------------- |
| 1    | 1st-level     | 2             | No            |
| 2    | 2nd-level     | 5             | No            |
| 3    | 3rd-level     | 9             | Yes           |
| 4    | 4th-level     | 13            | Yes           |
| 5    | 5th-level     | 17            | Yes           |

See the [[character-options/class-options/smite-paths/index|shared Smite Path rules]] for slot scaling, saving throws, and concentration.

## Smites

### Deteriorating Smite

_1st-tier smite · 1st-level slot · Paladin level 2nd+_

Your weapon radiates a menacing smog. The attack deals an extra 2d8 necrotic damage for a 1st-level slot, plus 1d8 for each slot level higher than 1st, to a maximum of 6d8. The damage increases by 1d8 if the target is a fey, an ooze, or a plant (this increase can exceed the maximum).

### Draining Smite

_2nd-tier smite · 2nd-level slot · Paladin level 5th+_

Your weapon and arm pulse with red energy, and the attack deals an extra 3d6* necrotic damage for a 2nd-level slot, plus 1d6* for each slot level higher than 2nd, to a maximum of 6d6*. You regain hit points equal to half the necrotic damage dealt.

### Hobbling Smite

_3rd-tier smite · 3rd-level slot · Paladin level 9th+ · concentration_

Veins of sickly green energy run through your weapon, and the attack deals an extra 4d10 necrotic damage for a 3rd-level slot, plus 1d10 for each slot level higher than 3rd, to a maximum of 6d10. For up to 1 minute or until your concentration is broken, the target must make a Constitution saving throw if it moves more than 10 feet during a turn. On a failure, it is knocked prone. The target can make a Constitution saving throw at the end of each of its turns while under this effect. On a success, the effect ends.

### Nauseating Smite

_4th-tier smite · 4th-level slot · Paladin level 13th+ · concentration_

The surface of your weapon seems to bubble momentarily, and the attack deals an extra 5d10 necrotic damage for a 4th-level slot, plus 1d10 for each slot level higher than 4th, to a maximum of 6d10. The target must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw or be affected by this smite for up to 1 minute or until your concentration is broken. Each time the target makes an attack while under this effect, it must make a Constitution saving throw. On a failure, it makes the attack with disadvantage.

### Wasting Smite

_5th-tier smite · 5th-level slot · Paladin level 17th+ · concentration_

Your weapon glows in a magenta hue, and the attack deals an extra 6d12 necrotic damage for a 5th-level slot. The target must make a Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, for up to 1 minute or until your concentration is broken, it has disadvantage on attack rolls, ability checks, and saving throws. It repeats the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success.
