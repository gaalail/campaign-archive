---
title: The Speaker
option_group: Avatar Feature
parent_class: Order of the Voice
min_level: 1
prerequisite: None
summary: You have an extraordinary voice.
source: Corpus Angelus
---

You have an extraordinary voice. With it, you can influence the emotions of your listeners, as if the divine itself were speaking through you. All who join in one of your prayers that lasts for 1 minute, are filled with a sense of safety and tranquility. All of you are affected by the protection from evil and good spell for 1 hour (requiring no concentration). You can affect a number of creatures equal to your paragon level at a time in this way.

Once you use this feature, you must finish a long rest to use it again.

**Avatar Form Only.** You emanate a sacred aura in a 15-foot radius. Your allies within the aura gain the benefits of the protection from evil and good spell as long as they are inside the aura.

**Prerequisite:** None  
**Minimum level:** 1
