# Monk Techniques

The Alternate Monk learns Mystic Techniques at 2nd level. Techniques can be replaced when gaining a Monk level, and their prerequisites must be met. Signature Techniques granted by a Monastic Tradition do not count against the number known and cannot be replaced.
