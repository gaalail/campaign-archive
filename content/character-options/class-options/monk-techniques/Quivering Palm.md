**Prerequisite:** 18th-level Monk

Once per turn when you hit a creature with a melee Martial Arts attack, you can spend 5 Ki to infuse its spirit with deadly vibrations, which persist for a number of days equal to your Monk level. If you and this creature are on the same plane of existence, you can use your action to end the vibrations and force it to make a Constitution saving throw. It is reduced to 0 hit points on a failure, and takes necrotic damage equal to eight Martial Arts Dice on a success.

You can only have one creature under the mystical effects of this Technique at a time. Using this Technique on another creature harmlessly ends the effect on previous creatures.
