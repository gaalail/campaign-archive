While wielding only a glaive, halberd, pike, quarterstaff, or spear and no shield, you gain a +1 bonus to damage rolls with it. Creatures provoke an opportunity attack from you when they enter or move within your reach with that weapon.

#### Polearm Expert

When you take the Attack action while using Polearm Fighting, you can use a bonus action to make a melee attack with the end of your weapon. On a hit, it deals 1d4 + your Strength modifier bludgeoning damage.

**Prerequisite:** Strength and Dexterity 11  
**Minimum level:** 1
