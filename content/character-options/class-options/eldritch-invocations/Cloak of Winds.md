When you fall while your [[character-options/class-options/pact-foci/Eldritch Shroud|Eldritch Shroud]] is equipped, you can subtract up to `100` feet from your fall before calculating your fall damage. Moreover, if you fall at least `5` feet, you can move horizontally `2` feet for every `1` foot you fall.

**Prerequisite:** [[character-options/class-options/pact-foci/Eldritch Shroud|Eldritch Shroud]]  
**Minimum level:** 3
