You gain proficiency in History, and you can use your Pact Ability for all History checks. If already proficient, you add double your Proficiency Bonus to all History checks.

If you make a History check related to an event in the place it occurred, you treat a d20 roll of `9` or lower as a `10`.

**Prerequisite:** [[character-options/class-options/pact-foci/Eldritch Hourglass|Eldritch Hourglass]]  
**Minimum level:** 3
