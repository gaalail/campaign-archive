# Grim Radiance

Choose a number of cantrips and spells from the cleric spell list equal to the number of wizard spells that your Flaming Soul feature allows you to select. This choice is permanent.

When you transform, choose either your wizard spells or your cleric spells; those are the spells you have active for the duration of this transformation.

**Prerequisite:** Flaming Soul feature
