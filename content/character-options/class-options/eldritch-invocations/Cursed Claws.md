# Cursed Claws

When you activate your demon soul transformation, you can magically fuse your pact weapon with your claws. Your claws gain all the magical benefits and bonuses granted to your pact weapon, which returns to its normal form once your transformation ends. This cannot change the reach of your claws.

**Prerequisites:** Carnal Claw feature, Pact of the Blade feature
