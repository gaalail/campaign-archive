Using your [[character-options/class-options/pact-foci/Eldritch Chalice|Eldritch Chalice]] as a Spellcasting Focus, you can cast the [[spells/2nd-level/Restoration|restoration]] spell at Slot Level without expending a spell slot. Once you do, you cannot cast the spell in this way again until you finish a short or long rest.

**Prerequisite:** [[character-options/class-options/pact-foci/Eldritch Chalice|Eldritch Chalice]]  
**Minimum level:** 3
