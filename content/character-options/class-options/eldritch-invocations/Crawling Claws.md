# Crawling Claws

When you finish a short rest, you can create a number of animated claws equal to your proficiency bonus. These claws obey your mental commands and last until they are killed or you use this feature again. They use the statistics of **Giant Rats**.

**Prerequisite:** The Nightcomer patron
