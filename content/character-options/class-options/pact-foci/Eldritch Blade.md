You focus your Patron's power into a sinister weapon. As a bonus action, conjure a nonmagical melee weapon of your choice in an empty hand. It can serve as a Trinket for one invocation. You are proficient with it and can use your Pact modifier instead of Strength or Dexterity for attack and damage rolls. Its attacks deal the same damage type as Eldritch Blast but do not apply its other effects. It disappears if it is more than 5 feet from you for 1 minute, if you conjure another blade, dismiss it, or die. You can bond a magic melee weapon in a 1-hour ritual; bonding another ends the previous bond.
