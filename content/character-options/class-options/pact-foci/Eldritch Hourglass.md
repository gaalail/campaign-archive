You channel your Patron's power to create an otherworldly timekeeping device. As a bonus action, you can conjure an Eldritch Hourglass in an empty hand. This Hourglass takes the form of a Tiny timekeeping device of your choice, clearly constructed of otherworldly craftsmanship.

The Eldritch Hourglass grants you the following benefits:

- It can be used as a Trinket for one Eldritch Invocation.
- When you fail a d20 roll, you can choose to re-roll, but you must use the new result. Once you use this feature to turn a failure into success, you must complete a short or long rest before you can use this feature again.
- So long as you are not Surprised or Incapacitated, you can add your Pact modifier (minimum of `+1`) to initiative rolls.

Your Eldritch Hourglass disappears if it is more than 5 feet from you for 1 minute, if you conjure another Hourglass, you dismiss your Hourglass (no action required), or if you die.
