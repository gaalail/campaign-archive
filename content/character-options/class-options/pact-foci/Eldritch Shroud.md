You weave your Patron's power into a mystical cloak to shield you from danger and hide your steps. As a bonus action, you can conjure an Eldritch Shroud on your shoulders, appearing as a spectral cloak reminiscent of your Patron's magic.

While wearing an Eldritch Shroud, you gain these benefits:

- When you move into an area of shadow, darkness, or an area obscured by natural phenomenon such as fog or mist, you can become invisible while within that effect.
- You are proficient in Stealth and can use your Pact Ability for Stealth checks. If already proficient in Stealth, you add double your Proficiency Bonus to your Stealth checks.

The Eldritch Shroud disappears if it is more than 5 feet from you for 1 minute, if you conjure another Shroud, if you dismiss your Shroud (no action required), or if you die.

You can bond a magic robe or cloak as an Eldritch Shroud by performing a 1-hour Ritual. You can use a bonus action to shunt it to an extradimensional space or conjure it. Bonding a second robe or cloak ends the bond for any previous Shroud.
