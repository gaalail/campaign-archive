As a bonus action, you conjure an Eldritch Shroud on your shoulders, appearing as a spectral cloak reminiscent of your Patron.

- When you move into shadow, darkness, fog, mist, or another area obscured by natural phenomena, you can become invisible while within that effect.
- You are proficient in Stealth and can use your Pact ability for Stealth checks. If already proficient, add double your Proficiency Bonus to those checks.

The Shroud disappears if it is more than 5 feet from you for 1 minute, if you conjure another Shroud, dismiss it (no action required), or die. You can bond a magic robe or cloak with a 1-hour Ritual. Use a bonus action to shunt it to an extradimensional space or conjure it. Bonding a second robe or cloak ends the bond on the first.

**Prerequisite:** None  
**Minimum level:** 3
