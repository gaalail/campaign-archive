You channel your Patron's power to twist and transform your body. As a bonus action, you can transform into an unnatural fusion of yourself and your Patron's otherworldly nature. This transformation lasts until you use a bonus action to revert to your normal form, or you become Unconscious.

While you are transformed, you gain the following benefits:

- When not wearing armor or a shield, your Armor Class is equal to `10` + your Pact and Constitution modifiers.
- Your body counts as a Spellcasting Focus for Warlock spells and Eldritch Blast. You must still expend material components that are consumed or have a gold cost.
- At the start of each of your turns, you can choose to grant yourself temporary hit points equal to your Pact modifier (a minimum of `1`).
- You become unnatural and horrifying to look upon. You are considered proficient in Intimidation, and you can use your Pact ability for any Intimidation checks.
