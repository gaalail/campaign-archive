As a bonus action, you transform into an unnatural fusion of yourself and your Patron's otherworldly nature. The transformation lasts until you use a bonus action to revert, or you become Unconscious.

While transformed, you gain the following benefits:

- Without armor or a shield, your Armor Class equals 10 + your Pact modifier + your Constitution modifier.
- Your body is a Spellcasting Focus for Warlock spells and Eldritch Blast. You must still expend material components that are consumed or have a gold cost.
- At the start of each of your turns, you can grant yourself temporary hit points equal to your Pact modifier (minimum 1).
- You become unnaturally horrifying to look upon. You are proficient in Intimidation and can use your Pact ability for Intimidation checks.

**Prerequisite:** None  
**Minimum level:** 3
