As a bonus action, you conjure an Eldritch Instrument of your chosen musical type in an empty hand.

- Your number of Invocations Known increases by 1. The bonus Invocation must have Eldritch Instrument as a prerequisite, and the Instrument is its Trinket.
- You are proficient with the Instrument and use your Pact ability for checks with it. If already proficient, add double your Proficiency Bonus to checks with it.
- When it is used as the Spellcasting Focus for a spell with a verbal component or for Eldritch Blast, you can change any damage dealt by that effect to Thunder damage.

The Instrument disappears if it is more than 5 feet from you for 1 minute, if you conjure another Instrument, dismiss it (no action required), or die. You can bond a magic musical instrument with a 1-hour Ritual. Use a bonus action to shunt it to an extradimensional space or conjure it. Bonding a second instrument ends the bond on the first.

**Prerequisite:** None  
**Minimum level:** 3
