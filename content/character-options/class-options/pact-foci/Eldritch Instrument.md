You focus your Patron's power into an Eldritch Instrument, a Musical Instrument of ethereal beauty. As a bonus action, you can conjure this Eldritch Instrument in an empty hand, and it takes the form of a Musical Instrument of your choice.

While wielding the Instrument, it grants certain benefits:

- Your number of Invocations Known increases by `1`. This bonus Invocation must have Eldritch Instrument as its prerequisite, and your Eldritch Instrument is its Trinket.
- You are considered proficient with this Instrument, and you can use your Pact Ability for ability checks with it. If you are already proficient with that Instrument, you can add double your Proficiency Bonus to checks with it.
- When you use this Instrument as the Spellcasting Focus for a spell with a Verbal component or for Eldritch Blast, you can change any damage dealt to Thunder.

Your Eldritch Instrument disappears if it is more than 5 feet from you for 1 minute, if you conjure another Instrument, if you dismiss the Instrument (no action required), or if you die.

You can bond a magic Musical Instrument as your Eldritch Instrument with a 1-hour Ritual. You can use a bonus action to shunt or conjure it from an extradimensional space. If you bond a second Instrument, any previous bonds are ended.
