You focus your Patron's power into a dark grimoire known as an Eldritch Tome. It contains one copy of each Warlock spell you know, and grants these benefits:

- Your number of Invocations Known increases by 1. The bonus Invocation must have no prerequisites, and your Tome serves as its Trinket. At the end of each long rest, you can replace this Invocation with another qualifying Invocation.
- At the end of a long rest, you can replace a Warlock spell you know with another Warlock spell of the same level.
- You can cast a Warlock spell contained in your Tome as a Ritual if that spell has the Ritual tag.
- You can spend 1 hour, during a short or long rest, to conjure an exact copy of your Tome, destroying any previous Tome.

If you die, your Tome crumbles to ash.

**Prerequisite:** None  
**Minimum level:** 3
