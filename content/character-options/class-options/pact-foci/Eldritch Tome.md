Your Tome contains your known Warlock spells and grants one additional invocation without prerequisites. The Tome is its Trinket. After a long rest you can replace that invocation and one known spell with another of the same level. You can cast ritual-tagged Warlock spells in the Tome as rituals. During a 1-hour ritual, conjure an exact replacement Tome, destroying the previous one. It crumbles to ash if you die.
