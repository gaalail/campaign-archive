You can weave your Patron's power into an otherworldly chalice. As a bonus action, you conjure an ornate, Tiny Eldritch Chalice in an empty hand.

- It can be used as a Trinket for one Eldritch Invocation.
- It counts as both Alchemist's Supplies and an Herbalism Kit. When you make an ability check using it for either tool, you are proficient, and you can use your Pact modifier for the check.
- At the end of each short or long rest, you can perform a Ritual to conjure one vial from the following options: acid, alchemist's fire, holy water, pure water, a potion of healing, or a potion of climbing.
- The liquid retains potency until the end of your next long rest, unless you perform the Ritual again, at which point the previous liquid disappears.

The Chalice disappears if it is more than 5 feet from you for 1 minute, if you conjure another Chalice, dismiss it (no action required), or die.

**Prerequisite:** None  
**Minimum level:** 3
