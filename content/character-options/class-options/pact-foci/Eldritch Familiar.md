You learn [[spells/1st-level/Find Familiar|find familiar]] and can cast it as a ritual without it counting against your Spells Known. Your familiar can take the forms listed in the [[character-options/class-options/familiars/index|Eldritch Familiar catalogue]] (also browsable under [[companions|Familiars & Companions]]). It shares your initiative, moves and uses reactions independently, and Dodges unless you command it as a bonus action. When you cast a spell or Eldritch Blast, it can originate from your familiar if it is within 60 feet.
