You focus your Patron's power to bind an Eldritch Familiar. You learn _conjure familiar_ and can cast it as a Ritual, but it does not count against your number of Spells Known.

When you cast **conjure familiar**, the familiar gains the following bonuses:

- **Eldritch Forms.** Your familiar can also take the form of one of the Eldritch Familiar stat blocks in the Familiar catalogue.
- **Combat.** The familiar shares your initiative and acts on your turn. It can move and use its reaction on its own, but takes only the Dodge action unless you use a bonus action to command it to take another action.
- **Spellcasting.** Whenever you cast a spell or use Eldritch Blast, you can choose for it or any number of beams to originate from the familiar if you are within 60 feet of it.

**Prerequisite:** None  
**Minimum level:** 3
