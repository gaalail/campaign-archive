You learn find familiar and can cast it as a ritual without it counting against your Spells Known. Your familiar can take the forms listed in the Eldritch Familiar catalogue. It shares your initiative, moves and uses reactions independently, and Dodges unless you command it as a bonus action. When you cast a spell or Eldritch Blast, it can originate from your familiar if it is within 60 feet.
