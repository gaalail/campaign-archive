# Metamagic

**Sorcerer-exclusive spell modifications.**

Your innate magical power lets you bend spells in unique ways. These **Metamagic** options let you modify spells you cast.

## How They Work

- You learn metamagic at 3rd level and higher levels
- Each costs a **Sorcery Point** to use
- You can modify spells you cast (not spells from items or scrolls)
- You know a certain number (check your [[Sorcerer]] class page for specifics)

## Popular Metamagic

- **[[Careful Spell]]** — Let allies automatically succeed on spell saves
- **[[Quickened Spell]]** — Cast a spell as a bonus action instead of full action
- **[[Subtle Spell]]** — Cast without visible components (sneaky magic)
- **[[Heightened Spell]]** — Make enemies less likely to resist your spell
- **[[Empowered Spell]]** — Reroll damage dice
- **[[Distant Spell]]** — Stretch a spell's range
- **[[Extended Spell]]** — Double a spell's duration
- **[[Twinned Spell]]** — Target a second creature with a single-target spell
