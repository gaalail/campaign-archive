## Stat Block

**Tiny Dragon, Patron Alignment**

- **Armor Class:** 13 + PB (natural armor)
- **Hit Points:** 5 + 3 × Warlock level
- **Speed:** 20 ft., fly 60 ft.
- **Ability Scores:** Str 3 (-4), Dex 16 (+3), Con 12 (+1), Int 10 (+0), Wis 8 (-1), Cha 13 (+1)
- **Other:** Resistance: chosen Draconic Essence; Blindsight 10 ft., Darkvision 60 ft.; Draconic, understands its Warlock.

### Traits

Draconic Essence. When conjured, choose Acid, Cold, Fire, Lightning, or Poison. The choice determines the familiar’s resistance and Bite damage.

Eldritch Bond. When the familiar is forced to make an ability check or saving throw, add the Warlock’s Proficiency Bonus.

Hit Dice. The familiar has a number of d4 Hit Dice equal to the Warlock level and gains the normal benefits of short and long rests.

Magic Resistance. The familiar has advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

### Actions

Bite. Melee Spell Attack: Warlock spell attack modifier to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 1d4 + 3 + PB Piercing plus 1d4 Essence damage.
