## Stat Block

**Tiny Fey, Patron Alignment**

- **Armor Class:** 11 + PB (natural armor)
- **Hit Points:** 5 + 3 × Warlock level
- **Speed:** 20 ft., fly 40 ft.
- **Ability Scores:** Str 3 (-4), Dex 16 (+3), Con 10 (+0), Int 8 (-1), Wis 13 (+1), Cha 12 (+1)
- **Other:** Immunity: Charmed, Frightened; Elvish, Sylvan, understands its Warlock.

### Traits

Eldritch Bond. When the familiar is forced to make an ability check or saving throw, add the Warlock’s Proficiency Bonus.

Hit Dice. The familiar has a number of d4 Hit Dice equal to the Warlock level and gains the normal benefits of short and long rests.

Magic Resistance. The familiar has advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

### Actions

Invisibility. The familiar turns invisible until it attacks or forces a creature to make a check or save. It concentrates as if concentrating on a spell.

Sting. Melee Spell Attack: Warlock spell attack modifier to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 1d4 + 3 + PB Piercing damage; the familiar learns the target’s emotional state.
