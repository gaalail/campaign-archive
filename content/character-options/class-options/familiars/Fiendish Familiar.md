## Stat Block

**Tiny Fiend, Patron Alignment**

- **Armor Class:** 11 + PB (natural armor)
- **Hit Points:** 5 + 3 × Warlock level
- **Speed:** 20 ft., fly 40 ft.
- **Ability Scores:** Str 3 (-4), Dex 16 (+3), Con 12 (+1), Int 10 (+0), Wis 8 (-1), Cha 13 (+1)
- **Other:** Immunity: Poisoned; Resistance: Cold, Fire, Poison; Devil’s Sight; Abyssal, Infernal, understands its Warlock.

### Traits

Eldritch Bond. When the familiar is forced to make an ability check or saving throw, add the Warlock’s Proficiency Bonus.

Hit Dice. The familiar has a number of d4 Hit Dice equal to the Warlock level and gains the normal benefits of short and long rests.

Magic Resistance. The familiar has advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

### Actions

Claw. Melee Spell Attack: Warlock spell attack modifier to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 1d4 + 3 + PB Slashing damage; target makes a Constitution save or is Poisoned until the familiar’s next turn.

Shapechanger. Transform into a rat (20-ft. speed, 20-ft. climb), lizard (20-ft. speed, 20-ft. swim), raven (20-ft. speed, 60-ft. fly), or its true form. Equipment does not transform; it returns to true form if it dies.
