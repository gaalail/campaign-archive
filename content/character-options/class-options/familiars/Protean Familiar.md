## Stat Block

**Tiny Elemental, Patron Alignment**

- **Armor Class:** 11 + PB (natural armor)
- **Hit Points:** 5 + 3 × Warlock level
- **Speed:** 10 ft., fly 40 ft. (hover)
- **Ability Scores:** Str 3 (-4), Dex 16 (+3), Con 12 (+1), Int 8 (-1), Wis 10 (+0), Cha 12 (+1)
- **Other:** Immunity: Exhaustion, Grappled, Paralyzed, Petrified, Poisoned, Prone, Restrained, Unconscious; Darkvision 120 ft.; Primordial, understands its Warlock.

### Traits

Elemental Chaos. When conjured, roll a d4: Air (Lightning), Earth (Acid), Fire (Fire), Water (Cold). The familiar is immune to its Essence damage type.

Eldritch Bond. When the familiar is forced to make an ability check or saving throw, add the Warlock’s Proficiency Bonus.

Hit Dice. The familiar has a number of d4 Hit Dice equal to the Warlock level and gains the normal benefits of short and long rests.

Magic Resistance. The familiar has advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

### Actions

Elemental Spark. Ranged Spell Attack: Warlock spell attack modifier to hit, range 60/90 ft., one target. Hit: 1d6 + 3 + PB Essence damage; target cannot take reactions until the familiar’s next turn.
