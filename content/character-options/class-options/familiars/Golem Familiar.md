## Stat Block

**Small Construct, Patron Alignment**

- **Armor Class:** 13 + PB (natural armor)
- **Hit Points:** 5 + 3 × Warlock level
- **Speed:** 30 ft.
- **Ability Scores:** Str 12 (+1), Dex 8 (-1), Con 16 (+3), Int 1 (-5), Wis 8 (-1), Cha 2 (-4)
- **Other:** Resistance: Lightning, Poison; Darkvision 60 ft.; understands its Warlock.

### Traits

Eldritch Bond. When the familiar is forced to make an ability check or saving throw, add the Warlock’s Proficiency Bonus.

Hit Dice. The familiar has a number of d4 Hit Dice equal to the Warlock level and gains the normal benefits of short and long rests.

Magic Resistance. The familiar has advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

Wondrous Servant. When conjured, choose one tool set. The familiar is proficient and uses the Warlock’s Pact Ability and Proficiency Bonus for checks with it.

### Actions

Lockdown. One creature within 5 feet makes a Strength save or is Grappled; Large or larger creatures have advantage.

Rend. Melee Spell Attack: Warlock spell attack modifier to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 1d6 + 3 + PB Bludgeoning damage.
