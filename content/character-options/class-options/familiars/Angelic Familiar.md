## Stat Block

**Tiny Celestial, Patron Alignment**

- **Armor Class:** 11 + PB (natural armor)
- **Hit Points:** 5 + 3 × Warlock level
- **Speed:** 25 ft., fly 30 ft.
- **Ability Scores:** Str 3 (-4), Dex 16 (+3), Con 8 (-1), Int 10 (+0), Wis 2 (-4), Cha 13 (+1)
- **Other:** Resistance: Necrotic, Radiant; Darkvision 60 ft.; Celestial, understands its Warlock.

### Traits

Eldritch Bond. When the familiar is forced to make an ability check or saving throw, add the Warlock’s Proficiency Bonus.

Hit Dice. The familiar has a number of d4 Hit Dice equal to the Warlock level and gains the normal benefits of short and long rests.

Magic Resistance. The familiar has advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

### Actions

Divine Gift. Touch a creature to grant temporary hit points equal to the Pact modifier. Using this again removes temporary hit points previously granted by this feature.

Strike. Melee Spell Attack: Warlock spell attack modifier to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 1d4 + 3 + PB Radiant damage.
