You can use your Sacred Language (Invective) feature against a creature that you can see within normal range, requiring no sound, by simply moving your lips. The creature does not need to hear you. Creatures that can see you must succeed on a DC 14 Wisdom (Perception) check to understand that the effect is originating from you.

**Avatar Form Only.** The range of your Sacred Language (Invective) feature increases to 90 feet.

**Prerequisite:** None  
**Minimum level:** 1
