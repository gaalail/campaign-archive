# Duskwalker

Many witches go through lengthy rituals to call forth specific spirits through the veil and into our world, but **Duskwalkers** have no time for such frippery. Their covens focus on speed and versatility, learning to adapt to situations as quickly as possible.

A Duskwalker's abilities depend entirely on whether they are bathed in bright light or submerged in darkness. When in light, spirits grant defensive insight, trap-detection, and truth-seeking; in darkness, they meld with the shadows, teleporting across the gloom and striking with terrifying stealth.

### Witchmark

Duskwalker witchmarks emphasize duality: one dark sclera while the other remains light, or white smoke curling up one arm while shadowy black vapor coils around the other.

## Initiate of Light and Dark

_3rd-level Duskwalker feature_

The lighting conditions of your current space govern your abilities:
- In **Darkness**, you gain Darkness abilities.
- In **Bright Light**, you gain Bright Light abilities.
- In **Dim Light**, you choose whether to channel Light or Dark abilities until you transition into another lighting condition.

## Crepuscular Combatant

_3rd-level Duskwalker feature_

- **When in Darkness:** You can use your Sneak Attack against a creature as long as you do not have disadvantage on the attack roll (you do not require advantage or an adjacent ally).
- **When in Light:** Once per turn, you can reroll one die of your damage rolls, taking the second result.

## Eyes of Twilight

_3rd-level Duskwalker feature_

- **When in Darkness:** You are immune to the blinded condition and gain darkvision out to 60 feet (or extend existing darkvision by 30 feet).
- **When in Light:** As a bonus action when looking at a creature also in bright light, you learn all damage vulnerabilities, resistances, and immunities it possesses (proficiency bonus uses per short or long rest).

## Step of Dusk

_9th-level Duskwalker feature_

- **When in Darkness:** As part of your movement, you can teleport without provoking opportunity attacks to an unoccupied space in darkness within half your speed (proficiency bonus uses per short or long rest).
- **When in Light:** You ignore all difficult terrain (magical or mundane) and instinctively detect whether any traps lie within 30 feet, including whether they are magical and what damage type they inflict.

## Penumbral Blades

_13th-level Duskwalker feature_

- **When in Darkness:** When rolling damage, you can reroll all 1s, taking the new roll.
- **When in Light:** When an ally within bright light damages a target, you can use your reaction to force the target to make a Wisdom saving throw (DC = 8 + proficiency bonus + Dexterity modifier). On a failure, the ally adds your Sneak Attack bonus to their damage (proficiency bonus uses per long rest).

## Master of Light and Dark

_17th-level Duskwalker feature_

As a bonus action, you can project an aura of either supernatural darkness or bright light (with 5 feet of dim light beyond) in a 5-foot radius centered on yourself. You can switch between light and dark or dismiss the aura as a bonus action, giving you direct control over your light-dependent abilities. You can use this feature 3 times per long rest.
