# Sleuth

Nothing escapes your keen intuition. By implementing superior deductive and inductive reasoning, you outmaneuver and track down any foe, no matter how sly.

---

## Build the Case & Accusation

_3rd-level Sleuth feature_

- **Build the Case:** Investigation, Nature, Perception, and Survival checks are "Evidence Checks" (add half proficiency bonus if not proficient). Success grants 1 Evidence (**Humanoid Evidence** in settlements, **Monstrous Evidence** in wilderness; maximum Evidence = Int score + PB + rogue level).
- **Accusation:** Bonus action to declare a creature within 60 feet the **Culprit** (1/short or long rest). Once per turn as a free action, spend Evidence to activate learned Sleuth Abilities (learn PB abilities, gaining another as PB increases):
  - *Analyze (1 Evidence):* Learn the Culprit’s score in one ability.
  - *Duck and Roll (1 Evidence):* Culprit cannot make opportunity attacks against you this turn.
  - *Crack in the Armor (2 Evidence):* Add +2d6 Sneak Attack damage on next hit against Culprit.
  - *Anticipate (4 Evidence):* Gain advantage on saving throws forced by Culprit for 1 minute.
  - *Befuddle (4 Evidence):* End charmed or frightened condition imposed by Culprit.
  - *Outmaneuver (4 Evidence):* Gain advantage on attacks against Culprit until end of next turn.
  - *Follow the Trail (X Evidence):* Add +2 per Evidence spent to a skill check (max +10 for 5 Evidence).
  - *Peerless Locator (6 Evidence):* Nonmagically cast [[locate-creature]] on Culprit.
  - *Spot the Tell (6 Evidence):* Nonmagically cast [[detect-thoughts]] on Culprit.
  - *Discombobulate (8 Evidence):* Intelligence save or Culprit is stunned until end of next turn (partial action on save).

## Informant Network

_9th-level Sleuth feature_

Establish up to (Int modifier + Cha modifier) informants in settled areas to acquire local gossip and gain 2 Humanoid Evidence weekly.

## Quick Draw

_13th-level Sleuth feature_

Conceal a light weapon on your person (DC = 8 + 2×PB + 2×Int modifier to detect). On your first turn in initiative, attack with it as a bonus action, ignoring loading properties on hand crossbows.

## Labyrinthian Mind

_17th-level Sleuth feature_

You can designate up to **six active Culprits** at once, and can reselect your known Sleuth Abilities once per month.
