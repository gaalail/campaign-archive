# Dark Jester

At the dawn of creation, an entity called the Toymaker began telling a joke. What followed has been every twist and turn of that joke’s setup: the birth and death of gods, the rise and fall of empires, every speck of dust in the cosmos—all details necessary to ensure the punchline is a real killer.

Dark Jesters are laughing shadows who have been clued into the Toymaker's joke by Death themself. Knives as sharp as their grins, they enthusiastically add macabre flourishes to the cosmos, turning suffering into an art form.

---

## Tools of the Trade & Killer Punchline

_3rd-level Dark Jester feature_

- **Tools of the Trade:** Gain proficiency with disguise kits and poisoner’s kits.
- **Killer Punchline:** Once per turn when damaging a creature at or below half its hit point maximum, inflict an extra **1d6 poison damage** (increasing to **1d8** at 9th, **1d10** at 13th, and **1d12** at 17th level).

## Laughing Shadow

_3rd-level Dark Jester feature_

As an action, summon a perfect illusory duplicate within 30 feet for 1 minute (concentration as a spell) and Hide as a free action:
- Move duplicate up to 30 feet as a bonus action (must remain within 90 feet).
- When duplicate is adjacent to an enemy, you have advantage on attacks against that enemy.
- Sneak Attack damage against an adjacent enemy can become poison damage.
- You can also cast [[silent-image]] as a 1st-level spell. You have Charisma modifier uses per short or long rest for duplicates or *silent image*.

## Terror Toys

_9th-level Dark Jester feature_

Whenever you end concentration on a Laughing Shadow or *silent image*, leave behind a gift-wrapped trap. If an enemy comes within 5 feet, a shrieking puppet pops out: the target must make a Wisdom save or become frightened and take psychic damage equal to a roll of your **Sneak Attack dice** (1/short or long rest).

## Poof!

_13th-level Dark Jester feature_

When taking damage, use your reaction to become invisible and teleport up to 30 feet, optionally leaving an illusory duplicate in your wake (2 uses per long rest).

## A Clown’s Arsenal

_17th-level Dark Jester feature_

Gain one additional use of each subclass feature. Creatures resistant to poison take normal damage from your poison; creatures immune to poison take half damage. In addition, once per short or long rest, increase poison damage dealt by your **rogue level**.
