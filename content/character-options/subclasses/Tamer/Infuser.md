# Infuser

Infusers meld primordial elemental power with their companions.

## Infuse

_3rd-level Infuser feature_

After a long rest, infuse each companion with acid, cold, fire, or lightning. The first time it hits a target on its turn, it deals 1d4 additional damage of that type.

The damage becomes 1d6 at 6th level, 1d8 at 10th level, and 1d10 at 14th level.

## Elemental Discharge

_7th-level Infuser feature_

When infusing a companion, it gains an action corresponding to its element. The save DC equals your Tamer spell save DC.

- **Acid: Caustic Mist.** A 20-foot cube around the companion lasts for one minute. Creatures entering or starting there make Constitution saves or take 4d4 acid damage, half on success.
- **Cold: Snap Freeze.** Creatures in a 10-foot-radius sphere make Constitution saves, taking 4d8 cold damage and suffering halved speed for one round on failure.
- **Fire: Flametongue.** Creatures in a 25-foot cone make Dexterity saves, taking 6d6 fire damage on failure, half on success.
- **Lightning: Sparkstep.** Creatures in a 10-foot-radius sphere make Dexterity saves, taking 4d6 lightning damage on failure, half on success. The companion can then fly 30 feet.

Once used, the companion cannot use its elemental action again until you finish a short or long rest. At 18th level, it can use the action twice before a rest.

## Primordial Shield

_10th-level Infuser feature_

A companion gains resistance to its infused element. When it takes damage of that type, use your reaction to grant immunity to that damage until the start of your next turn. Once used, regain after a short or long rest.

## Unstable Fusion

_14th-level Infuser feature_

As an action, infuse a companion with a second element for one minute. It gains both infusions, discharges, and shields. Once used, regain after a long rest.
