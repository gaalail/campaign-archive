# Necromancer

Necromancers manipulate life force and create undead companions through taming magic.

## Life Hack

_3rd-level Necromancer feature_

Once per turn when a companion hits a creature, you can command it to shed life force. It takes 1d4 necrotic damage, gains that many temporary hit points, and deals that much additional necrotic damage to its target.

The damage becomes 1d6 at 6th level, 1d8 at 10th level, and 1d10 at 14th level.

## Post-Mortem

_3rd-level Necromancer feature_

Within one minute of a creature dying, you can attempt to tame it. Its soul makes a Charisma saving throw against your Tamer spell save DC. Humanoids and giants do not automatically succeed. On a failure, its soul binds to its body, its type becomes undead, and it becomes your companion.

## Necromancer Spells

These spells count as Tamer spells and do not count against spells known. Appendix B spells remain unimported.

## Animate Dead

_7th-level Necromancer feature_

You learn _animate dead_, always have it prepared, and can cast it once without a spell slot. Regain this use after a long rest.

At 18th level, creatures animated through this feature can use ghast statistics for Medium or smaller corpses or minotaur skeleton statistics for larger corpses. You can maintain control of one such creature per casting.

## Enervate

_10th-level Necromancer feature_

As an action while a summoned companion is within 100 feet, deal necrotic damage to it up to twice your Tamer level and regain that many hit points. Alternatively, take the damage yourself and restore that many hit points to the companion.

## Aura of Undeath

_14th-level Necromancer feature_

As an action, give a companion a 15-foot aura for one minute. Creatures in the aura make Constitution saves or take 4d8 necrotic damage, while the companion gains temporary hit points equal to half the damage dealt.

The companion also remains at 1 hit point instead of falling unconscious when reduced to 0 hit points, gaining failed death saves. It dies if it reaches the specified failed-save limit. After one use, regain after a long rest.
