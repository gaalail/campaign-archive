# Tamer Training Paradigms

At 3rd level, a Tamer chooses a Training Paradigm. Each paradigm grants features at 3rd, 7th, 10th, and 14th level, with its 7th-level feature improving at 18th level.

- [[character-options/subclasses/Tamer/Leader|Leader]] — Coordinate companions through encouragement and tactical direction.
- [[character-options/subclasses/Tamer/Infuser|Infuser]] — Empower companions with primordial elements.
- [[character-options/subclasses/Tamer/Necromancer|Necromancer]] — Bind and empower undead companions.
- [[character-options/subclasses/Tamer/Splicer|Splicer]] — Modify companions through biomancy.
