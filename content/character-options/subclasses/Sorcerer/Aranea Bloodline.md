# Aranea Bloodline

Through fluke, bargain, bite, or curse, your bloodline has been infused with the strange, otherworldly magics of arachnids. Chitinous scales, multi-faceted eyes, and spiritual spiderwebs govern your innate spellcasting.

---

## Arachnid’s Kiss & Aranea Magic

_1st-level Aranea Bloodline feature_

- **Arachnid’s Kiss:** Exoskeleton hardens and extra eyes manifest. Disadvantage on Persuasion checks, advantage on Intimidation checks, proficiency in Perception, and a climbing speed equal to your walking speed.
- **Aranea Magic:** You learn bonus spells that count as sorcerer spells:

| Sorcerer Level | Spells |
| :---: | :--- |
| 1st | [[entangle\|entangle]], [[find-familiar\|find familiar]] (spider only) |
| 3rd | [[pass-without-trace\|pass without trace]], [[web\|web]] |
| 5th | [[bestow-curse\|bestow curse]], [[fear\|fear]] |
| 7th | [[compulsion\|compulsion]], [[giant-insect\|giant insect]] |
| 9th | [[hold-monster\|hold monster]], [[insect-plague\|insect plague]] |

## Spider Swarm

_6th-level Aranea Bloodline feature_

Bonus action for 1 minute (2 uses per long rest):
- Ignore difficult terrain as swarming spiders carry you.
- Hostile creatures within 15 feet treat the area as difficult terrain.
- Knock targets prone whenever you deal damage to a creature within 15 feet.
- Summon a **Swarm of Spiders** acting on your initiative following mental commands.

## Preternatural Senses

_14th-level Aranea Bloodline feature_

You cannot be surprised. When within 5 feet of a web and another creature touches any part of that web, you perceive its exact location, ignoring invisibility and obscurity.

## Avatar of the Arachnid

_18th-level Aranea Bloodline feature_

Bonus action for 1 minute (2 uses per long rest): activate Spider Swarm for free, cast [[web]] without expending spell slots, and maintain webs without requiring concentration.
