# Coven of Spirits

There are families of witches that go back for generations upon generations, each member devoting their lives to the study of witchcraft. Their devotion changes their very bloodline until the art of witchery is in the veins of all born under the family tree.

These **Witch Sorcerers** carry ancestral spirits who watch over them at all times, granting guidance across dreamscapes, warding loved ones from the brink of death, and manipulating arcane DCs and spell saving throws.

### Witchmark

Witchmarks for sorcerers are hereditary family crests passed down with little divergence, displayed proudly on faces, necks, or hands.

---

## Ethereal Coven

_1st-level Coven of Spirits feature_

During a short or long rest, you can project your sleeping consciousness into a pocket plane to commune with your witch ancestors, functioning as the [[augury]] spell. At 10th level, this communion expands to function as the [[commune]] spell.

## Favored Heir

_1st-level Coven of Spirits feature_

Your ancestral spirits shield you and your allies from death. When you or a creature you can see within 60 feet rolls a failure on a death saving throw, you can spend **1 sorcery point** to reroll that die.

Additionally, rolling a natural 1 on a death saving throw counts as only one failure instead of two, and any attacks that would automatically inflict two failures only inflict one.

## Weakening Spirits

_6th-level Coven of Spirits feature_

As a reaction when a creature you can see is targeted by a spell requiring a saving throw, you can spend **2 sorcery points** to summon ancestral spirits to assist it: the creature adds your Charisma modifier to its saving throw. Once a creature has benefited from this feature, it cannot do so again until finishing a short or long rest.

## Emboldening Spirits

_14th-level Coven of Spirits feature_

When you cast a spell requiring a saving throw, you can spend **4 sorcery points** to add your Charisma modifier to your spell save DC a second time for that casting. Once used against a creature, you cannot target that creature with this feature again until completing a short or long rest.

## Shield of the Coven

_18th-level Coven of Spirits feature_

As a bonus action, you can spend **4 sorcery points** to summon ancestral spirits around one non-hostile creature you can see. That creature gains a bonus to its Armor Class equal to your Charisma modifier for 1 minute. Once a creature has benefited from this feature, it cannot receive it again until you finish a short or long rest.
