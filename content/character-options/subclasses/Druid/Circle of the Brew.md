# Circle of the Brew

When mentioning the word witch, many instantly think of the witch druids belonging to the **Circle of the Brew**. After all, their practice is so well-known it has codified the general idea of witch.

Circle of the Brew druids worship nature and spirits equally, distilling herbal and spiritual energy into potent consumable brews. Unlike more reclusive circles, brew witches frequently dwell in bustling villages and cities as apothecaries, potion-masters, and healers.

### Witchmark

Witchmarks for Circle of the Brew druids range from winding ivy vines or damp dark loam clinging to their feet, to elaborate brewing recipes tattooed across their arms and spine.

## Collapsible Cauldron

_2nd-level Circle of the Brew feature_

You can collapse a cauldron into the size of a thimble or expand it back to full size as an action (taking 1 minute to transition). In thimble form, its weight is negligible; in full form, it weighs 70 lbs. You can turn any cauldron worth at least 20 gp into a collapsible cauldron by concentrating on it for 1 hour.

## Brew of Wild Shape

_2nd-level Circle of the Brew feature_

You can bottle your Wild Shape ability. Brewing takes 10 minutes in your collapsible cauldron and expends one use of your Wild Shape.

As an action, you or a willing creature can drink the brew, instantly entering Wild Shape as if they were a druid choosing a beast form. If the imbiber is not a druid, they can only maintain the beast form for half the normal duration, after which they must succeed on a Constitution saving throw against your spell save DC or become stunned for 1 round.

You cannot regain the expended Wild Shape use until the brew is consumed, destroyed, or you finish a long rest.

## Magical Brew

_3rd-level Circle of the Brew feature_

You can infuse your circle spells into consumable potions. Brewing takes 10 minutes and uses a spell slot one level lower than normal (minimum 1st level). Only circle spells can be brewed this way, and any material components are still consumed.

A creature consuming a magical brew suffers its effects even if taken unwillingly or unknowingly; saving throws made against hostile brews (such as *toil and trouble*, *polymorph*, or *hold monster*) are made with disadvantage. You can maintain a number of active magical brews equal to your Wisdom modifier at one time.

## Refined Brew of Wild Shape

_6th-level Circle of the Brew feature_

Any creature that drinks your Brew of Wild Shape—even unwillingly or unknowingly—is transformed into a beast of your choice from your available forms. Non-druid imbibers no longer need to make Constitution saving throws against being stunned when the transformation ends.

## Expert Ritualist

_10th-level Circle of the Brew feature_

When you cast a spell as a ritual, the casting time and the gold cost of any consumed material components are reduced by half.

## Master Brewer

_14th-level Circle of the Brew feature_

When you prepare spells after a long rest, choose one spell from the druid spell list that targets only one creature. You can craft magical brews of that spell even if it is not on your circle spell list.
