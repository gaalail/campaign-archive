_Divine Domain (Alternate Cleric subclass)_

Deities who bestow the Life Domain upon Clerics are many, but they are invariably aligned with the cosmic forces of Goodness. They include deities of health and healing, preservation, growth, community, and family. Despite their varied patronages, deities who bestow this Divine Domain universally despise Undead and Necromancy magic as the highest blasphemies.

## Features

> [!info]- Disciple of Life (level 2)
> The life-giving energies of the divine infuse your healing magic. Whenever you cast a Cleric spell that restores hit points to a creature, it regains additional hit points equal to twice the level of the spell slot used to cast the spell.

> [!info]- Channel Divinity: Preserve Life (level 3)
> You can directly call on the restorative power of your deity. As an action, you hold aloft your Holy Symbol and Channel Divinity to unleash a wave of divine healing. You restore a total number of hit points equal to five times your Cleric level, distributed among creatures of your choice within 30 feet.
>
> You cannot use this Channel Divinity to restore hit points to Constructs or Undead creatures.

> [!info]- Channel Divinity: Turn Undead (level 3)
> You use divine magic to destroy blasphemous Undead. As an action, you hold aloft your Holy Symbol and Channel Divinity to rebuke creatures of unlife. Undead within 30 feet must make a Wisdom saving throw. On a failed save, Undead take radiant damage equal to five times your Cleric level, then are Turned for 1 minute, or until they take damage.
>
> A Turned creature cannot take reactions, and will only use its turns to safely move as far from you as possible, try to escape effects preventing its movement, or take the Dodge action.

> [!info]- Blessed Healer (level 6)
> Channeling your deity's power to restore others also restores you. Any time you cast a Cleric spell that restores hit points to another creature, you also regain hit points equal to twice the level of the spell slot used to cast the spell.

> [!info]- Conduit of Life (level 18)
> Devotion to your deity allows you to channel their pure divine essence to heal. Whenever you restore hit points to a creature with a Cleric spell, you treat any dice as the maximum roll.

## Resurrection and Undeath

Despite their zealous opposition to Necromancy magic and Undead creatures, Life Domain Clerics do not consider raising the dead blasphemy unless they are raised to become Undead thralls.
