# Domain of Witchcraft (Medium)

Preserving an oral tradition requires dedicated members devoting their lives to the safekeeping of that knowledge. Clerics of Witchcraft are traditionally the custodians of the oral tradition of witchcraft. The biggest threat to the survival of any oral tradition is the death of those that practice it, which is why these clerics' ability to invite spirits into their minds and learn their knowledge is vital to the survival of the practice.

They are experts in the art of possession: both self-possession to learn secrets of those who passed, and possession of others to weaken foes from within. Many go by the term **Medium**, acting as a psychopomp and bridge across the veil.

### Witchmark

Mediums usually have witchmarks on their eyes, hands, or tongues, symbolizing their ability to contact the dead. These marks often take the shape of stylized eyes or star-shaped pupils. When inviting spirits into their bodies or those of others, the witchmark's glow reflects the emotional state of the possessing spirit.

## Medium Possession

_1st-level Domain of Witchcraft feature_

You can allow spirits to enter your mind, acting as a bridge between the living and the dead. By spending 10 minutes meditating, you reach into the Ethereal Plane and summon a deceased creature whose true name you know. The spirit may choose to speak through you at the GM's discretion.

The spirit can speak languages it knew in life using your voice (which changes to match the spirit's tone), but cannot move your body unless permitted (moving slowly and laboriously). The connection lasts for 5 minutes, after which you must immediately take a short rest or suffer 1 level of exhaustion. Once you use this feature, you cannot use it again until you finish a short or long rest.

## Whispered Secrets

_1st-level Domain of Witchcraft feature_

You can dispatch spirits to infiltrate a creature's form. As a bonus action, you send spirits into a creature you can see within 30 feet. On your next turn, the spirits return and reveal the creature's damage resistances, immunities, and vulnerabilities, if any. You can use this feature a number of times equal to your Wisdom modifier (minimum of once), regaining all uses upon completing a long rest.

## Channel Divinity: Weakening Possession

_2nd-level Domain of Witchcraft feature_

You can use your Channel Divinity to weaken a creature's defenses through spiritual possession. As an action, send spirits into a creature within 30 feet. The target must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw against your spell save DC or become vulnerable to radiant, necrotic, or psychic damage (your choice). If the creature was resistant or immune to the chosen type, it loses that resistance or immunity for the duration. This effect lasts for 1 minute or until you lose concentration (as if concentrating on a spell).

## Channel Divinity: Envoy of Feebleness

_6th-level Domain of Witchcraft feature_

You can pass debilitating conditions from your body to your foes. As an action, whenever you are blinded, charmed, deafened, frightened, paralyzed, poisoned, exhausted, or stunned, you can attempt to pass that condition to a creature you can see within 30 feet.

The target must make a Constitution saving throw against your spell save DC. On a failure, the creature becomes afflicted with your condition and you are cured of it. If the condition is exhaustion, the target receives your current exhaustion level.

## Potent Spellcasting

_8th-level Domain of Witchcraft feature_

You add your Wisdom modifier to the damage you deal with any cleric cantrip.

## Master Medium

_17th-level Domain of Witchcraft feature_

Your authority over spiritual possession achieves mastery:
- When using **Whispered Secrets**, you also learn the target's highest and lowest ability scores.
- You can use any creature under a negative condition within sight as the origin source to pass conditions with **Envoy of Feebleness**.
- When a creature fails its saving throw against **Weakening Possession**, the possessing spirit can seize control of its motor functions, subjecting the target to the effects of [[command]], [[compulsion]], or [[otto's-irresistible-dance|irresistible dance]] (your choice).
