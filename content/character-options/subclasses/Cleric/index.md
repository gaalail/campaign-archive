# Divine Domains (Cleric Subclasses)

**26 Divine Domains available** | Alternate Cleric chooses at **2nd level** (see [[character-options/classes/Cleric#Divine Domain|Divine Domain]] & [[character-options/classes/Cleric#Sacred Calling|Sacred Calling]])

Every Cleric subclass represents a **Divine Domain** bestowed by a deity or cosmic ideal. Core **Alternate Cleric** domains grant Domain Spells and features at **2nd, 3rd (Channel Divinity), 6th, and 18th levels**, while **Divine Domains Supplement** and archive third-party domains follow classic 1st/2nd/6th/8th/17th progression milestones.
