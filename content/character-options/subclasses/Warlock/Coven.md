Grandmother, wode witch, medicine woman, crone; Hags go by many names. Their motivations are nearly always sinister, and they delight in suffering. If hags gather and form a Coven, they unlock vile magic power beyond what any of them could achieve individually, and gain the ability to create Warlocks.

## Coven Magic

_2nd-level Coven Patron feature_

The Coven you serve infuses you with its strange magicks. You can choose for your Eldritch Blast beams to deal Poison damage equal to `1d10` + your Pact modifier on hit. Creatures that take this Poison damage cannot regain hit points until the start of your next turn.

You also learn certain spells at the Warlock levels below. They are Warlock spells for you, but they don't count against your number of Spells Known, and they cannot be replaced:

## Weird Magicks

_2nd-level Coven Patron feature_

Your Coven grants you knowledge of their strange magicks. Any time you cast a Warlock spell or use Eldritch Blast, you can replace its damage with the damage type from a Warlock spell you know, altering the spell for this casting only.

Spells cast in this way resemble the strange magic of hags, and may be unrecognizable to traditional spellcasters, though your spells can be dispelled and counterspelled as normal.

## Crooked Blast

_6th-level Coven Patron feature_

Your magicks have a mind of their own. When you produce an Eldritch Blast that deals Poison damage, it ignores half cover and treats three-quarters cover as half cover.

## Eldritch Gift

_6th-level Coven Patron feature_

When you finish a short rest, you can forgo recovering one expended Pact Magic spell slot to impart its magic to another creature who completed the short rest with you. So long as it has the Spellcasting feature, it regains expended spell slots of a combined level equal to your Slot Level.

## Vile Resilience

_10th-level Coven Patron feature_

Your Coven infuses you with a magical ward. When you take damage from a spell or magical effect, you can use a reaction to gain Resistance to one type of damage from the effect.

This Resistance lasts until you finish a long rest, and if you use this reaction again, any previous Resistance is replaced.

## Shared Spellcasting

_14th-level Coven Patron feature_

You are powerful enough to form your own Coven. During the course of a long rest, you can perform a 1-hour Ritual with up to two willing creatures of your choice who have the ability to cast a spell. You and the creatures become a Coven until you perform this Ritual again, or you die.

While Coven members are within `30` feet of one another, they can cast any spell another Coven member can. However, they must still expend their own spell slots to do so.

Coven members know the direction and distance between them and the other members, so long as they are on the same plane of existence.
