You entered a Pact with a Noble Genie, ruler of one of the elemental planes. Only the most powerful dao, djinn, efreeti, and marids can grant such Pacts. Noble Genies are bound by bargains, grandeur, and ancient elemental law.

## Patron Magic

You can choose for Eldritch Blast to deal damage of your Noble Genie’s Element type: Acid, Cold, Fire, Lightning, or Thunder.

You also learn the spells in the following table at the Warlock levels shown. They count as Warlock spells for you, do not count against Spells Known, and cannot be replaced.

| Warlock Level | Spells                          |
| ------------- | ------------------------------- |
| 2nd           | [[Sleep]], Genie Spell          |
| 3rd           | [[Enlarge-Reduce]], Genie Spell |
| 5th           | [[Fly]], Genie Spell            |
| 7th           | Polymorph, Genie Spell          |
| 9th           | [[Arcane Hand]], Genie Spell    |

## Noble Magic

_2nd-level Noble Genie Patron feature_

Choose the Element associated with your Patron: Acid, Cold, Fire, Lightning, or Thunder. You gain Resistance to that damage type and add its damage to the genie features granted by this Patron. The genie spell entry is determined by the Patron’s vessel type and elemental list.

## Ethereal Nature

_6th-level Noble Genie Patron feature_

You gain Resistance to your Noble Genie’s Element damage. Once per turn, when you deal Element damage with Eldritch Blast, the target makes a Charisma saving throw. On a failure, it instantly switches places with you.

## Mystical Sanctuary

_10th-level Noble Genie Patron feature_

You learn to create a magical sanctuary tied to your element. Creatures designated by you gain concealment and cannot be tracked while inside it.

## Limited Wish

_14th-level Noble Genie Patron feature_

Once per long rest, you can briefly alter reality through the Vessel granted by your Noble Genie, casting one spell of 6th level or lower from any spell list without consuming a spell slot or providing material components.
