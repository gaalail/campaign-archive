You entered a Pact with a Noble Genie, ruler of one of the elemental planes. Only the most powerful dao, djinn, efreeti, and marids can grant such Pacts. Noble Genies are bound by bargains, grandeur, and ancient elemental law.

## Choose Your Noble Genie

Choose Dao, Djinn, Efreeti, or Marid. Your choice determines the Element and damage type associated with your Patron.

| Noble Genie | Element | Damage Type |
| ----------- | ------- | ----------- |
| Dao         | Earth   | Bludgeoning |
| Djinn       | Air     | Thunder     |
| Efreeti     | Fire    | Fire        |
| Marid       | Water   | Cold        |

## Magic Vessel

_2nd-level Noble Genie Patron feature_

Your Patron gifts you a magic Vessel as a sign of your Pact. It appears as a Tiny container of your choice. It has hit points equal to twice your Warlock level and an Armor Class equal to your spell save DC. If it is lost or destroyed, it reappears next to you at the end of your next long rest. The Vessel can serve as a Trinket for one of your Eldritch Invocations.

Inside the Vessel is a 20-foot cube extradimensional space. As an action, you can hold a Medium or smaller object to its opening and store it inside. As an action, you can touch your Vessel and enter this space. You can remain inside for a number of hours equal to half your Warlock level.

You exit early by using an action or if the Vessel is destroyed. Whenever you exit, you can bring objects out of the space with you, and you appear in the Vessel’s space.

## Noble Magic

_2nd-level Noble Genie Patron feature_

Your Patron infuses your magic with its power. You can choose for Eldritch Blast to deal the damage type of your Noble Genie's Element equal to 1d10 + your Pact modifier.

You learn the spells in the following table at the Warlock levels shown. They count as Warlock spells for you, do not count against Spells Known, and cannot be replaced.

| Warlock Level | Spells                               |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| 2nd           | [[Sleep]], your Genie spell          |
| 3rd           | [[Enlarge-Reduce]], your Genie spell |
| 5th           | [[Fly]], your Genie spell            |

| 9th           | [[Arcane Hand]], your Genie spell    |

The first spell shown at each level is granted by every Noble Genie. Your choice of Dao, Djinn, Efreeti, or Marid determines the second spell.

## Ethereal Nature

_6th-level Noble Genie Patron feature_

Your elemental nature begins to physically change you. You gain Resistance to damage of your Noble Genie’s Element.

Once per turn, when you deal Element damage with Eldritch Blast, you can force the target to make a Charisma saving throw. On a failure, the target instantly switches places with you.

## Mystical Sanctuary

_10th-level Noble Genie Patron feature_

When you enter your Vessel, you can choose up to five willing creatures within 30 feet to enter with you. These creatures can remain inside for up to 1 hour, but they exit early if you leave, the Vessel is destroyed, or you use an action to eject them.

While inside the Vessel, creatures can complete a short rest in 10 minutes, following the normal limitations of short rests.

## Limited Wish

_14th-level Noble Genie Patron feature_

As an action, you can hold aloft your Vessel to cast one spell of 6th level or lower from any spell list. The spell must have a casting time of one action or one bonus action. Your Vessel replaces material components, including components with a gold cost.

Once you use this feature, you must finish three long rests before using it again.
