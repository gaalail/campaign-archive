# The Witch Patron

Witches require mentors; warlocks require patrons. For a Witch Patron warlock, your mentor and patron are one and the same: an immensely powerful archwitch who inducted you into their coven. Under the watchful, unblinking gaze of a special familiar, your patron guides your arcane development and shares their immense wellspring of power.

### Witchmark

Witchmarks for Witch Patron warlocks vary by mentor: family crests, arcane rings inked around fingers, or spectral chains coiling across arms to represent the pact covenant.

## Greater Familiar

_1st-level Witch Patron feature_

You learn the [[find-familiar]] spell and can cast it as a ritual (material costs ignored, casting time halved). Your familiar possesses unique traits:
- Intelligence is 10 or the beast's score, whichever is higher.
- Speaks all languages you do and communicates telepathically within 100 feet.
- You are not blinded or deafened when perceiving through its senses.
- It adds your Charisma modifier to its AC, and your Charisma modifier + proficiency bonus to its maximum HP.

## Coven Magic

_1st-level Witch Patron feature_

You can bond with one willing spellcaster within close proximity:
- When within 30 feet of each other, ritual casting time and material costs are halved.
- Members cannot be targeted by divination scrying spells (*arcane eye*, *scrying*, *clairvoyance*).
- Choose a shared spell known: when one member casts it, another can use their reaction to cast it as well without increasing spell level (1/long rest).

## Familiar Bond

_6th-level Witch Patron feature_

- Your familiar can use the Help action as both an action and a bonus action on the same turn (range 30 ft when helping you).
- Your familiar can make saving throws using your ability modifiers.
- If your familiar drops to 0 HP, you can expend a spell slot as an action to instantly restore it to full HP (1/long rest).

## Coven Bond

_10th-level Witch Patron feature_

Your coven expands up to three willing spellcasters:
- When within 50 feet of each other, members cannot be charmed or frightened, and gain advantage on Charisma saving throws.
- Members take **no damage** from friendly area-of-effect spells cast by fellow coven members (such as *fireball*).

## Final Familiar

_14th-level Witch Patron feature_

As an action, you empower your familiar for a number of minutes equal to your Charisma modifier:
- Familiar grows one size larger; speeds increase by 10 feet.
- HP becomes equal to your maximum HP; Charisma becomes equal to your Charisma.
- Familiar can make attacks, actions, bonus actions, and reactions on its own initiative.
- On its turn, it can cast any cantrip you know (benefiting from your character level and Eldritch Invocations).
