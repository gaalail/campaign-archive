Not all those who serve nature seek balance. Older, and more sinister forces are at work in the ancient forests. Eldest of all plant life, ancient trees and sacred groves remember an age when plants, not mortals, ruled creation. They are Primeval Growths, their power limited only by their immovable roots.

## Primeval Magic

_2nd-level Primeval Growth Patron feature_

Your Patron infuses your magic with its verdant power. You can choose for your Eldritch Blast to deal Poison, Piercing, or Slashing damage equal to `1d10` + your Pact modifier.

You also learn certain spells at the Warlock levels below. They are Warlock spells for you, but they don't count against your number of Spells Known, and they cannot be replaced:

## Primeval Grasp

_2nd-level Primeval Growth Patron feature_

You have been infused with a seed of elder verdant magic. You learn the *thorn whip* Cantrip. It is a Warlock spell for you, but it doesn't count against your Cantrips Known.

Moreover, if you have a creature Restrained by a Warlock spell or feature, you can use a bonus action to drain its life, gaining temporary hit points equal to your Pact modifier (a minimum of `1`) for each creature currently Restrained.

## Primeval Thirst

_6th-level Primeval Growth Patron feature_

Absorbing a foe's essence causes your body to become rough and treelike. When you damage a creature with *thorn whip*, you can gain temporary hit points equal to half the damage.

While you have temporary hit points from Primeval Thirst or Primeval Grasp, your Armor Class equals your Spell save DC, unless it was already higher.

## Verdant Blast

_6th-level Primeval Growth Patron feature_

Your magic conjures verdant growths. After you hit a creature with two or more beams of Eldritch Blast that deal Piercing, Poison, or Slashing damage, it must make a Strength saving throw or be Restrained until the start of your next turn. Huge and larger creatures have advantage on this saving throw.

## Arboreal Guardian

_10th-level Primeval Growth Patron feature_

You are now more plant than mortal. You gain Resistance to non-magical Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing damage.

Additionally, you no longer require food or drink, as long as you spend at least 1 hour in sunlight each day.

## Wrath of the Forest

_14th-level Primeval Growth Patron feature_

You can call upon the ancient roots of your primeval Patron. As an action, you can force creatures of your choice that are touching the ground within `30` feet to make a Strength saving throw. Huge or larger creatures have advantage on this save.

On a failed save, creatures are Restrained for 1 minute. As an action, a Restrained creature can make a Strength check against your Spell save DC to escape. You must concentrate on this effect, and while concentrating your speed is `0`.
