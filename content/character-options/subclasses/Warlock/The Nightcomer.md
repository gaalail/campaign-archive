# The Nightcomer

Individuals embark upon this grim path following an encounter with the otherworldly creature known as Davra, being Marked for the Nightcomers, or after direct exposure to the Pale. As Nightcomers progress, they gain fine metaphysical control over flesh and marrow—carrying their own excised hearts, grafting body parts harvested from fresh corpses, and dissolving into floating swarms of autonomous limbs.

## Marked Heart

_1st-level Nightcomer feature_

You have removed your own heart through dark rites, carrying the shriveled organ as an arcane focus while kept alive by the Nightcomer's curse:
- Use your heart as a spellcasting focus as long as it is within 30 feet.
- Calculate the origin range of your warlock spells from either your space or your heart's space.
- If destroyed or separated by more than 1 mile, it regrows after a long rest and requires a 1-hour extraction ritual.

## Pale-Touched

_6th-level Nightcomer feature_

While frightened or possessing Fear greater than or equal to half your Fear Threshold, add your proficiency bonus to damage dealt by your spells. Whenever you finish a short or long rest, gain temporary hit points equal to your current Fear.

## Flesh-Tailor

_10th-level Nightcomer feature_

Spend 1 minute to graft body parts from corpses deceased for less than 1 hour onto your form (up to your proficiency bonus in total body parts; 1 graft per short or long rest):
- **Sensory Organ (eye, ear, nose):** +2 bonus to Perception, Insight, or Investigation.
- **Leg:** Speed increases by 5 feet.
- **Natural Defense (scales, shell):** AC increases by 1.
- **Other Limb (arm, wing, tail):** +2 bonus to Acrobatics, Athletics, or Intimidation.

## Pale Swarm

_14th-level Nightcomer feature_

As an action for 1 minute, your body explodes into an autonomous swarm of floating limbs (Large swarm of Tiny creatures):
- Occupy other creatures' spaces and squeeze through any opening large enough for your skull.
- Resistance to bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing damage; cannot be grappled or restrained.
- Regain 2d8 hit points whenever you kill a creature in this form, assimilating its tissue (1/short or long rest).
