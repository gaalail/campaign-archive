For those with the willpower and time, arcane magic is a path to unimaginable power. Those who ascend these heights are known as Archmages, masters of magic. So wondrous is their power and strange their goals, they enlist Warlocks to aid in their esoteric schemes.

## Archmage Magic

_2nd-level Archmage Patron feature_

Your Patron infuses your magic with their own. Choose one type of damage dealt by one Warlock spell you know. You can choose for your Eldritch Blast beams to deal damage of that type equal to `1d10` + your Pact modifier on hit.

You also learn certain spells at the Warlock levels below. They are Warlock spells for you, but don't count against your Spells Known, and they can't be replaced:

## Arcane Apprentice

_2nd-level Archmage Patron feature_

You gain proficiency in Arcana, and learn one Wizard Cantrip of your choice. It counts as a Warlock spell for you.

Moreover, during each long rest, you can perform a 1-hour arcane Ritual to beseech your Archmage Patron for a portion of their secret knowledge and replace one of the following:

- The special damage type of your Eldritch Blast that you gained from Archmage Magic, with another damage type from one Warlock spell you know.
- One of your Archmage Magic spells with another spell of the same spell level from the Wizard spell list.
- The Wizard Cantrip that you learned through this feature above with another Wizard Cantrip of your choice.

## Astral Slip

_6th-level Archmage Patron feature_

You can channel the power of your patron to shield yourself from harm. When you take damage from a source you can see, you can use your reaction to teleport through the Astral Plane, taking no damage, then reappear in an unoccupied space of your choice that you can see within `30` feet.

You can use this reaction once per short or long rest at no cost, after which you must expend a Pact Magic spell slot.

## Destabilizing Blast

_6th-level Archmage Patron feature_

The arcane power of your Patron undoes the magic of others. When you deal damage with Eldritch Blast to a creature that is concentrating on a spell, that creature has disadvantage on its saving throw to maintain concentration.

## Spell Ward

_10th-level Archmage Patron feature_

Infused knowledge of the arcane allows you to resist magical effects. You gain Resistance to Force damage, and when you make a saving throw to resist a spell, you can use your Pact Ability for the saving throw.

You can use your Pact Ability for a saving throw in this way a number of times equal to your Pact modifier, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a long rest.

## Otherworldly Prison

_14th-level Archmage Patron feature_

When you hit a creature with an Eldritch Blast, you can forgo dealing damage to instead force it to make a Charisma saving throw. On a failure, it is shunted to a demiplane created by your Patron for up to 1 minute, where it is Incapacitated. You must concentrate on this effect as if concentrating on a spell.

When this ends, the creature returns to the unoccupied space nearest where it was shunted from, then it must make another Charisma saving throw. It takes `5d10` Force damage on a failure, and half as much on a success.

You can use this feature once per long rest at no cost, after that, you must expend a Pact Magic spell slot to use it again.
