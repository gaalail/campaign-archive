_2nd-level Otherworldly Patron feature_

You have entered a Pact with a powerful Fiend from the lower planes. Whether you struck a bargain, were coerced, or tricked your Patron, you are now linked to a being of absolute evil. Only the most powerful Fiends can serve as Patrons; archdevils, demon lords, and especially mighty servants of extraplanar evil are common.

## Fiendish Magic

Your Patron infuses your magic with infernal flame. You can choose for Eldritch Blast to deal Fire damage equal to 1d10 + your Pact modifier on a hit, ignoring Resistance to Fire damage.

You also learn the spells in the following table at the Warlock levels shown. They count as Warlock spells for you but do not count against your Spells Known, and you cannot replace them.

| Warlock Level | Spells                             |
| ------------- | ---------------------------------- |
| 2nd           | [[Command]], [[Hellish Rebuke]]    |
| 3rd           | [[Blindness-Deafness]], Flame Whip |
| 5th           | [[Bestow Curse]], [[Fireball]]     |
| 7th           | [[Blight]], [[Wall of Fire]]       |
| 9th           | [[Cloudkill]], Immolation          |

## Dark Vitality

_2nd-level Fiend Patron feature_

You draw vitality from death and pain. Whenever you reduce a hostile creature to 0 hit points, you gain temporary hit points equal to your Warlock level + your Pact modifier. You can also use this feature as a reaction when a hostile creature within 10 feet of you is reduced to 0 hit points.

## Accursed Flames

_6th-level Fiend Patron feature_

Your magic scours the souls of your foes. Whenever you deal Fire damage to a creature, it cannot regain hit points or gain temporary hit points until the start of your next turn.

## Dark Bargain

_6th-level Fiend Patron feature_

You sacrifice your vitality to alter fate. When you fail an ability check or saving throw, roll a d10 and add the result to your roll, reducing your hit point maximum by the same amount. This reduction is restored at the end of your next long rest.

## Fiendish Resilience

_10th-level Fiend Patron feature_

At the end of each short or long rest, choose one damage type. You gain Resistance to that type until the end of your next short or long rest. Damage you take from magical or silvered weapons ignores this Resistance.

## Hurl Into Hell

_14th-level Fiend Patron feature_

When you deal damage to a creature with Eldritch Blast, you can banish it to your Patron's domain until the end of your next turn. When it returns to the space it left or the nearest unoccupied space, it takes 6d10 Psychic damage if it is not a Fiend.

Once you use this feature, you must finish a short or long rest before using it again.
