Immortal guardians of divine treasures, Sphinxes live to test mortals who desire the relics of ancient deities. They are the masters of riddles and trials that reveal the true natures of those they put to the test. To an Elder Sphinx, time has no meaning, and the most powerful can influence the flow of time with their ancient magic.

## Divine Ward

_2nd-level Elder Sphinx Patron feature_

The power of your Patron prevents the unworthy from approaching. As an action, you can project a Divine Ward out to a `10`-foot radius. This Ward moves with you, it requires your concentration, and it lasts for up to 1 minute.

When a creature enters this Ward or starts its turn within it, you can force it to make a Strength saving throw, or suffer effects until the start of your next turn. On a failed save, its speed becomes `0`. On a success, it treats the Ward's area as difficult terrain.

You can use this feature once between each long rest at no cost, after that, you must expend a Pact Magic spell slot to use it again.

At certain Warlock levels, the radius of your Divine Ward increases: at 6th level (`15` feet), 10th level (`20` feet), and finally at 14th level (`30` feet).

## Time Slip

_6th-level Elder Sphinx Patron feature_

You can manipulate time in minor ways, like the great Sphinxes of legend. When you take the Dash action, you can cause time to stop temporarily for all creatures other than yourself, then move up to your movement speed without provoking opportunity attacks. Time resumes when you stop moving, and you can only move while time is stopped. You can take no other actions, bonus actions, or reactions.

You can use this action once per short or long rest at no cost. After that, you must expend a Pact Magic spell slot.

## Untethering Blast

_6th-level Elder Sphinx Patron feature_

You can assault a creature's very grasp on the passage of time. When you deal Radiant damage to a creature with Eldritch Blast, that creature has disadvantage on the first ability check or saving throw it makes using Strength or Dexterity before the start of your next turn.

## Eternal Guardian

_10th-level Elder Sphinx Patron feature_

The Sphinx you serve has blessed you with a semblance of its immortality. You gain the [[character-options/class-options/eldritch-invocations/Otherworldly Nature|Otherworldly Nature]] Invocation and it cannot be replaced.

If you already know the [[character-options/class-options/eldritch-invocations/Otherworldly Nature|Otherworldly Nature]] Invocation, you instead learn another [[character-options/class-options/eldritch-invocations/index|Eldritch Invocation]] of your choice.

## Inscrutable

_10th-level Elder Sphinx Patron feature_

Like your Patron, you have Immunity to Divination spells or effects that sense emotion or read thoughts if you choose to. Also, Insight checks made against you have disadvantage.

## Power of the Ancients

_14th-level Elder Sphinx Patron feature_

You gain empowered mastery over time, but only in your area of influence. While your Divine Ward is active, you can use a bonus action to cast a limited version of the [[spells/9th-level/Time Stop|time stop]] *(LL)* spell, as if cast at 7th-level. While time is stopped, you can only act and interact with creatures and objects within Divine Ward.

When the spell ends, Divine Ward ends as well. You can use this feature once per long rest.
