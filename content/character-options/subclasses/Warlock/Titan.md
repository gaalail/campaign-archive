At the dawn of time, the world was ruled by titanic creatures; powerful Giants, hulking elementals that would become the landscape itself, and legendary Titans, like the Tarrasque. In remote places of the world, these ancient beings sleep, their power dormant, waiting for an opportunity to wake again.

## Titan Magic

_2nd-level Titan Patron feature_

Your Patron has infused your magic with titanic power. You can choose for your Eldritch Blast to deal Thunder damage equal to `1d10` + your Pact modifier.

You also learn certain spells at the Warlock levels listed below. They are Warlock spells for you, but they do not count against your number of Spells Known, and they cannot be replaced:

## Colossal Form

_2nd-level Titan Patron feature_

As a bonus action, you can draw on your Patron's elder might to transform and increase in size, gaining these benefits:

- You gain temporary hit points equal to your Warlock level.
- Your size increases by one category. For example, if you are a Medium creature you would become Large.
- When you make a Strength-based ability check or saving throw, you add your Pact modifier to the roll.
- When you force a creature at least one size smaller than you to make a saving throw against a Titan Magic spell or Titan Patron feature, it has disadvantage on its roll.

Your Colossal Form lasts for 1 minute, but ends early if you are Incapacitated or you end it with a bonus action. Once you use Colossal Form, you must finish a short or long rest before you can use it again. If you have no uses left, you can expend a Pact Magic spell slot to transform again.

## Elder Soul

_2nd-level Titan Patron feature_

The elder magic of your titanic Patron enhances your vigor. Your maximum hit points increase by `2`, and they increase by `1` again whenever you gain a Warlock level.

You also learn to speak, read, and write either Primordial or Giant. Whenever you make a check when interacting with Elementals or Giants, your Proficiency Bonus is doubled.

## Mighty Blast

_6th-level Titan Patron feature_

The force of your titanic Patron is infused in every blast. If you deal Thunder damage to a creature two or more times in one turn with Eldritch Blast, it must make a Strength saving throw or be knocked Prone.

## Titanic Endurance

_6th-level Titan Patron feature_

You can channel the resilience of your Patron to reduce pain. You gain a special pool of dice, with a number of `d8`s equal to `1` + your Warlock level, that you can use to absorb incoming damage. This pool regains expended dice equal to your Pact modifier (minimum of `1`) at the end of a short rest, and all of your expended dice after a long rest.

When you take damage, you can use a reaction to expend any number of dice from this pool, and reduce the damage by the total dice roll + your Pact modifier.

## Stalwart Blood

_10th-level Titan Patron feature_

Your connection to your gargantuan Patron deepens. In your Colossal Form, you add your Pact modifier to all Constitution ability checks and saving throws, and you have Resistance to non-magical Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing damage.

## Legendary Behemoth

_14th-level Titan Patron feature_

You have mastered the ancient power of your Titanic Patron. When you use Colossal Form, you can choose to grow by up to two size categories, for example, from Medium to Huge.

Finally, when in Colossal Form, Titanic Endurance does not require your reaction to use, and you regain one of your Titanic Endurance dice at the start of each of your turns.
