You remember the dying: cold creeping up from your fingertips, the room's sounds draining away, and then a hand that wasn't there closing around your spirit. Maybe you led a life of good deeds with a part still to play, or carried a potential you never knew about, or lived in sin with no idea what earned the second chance. The angel named its terms, and when you accepted them it fused itself to your body and spirit. The wound that killed you has closed into a seam of light.

## Returned Magic

_2nd-level Returned Patron feature_

Your savior infuses your magic with celestial light. You can choose for your Eldritch Blast beams to deal Radiant damage equal to 1d10 + your Pact modifier on hit.

You also learn certain spells at the Warlock levels below. They are Warlock spells for you, but they don't count against your number of Spells Known, and they cannot be replaced:

| Warlock Level | Spells                                      |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| 2nd           | [[False Life]], [[Bless]]                   |
| 3rd           | [[Find Steed]], [[Zone of Truth]]           |
| 5th           | [[Mass Healing Word]], [[Spirit Guardians]] |
| 7th           | [[Death Ward]], [[Guardian of Faith]]       |
| 9th           | [[Mass Cure Wounds]], [[Raise Dead]]        |

## Angelic Ward

_2nd-level Returned Patron feature_

Your savior stands between you and harm. When you take damage from an attack or effect you can see, you can use your reaction to reduce that damage by an amount equal to your Warlock level + your Pact modifier.

You can use this feature a number of times equal to your Pact modifier (minimum of once), and you regain all expended uses when you finish a long rest. If you have no uses left, you can expend a Pact Magic spell slot to use this feature again.

## Sanctuary

_6th-level Returned Patron feature_

Your savior can pull you back to the place your spirit stood when it found you, a Sanctuary such as a heavenly room or a quiet mountaintop where nothing can harm you. Decide its look with your DM.

As an action, you vanish into your Sanctuary. You can't be targeted or affected by anything until the start of your next turn, when you reappear in the space you left, or the nearest unoccupied space, and regain hit points equal to your Warlock level + your Pact modifier.

You can use this feature once between each short or long rest at no cost. After that, you must expend a Pact Magic spell slot to use it again.

## Angelic Wings

_10th-level Returned Patron feature_

Your savior's wings unfold from your shoulder blades. As a bonus action, you manifest a pair of wings of light for 1 minute. While they are manifested, you have a flying speed equal to your walking speed and can hover, and the wings shed bright light in a 10-foot radius and dim light for an additional 10 feet. You can retract them early with a bonus action.

You can manifest your wings once in between each short or long rest at no cost. After that, you must expend a Pact Magic spell slot to manifest them again.

## Grace Beyond Death

_14th-level Returned Patron feature_

The grace that returned you once returns you again. When you are reduced to 0 hit points but not killed outright, your savior pulls you back instead. You drop to a number of hit points equal to twice your Warlock level, and light flares from the wound that would have ended you. Once you use this feature, you must finish a long rest before you can use it again.
