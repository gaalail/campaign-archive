The Wild Hunt is a strange phenomenon which takes hold of mortals, driving them to hunt whatever they perceive as their prey. Its source is unclear, but legends tell of raiding parties from the Feywild that draw wayward mortals into their hunt, or of individuals cursed by sinister magic during a full moon.

## Wild Hunt Magic

_2nd-level Wild Hunt Patron feature_

Your Patron empowers your magic with its wild ferocity. You can choose for your Eldritch Blast to deal Piercing or Psychic damage equal to `1d10` + your Pact modifier.

You also learn certain spells at the Warlock levels below. They are Warlock spells for you, but they don't count against your number of Spells Known, and they cannot be replaced:

## Spirit of the Hunt

_2nd-level Wild Hunt Patron feature_

When you deal damage to a creature, you can cause an intangible Spirit of the Hunt to swirl about the target, moving with it. This Spirit of the Hunt remains until you mark another creature, the target creature is slain, or you become Incapacitated. It grants you the benefits here:

- **Deadly Critical.** Both your weapon attacks and spell attacks against that creature score a critical hit on a roll of `19` or `20` on the d20.
- **Wild Smite.** When you hit it with a weapon or spell attack, you can expend one Pact Magic spell slot to deal bonus Psychic damage equal to `1d10` + `1d10` per level of your Pact Magic spell slot.
- **Otherworldly Pursuit.** Whenever you make an ability check to track the creature, you add your Warlock level to the result of your roll.
- **Uses.** You can use this feature a number of times equal to your Pact modifier (a minimum of `1`). You regain one use after a short rest, and all uses after a long rest.

## Wild Huntsman

_2nd-level Wild Hunt Patron feature_

The power of the Wild Hunt imbues you with enhanced skill. You gain proficiency in Survival, and you can use your Pact ability, in place of Wisdom, for Survival checks.

Also, whenever you deal Piercing or Psychic damage with an Eldritch Blast, it counts as a weapon attack.

## Hunter's Grasp

_6th-level Wild Hunt Patron feature_

The magic of your Patron thwarts those who would flee. Each time you hit a creature with Eldritch Blast, its speed is reduced by `10` feet until the start of your next turn.

If the creature is marked by Spirit of the Hunt, it also has disadvantage on attacks against creatures other than you until the start of your next turn, so long as you remain within `15` feet of it.

## Wild Madness

_6th-level Wild Hunt Patron feature_

When you start your turn within `15` feet of a creature marked by Spirit of the Hunt, you can give yourself over to the Hunt. When you do, you have advantage on attack rolls against that creature, but all attack rolls that target you have advantage until the start of your next turn.

## Predatory Resolve

_10th-level Wild Hunt Patron feature_

The fervor of the Wild Hunt heightens your predatory instincts. When the target of Spirit of the Hunt hits you with an attack, you can add your Pact modifier to your Armor Class against that attack as a reaction, possibly causing it to miss.

## Master of the Hunt

_14th-level Wild Hunt Patron feature_

You can call on your allies to join you in the Wild Hunt. When you mark a creature with Spirit of the Hunt, you can choose willing creatures within `30` feet (up to your Pact modifier). Their attacks now score a critical hit on a roll of `19` or `20` against that target.

Finally, the range of Hunter's Grasp and Wild Madness both double to `30` feet.
