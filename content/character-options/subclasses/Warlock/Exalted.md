You have entered a Pact with a powerful Celestial native to the Upper Planes. Whether your Patron sought you as an ally or you proved yourself worthy, your pact carries the responsibility of supporting good and opposing evil.

## Patron Magic

You can choose for Eldritch Blast to deal Radiant damage equal to 1d10 + your Pact modifier.

You also learn the spells in the following table at the Warlock levels shown. They count as Warlock spells for you, do not count against Spells Known, and cannot be replaced.

| Warlock Level | Spells                                  |
| ------------- | --------------------------------------- |
| 2nd           | [[Cure Wounds]], [[Guiding Bolt]]       |
| 3rd           | [[Flaming Sphere]], Restoration         |
| 5th           | [[Daylight]], [[Revivify]]              |
| 7th           | [[Guardian of Faith]], [[Wall of Fire]] |
| 9th           | Conjure Celestial, [[Flame Strike]]     |

## Celestial Light

_2nd-level Exalted Patron feature_

As a bonus action, choose one of your hands to shed bright light in a 20-foot radius and dim light for another 20 feet. You can use Celestial Light once between rests at no cost, or expend a Pact Magic spell slot to use it again. You can restore hit points using this feature as described in the source.

## Exalted Magic

_2nd-level Exalted Patron feature_

You gain the listed Exalted Patron spells. They count as Warlock spells for you, do not count against Spells Known, and cannot be replaced.

## Radiant Conduit

_6th-level Exalted Patron feature_

You gain Resistance to Radiant damage. While Celestial Light is active, you can expend additional uses to restore extra hit points and gain restorative benefits against conditions as specified by the source.

## Celestial Fortitude

_10th-level Exalted Patron feature_

You gain temporary hit points equal to your Warlock level after finishing a long rest and cannot be reduced below 1 hit point by Radiant damage while Celestial Light is active.

## Blinding Defiance

_14th-level Exalted Patron feature_

When a creature strikes you with an attack, you can reveal its location with holy light and force it to make a Constitution saving throw or be Blinded until the end of its next turn.
