Ancient emperors who sought to extend their mortal life turn to sinister entities, offering their eternal soul for preservation. Known as Mummy Lords, the tyrants of ages past are found deep in the ruins of dead empires. They imbue mortals with a fraction of their power, and look to rebuild their lost empires.

## Dead Knowledge

_2nd-level Mummy Lord Patron feature_

Your accursed Patron infuses you with ancient knowledge. You gain proficiency in either History or Religion. You can use your Pact Ability for checks with both skills, and when you do so, you can treat a d20 roll of `7` or lower as an `8`.

## Dread Curse

_2nd-level Mummy Lord Patron feature_

You utter curses in long-dead tongues. As an action, you can force one creature that can hear you within `60` feet to make a Wisdom saving throw. On a failure, it is Blinded, Deafened, and can't speak for 1 minute. It can repeat this saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on a success.

You can use Dread Curse a number of times equal to your Pact modifier (minimum of once). You regain one use after a short rest, and all uses after a long rest. If you have no uses, you can expend a Pact Magic spell slot to use it again.

## Scouring Blast

_6th-level Mummy Lord Patron feature_

The sands of your Patron's magic scour both flesh and spirit. When you deal Slashing damage to a creature with Eldritch Blast, it has disadvantage on the first saving throw you force it to make before the end of your next turn.

## Shifting Sands

_6th-level Mummy Lord Patron feature_

You can disappear into the sands when in danger. When you take damage, you can use your reaction to dissolve into sand, halving the damage you take, then reappear in an unoccupied space of your choice that you can see within `30` feet.

You can use this reaction a number of times equal to your Pact modifier (minimum of once). You regain one use after a short rest, and all uses after a long rest. If you have no uses, you can expend a Pact Magic spell slot to use it again.

## Canoptic Preservation

_10th-level Mummy Lord Patron feature_

Your Patron has begun to reveal the sinister secrets of their preservation. During a long rest, you perform a vile 1-hour Ritual where you remove one (or more) of your organs and seal each within a Canoptic Jar outside your body. For each Jar you create beyond the first, you expend one Pact Magic spell slot and cannot regain it while the Jar exists.

Each Canoptic Jar has an Armor Class equal to your Spell save DC and hit points equal to your Warlock level. At the end of your next long rest, the vile magic of all Canoptic Jars fades and they are destroyed, returning your organs to your body.

After you are reduced to `0` hit points (even if killed), then at the start of your next turn you can consume the magic of one Canoptic Jar, destroy it, and return to life with `1` hit point.

## Ancient Blasphemies

_14th-level Mummy Lord Patron feature_

As an action, you can speak vile ancient blasphemies, forcing creatures of your choice that can hear you within `30` feet to make a Wisdom saving throw. On a failure, they are Stunned until the start of your next turn.

You can use this feature once per long rest at no cost. After that, you must expend a Pact Magic spell slot to use it again.
