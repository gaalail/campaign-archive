You have entered the service of a paranoid tyrant known as a Beholder. Their origins are unknown, but their presence is in every major mortal settlement. Preferring to have others do their dirty work, they are known for employing Warlocks.

## Unblinking Magic

_2nd-level The Unblinking Patron feature_

You learn certain spells at the Warlock levels below. They become Warlock spells for you, but they don't count against your number of Spells Known, and they cannot be replaced:

| Warlock Level | Spells |
| :---: | :--- |
| 2nd | [[spells/1st-level/Chaos Bolt\|chaos bolt]] *(XGE)*, [[spells/1st-level/Ray of Sickness\|ray of sickness]] |
| 3rd | *arcane scorcher*, [[spells/2nd-level/Levitate\|levitate]] |
| 5th | [[spells/3rd-level/Clairvoyance\|clairvoyance]], *minute meteors (XGE)* |
| 7th | [[spells/4th-level/Arcane Eye\|arcane eye]], [[spells/4th-level/Dominate Creature\|dominate creature]] *(LL)* |
| 9th | [[spells/5th-level/Cone of Cold\|cone of cold]], [[spells/5th-level/Telekinesis\|telekinesis]] |

## Unblinking Eye

_2nd-level The Unblinking Patron feature_

When you enter this Pact, you manifest an Unblinking Eye, reminiscent of your Patron's, in the center of your forehead. You gain proficiency in Perception, and you use your Pact Ability for Perception checks with it.

You can use this Eye to produce Eldritch Blast beams or as a spellcasting focus for any Warlock spell that requires a spell attack roll. When you cast a spell or use Eldritch Blast in this way, roll a `d10` and its damage type changes to match the roll:

| d10 | Damage |
| :---: | :--- |
| 1 | Acid |
| 2 | Cold |
| 3 | Fire |
| 4 | Lightning |
| 5 | Poison |
| 6 | Psychic |
| 7 | Thunder |
| 8 | Bludgeoning |
| 9 | Necrotic |
| 10 | Force |

## Occular Ray

_6th-level The Unblinking Patron feature_

Once per turn when you hit a creature with your Unblinking Eye Eldritch Blast, you can force it to make a Constitution saving throw. On a failure, it suffers the corresponding effect or Condition listed below until the start of your next turn:

| Damage Type | Effect / Condition |
| :--- | :--- |
| Acid | Frightened of you |
| Cold | Speed becomes `0` |
| Fire | Charmed by you |
| Lightning | Cannot take reactions and can only use an action or bonus action |
| Poison | Poisoned |
| Psychic | Unconscious |
| Thunder | Moved up to `30` feet in a line, in a direction of your choice |
| Bludgeoning | Petrified |
| Necrotic | `2d10` bonus Necrotic damage |
| Force | Roll twice and choose an effect |

## Unsleeping Awareness

_10th-level The Unblinking Patron feature_

The power of your otherworldly Unblinking Eye has grown. You cannot be Surprised, even if you are Incapacitated or you are sleeping, as your Unblinking Eye remains ever alert.

Also, if you hit a creature with two or more beams of your Occular Ray Eldritch Blast, it has disadvantage on its save.

## Eye Tyrant

_14th-level The Unblinking Patron feature_

Once per long rest, you can use an action to cause your Unblinking Eye to emanate an antimagic zone in a `30`-foot cone, placing the area under the effects of an [[spells/8th-level/Antimagic Field|antimagic field]] spell. You can change its direction at the start of each of your turns.

This effect lasts for 1 minute. It ends early if you choose to end it at the start of your turn, or if you are Incapacitated.
