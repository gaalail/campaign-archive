Rather than forge a Pact with a single otherworldly entity, you have entered multiple minor Pacts with lesser entities. Using the many powers and desires of your entities, you gain varied abilities and are able to play their goals off one another.

## Collective Knowledge

_2nd-level Collective Patron feature_

The many varied entities which hold to your Pact allow you to shift the knowledge you have. You gain proficiency in Arcana, History, Nature, or Religion, or you learn to speak, read, and write one language of your choice.

During a long rest, you can perform a 1-hour Ritual and replace this skill proficiency or language with another option from the list above. You can forgo this benefit to replace one of your [[character-options/class-options/eldritch-invocations/index|Eldritch Invocations]] with another Invocation, so long as you meet its prerequisites.

## Many Magicks

_2nd-level Collective Patron feature_

Your many Patrons empower your magic with one of their powers. During each long rest, choose Cold, Fire, Lightning, or Poison. Until your next long rest, you can choose for each beam of your Eldritch Blast to deal that type of damage equal to `1d10` + your Pact modifier on hit.

In place of predetermined Pact Spells, you can choose any two 1st-level Warlock spells to be your 1st-level Pact Spells.

You choose two more Pact Spells in the same way again at 3rd, 5th, 7th, and 9th level in this class. The spells you choose must be Warlock spells equal to your Slot Level or lower.

## Alter Pact

_6th-level Collective Patron feature_

Due to the tension amongst your many Patrons, you can alter your Pact on the fly. As an action, you can spend a Pact Magic spell slot to change your Collective Knowledge proficiency or language, or to replace one Eldritch Invocation. Also, you can perform the Collective Knowledge Ritual during a short rest.

## Pact Bind

_6th-level Collective Patron feature_

The many magicks of your Pact inflict varied effects on foes. Once per turn, when you deal damage to a creature with your Eldritch Blast modified by Many Magicks, you can inflict the effect below that corresponds to the damage. This effect lasts until the beginning of your next turn:

| Damage | Effect |
| :--- | :--- |
| Cold | Its speed is halved |
| Fire | It takes a bonus `1d10` damage |
| Lightning | It cannot take reactions |
| Poison | It is Poisoned |

## Shatter-Pact Shield

_10th-level Collective Patron feature_

You can sacrifice your occult power to shield yourself. When you take damage, you can use your reaction to destroy one of your Trinkets and halve the damage you would take. However, you lose all the benefits of the Eldritch Invocation within that Trinket until the end of your next long rest.

## Master of Many Pacts

_14th-level Collective Patron feature_

You have mastered playing your many Patrons against one another. As a bonus action, you can do one of the following:

- Replace your Collective Knowledge skill proficiency or language with another option from that feature.
- Replace one Eldritch Invocation you know with another Eldritch Invocation you meet the prerequisites for. You cannot replace an Invocation you sacrificed with your Shatter-Pact Shield feature.
- Change the special damage type of your Eldritch Blast granted to you by your Many Magicks feature.
- Replace a Warlock spell you know of 1st-level or higher with another Warlock spell of the same level.

You can use this feature a number of times equal to your Pact modifier (minimum of once). You regain one use when you finish a short rest, and all uses after a long rest.
