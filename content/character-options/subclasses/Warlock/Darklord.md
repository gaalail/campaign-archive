You have entered into a Pact with a sinister ruler of the Shadowfell, imprisoned within a Domain of Dread and able to affect the outside world only through trusted servants. Your Darklord grants you a fraction of its umbral power.

## Patron Magic

You can choose for Eldritch Blast to deal Necrotic damage equal to 1d10 + your Pact modifier.

You also learn the spells in the following table at the Warlock levels shown. They count as Warlock spells for you, do not count against Spells Known, and cannot be replaced.

| Warlock Level | Spells                                |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| 2nd           | Hex, [[Inflict Wounds]]               |
| 3rd           | [[Blindness-Deafness]], [[Blur]]      |
| 5th           | [[Fear]], Enemies Abound              |
| 7th           | Accursed Touch, [[Phantasmal Killer]] |
| 9th           | [[Dream]], Enervation                 |

## Fell Curses

_2nd-level Darklord Patron feature_

Your concentration on Darklord Magic spells cannot be broken by taking damage. Attack rolls against a creature under a Darklord Magic spell score a critical hit on a d20 roll of 19 or 20. When you deal Necrotic damage to such a creature, you gain temporary hit points equal to half the damage, up to your Warlock level.

## Shadow Warrior

_2nd-level Darklord Patron feature_

You learn [[Armor of Shadows]] even if you do not meet its prerequisites, and this Invocation cannot be replaced. While Armor of Shadows is active, add your Pact modifier to Stealth checks made in dim light or darkness.

## Cursed Specter

_6th-level Darklord Patron feature_

When a Humanoid under a Darklord Magic spell dies, you can capture its spirit as a reaction. You can have one spirit at a time. As an action, consume it to summon a Specter at an unoccupied space within 10 feet. It uses your spell save DC and spell attack modifier, acts on your turn, and remains for 1 minute unless destroyed or you become Unconscious.

## Dread Mantle

_10th-level Darklord Patron feature_

When a creature under a Darklord Magic spell damages you, reduce the damage by your Pact modifier, minimum 1. You also add your Pact modifier, minimum +1, to death saving throws.

## Right Hand of Dread

_14th-level Darklord Patron feature_

When a creature under a Darklord Magic spell is slain, use your reaction to regain one expended Pact Magic spell slot instead of capturing it with Cursed Specter. You may expend a spell slot when using Cursed Specter to conjure a Specter directly; only one Specter can be conjured this way at a time.
