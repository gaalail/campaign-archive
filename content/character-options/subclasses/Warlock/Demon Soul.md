# Demon Soul

You have invited a fiend to dwell within you in a tense cosmic symbiosis. This fiend offers tremendous power, but occasionally demands that it be unleashed. Keeping its hunger satiated is an unending trial, but the rewards are terrifyingly potent.

## Dark Bargain

_1st-level Demon Soul feature_

Choose the nature of your internal fiend. As a bonus action, surrender control to the demon for 1 minute (2 uses per long rest):
- **Carnal Claw:** Arms sprout spines; gain 1d8 finesse slashing unarmed natural weapon. Advantage on Con saves, +2 damage on attacks, physical damage resistance, cannot cast spells.
- **Evil Eye:** Third eye opens on forehead. Gain [[eldritch-blast]] with +1 attack/damage, dealing an extra 1d6 fire damage on hit.
- **Flaming Soul:** Spectral flames shed 20 ft dim light. Cast 3 chosen wizard cantrips and 4 chosen 1st-level wizard spells using Charisma (roll d6 on cast; re-castable on a 6).

## Demon’s Domain

_6th-level Demon Soul feature_

Target a creature damaged this turn: it must succeed on a Charisma saving throw or you and the target are pulled into your demon’s personal demiplane to fight to the death (1/long rest):
- **Carnal Claw Arena:** Equalized size and range. HP becomes (10 × level), AC becomes 10, immune to all conditions.
- **Evil Eye Arena:** 200 ft × 5 ft hallway in dim light; target treats ground as difficult terrain. HP becomes (4 × level); critical hits on 19 or 20.
- **Flaming Soul Arena:** 20 ft radius ring dealing 1d6 damage per 5 ft moved. HP becomes (6 × level).

*Warning:* Slaying the target returns you with its corpse. If your demon soul is slain, your Essence shatters, reducing ability scores to 8 and causing character death.

## Abyssal Knowledge

_10th-level Demon Soul feature_

On completing a long rest, trade proficiency in two skills for two other skills, or swap one known warlock spell for another.

## Demonic Conquest

_14th-level Demon Soul feature_

You gain 4 uses of your transformation per long rest, plus:
- *Carnal Claws:* Gain 1d10 temporary hit points whenever you damage a creature with your claws.
- *Evil Eye:* Gain truesight out to 30 feet.
- *Flaming Soul:* Permanently choose one 2nd-level and one 3rd-level wizard spell to cast once during transformation.
