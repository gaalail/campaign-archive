# Way of the Specter

Monks of the **Way of the Specter** undergo harrowing initiation rituals to acquire their supernatural arts. Following years of physical conditioning, pupils undergo a death covenant ceremony overseen by mentors: disciples undergo clinical death, their souls briefly visiting the ethereal home of spirits before being revived by witch healers.

Those who return whole gain the ability to turn their physical bodies into conduits for spirits. By driving spiritual energy directly into the flesh and marrow of their opponents, they induce rapid exhaustion and siphon away the vitality and essence of their foes.

### Witchmark

Rather than a tattoo or sigil, a Specter monk's hands become their witchmark, twisting from prolonged contact with ethereal entities:

| d6 | Witchmark Description |
| :---: | :--- |
| **1** | Hands covered in burn scars that encircle in blue flames when channeling spirits. |
| **2** | Fingertips stained inky black, leaking dark fluid that creeps up to the elbows over time. |
| **3** | Hands translucent like cracked glass, shifting into vibrant stained glass patterns. |
| **4** | Transparent, mist-shrouded hands that perpetually waft cold vapors. |
| **5** | Disproportionately long, curved nails with metallic sheens resembling razor-sharp talons. |
| **6** | Glowing ceremonial runes carved into skin that spread up the forearms as covenant script. |

## Weakening Blows

_3rd-level Way of the Specter feature_

You summon spirits that coil around your fists and weapons. When you hit a creature with an attack, you can expend 1 ki point to force the target to make a Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, the creature's vitality is weakened, and it suffers **one level of exhaustion**.

You can expend 1 ki point per attack to repeat this effect, but you cannot inflict more than three levels of exhaustion on a single creature. At the end of each of the affected creature's turns, it sheds one level of exhaustion until none remain.

## Death Spiral

_6th-level Way of the Specter feature_

You can manipulate the spiritual energy lodged inside an enemy's anatomy. Whenever you hit a creature afflicted with at least one level of exhaustion with an unarmed strike, you deal **one additional damage die** of the attack for each level of exhaustion the target currently suffers.

## Siphoned Life

_11th-level Way of the Specter feature_

Whenever you use **Weakening Blows** to inflict a level of exhaustion on a creature, you immediately regain hit points equal to the damage roll of that attack.

You can use this feature a number of times equal to your Wisdom modifier (minimum of once), regaining all expended uses upon finishing a long rest.

## Siphoned Essence

_17th-level Way of the Specter feature_

You can inject spirits directly into a foe's metaphysical core. Choose one ability score to target other than Constitution. When you make an unarmed strike, you can expend 5 ki points to force your target to make a saving throw using the chosen ability score (with advantage if the target is not surprised).

On a failed save, roll your Martial Arts die: the chosen ability score is reduced by half the number rolled. If an ability score is reduced to 0, the creature automatically fails any ability check or saving throw relying on that score.

You must maintain concentration on this effect as if concentrating on a spell (up to 1 minute), though it cannot be dispelled by *dispel magic*. You can use this feature a number of times equal to your Wisdom modifier, regaining all uses on a long rest. Once used on a creature, you cannot target that creature again until you finish a long rest.
