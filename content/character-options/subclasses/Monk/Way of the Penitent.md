# Way of the Penitent

All mortals are fallible, and the Penitent is the first to admit their shortcomings. Viewing moral scruples with grave serenity, they scourge their own flesh to sharpen the mind and purge sin. Flesh is a crucible for the spirit, and by disciplining it relentlessly, they draw out the divine core within.

---

## Flagellation

_3rd-level Way of the Penitent feature_

You shed your spiritual transgressions through ritual self-discipline. When you take a long rest, you can ignore X points of Fear when determining if you exceed your Threshold. Upon finishing the rest, you suffer Xd8 slashing damage as penance.

## Scourge

_3rd-level Way of the Penitent feature_

You master the ritual scourge, which has the statistics of a whip and counts as a monk weapon for you:

- **Cleanse:** On your turn, you can spend 1 ki point and replace one attack to strike yourself or a willing ally with your scourge, dealing damage as normal. This removes the **charmed** or **frightened** condition from the target and makes it immune to that condition for 1 minute.
- **Flay:** When you have no remaining hit dice, you can spend 1 ki point as a free action to sharpen your strikes: until the end of your turn, all unarmed strikes and monk weapon attacks score critical hits on rolls of **19 or 20**.

## Penitent Soul

_6th-level Way of the Penitent feature_

When you fail a saving throw, you can expend one available hit die and add the roll to your total, potentially turning failure into success.

## Godfearing

_11th-level Way of the Penitent feature_

When you would drop to 0 hit points, you can expend 1 ki point to instead gain 4 Fear and immediately regain **4d8 hit points**.

## Confessional

_17th-level Way of the Penitent feature_

When you take a long rest, you can consecrate a temporary Haven with a sacred Shrine. The Shrine allows any ally resting within the haven to use your **Flagellation** feature to purge Fear and gain the haven's sanctuary.
