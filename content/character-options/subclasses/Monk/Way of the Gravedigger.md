# Way of the Gravedigger

Graveyards are meant to be a final resting place, but all too often, undead blaspheme against the natural order. As a Gravedigger, you are devoted to keeping the dead in the ground where they belong. You have learned to master the elements of earth and spirit and bend them to your will.

Motivated by deep personal encounters with death, former cemetery wardens, or those seeking atonement for putting people into an early grave, Gravediggers master the subterranean forces of soil and the ethereal presence of the departed to negate dark necromancy.

---

## Elemental Bind

_3rd-level Way of the Gravedigger feature_

After every long rest, choose whether you bind to the element of **Earth** or **Spirit** for the day:

- **Earth Step (Earth):** Spend 2 ki points to sink into the soil below, reappearing in an unoccupied space within 20 feet without provoking opportunity attacks.
- **Spirit Guide (Spirit):** Spend 2 ki points to contact a spirit for guidance, asking one question that can be answered with yes or no based on what it witnessed in life or nearby death.

In addition, you can spend 2 ki points to cast [[protection-from-evil-and-good]] without material components.

## Path of Earth and Spirit

_6th-level Way of the Gravedigger feature_

When you bind to an element, you unlock an advanced elemental transformation:

- **Form of Dirt (Earth):** As an action (or as a bonus action by spending 3 ki points), you transform into living dirt for up to 1 minute. When hit by a nonmagical weapon attack, you take only half damage. If you take more than 20 damage from a single attack, you must succeed on a Constitution saving throw or revert early (2 uses per long rest).
- **Spirit Aid (Spirit):** Spend 3 ki points to summon a spirit using the stat block of a **Specter**. It obeys your commands and vanishes when dismissed or reduced to 0 hit points.

In addition, you can spend 2 ki points to cast [[earth-tremor]] or [[detect-evil-and-good]].

## Trap Undead

_11th-level Way of the Gravedigger feature_

As an action, choose a point within 60 feet. Undead creatures within a 30-foot radius must make a Dexterity saving throw (DC = 8 + proficiency bonus + Wisdom modifier).

On a failed save, the earth swallows them: they become buried in the ground and are **incapacitated**. On a success, they fall prone. An incapacitated creature can make a Strength saving throw at the end of each of its turns, digging its way to the surface as prone on a success. You can use this feature twice per long rest.

## Negate Necromancy

_17th-level Way of the Gravedigger feature_

You touch an undead creature and attempt to liberate its trapped soul, negating the dark magic animating it. The target must make a Wisdom saving throw (DC = 8 + proficiency bonus + Wisdom modifier). On a failed save, the creature instantly **dies**. On a success, it is **stunned for 1 round**.

The number of ki points you expend determines the maximum Challenge Rating of the target creature:

| Ki Points | Maximum Undead CR |
| :---: | :---: |
| 3 | CR 1 |
| 4 | CR 2 |
| 6 | CR 3 |
| 8 | CR 4 |
| 10 | CR 5 |
| 12 | CR 6 |
