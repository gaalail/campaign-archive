# Coven Conclave (Guardian Ranger)

There are spirits that rule over nature, protecting the wilderness from outside threats. They are stewards of forests, deserts, snow-capped mountains, and all things that fall under the domain of nature, and their power is immeasurable. In order to enact their will upon the material world, these spirits lend their power to unique witches known as **Guardian Rangers**.

Guardian rangers commune with primal guardians—the **Tree-Horned Stag**, the **Snake of Many Faces**, and the **Six-Legged Wolf**—embodying their aspects and ultimately transforming into their colossal avatars in times of dire need.

### Witchmark

A guardian ranger’s witchmark shifts based on the spirit they revere: antlers etched across the forehead for the Tree-Horned Stag, opalescent scales along the limbs for the Snake of Many Faces, or white wolf fangs at the corners of the mouth for the Six-Legged Wolf.

## Devotee of the Guardians

_3rd-level Coven Conclave feature_

You devote yourself to serving the nature spirits that watch over the wilds. Choose one spirit in whose domain you have completed a long rest:
- **Tree-Horned Stag (Forests & Jungles):** When you use your action to cast a spell, you can make one weapon attack as a bonus action. You can use this feature a number of times equal to your Wisdom modifier, regaining all uses on a long rest.
- **Snake of Many Faces (Deserts & Caves):** Opalescent scales protect you. While your HP is below 50% of your maximum, your AC increases by 1. While your HP is below 15%, your AC increases by an additional 1.
- **Six-Legged Wolf (Tundras & Mountains):** While in combat, if you use at least 10 feet of movement to move directly toward a target you attack, you have advantage on attacks against that creature.

## Gift of the Spirits

_3rd-level Coven Conclave feature_

Once per day, you can meditate with bare feet touching the earth for 10 minutes to commune with your guardian spirit. Make a check: roll a d20 + your proficiency bonus (with advantage if in the spirit’s native domain). On a result of 11 or higher, the spirit grants a boon lasting until your next long rest:
- **1:** Favorable weather throughout your journey.
- **2:** Foraging and hunting yields bountiful supplies.
- **3:** The spirit truthfully answers one yes-or-no question.
- **4:** Add a +1 bonus to ability checks using one skill of your choice.
- **5:** Path is clear of hostile random beast or plant encounters.
- **6:** Choose any one gift from options 1–5.

## Chosen of the Spirits

_7th-level Coven Conclave feature_

Your bond deepens, granting an enhanced boon (from your current spirit or another whose domain you've visited):
- **Tree-Horned Stag:** Learn any cantrip from the cleric or druid spell list to cast at will (Wisdom is your spellcasting ability), plus one additional ranger spell of your choice.
- **Snake of Many Faces:** When subjected to an effect allowing a Strength or Dexterity saving throw to take half damage, you take no damage on a success.
- **Six-Legged Wolf:** You have advantage on weapon attack rolls against a creature if at least one ally is within 5 feet of the creature and not incapacitated.

## Favored Domain

_11th-level Coven Conclave feature_

When fighting within your chosen spirit’s native domain:
- **Tree-Horned Stag:** Cast one 1st-level healing spell without expending a spell slot (1/long rest); maximize the healing of one spell (1/short or long rest).
- **Snake of Many Faces:** Automatically pierce all illusions; cast an altered *project image* (lasts 1 hour, appearing as yourself or a white serpent) without a spell slot (1/long rest).
- **Six-Legged Wolf:** Use the Help action as a bonus action; gain +10 ft speed, cold resistance, immunity to extreme cold, and normal vision in snowfall.

## Final Summoning

_15th-level Coven Conclave feature_

As an action, summon a spectral avatar of a chosen guardian for up to 3 rounds. Your size becomes Large, speed becomes 40 ft, HP is restored to maximum, and you become immune to being charmed, paralyzed, petrified, or knocked prone:
- **Tree-Horned Stag:** Wisdom +2; cast a leveled action spell and bonus action spell on the same turn; cast one free ranger spell per round; horn attack (reach 10 ft, +Wis+PB to hit, 2d8+Wis force damage).
- **Snake of Many Faces:** AC +2; resistance to nonmagical damage; dispel all illusions within 50 ft; tail attack (reach 10 ft, 2d8+mod force damage and grappled) and bite attack (1d12+mod force damage and poisoned).
- **Six-Legged Wolf:** Strength or Dexterity +2; movement doesn't provoke opportunity attacks; advantage on all attacks; pounce attack (2d8+mod force damage and knocked prone) and bite attack (1d12+mod force damage and frightened).

*Aftermath:* When the manifestation ends, you take psychic damage equal to half your maximum HP, your max HP is halved until a long rest, you suffer 1 level of exhaustion, and you must make a Wisdom saving throw against your own spell DC (minus rounds active) or fall unconscious. Recharges after 1d4 long rests.
