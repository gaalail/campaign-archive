# Vampire Hunter Conclave

Dedicated to hunting down and eradicating the risen dead, Vampire Hunters learn to craft an array of specialized tools and trick weapons designed to exploit the weaknesses of the profane. United by a singular decree—that the dead should remain buried—they relentlessly track down night-stalkers and clean the world of necrotic infestation.

---

## Hunter’s Tools

_3rd-level Vampire Hunter Conclave feature_

Craft two specialized tools during a long rest (each possessing a separate pool of uses equal to your proficiency bonus; gain a 3rd at 7th and 4th at 11th level):

- **Throwing Crucifix:** Bonus action ranged attack (range 30 ft, returns to hand), dealing 1d6 + Dexterity modifier slashing damage even against unseen targets.
- **Combat Trap:** Action to deploy a concealed trap within 5 feet. Triggering creature takes (PB)d4 piercing damage and has its walking speed halved.
- **Repeating Crossbow:** Hand crossbow without the loading property. When taking the Attack action, fire additional bolts equal to your proficiency bonus (+2 damage per extra bolt).
- **Scatter Spark:** Improvised thrown weapon (range 20 ft) blinding the target until the end of its next turn.

## Tool Expert & Profane Hunt

_7th-level Vampire Hunter Conclave feature_

- **Tool Expert:** Expend a spell slot to make tool damage magical for 1 minute and upgrade one tool:
  - *Throwing Crucifix:* Hits all creatures in a 5-foot radius; favored enemies suffer 1d4 bleeding damage per turn.
  - *Combat Trap:* Deals fire (igniting 1d4 damage/turn) or acid (disadvantage on first attack).
  - *Repeating Crossbow:* Extra attack on bonus action off-hand strikes.
  - *Scatter Spark:* Affects a 10-foot cone (Constitution save or blinded).
- **Profane Hunt:** Track favored enemies via Survival check (DC 5 + CR). Sense direction and distance within 24 hours, cannot be surprised by them, and add Wisdom modifier to damage rolls against the target.

## Enduring Hunter & Tool Master

_11th-level Vampire Hunter Conclave feature_

- **Enduring Hunter:** Gain necrotic resistance and advantage on saves against charm/fear from favored enemies.
- **Tool Master:** Upgrade your primary tool to master tier:
  - *Throwing Crucifix:* Action to strike a 30-foot line dealing (PB)d6 slashing damage.
  - *Combat Trap:* Triggers in a 5-foot radius; allies can trigger traps remotely via weapon strikes.
  - *Repeating Crossbow:* Action to pepper a 10-foot radius sphere dealing (PB)d6 slashing damage.
  - *Scatter Spark:* 15-foot cone; failed targets are dazed (can only take action, bonus action, or move).

## Master Hunter

_15th-level Vampire Hunter Conclave feature_

Bonus action to activate Profane Hunt on up to Wisdom modifier favored enemies for 1 minute: attacks score critical hits on **19 or 20**, you are immune to their charm and fear, and you perceive their exact locations within 30 feet even if invisible or obscured.
