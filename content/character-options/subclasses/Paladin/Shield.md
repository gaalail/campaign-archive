# Oath of the Shield

Divine Shields defend the innocent by standing between allies and danger. **Tenets:** Defend—protect those who seek aid; Immovable—keep your vows; Resilient—stand firm while innocents are at risk.

| Paladin level | Oath spells                      |
| ------------- | -------------------------------- |
| 3rd           | shield, shield of faith          |
| 5th           | aid, warding bond                |
| 9th           | protection from energy, tiny hut |
| 13th          | fire shield, resilient sphere    |
| 17th          | circle of power, wall of stone   |

**Shimmering Ward (3rd, Channel Divinity, 4 Resolve).** As an action, project a 10-foot-radius shimmering aura that lasts until you move. Creatures you choose in it gain half cover.

**Holy Abjuration (3rd, Channel Divinity, 2 Resolve).** When a spell or magical effect damages you, use a reaction to reduce it by Charisma modifier (minimum 1) + Paladin level. If reduced to 0, dispel the entire spell or effect.

**Aura of Defense (7th).** When another creature in your aura is hit, use a reaction to add Charisma modifier to its AC against the attack.

**Adamant Stance (15th).** A creature trying to move you against your will must succeed on a Strength check, DC 10 + Paladin level + Charisma modifier (minimum 1).

**Divine Bulwark (20th).** As a bonus action, empower your aura for 1 minute: at the start of your turn distribute 20 temporary hit points among living creatures in it; creatures you choose gain three-quarters cover; whenever a creature in the aura takes damage, you can take it instead.
