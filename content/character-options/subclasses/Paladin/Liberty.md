# Oath of Liberty

Liberty paladins stand with the oppressed against tyranny and seek justice fairly applied. **Tenets:** Equality—treat all equally; Equity—ensure access to basic needs; Freedom—free the oppressed and bring justice to oppressors.

| Paladin level | Oath spells                             |
| ------------- | --------------------------------------- |
| 3rd           | heroism, shield of faith                |
| 5th           | prayer of healing, warding bond         |
| 9th           | life transference XGE, revivify         |
| 13th          | aura of purity, freedom of movement     |
| 17th          | mass cure wounds, skill empowerment XGE |

**Admonish the Tyrannical (3rd, Channel Divinity, 4 Resolve).** When a creature you see within 30 feet damages another creature, use a reaction to rebuke it. For 1 minute or until concentration ends, it has disadvantage on attacks that don't target you.

**Shield the Meek (3rd, Channel Divinity, 2 Resolve).** When a creature you see within 30 feet is hit, use a reaction to add Charisma modifier (minimum +1) to its AC against the attack.

**Aura of Solidarity (7th).** When an ally in your aura takes damage, reduce it by Charisma modifier (minimum 1).

**Liberator (15th).** As a bonus action on each turn, let one creature in your aura repeat a save to end one hostile effect affecting it.

**Shield of Freedom (20th).** As a bonus action, transform for 1 minute: gain flying speed equal to walking speed; once per turn when damaging a creature, choose another creature in your aura to regain hit points equal to half the damage dealt; use Shield the Meek as a reaction each turn without Resolve.
