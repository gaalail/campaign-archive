# Oath of Prosperity

Prosperity paladins pursue material abundance, viewing commerce, trade, and economic thriving as the essential lifeblood of civilization.

> [!tenets] Sacred Tenets of Prosperity
> - **Analyze the Benefit:** Calculate gains and consequences before taking action; let wisdom guide your ventures.
> - **Make Every Action Pay:** Invest time, resolve, and resources where they yield tangible prosperity for your allies.
> - **Profit:** Build lasting wealth for your community and never enter a ruinous or fraudulent deal.

See the shared [[character-options/subclasses/Paladin/index#oath-smites|Oath Smite rules]] and [[character-options/subclasses/Paladin/index#avatar-terms|Avatar terms]].

**Distilled Fortune (3rd, Channel Divinity, 4 Resolve).** As an action, touch a Large or smaller nonmagical object not worn or carried with your holy symbol, transforming it into a Tiny gemstone worth the same amount. As an action, touch the gem with your holy symbol to restore the original object.

**Vault of Prosperity (3rd, Channel Divinity, 2 Resolve).** When casting a Paladin spell with a material component with stated gold cost, spend 2 Resolve to reduce the total cost by 50 gp per Paladin level.

**Aura of Prosperity (7th).** Once per turn per creature, when a creature you choose in your aura is targeted by a spell restoring hit points or granting temporary hit points, or by Lay on Hands, add Charisma modifier (minimum +1) to the amount granted.

**Fortunate Defense (15th).** When damaged by a creature you see, use a reaction to destroy a Distilled Fortune gemstone and reduce the damage by 1d10 per 100 gp of its value.

**Golden Guardian (20th).** As a bonus action, transform for 1 minute: gain temporary hit points equal to twice your level; as a bonus action grant a creature within 30 feet temporary hit points equal to your Charisma modifier; while you or another creature has these temporary hit points, you have a golden sheen and resist bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing damage.
