# Oath of the Throne

You serve your sovereign, uphold their laws, and protect their lands and subjects. **Tenets:** Bravery—carry out orders despite overwhelming odds; Loyalty—serve your liege's interests; Order—uphold the law as civilization's bedrock.

**Challenging Smite (3rd, Oath Smite, radiant).** Wisdom save or disadvantage on attacks against creatures other than you until the end of its next turn.

**Rallying Command (3rd, Channel Divinity, 4 Resolve).** As a bonus action, a creature within 30 feet that can hear and understand you gains temporary hit points equal to 1d6 + your Charisma modifier.

**Oathbound Guard (7th).** When a creature in your aura takes damage, use your reaction to take that damage instead. It can't be reduced, and non-damage effects don't transfer.

**Unwavering Knight (15th).** While conscious, you have advantage on saves to resist paralyzed and stunned.

**Avatar of Loyalty (20th).** Gain resistance to bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing damage. Creatures under your aura have advantage on Wisdom saves and death saves. A creature that fails its save against Challenging Smite also can't willingly move more than 30 feet away from you until its next turn ends.
