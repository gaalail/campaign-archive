# Sacred Oaths, Part 2

The Channel Divinity oaths, plus Zealot and Templar, converted for the revised Paladin. Part 1 rules apply: Oath spells are Paladin spells for you, don't count against Spells Known, and can't be replaced; Resolve options are Channel Divinity options with listed costs and no other limit; Oath Smites use the shared tier-1 damage and rider rules; no alignment references; and Avatar terms apply. Channel Divinity costs are 4 Resolve unless a feature lists another cost. Spell source marks such as `XGE`, `*`, and `ll` are retained as supplied. The meaning of `ll` is the laserllama Spell Compendium, per the archive's existing spell-list note; listed entries whose versions exceed the normal slot progression still need their source-version levels confirmed.

## Oath of Beauty

Paladins of Beauty strive for excellence and a gleaming presence, inspiring the downtrodden or manipulating those they consider beneath them. **Tenets:** Grace—act with quiet dignity; Perfection—improve combat skill and social standing; Reputation—uphold the highest social standard.

| Paladin level | Oath spells                   |
| ------------- | ----------------------------- |
| 3rd           | charm person, disguise self   |
| 5th           | enthrall, suggestion          |
| 9th           | fear, hypnotic pattern        |
| 13th          | charm monster XGE, compulsion |
| 17th          | dominate person, seeming      |

**Otherworldly Elegance (3rd, Channel Divinity, 4 Resolve).** As an action, touch your holy symbol to enhance your appearance with divine glamour for 1 hour. Treat a 9 or lower on the d20 as a 10 on Charisma (Deception) and Charisma (Persuasion) checks.

**Quell the Defiler (3rd, Channel Divinity, 4 Resolve).** When a creature you see within 30 feet attacks you, use a reaction to force a Wisdom save. On failure, its attack misses and it is charmed by you until the end of your next turn or until you attack it.

**Aura of Elegance (7th).** Creatures you choose in your aura have disadvantage on saves against your illusion or enchantment spells. You have advantage on Charisma (Deception) and Charisma (Persuasion) checks against creatures in the aura while not fighting them.

**Beauty Revealed (15th).** When hit by an attack, use a reaction to add Charisma modifier (minimum +1) to AC against it. Uses per long rest equal Charisma modifier (minimum 1).

**Paragon of Beauty (20th).** As a bonus action, reveal your divine beauty for 1 minute: immune to charmed; weapon hits deal extra damage equal to Charisma modifier; cast charm person as a 1st-level spell as a bonus action each turn, including this turn, without a slot.

## Oath of the Blade

Blade Masters dedicate their lives to martial mastery. **Tenets:** Fair Play—level the field for a fair fight; Respect—respect every fellow warrior, even a bitter foe.

**Sacred Duel (3rd, Channel Divinity, 4 Resolve).** As a bonus action, begin a 1-minute duel with a willing creature within 30 feet, ending if either duelist becomes unconscious. Duelists resist all damage from others, and can target only each other with attacks, spells, and features.

**Blade Paths (3rd).** Learn one additional Smite Path. Your 11th-level Second Smite Path feature grants a third path.

**Honed Edge (7th).** When rolling a smite's extra damage dice, reroll any 1s or 2s once and use the new rolls.

**Skillful Counter (15th).** When a creature you see hits you with a melee attack, use a reaction to make one melee attack against it with advantage. On a hit, you can expend a spell slot to smite it.

**Warrior of Legend (20th).** As a bonus action, enter a 1-minute battle trance, ending if incapacitated. Once on each of your turns when you hit with a melee weapon attack, use a smite you know whose lowest slot is 4th level or lower without a slot, treating it as a 5th-level slot.

## Oath of the Corsair

Corsairs are fearsome sea warriors devoted to personal freedom and loyalty to their crew. **Tenets:** Bravery—live so others sing of your deeds; Freedom—be free of rules you did not agree to; Loyalty—reward loyalty and judge traitors ruthlessly.

| Paladin level | Oath spells                      |
| ------------- | -------------------------------- |
| 3rd           | fog cloud, zephyr strike XGE     |
| 5th           | arcane lock, warding wind XGE    |
| 9th           | thunder step XGE, tidal wave XGE |
| 13th          | control water, storm sphere XGE  |
| 17th          | control winds XGE, maelstrom XGE |

**All Hands on Deck (3rd, Channel Divinity, 4 Resolve).** As a bonus action, choose up to your Charisma modifier creatures within 30 feet who can see or hear you. Each can use its reaction to move up to its speed without provoking opportunity attacks.

**Wrath of the Sea (3rd, Oath Smite, cold, lightning, or thunder).** Choose the damage type. Treat any 1 or 2 on the smite's damage dice as 3.

**Aura of Gallantry (7th).** You and creatures you choose in your aura have advantage on saves and ability checks to resist being grappled, knocked prone, or otherwise moved against your will.

**Fury of the Seas (15th).** When a creature you see hits you with a melee attack, it takes your choice of cold, lightning, or thunder damage equal to your Charisma modifier (minimum 1).

**Grand Captain (20th).** As a bonus action, transform for 1 minute: immune to cold, lightning, and thunder; cast command at 1st level as a bonus action each turn without a slot; when you hit a Huge or smaller creature with a melee attack, it must succeed on a Strength save or be knocked prone.

## Oath of the Exorcist

Exorcists hunt the undead and protect the living, sacrificing ordinary lives to defend the world. **Tenets:** Defend—protect life, especially the innocent; Destroy—hunt and destroy undead; Focus—bear no titles, lands, family, or children.

| Paladin level | Oath spells                              |
| ------------- | ---------------------------------------- |
| 3rd           | protection from evil and good, sanctuary |
| 5th           | invisibility, see invisibility           |
| 9th           | magic circle, spirit guardians           |
| 13th          | banishment, greater invisibility         |
| 17th          | dispel evil and good, holy weapon XGE    |

**Sinister Insights (3rd, Channel Divinity, 4 Resolve).** As an action, grasp your holy symbol to gain advantage for 1 hour on ability checks and saving throws related to undead or necromancy magic.

**Warrior of the Dawn (3rd, Channel Divinity, 4 Resolve).** As a bonus action, choose a creature within 30 feet. For 1 minute, each time you deal damage to it, deal an extra 1d6 radiant damage.

**Spectral Form (7th).** As a bonus action, become spectral until the end of your turn: resist bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing damage from nonmagical or non-silvered attacks; move through objects and creatures as difficult terrain. If you end inside one, you are shunted to the nearest free space and take 1d10 force damage per 5 feet traveled. Once per short or long rest; when out of uses, expend a 2nd-level or higher slot to use it again. At 18th level, use each turn without a resource.

**Scouring Strike (15th).** When you deal radiant damage to a creature, it can't regain hit points or become incorporeal until your next turn starts.

**Sacrificial Offering (20th).** When reduced to 0 hit points but not killed outright, spend one Hit Die to drop to 1 hit point instead.

## Oath of the Forge

Forge paladins pursue progress and order through a chosen craft. **Tenets:** Order—create order from disorder; Perfection—strive for perfection; Progress—pass knowledge on so successors surpass you.

| Paladin level | Oath spells                              |
| ------------- | ---------------------------------------- |
| 3rd           | identify, burning hands                  |
| 5th           | heat metal, magic weapon                 |
| 9th           | elemental weapon, protection from energy |
| 13th          | fabricate, wall of fire                  |
| 17th          | animate objects, creation                |

**Molten Weapon (3rd, Channel Divinity, 4 Resolve).** As a bonus action, imbue a melee weapon for 1 minute; it deals an extra 1d6 fire damage on a hit.

**Ironworks (3rd, Channel Divinity, 4 Resolve).** As an action, touch your holy symbol to a Medium or smaller nonmagical metal object and reshape it. A shape requiring skill or training requires proficiency with the relevant artisan's tools.

**Aura of Iron (7th).** Metal weapons and armor of your choice in your aura gain masterful quality: armor grants +1 AC to its wearer; mundane weapons count as magical.

**Unyielding Steel (15th).** Once per turn when damaged while wearing heavy armor, use a reaction to halve the damage. Uses per short or long rest equal Charisma modifier (minimum 1).

**Champion of the Forge (20th).** As a bonus action, transform for 1 minute, ending if incapacitated: Aura of Iron grants allies resistance to bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing; use Unyielding Steel each turn without expending a use; your fire damage ignores resistance and treats immunity as resistance.

## Oath of Inquisition

Inquisitors reveal what is hidden and punish those who deceive mortals by obscuring their true form. **Tenets:** Truth—bring truth to light; Immutability—distrust those who alter or hide their form; Retribution—fit punishment to the crime.

| Paladin level | Oath spells                   |
| ------------- | ----------------------------- |

| 5th           | moonbeam, zone of truth       |
| 9th           | clairvoyance, magic circle    |
| 13th          | arcane eye, charm monster XGE |
| 17th          | legend lore, scrying          |

**Divine Interrogation (3rd, Channel Divinity, 4 Resolve).** As an action, choose a creature within 10 feet that can see or hear you. Charisma save or you learn its true name, and it has disadvantage on the first Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma save you force it to make within 1 minute.

**Surprise Inquisition (3rd, Channel Divinity, 4 Resolve).** When rolling initiative, if not surprised or incapacitated, use a reaction to choose up to three creatures that can see, hear, and understand you. Wisdom save or disadvantage on attack rolls and no reactions until the end of its first turn in the encounter.

**Aura of Truth (7th).** Creatures you choose in your aura have disadvantage on Charisma (Deception) checks and advantage on Wisdom (Insight) checks; choose which effects apply.

**Iron Will (15th).** Immune to charmed and enchantment spell effects.

**Grand Inquisitor (20th).** As a bonus action, transform for 1 minute: gain truesight to 120 feet; instantly know if a creature is lying to you; a shapechanger hit by your attack reverts to its original form and takes 2d8 extra radiant damage.

## Oath of Liberty

Liberty paladins stand with the oppressed against tyranny and seek justice fairly applied. **Tenets:** Equality—treat all equally; Equity—ensure access to basic needs; Freedom—free the oppressed and bring justice to oppressors.

| Paladin level | Oath spells                             |
| ------------- | --------------------------------------- |

| 5th           | prayer of healing, warding bond         |
| 9th           | life transference XGE, revivify         |

| 17th          | mass cure wounds, skill empowerment XGE |

**Admonish the Tyrannical (3rd, Channel Divinity, 4 Resolve).** When a creature you see within 30 feet damages another creature, use a reaction to rebuke it. For 1 minute or until concentration ends, it has disadvantage on attacks that don't target you.

**Shield the Meek (3rd, Channel Divinity, 2 Resolve).** When a creature you see within 30 feet is hit, use a reaction to add Charisma modifier (minimum +1) to its AC against the attack.

**Aura of Solidarity (7th).** When an ally in your aura takes damage, reduce it by Charisma modifier (minimum 1).

**Liberator (15th).** As a bonus action on each turn, let one creature in your aura repeat a save to end one hostile effect affecting it.

**Shield of Freedom (20th).** As a bonus action, transform for 1 minute: gain flying speed equal to walking speed; once per turn when damaging a creature, choose another creature in your aura to regain hit points equal to half the damage dealt; use Shield the Meek as a reaction each turn without Resolve.

## Oath of Mysticism

Mystics awaken their own inner psionic power. Optional rule: use Intelligence instead of Charisma for all Paladin features. **Tenets:** Asceticism—forsake attachments; Enlightenment—realize potential and help others; Knowledge—seek deeper enlightenment through knowledge.

| Paladin level | Oath spells                      |

| 5th           | detect thoughts, levitate        |
| 9th           | clairvoyance, slow               |
| 13th          | confusion, phantasmal killer     |
| 17th          | telekinesis, wall of force       |

**Enlightened Spell (3rd, Channel Divinity, 4 Resolve).** When casting a Mysticism spell, grasp your holy symbol to cast without verbal or somatic components.

**Psionic Smite (3rd, Oath Smite, psychic).** Wisdom save or target's speed is halved until its next turn ends.

**Aura of Enlightenment (7th).** Creatures you choose in your aura resist force and psychic damage and have advantage on saves against charmed or frightened.

**Psionic Rebuke (15th).** When a creature in your aura hits you while you aren't incapacitated, it takes psychic damage equal to your Charisma modifier (minimum 1).

**Mystic Ascension (20th).** As a bonus action, become luminous for 1 minute: emit bright light in 5 feet and dim light 5 feet farther; gain flying speed equal to walking speed; become immune to psychic and resistant to nonmagical bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing; move through creatures and objects as difficult terrain, taking 1d10 force damage per 5 feet if shunted after ending inside one.

## Oath of Prosperity

Prosperity paladins pursue material wealth, measuring actions and lives by their value in gold. **Tenets:** Benefit—analyze gains before acting; Investment—make every action pay; Profit—never enter a bad deal.

| Paladin level | Oath spells                        |
| ------------- | ---------------------------------- |
| 3rd           | alarm, identify                    |
| 5th           | arcane lock, locate object         |
| 9th           | glyph of warding, tiny servant XGE |
| 13th          | fabricate, secret chest            |
| 17th          | animate objects, legend lore       |

**Distilled Fortune (3rd, Channel Divinity, 4 Resolve).** As an action, touch a Large or smaller nonmagical object not worn or carried with your holy symbol, transforming it into a Tiny gemstone worth the same amount. As an action, touch the gem with your holy symbol to restore the original object.

**Vault of Prosperity (3rd, Channel Divinity, 2 Resolve).** When casting a Paladin spell with a material component with stated gold cost, spend 2 Resolve to reduce the total cost by 50 gp per Paladin level.

**Aura of Prosperity (7th).** Once per turn per creature, when a creature you choose in your aura is targeted by a spell restoring hit points or granting temporary hit points, or by Lay on Hands, add Charisma modifier (minimum +1) to the amount granted.

**Fortunate Defense (15th).** When damaged by a creature you see, use a reaction to destroy a Distilled Fortune gemstone and reduce the damage by 1d10 per 100 gp of its value.

**Golden Guardian (20th).** As a bonus action, transform for 1 minute: gain temporary hit points equal to twice your level; as a bonus action grant a creature within 30 feet temporary hit points equal to your Charisma modifier; while you or another creature has these temporary hit points, you have a golden sheen and resist bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing damage.

**Shimmering Ward (3rd, Channel Divinity, 4 Resolve).** As an action, project a 10-foot-radius shimmering aura that lasts until you move. Creatures you choose in it gain half cover.

**Holy Abjuration (3rd, Channel Divinity, 2 Resolve).** When a spell or magical effect damages you, use a reaction to reduce it by Charisma modifier (minimum 1) + Paladin level. If reduced to 0, dispel the entire spell or effect.

**Divine Bulwark (20th).** As a bonus action, empower your aura for 1 minute: at the start of your turn distribute 20 temporary hit points among living creatures in it; creatures you choose gain three-quarters cover; whenever a creature in the aura takes damage, you can take it instead.

## Oath of the Zealot

Zealots are master orators and warriors whose strict faith makes heretics and infidels their mortal enemies. **Tenets:** Divine Might—prepare to fight your deity's enemies; God's Kingdom—rule with wisdom and strength; True Path—those who defy it are infidels.

| Paladin level | Oath spells                          |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| 3rd           | bane, detect magic                   |
| 5th           | flame blade, pass without trace      |
| 9th           | bestow curse, protection from energy |
| 13th          | confusion, freedom of movement       |
| 17th          | hallow, planar binding               |

**Dedicated Faithful (3rd).** When you would expend a spell slot to cast a spell or use a smite, you can spend 4 Resolve per slot level instead.

**Call of the Zealot (3rd, Channel Divinity, 4 Resolve).** As an action, choose creatures up to your Charisma modifier (minimum 1) that can hear you within 30 feet. Choose charmed, frightened, bless, or bane for each. Charm and fear last until the end of your next turn; bless and bane last 1 minute without concentration. Unwilling targets can make a Charisma save. At 10th level, double range and target count.

**Turn the Heretic (3rd, Channel Divinity, 4 Resolve).** As an action, enemies that don't worship your deity and can see or hear you within 30 feet make a Wisdom save. Failure: until the end of their next turn, they can't willingly move closer to you or take reactions.

**Eyes of the Faithful (7th).** When you or a friendly creature within 10 feet hits with a weapon attack, the target sheds bright light in 10 feet, is visible and can't benefit from invisibility, automatically fails Dexterity (Stealth) checks, and the next melee weapon attack against it has advantage, all until its next turn ends. Choose light color; you and allies may opt out.

**Will of the Zealot (15th).** Advantage on saves against enchantment and illusion spells.

**Shadow of the Divine (20th).** Reroll an ability check, attack roll, damage roll, or save after rolling and after the DM declares the result; choose which result to use. Uses per long rest equal Charisma modifier (minimum 2). A creature with truesight sees an immense ethereal hand of your deity reaching from the sky to protect and guide you.

## Oath of the Templar

Templars are temple-trained knights who withstand foes, aid allies, and comfort the faithful. Their temple may provide quests, tokens, and shelter. **Tenets:** Faith—let faith be your shield; Loyalty—let temple and allies be your weapons; Unwavering—do not fear the divine.

| 5th           | silence, zone of truth        |
| 9th           | daylight, spirit guardians    |
| 13th          | divination, guardian of faith |

**Hand of Temple (3rd, Channel Divinity, 4 Resolve).** As an action, shed bright light in a 30-foot radius. Up to five allies of your choice in the area regain 1 hit point at the end of each of their turns for 1 minute.

**Will of Temple (3rd, Channel Divinity, 4 Resolve).** As an action, shed bright light in a 30-foot radius. Each ally in the area can repeat a save of its choice within 1 minute, before the DM declares the first roll's result.

**Aura of Temple (7th).** You and creatures you choose in your Aura of Protection reduce damage from spell and weapon attacks by 2. At 18th level, reduction becomes 4.

**Temple's Favorite Soul (15th).** Always under heroism; it grants 7 temporary hit points at the start of each turn. This amount doesn't change.

**High Templar (20th).** Shed bright light in 10 feet and dim light 10 feet farther; opaque covering blocks it. As an action, once per long rest, emanate a mobile 30-foot-radius divine aura for 1 minute; enemies inside take -4 to ability checks, attacks, and saves.

## Part 2 notes

Channel Divinity costs default to 4 Resolve, with the listed 2-Resolve defensive options and Vault of Prosperity. Corsair's Wrath of the Sea and Mysticism's Psionic Smite are Oath Smites. Molten Weapon no longer has a smite clause and instead gives a flat 1d6 fire damage for 1 minute. Divine Aegis became Shimmering Ward; Sundering Strike became Scouring Strike. Forge replaces searing smite with burning hands. Dedicated Faithful replaces Faith Points with Resolve at 4 per slot level. Call of the Zealot and Turn the Heretic riders end by the end of the target's next turn; Eyes of the Faithful grants advantage only to the next melee attack. Surprise Inquisition now limits its control to the creature's first turn and no longer removes a turn. Prosperity's aura also improves Lay on Hands once per turn per creature. Aura of Temple uses the Paladin Aura of Protection. Corsair's flavor avoids alignment. Blade is rebuilt as a smite specialist; Superiority Dice and Maneuvers are removed.
