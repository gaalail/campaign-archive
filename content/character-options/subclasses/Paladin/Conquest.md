# Oath of Conquest

Conquerors seek glory through absolute domination, crushing adversaries in body and spirit and imposing order through iron justice.

> [!tenets] Sacred Tenets of Conquest
> - **Douse the Flame of Hope:** Crush your enemies in body and spirit so that they never harbor the will to oppose you again.
> - **Iron Law:** Your word is law. Reward fierce loyalty, root out sedition, and punish dissent with unwavering resolve.
> - **Strength Above All:** Might makes right, and you must remain the mightiest force in the realm.

See the shared [[character-options/subclasses/Paladin/index#oath-smites|Oath Smite rules]] and [[character-options/subclasses/Paladin/index#avatar-terms|Avatar terms]].

**Conquering Smite (3rd, Oath Smite, psychic).** Wisdom save or frightened of you until the end of its next turn.

**Lethal Strike (3rd, Channel Divinity, 2 Resolve).** Once per turn before you know whether a weapon attack hits, add twice your Charisma modifier to its attack roll.

**Aura of Dominion (7th).** Creatures you choose in your aura treat the area as difficult terrain. A creature frightened of you has its speed halved while in the aura. Total cover blocks this effect.

**Ruthless Reprisal (15th).** When a creature in your aura damages you or forces a save, it takes damage equal to your Charisma modifier (minimum 1), of a type you could choose for Righteous Strikes.

**Avatar of Conquest (20th).** Gain resistance to all damage. At the start of each turn, one creature in your aura must succeed on a Wisdom save or be frightened of you until the start of your next turn. Whenever you take the Attack action, make one additional weapon attack as part of it.
