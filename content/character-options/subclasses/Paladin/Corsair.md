# Oath of the Corsair

Corsairs are fearsome sea warriors devoted to personal freedom and loyalty to their crew. **Tenets:** Bravery—live so others sing of your deeds; Freedom—be free of rules you did not agree to; Loyalty—reward loyalty and judge traitors ruthlessly.

**All Hands on Deck (3rd, Channel Divinity, 4 Resolve).** As a bonus action, choose up to your Charisma modifier creatures within 30 feet who can see or hear you. Each can use its reaction to move up to its speed without provoking opportunity attacks.

**Wrath of the Sea (3rd, Oath Smite, cold, lightning, or thunder).** Choose the damage type. Treat any 1 or 2 on the smite's damage dice as 3.

**Aura of Gallantry (7th).** You and creatures you choose in your aura have advantage on saves and ability checks to resist being grappled, knocked prone, or otherwise moved against your will.

**Fury of the Seas (15th).** When a creature you see hits you with a melee attack, it takes your choice of cold, lightning, or thunder damage equal to your Charisma modifier (minimum 1).

**Grand Captain (20th).** As a bonus action, transform for 1 minute: immune to cold, lightning, and thunder; cast command at 1st level as a bonus action each turn without a slot; when you hit a Huge or smaller creature with a melee attack, it must succeed on a Strength save or be knocked prone.
