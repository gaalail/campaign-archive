# Oath of the Hellbound

Having clawed their way from the very pits of the inferno, the Hellbound walks a razor-thin line between zeal and sacrilege; empowered to act by the brand of the Hells upon their soul. Through sheer will and command of darker forces, these warriors supplant the divine with the profane, seeking ever to redeem themselves from past sins and break the infernal chains binding others.

---

> [!tenets] Sacred Tenets of the Hellbound
> - **Forgiveness:** I will make good on my past sins and will not repeat the mistakes that damned me to Hell.
> - **Freedom:** Neither I nor any other creature will be bound against their will to another in infernal servitude.
> - **Vile Fiends:** Fiends cannot be trusted; bargaining with them is damnation, a fate I will not suffer again.
> - **Redemption:** There is hope in even the darkest souls. Anyone can be redeemed if they possess the will.
> - **Second Chances:** All mortals are worthy of a second chance—but never a third.

## Channel Divinity

_3rd-level Oath of the Hellbound feature_

- **Flameblade:** As a bonus action, you wreathe a melee weapon in righteous hellfire for 1 minute. It deals fire damage using your Charisma modifier for attack and damage rolls, counts as magical, deals an extra 1d6 fire damage, and ignores fire resistance.
- **Fuel the Flames:** As an action, expend a spell slot of a level equal to or greater than half your proficiency bonus (rounded up) to regain one expended use of your Channel Divinity (1/short or long rest).

## Tainted by Fire

_3rd-level Oath of the Hellbound feature_

Your Divine Smites deal fire damage instead of radiant. Any creature you smite must make a Dexterity saving throw against your spell save DC. On a failure, the creature is set ablaze for 1 minute, taking 1d6 fire damage (ignoring resistance) at the start of each of its turns until it takes an action to extinguish the flames.

## Hellfire

_7th-level Oath of the Hellbound feature_

Fire damage you deal while Flameblade is active reduces damage immunity to resistance. You score critical hits on rolls of **19 or 20** against any creature set ablaze.

Additionally, any ablaze creature within your melee reach provokes a free opportunity attack (requiring no reaction) when leaving your reach; on a hit, its speed is reduced to 0 until the start of its next turn.

## Redemptive Strike

_15th-level Oath of the Hellbound feature_

Whenever you hit a creature that is set ablaze, you can choose to make the attack an automatic **critical hit**. Fire damage dealt with a Redemptive Strike ignores both resistance and immunity (2 uses per long rest).

## Ascension

_20th-level Oath of the Hellbound feature_

As an action, you encircle yourself in hellfire and assume the mantle of a hellish archon for 1 minute:
- Flameblade activates automatically, with its bonus fire damage increasing to **2d6**.
- An aura of flames extends in a 30-foot radius. Any creature entering or starting its turn within must make a Dexterity save or be set ablaze, taking 10 fire damage at the start of each turn.
- You become immune to fire damage and can use your reaction when subjected to fire damage to heal for half the damage inflicted.
- You have advantage on saving throws against the spells and abilities of fiends and undead.
