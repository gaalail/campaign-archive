# Oath of Vigilance

You protect the material plane from extraplanar influence, whatever its nature. **Tenets:** Discipline—hone body and mind; Purity—don't bargain with or fall under extraplanar influence; Vigilance—find and root it out.

| Paladin level | Oath spells                   |
| ------------- | ----------------------------- |
| 3rd           | alarm, ethereal anchor ll     |
| 5th           | locate creature ll, moonbeam  |
| 9th           | counterspell ll, nondetection |
| 13th          | aura of purity, banishment    |
| 17th          | hold monster, scrying         |

Unless your DM or table decides otherwise, aberrations, celestials, elementals, fey, and fiends are extraplanar, as are many creatures not native to the material plane.

**Abjuring Smite (3rd, Oath Smite, force).** Charisma save or push the target 10 feet away. Extraplanar creatures have disadvantage.

**Divine Vigilance (3rd, Channel Divinity, 4 Resolve).** When you or a creature within 10 feet fails an Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma save, use your reaction and spend 4 Resolve to let it repeat the save. At 6th level, this can help any creature under your Aura of Protection.

**Aura of Vigilance (7th).** Creatures under your aura can't be surprised and add your Charisma modifier to initiative.

**Vigilant Sight (15th).** Divine Sense also grants truesight within the same radius.

**Avatar of Vigilance (20th).** Divine Sense reaches 120 feet; your attacks against extraplanar creatures within truesight range have advantage; a creature that fails against Abjuring Smite is pushed 30 feet and has speed halved until its next turn ends.
