# Oath of the Wilds

Wilds paladins protect the balance between civilization and nature and embody the power of beasts. **Tenets:** Balance—keep nature and civilization in balance; Freedom—let living things choose their path; Natural Law—uphold the world's natural order.

See the shared [[character-options/subclasses/Paladin/index#oath-smites|Oath Smite rules]] and [[character-options/subclasses/Paladin/index#avatar-terms|Avatar terms]].

| Paladin level | Oath spells                  |
| ------------- | ---------------------------- |
| 3rd           | command, speak with animals  |
| 5th           | beast sense, find steed      |
| 9th           | conjure animals, fear        |
| 13th          | dominate beast, giant insect |
| 17th          | awaken, insect plague        |

**Predator's Mark (3rd, Channel Divinity, 4 Resolve).** As a bonus action, mark a creature you see within 120 feet as your Prey for 1 hour. Add Charisma modifier (minimum +1) to checks to track, tame, grapple, or subdue it.

**Soothe Beast (3rd, Channel Divinity, 4 Resolve).** As an action, touch a beast with your holy symbol. Wisdom save or charmed for 1 hour, ending if you or allies harm it.

**Aura of Savagery (7th).** Creatures you choose in your aura score a melee weapon critical hit on a roll of 19 or 20.

**Undying Predator (15th).** When reduced to 0 hit points but not killed outright, you can drop to 1 hit point (once per long rest). You suffer no drawbacks of old age and can't be aged magically.

**Primal Champion (20th).** As a bonus action, transform for 1 minute, ending if incapacitated: creatures in your Aura of Savagery score melee critical hits on 18–20; gain 10 temporary hit points at the start of each turn; as a bonus action each turn, move up to your speed toward a hostile creature you see and, if ending within 5 feet, make a melee weapon attack with advantage.
