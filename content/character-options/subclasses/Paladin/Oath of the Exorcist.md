# Oath of the Exorcist

Exorcists hunt the undead and protect the living, sacrificing ordinary lives to defend the world. **Tenets:** Defend—protect life, especially the innocent; Destroy—hunt and destroy undead; Focus—bear no titles, lands, family, or children.

See the shared [[character-options/subclasses/Paladin/index#oath-smites|Oath Smite rules]] and [[character-options/subclasses/Paladin/index#avatar-terms|Avatar terms]].

| Paladin level | Oath spells                              |
| ------------- | ---------------------------------------- |
| 3rd           | protection from evil and good, sanctuary |
| 5th           | invisibility, see invisibility           |
| 9th           | magic circle, spirit guardians           |
| 13th          | banishment, greater invisibility         |
| 17th          | dispel evil and good, holy weapon XGE    |

**Sinister Insights (3rd, Channel Divinity, 4 Resolve).** As an action, grasp your holy symbol to gain advantage for 1 hour on ability checks and saving throws related to undead or necromancy magic.

**Warrior of the Dawn (3rd, Channel Divinity, 4 Resolve).** As a bonus action, choose a creature within 30 feet. For 1 minute, each time you deal damage to it, deal an extra 1d6 radiant damage.

**Spectral Form (7th).** As a bonus action, become spectral until the end of your turn: resist bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing damage from nonmagical or non-silvered attacks; move through objects and creatures as difficult terrain. If you end inside one, you are shunted to the nearest free space and take 1d10 force damage per 5 feet traveled. Once per short or long rest; when out of uses, expend a 2nd-level or higher slot to use it again. At 18th level, use each turn without a resource.

**Scouring Strike (15th).** When you deal radiant damage to a creature, it can't regain hit points or become incorporeal until your next turn starts.

**Sacrificial Offering (20th).** When reduced to 0 hit points but not killed outright, spend one Hit Die to drop to 1 hit point instead.
