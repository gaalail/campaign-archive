# Oath of the Coven

Nobody is more acutely aware of the dangerous potential of magic than witches themselves. Paladins who swear the **Oath of the Coven** act as peacekeepers, guardians, and inquisitors within witch societies. They protect witches from outside perils and hunt down rogues who abuse arcane power to commit atrocities.

Equipped with antimagic techniques and witch-honed swords, these paladins neutralize rogue spellcasters and dismantle harmful enchantments.

### Witchmark

Witchmarks for Oath of the Coven paladins frequently resemble ancestral family crests, arcane burn scars from ancient duels, or glowing verses of their sacred oath winding up their sword arms.

---

## Tenets of the Coven

- **Punish the Wicked, Help the Good:** I am the sword that punishes those who use magic for evil, and the shield that protects those who use it for good.
- **Seek Out the Wicked:** Nothing in this world will deter me from pursuing those who commit magical atrocities.
- **Neutralize or Exterminate:** Those who abuse magic will not go free without answering for their crimes.
- **Magic is Neutral:** Magic is a neutral force of nature; I will never treat magic or its practitioners as inherently evil.

## Channel Divinity

_3rd-level Oath of the Coven feature_

- **Divine Dispel:** As an action, choose any creatures you can see that are maintaining a magical condition (such as charmed, frightened, or restrained). Each target must make a Charisma saving throw against your spell save DC; on a failed save, the magical effect immediately ends.
- **Spell Lock:** When a creature within 60 feet casts a spell, you can use your reaction to force it to make a Charisma saving throw against your spell save DC. On a failure, the creature is rendered unable to cast that specific spell again. The target can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns.

## Aura of Antimagic

_7th-level Oath of the Coven feature_

Hostile creatures within 10 feet of you have disadvantage on Constitution saving throws to maintain concentration. In addition, you and friendly creatures within 10 feet have advantage on Charisma saving throws.

At 18th level, the range of this aura increases to 30 feet.

## Ward of the Antimage

_15th-level Oath of the Coven feature_

Whenever a spell forces you to make an Intelligence or Wisdom saving throw, you can make a Charisma saving throw instead. Furthermore, you can see through magical darkness and cannot be slowed by magical difficult terrain.

## Witch Knight

_20th-level Oath of the Coven feature_

As an action, you undergo a 1-minute transformation into an avatar of arcane justice:
- You gain resistance against damage from all spells.
- Every spell you cast is treated as though cast using a spell slot one level higher than the slot expended.
- You and friendly creatures within 30 feet cannot be blinded, charmed, deafened, frightened, paralyzed, or petrified by magical means.

Once you use this feature, you cannot use it again until you finish a long rest.
