# Oath of the Ancients

Often called green knights, these paladins guard life and natural beauty. **Tenets:** Beauty—protect natural beauty and innocent life; Hope—kindle hope and never give in to despair; Light—delight in song, dance, and laughter, and spread joy.

See the shared [[character-options/subclasses/Paladin/index#oath-smites|Oath Smite rules]] and [[character-options/subclasses/Paladin/index#avatar-terms|Avatar terms]].

| Paladin level | Oath spells                      |
| ------------- | -------------------------------- |
| 3rd           | absorb elements*, entangle       |
| 5th           | moonbeam, spike growth           |
| 9th           | grasping vine ll, plant growth   |
| 13th          | guardian of nature*, ice storm   |
| 17th          | commune with nature, tree stride |

**Elder Shield (3rd, Channel Divinity, 2 Resolve).** As a reaction when you or a creature within 10 feet takes cold, fire, lightning, poison, or thunder damage, reduce it by your Paladin level + Charisma modifier. At 6th level, this can protect any creature under your Aura of Protection.

**Verdant Smite (3rd, Oath Smite, bludgeoning).** The target makes a Dexterity save; on a failure, vines entangle it and its speed becomes 0 until the end of its next turn.

**Aura of the Elder Ward (7th).** Creatures under your Aura of Protection have resistance to damage from spells.

**Undying Sentinel (15th).** You age 1 year for every 10 that pass, and magic can't age you. Once per long rest, when you start your turn at 0 hit points and aren't dead, you stand with 1 hit point.

**Avatar of the Ancients (20th).** For 10 minutes, regain 10 hit points at the start of each turn, and your aura is difficult terrain for creatures you choose. Other Avatar terms apply.
