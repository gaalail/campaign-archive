# Sacred Oaths, Part 1

Converted for the revised Paladin. Oath spells are Paladin spells for you, don't count against Spells Known, and can't be replaced. Oath features that spend Resolve are Channel Divinity options with listed costs and no other limit. An Oath Smite is an additional smite with no prerequisite, belonging to no Smite Path. It deals 2d6* damage of its listed type for a 1st-level slot, plus 1d6* per slot level above 1st, to a maximum of 6d6*. A marked die increases one size at 9th level. Its rider is one instant effect or one effect ending by the end of the target's next turn, comparable to a tier-1 path rider, and does not scale with slot level. It replaces a path smite on a hit; the spell slot is its only cost. Oaths refer to creature types or their own Tenets, never alignment. Smite riders last at most 1 minute and never take a creature's turn; other features retain their full duration. Each Avatar lasts 1 minute unless stated otherwise, ends early if you become unconscious or use a bonus action to end it, and can be used once per long rest at no cost; after that, expend a 5th-level spell slot to transform again.

## Oath of the Ancients

Often called green knights, these paladins guard life and natural beauty. **Tenets:** Beauty—protect natural beauty and innocent life; Hope—kindle hope and never give in to despair; Light—delight in song, dance, and laughter, and spread joy.

| Paladin level | Oath spells                      |
| ------------- | -------------------------------- |
| 3rd           | absorb elements*, entangle       |
| 5th           | moonbeam, spike growth           |
| 9th           | grasping vine ll, plant growth   |
| 13th          | guardian of nature*, ice storm   |
| 17th          | commune with nature, tree stride |

**Elder Shield (3rd, Channel Divinity, 2 Resolve).** As a reaction when you or a creature within 10 feet takes cold, fire, lightning, poison, or thunder damage, reduce it by your Paladin level + Charisma modifier. At 6th level, this can protect any creature under your Aura of Protection.

**Verdant Smite (3rd, Oath Smite, bludgeoning).** The target makes a Dexterity save; on a failure, vines entangle it and its speed becomes 0 until the end of its next turn.

**Aura of the Elder Ward (7th).** Creatures under your Aura of Protection have resistance to damage from spells.

**Undying Sentinel (15th).** You age 1 year for every 10 that pass, and magic can't age you. Once per long rest, when you start your turn at 0 hit points and aren't dead, you stand with 1 hit point.

**Avatar of the Ancients (20th).** For 10 minutes, regain 10 hit points at the start of each turn, and your aura is difficult terrain for creatures you choose. Other Avatar terms apply.

## Oath of Conquest

Conquerors seek glory through domination, crushing foes in body and spirit and upholding law through iron justice. **Tenets:** Conquest—crush foes so they never oppose you again; Iron Law—your word is law, reward loyalty and punish dissent; Might—might makes right, and you are mighty.

| Paladin level | Oath spells                      |
| ------------- | -------------------------------- |
| 3rd           | cause fear*, command             |
| 5th           | hold person, find steed ll       |
| 9th           | bestow curse, fear               |
| 13th          | dominate creature ll, compulsion |
| 17th          | dominate person, vorpal blade ll |

**Conquering Smite (3rd, Oath Smite, psychic).** Wisdom save or frightened of you until the end of its next turn.

**Lethal Strike (3rd, Channel Divinity, 2 Resolve).** Once per turn before you know whether a weapon attack hits, add twice your Charisma modifier to its attack roll.

**Aura of Dominion (7th).** Creatures you choose in your aura treat the area as difficult terrain. A creature frightened of you has its speed halved while in the aura. Total cover blocks this effect.

**Ruthless Reprisal (15th).** When a creature in your aura damages you or forces a save, it takes damage equal to your Charisma modifier (minimum 1), of a type you could choose for Righteous Strikes.

**Avatar of Conquest (20th).** Gain resistance to all damage. At the start of each turn, one creature in your aura must succeed on a Wisdom save or be frightened of you until the start of your next turn. Whenever you take the Attack action, make one additional weapon attack as part of it.

## Oath of Devotion

White knights stand as paragons of virtue, justice, and honor. **Tenets:** Compassion—protect the vulnerable and temper mercy with caution; Honesty—don't deceive for your own benefit and keep your word; Honor—do the most good with the least harm and obey rightful authority.

| Paladin level | Oath spells                      |
| ------------- | -------------------------------- |
| 3rd           | bless, shield of faith           |
| 5th           | find steed ll, restoration ll    |
| 9th           | beacon of hope, remove curse     |
| 13th          | death ward ll, guardian of faith |
| 17th          | dawn*, dispel evil and good      |

**Consecrated Smite (3rd, Oath Smite, radiant).** Wisdom save or, until the end of its next turn, the target can't willingly move closer to you or take reactions. Fiends and undead have disadvantage on the save.

**Sacred Weapon (3rd, Channel Divinity, 4 Resolve).** As a bonus action, imbue a melee weapon you hold for 1 minute: it sheds bright light in 30 feet, adds your Charisma modifier to its attack rolls, and is magical. The effect ends if it leaves your hand. When you activate it, you can spend 2 more Resolve to share the fire: choose creatures up to your Charisma modifier (minimum 1) within 30 feet, including yourself. Until the end of each chosen creature's next turn, its weapons deal +1d4 radiant damage on a hit (+1d8 at 10th level).

**Aura of Devotion (7th).** Creatures under your Aura of Protection are immune to charmed. You can suspend the effect on a charmed creature while it remains in your aura.

**Purity of Spirit (15th).** You are always under protection from evil and good.

**Avatar of Devotion (20th).** Manifest angelic wings (flying speed 60 feet); every melee weapon you hold becomes a Sacred Weapon; you radiate bright sunlight within your Aura of Protection.

## Oath of Glory

You strive to become a legendary hero through mighty deeds and deadly challenges. **Tenets:** Glory—be known for deeds, not words; Might—seek physical, spiritual, and mental perfection; Tenacity—meet worthy adversity without fleeing.

| Paladin level | Oath spells                        |
| ------------- | ---------------------------------- |
| 3rd           | compelled duel ll, jump ll         |
| 5th           | enhance ability, magic weapon ll   |
| 9th           | haste, thunder step*               |
| 13th          | death ward ll, freedom of movement |
| 17th          | steel wind strike, circle of power |

**Glorious Smite (3rd, Oath Smite, radiant).** Immediately after your attack, distribute temporary hit points equal to your Charisma modifier + twice the expended slot's level among creatures you choose within 10 feet. From 6th level, the range is your Aura Radius.

**Mighty Deed (3rd, Channel Divinity, 2 Resolve).** When you make a Strength, Dexterity, or Constitution check, or a long or high jump, add your Paladin level to the result. You can use this after rolling or attempting the jump.

**Mythic Alacrity (7th).** If not surprised, add Charisma modifier to initiative. At the start of your turn, you can suspend all Aura of Protection effects to gain a speed bonus equal to its radius, both until your next turn starts.

**Glorious Shield (15th).** When a creature under your aura is hit, use a reaction to add your Charisma modifier to its AC against that attack. Uses per long rest equal Charisma modifier (minimum 1); after uses are spent, spend 2 Resolve per use.

**Avatar of Glory (20th).** For 10 minutes, spend 2 Resolve when making a Charisma check to treat the d20 as 20; use Mighty Deed without Resolve; and once per turn turn a missed weapon attack into a hit.

## Oath of Splendor

You serve a primordial power and remake the material plane in its elemental image. **Tenets:** Destruction—display elemental power; Nobility—act with beauty befitting your patron; Wrath—destroy those who oppose or disrespect you.

**Primordial Sovereign (3rd).** Choose your element; only a wish can change it. Use Charisma instead of Dexterity to calculate AC.

| ------- | ----------- |
| Air     | thunder     |
| Earth   | bludgeoning |
| Fire    | fire        |
| Water   | cold        |

| Paladin level | Oath spells                                                |
| ------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| 3rd           | absorb elements*, burning hands                            |
| 5th           | mirror image, scorching ray                                |
| 9th           | elemental bane ll, summon elemental ll (your element only) |
| 13th          | wall of fire, ice storm                                    |
| 17th          | contact other plane, flame strike                          |

Whenever you cast a Splendor spell, its damage uses your element's type instead of its normal type.

**Elemental Smite (3rd, Oath Smite, your element's damage).** Air: Strength save or push 10 feet and no reactions until its next turn starts. Earth: Strength save or prone. Fire: target ignites and takes 1d6 fire damage at the start of its next turn. Water: Strength save or encased in ice, speed halved until its next turn ends.

**Aura of Destruction (7th).** When a creature in your aura takes your element's damage, you can make that instance ignore resistance and treat immunity as resistance.

**Elemental Rebuke (15th).** When a creature in your aura damages you, use a reaction to force a Dexterity save; on failure, it takes 2d8 damage of your element's type and suffers your Elemental Smite rider.

**Avatar of the Elements (20th).** Gain elemental wings (flying speed 60 feet); creatures you choose in your aura gain resistance to your element's damage and you gain immunity to it; your element's damage to structures is maximized.

## Oath of the Throne

You serve your sovereign, uphold their laws, and protect their lands and subjects. **Tenets:** Bravery—carry out orders despite overwhelming odds; Loyalty—serve your liege's interests; Order—uphold the law as civilization's bedrock.

| Paladin level | Oath spells                        |
| ------------- | ---------------------------------- |
| 3rd           | command, compelled duel ll         |
| 5th           | find steed ll, warding bond        |
| 9th           | aura of vitality, spirit guardians |
| 13th          | banishment, guardian of faith      |
| 17th          | circle of power, geas              |

**Challenging Smite (3rd, Oath Smite, radiant).** Wisdom save or disadvantage on attacks against creatures other than you until the end of its next turn.

**Rallying Command (3rd, Channel Divinity, 4 Resolve).** As a bonus action, a creature within 30 feet that can hear and understand you gains temporary hit points equal to 1d6 + your Charisma modifier.

**Oathbound Guard (7th).** When a creature in your aura takes damage, use your reaction to take that damage instead. It can't be reduced, and non-damage effects don't transfer.

**Unwavering Knight (15th).** While conscious, you have advantage on saves to resist paralyzed and stunned.

**Avatar of Loyalty (20th).** Gain resistance to bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing damage. Creatures under your aura have advantage on Wisdom saves and death saves. A creature that fails its save against Challenging Smite also can't willingly move more than 30 feet away from you until its next turn ends.

## Oath of Vengeance

Avengers dedicate their lives to destroying a sworn foe or corrupt organization. **Tenets:** Destroy Evil—fight sworn foes and the greater evil; No Mercy—defeat evil by any means; Restitution—make amends for your foes' evil and other evildoers.

| Paladin level | Oath spells                |
| ------------- | -------------------------- |
| 3rd           | bane, compelled duel ll    |
| 5th           | hold person, misty step    |
| 9th           | haste, nondetection        |
| 13th          | banishment, dimension door |
| 17th          | far step*, hold monster    |

**Vow of Enmity (3rd, Channel Divinity, 4 Resolve).** As a bonus action, mark a creature you see within 30 feet as your Sworn Foe for 1 minute or until it dies. You have advantage on all attacks against it. You can't use this again while your Sworn Foe lives.

**Vengeful Smite (3rd, Oath Smite, fire).** Wisdom save or the target's speed is halved until its next turn ends. Your Sworn Foe has disadvantage on the save.

**Shield of Vengeance (7th).** Creatures under your aura have advantage on ability checks and saves your Sworn Foe forces them to make.

**Relentless Avenger (15th).** When your Sworn Foe attacks or forces another creature to save, use a reaction to move up to your speed toward it and make one attack. If hit by an opportunity attack during this reaction, add Charisma modifier to your attack roll.

**Avatar of Vengeance (20th).** Use Vow of Enmity without Resolve; cast misty step each turn at will without a slot and, if reappearing within 5 feet of a creature, make a melee weapon attack against it; once per turn when making a weapon attack with advantage, forgo that advantage to make one additional attack against that creature.

## Oath of Vigilance

You protect the material plane from extraplanar influence, whatever its nature. **Tenets:** Discipline—hone body and mind; Purity—don't bargain with or fall under extraplanar influence; Vigilance—find and root it out.

| Paladin level | Oath spells                   |
| ------------- | ----------------------------- |
| 3rd           | alarm, ethereal anchor ll     |
| 5th           | locate creature ll, moonbeam  |
| 9th           | counterspell ll, nondetection |
| 13th          | aura of purity, banishment    |
| 17th          | hold monster, scrying         |

Unless your DM or table decides otherwise, aberrations, celestials, elementals, fey, and fiends are extraplanar, as are many creatures not native to the material plane.

**Abjuring Smite (3rd, Oath Smite, force).** Charisma save or push the target 10 feet away. Extraplanar creatures have disadvantage.

**Divine Vigilance (3rd, Channel Divinity, 4 Resolve).** When you or a creature within 10 feet fails an Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma save, use your reaction and spend 4 Resolve to let it repeat the save. At 6th level, this can help any creature under your Aura of Protection.

**Aura of Vigilance (7th).** Creatures under your aura can't be surprised and add your Charisma modifier to initiative.

**Vigilant Sight (15th).** Divine Sense also grants truesight within the same radius.

**Avatar of Vigilance (20th).** Divine Sense reaches 120 feet; your attacks against extraplanar creatures within truesight range have advantage; a creature that fails against Abjuring Smite is pushed 30 feet and has speed halved until its next turn ends.

## Oath of Repentance

You seek forgiveness and use violence only as a last resort. **Tenets:** Peace—violence is a last resort; Poverty—give excess wealth to those suffering; Redemption—no mortal is beyond forgiveness.

| Paladin level | Oath spells                          |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| 3rd           | sanctuary, sleep                     |
| 5th           | calm emotions, hold person           |
| 9th           | counterspell ll, hypnotic pattern ll |
| 13th          | aura of life, resilient sphere       |
| 17th          | hold monster, wall of force ll       |

**Righteous Emissary (3rd, Channel Divinity, 2 Resolve).** When you make a Persuasion check, spend 2 Resolve to add your Paladin level.

**Subduing Smite (3rd, Channel Divinity, 2 Resolve).** When a smite reduces a creature to 0 hit points, spend 2 Resolve to leave it at 1 hit point instead and charm it for 1 minute. It is docile and takes no movement or actions unless you use a bonus action to order it; you can't order it to attack or force checks or saves. Ends if you are incapacitated, use it again, or you or allies act hostile toward it.

**Aura of Penance (7th).** When a creature under your aura takes damage, use a reaction to take half that damage (rounded up) instead. It can't be reduced, and non-damage effects don't transfer.

**Redemptive Regeneration (15th).** If you start your turn below half your hit points but with at least 1, regain hit points equal to your Charisma modifier.

**Avatar of Redemption (20th).** Gain resistance to all damage and use Aura of Penance without a reaction. If you damage a creature or force it to save, these benefits are lost until the start of your next turn.

## The Oathless

Oathless paladins broke their oath for reasons other than evil but retain some power. They owe allegiance to none; their safety and power come first.

| Paladin level | Oathless spells                      |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| 3rd           | charm person, false life             |
| 5th           | invisibility, suggestion             |
| 9th           | gaseous form, vampiric touch         |
| 13th          | charm monster*, greater invisibility |
| 17th          | dominate person, passwall            |

**Shattered Oath (3rd).** Subtract your Paladin level from ability checks to convince a creature to trust you or enter any deal or agreement; creatures distrust you.

**Selfish Smite (3rd, Oath Smite, necrotic).** Gain temporary hit points equal to half the necrotic damage dealt.

**Illusory Escape (3rd, Channel Divinity, 4 Resolve).** When a source you can see damages you, use a reaction and spend 4 Resolve to become invisible and move up to your speed. Invisibility lasts until your next turn starts or you attack or force a save.

**Aura of Deception (7th).** When using Illusory Escape, force a creature in your aura to make a Charisma save. On failure, you and it switch places; it takes the triggering damage, and you gain Illusory Escape's normal benefits from your new space. On success, you gain the normal benefits.

**Forceful Will (15th).** A creature in your aura has disadvantage on its initial save against an enchantment spell you force it to make.

**Avatar of Dishonor (20th).** Become invisible as by greater invisibility; take Dash or Hide as a bonus action; when a creature misses you with a melee attack, make it repeat the attack against another creature you choose within its normal range.

Blackguards devote themselves to cruelty and ruin. Sparing the helpless or protecting the weak at personal cost breaks their vow.

| 9th           | animate dead ll, conjure fiend ll       |

**Dreadful Smite (3rd, Oath Smite, necrotic).** Wisdom save or frightened of you until its next turn ends.

**Marshal of Darkness (3rd, Channel Divinity, 4 Resolve).** As an action, choose a fiend or undead within 30 feet with CR no higher than half your Paladin level (rounded down). Charisma save or obey you for 1 hour or until you use this again; it repeats the save each time it takes damage from you or allies.

**Aura of Hate (7th).** Fiends and undead under your aura add your Charisma modifier to attack rolls.

**Sinister Resilience (15th).** You no longer need to eat, drink, or breathe; gain resistance to necrotic and nonmagical bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing damage.

**Avatar of Dread (20th).** Bright light within 30 feet becomes dim light. A creature frightened of you that starts its turn in your aura takes 4d10 necrotic damage. When you deal necrotic damage, gain temporary hit points equal to half the damage dealt.

## Additional Fighting Styles

Available to Paladins when choosing a Fighting Style at 1st level, in addition to Player's Handbook options.

- **Classical Swordplay.** With a finesse weapon and no other weapons, gain +2 to attack rolls and +1 AC if not in heavy armor or using a shield.
- **Great Weapon Fighting.** When making an attack with a heavy melee weapon as part of your Attack action while wielding it in two hands, treat a total roll of 5 or lower on its damage dice as 6.
- **Heavyweight Fighting.** While wielding a heavy weapon, gain +1 damage and advantage on Strength (Athletics) checks to shove.
- **Mariner (Corsair only).** Without medium or heavy armor or a shield, gain a swimming speed equal to walking speed and +1 AC.
- **Mounted Warrior.** While riding a controlled mount, both of you gain +1 AC; as a bonus action on each turn, command the mount to take one action in its stat block.
- **Protector.** When you or a creature within 5 feet is hit by a melee attack, use a reaction to add proficiency bonus to the target's AC against that attack. Wield a shield or melee weapon.
- **Shield Warrior (Shield only).** Gain proficiency with shields as martial melee weapons; on hit deal 2d4 bludgeoning damage. With shield and no other weapon, gain +1 to shield attack rolls and AC.
- **Standard Bearer.** When a creature within 5 feet attacks another creature you see, use a reaction to grant advantage. Hold a standard or banner in that hand and nothing else.
- **Strongbow.** Use Strength instead of Dexterity for longbow and shortbow attacks and damage; when doing so, gain +1 damage.
- **Versatile Fighting.** With a single versatile weapon and no shield, gain +1 attack; you can use a bonus action to grapple or shove, or take the Use an Object action.

## Revision notes

Oath Smite types: Ancients bludgeoning; Conquest psychic; Devotion radiant; Glory radiant; Splendor elemental; Throne radiant; Vengeance fire; Vigilance force; Oathless necrotic; Blackguard necrotic. Repentance's Subduing Smite is a finisher, not an Oath Smite. Righteousness features were folded into Devotion or removed: Righteous Fire is an option within Sacred Weapon; Ally of the Righteous, Aura of the Righteous, Enemy of All Dishonorable, and Might of the Righteous were dropped. The base-file Oathless chassis is used; Devious Escape, Selfish Restoration, and Ruthless Counter were dropped. Renames: Divine Aegis to Oathbound Guard; Aura of Warding to Aura of the Elder Ward; Oathless Draining Smite to Selfish Smite. Conquest and Glory spell replacements and Ancients' ensnaring strike-to-entangle replacement are reflected above. `ll` marks a laserllama Spell Compendium spell, as documented in the existing Warden spell list; it is a source tag, not a spell-level adjustment. In these lists, Grasping Vine and Elemental Bane appear at 9th-level Paladin, Locate Creature at 5th, and Create Undead at 13th; those supplied pairings are above the normal Paladin slot progression and require the corresponding `ll` versions' actual compendium levels to be confirmed before play. Conquest's resistance/additional attack; Glory's automatic hit; Redemption's resistance; Vigilance's push substitution.
