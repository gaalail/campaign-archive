# The Oathless

Oathless paladins broke their oath for reasons other than evil but retain some power. They owe allegiance to none; their safety and power come first.

See the shared [[character-options/subclasses/Paladin/index#oath-smites|Oath Smite rules]] and [[character-options/subclasses/Paladin/index#avatar-terms|Avatar terms]].

| Paladin level | Oathless spells                      |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| 3rd           | charm person, false life             |
| 5th           | invisibility, suggestion             |
| 9th           | gaseous form, vampiric touch         |
| 13th          | charm monster*, greater invisibility |
| 17th          | dominate person, passwall            |

**Shattered Oath (3rd).** Subtract your Paladin level from ability checks to convince a creature to trust you or enter any deal or agreement; creatures distrust you.

**Selfish Smite (3rd, Oath Smite, necrotic).** Gain temporary hit points equal to half the necrotic damage dealt.

**Illusory Escape (3rd, Channel Divinity, 4 Resolve).** When a source you can see damages you, use a reaction and spend 4 Resolve to become invisible and move up to your speed. Invisibility lasts until your next turn starts or you attack or force a save.

**Aura of Deception (7th).** When using Illusory Escape, force a creature in your aura to make a Charisma save. On failure, you and it switch places; it takes the triggering damage, and you gain Illusory Escape's normal benefits from your new space. On success, you gain the normal benefits.

**Forceful Will (15th).** A creature in your aura has disadvantage on its initial save against an enchantment spell you force it to make.

**Avatar of Dishonor (20th).** Become invisible as by greater invisibility; take Dash or Hide as a bonus action; when a creature misses you with a melee attack, make it repeat the attack against another creature you choose within its normal range.
