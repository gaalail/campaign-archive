# Oath of the Saints

The Oath of the Saints is walked by zealots who seek not merely to venerate the saints and martyrs who came before them, but to embody them—calling upon their divine purity so that miraculous saints might once again walk the mortal realm. Adorned in hulking reliquary plate engraved with passions of martyrdom and carrying holy finger bones bound in gold wire, they serve as living conduits of divine will.

---

> [!tenets] Sacred Tenets of the Saints
> - **Vow of Sacrifice:** I must eagerly seek opportunities to sacrifice my blood, flesh, and comfort in service to the incorruptible.
> - **Vow of Expiation:** I recognize that mortals are imperfect beings of sin. I must atone for my failings and guide others to expiate their guilt.
> - **Vow of Censure:** I must destroy that which is anathema to the saints and dismantle the foul works of the wicked.

## Channel Divinity

_3rd-level Oath of the Saints feature_

- **Sanction of the Martyr:** As an action, radiate holy energy from saintly engravings. Enemies within 20 feet must make a Wisdom saving throw (fiends and undead make this save with disadvantage). On a failure, a target has disadvantage on attacks against anyone other than you and suffers a penalty to damage against others equal to your Charisma modifier for 1 minute. You gain temporary hit points equal to your Charisma modifier per failed enemy.
- **Infallibility:** When you or an ally within 20 feet fails an Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma saving throw, use your reaction to inflict psychic damage on the forcing creature equal to 1d8 + Charisma modifier (scaling to 2d8 at 5th, 3d8 at 9th, 4d8 at 13th, and 5d8 at 17th level).

## Vessel of the Saints

_7th-level Oath of the Saints feature_

As a bonus action, open yourself to be inhabited by saintly soul-vestiges for 1 minute, gaining +1 AC and resistance to bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing damage:

- **Saint of Censure:** Add your Charisma modifier to saving throws against spells and magical effects (allies within 20 ft gain half this bonus). At 9th level, expend a 3rd-level spell slot to cast [[counterspell]].
- **Saint of Expiation:** Once per turn when you take damage, an ally within 20 feet gains temporary hit points equal to 1 + your Charisma modifier.
- **Saint of Sacrifice:** When an ally within 20 feet takes damage, grant them resistance; you take unpreventable typeless damage equal to half the unmitigated amount.

## Incorruptible

_15th-level Oath of the Saints feature_

You cease to visibly age, gain resistance to poison and necrotic damage, and are permanently protected by the effects of [[gentle-repose]] upon death.

## Miraculous Canonization

_20th-level Oath of the Saints feature_

You become the vessel of all saints. Activate Vessel of the Saints as a free action when rolling initiative (regaining 1 use if empty). When activating, choose **two saintforms simultaneously**.
