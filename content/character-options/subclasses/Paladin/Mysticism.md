# Oath of Mysticism

Mystics awaken their own inner psionic power. Optional rule: use Intelligence instead of Charisma for all Paladin features. **Tenets:** Asceticism—forsake attachments; Enlightenment—realize potential and help others; Knowledge—seek deeper enlightenment through knowledge.

See the shared [[character-options/subclasses/Paladin/index#oath-smites|Oath Smite rules]] and [[character-options/subclasses/Paladin/index#avatar-terms|Avatar terms]].

| Paladin level | Oath spells                      |
| ------------- | -------------------------------- |
| 3rd           | catapult XGE, dissonant whispers |
| 5th           | detect thoughts, levitate        |
| 9th           | clairvoyance, slow               |
| 13th          | confusion, phantasmal killer     |
| 17th          | telekinesis, wall of force       |

**Enlightened Spell (3rd, Channel Divinity, 4 Resolve).** When casting a Mysticism spell, grasp your holy symbol to cast without verbal or somatic components.

**Psionic Smite (3rd, Oath Smite, psychic).** Wisdom save or target's speed is halved until its next turn ends.

**Aura of Enlightenment (7th).** Creatures you choose in your aura resist force and psychic damage and have advantage on saves against charmed or frightened.

**Psionic Rebuke (15th).** When a creature in your aura hits you while you aren't incapacitated, it takes psychic damage equal to your Charisma modifier (minimum 1).

**Mystic Ascension (20th).** As a bonus action, become luminous for 1 minute: emit bright light in 5 feet and dim light 5 feet farther; gain flying speed equal to walking speed; become immune to psychic and resistant to nonmagical bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing; move through creatures and objects as difficult terrain, taking 1d10 force damage per 5 feet if shunted after ending inside one.
