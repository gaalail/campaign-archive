# Paladin Sacred Oaths

**24 oaths available** | Choose at 3rd level | Features at 3rd, 7th, 15th, and 20th level

Oath spells are Paladin spells for you, don't count against Spells Known, and can't be replaced. Resolve options are Channel Divinity options with their listed costs. Oath Smites use the shared rules in [[character-options/subclasses/Paladin/Sacred Oaths Part 1|Sacred Oaths, Part 1]].

## Part 1: Core Oaths

[[character-options/subclasses/Paladin/Sacred Oaths Part 1|Read all Part 1 Oaths and rules]]

Includes Ancients, Conquest, Devotion, Glory, Splendor, Throne, Vengeance, Vigilance, Repentance, Oathless, and Blackguard.

## Part 2: Channel Divinity Oaths

[[character-options/subclasses/Paladin/Sacred Oaths Part 2|Read all Part 2 Oaths, Zealot, Templar, and Fighting Styles]]

Includes Beauty, Blade, Corsair, Exorcist, Forge, Inquisition, Liberty, Mysticism, Prosperity, Shield, Wilds, Zealot, and Templar.
