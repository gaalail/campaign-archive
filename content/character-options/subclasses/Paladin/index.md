# Paladin Sacred Oaths

**24 oaths available** | Choose at 3rd level | Features at 3rd, 7th, 15th, and 20th level

Oath spells are Paladin spells for you, don't count against Spells Known, and can't be replaced. Resolve options are Channel Divinity options with their listed costs. Oath Smites use the shared rules in [[character-options/subclasses/Paladin/Sacred Oaths Part 1#sacred-oaths-part-1|Sacred Oaths, Part 1]].
