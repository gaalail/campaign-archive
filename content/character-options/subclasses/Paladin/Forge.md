# Oath of the Forge

Forge paladins pursue progress and order through a chosen craft. **Tenets:** Order—create order from disorder; Perfection—strive for perfection; Progress—pass knowledge on so successors surpass you.

See the shared [[character-options/subclasses/Paladin/index#oath-smites|Oath Smite rules]] and [[character-options/subclasses/Paladin/index#avatar-terms|Avatar terms]].

**Molten Weapon (3rd, Channel Divinity, 4 Resolve).** As a bonus action, imbue a melee weapon for 1 minute; it deals an extra 1d6 fire damage on a hit.

**Ironworks (3rd, Channel Divinity, 4 Resolve).** As an action, touch your holy symbol to a Medium or smaller nonmagical metal object and reshape it. A shape requiring skill or training requires proficiency with the relevant artisan's tools.

**Aura of Iron (7th).** Metal weapons and armor of your choice in your aura gain masterful quality: armor grants +1 AC to its wearer; mundane weapons count as magical.

**Unyielding Steel (15th).** Once per turn when damaged while wearing heavy armor, use a reaction to halve the damage. Uses per short or long rest equal Charisma modifier (minimum 1).

**Champion of the Forge (20th).** As a bonus action, transform for 1 minute, ending if incapacitated: Aura of Iron grants allies resistance to bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing; use Unyielding Steel each turn without expending a use; your fire damage ignores resistance and treats immunity as resistance.
