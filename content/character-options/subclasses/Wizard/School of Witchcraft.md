# School of Witchcraft

A wizard learns through dusty old tomes and rigorous study. A witch learns through secret whispers and strict mentorship. A wizard witch melds these two ways of teaching into the **School of Witchcraft**, seeking and seeing knowledge in places that both traditional wizards and covens cannot imagine.

To a wizard witch, a spell or martial art can be learned from a book, from a living mentor, or directly from the trapped spirits of fallen foes.

### Witchmark

Wizards of this school bear witchmarks related to language, memory, and revelation: the dying words of a vanquished adversary, insightful proverbs, the runes of mentors, or glowing arcane eyes inscribed across their palms.

---

## Teachers from the Beyond

_2nd-level School of Witchcraft feature_

After completing a long rest, you can spend 10 minutes communing with spirits to learn one cantrip from the sorcerer, warlock, druid, cleric, bard, or wizard spell list. It counts as a wizard cantrip for you and lasts until your next long rest.

## Oral Tradition

_2nd-level School of Witchcraft feature_

When discovering a spell you are capable of learning, you can commit it to memory instead of inscribing it into your spellbook. Spending 4 hours per spell level practicing commits the spell to memory, allowing you to prepare it normally (including from spell scrolls with the normal Arcana check).

## Spectral Learning

_6th-level School of Witchcraft feature_

When a creature you can see drops to 0 hit points, roll a d20 + your proficiency bonus. If your result exceeds the creature’s Challenge Rating, you temporarily bind its spirit and gain one of its abilities (determined by the GM) until your next long rest.

If the ability relies on a physical feature (such as claws, a stinger, or a prehensile tail), you conjure a spectral manifestation of that appendage. Intelligence is your ability modifier for all attacks and saving throw DCs using these spectral features. If the ability is a spell you don't know, you can commit it to memory via Oral Tradition. Once you use this feature, you cannot do so again until finishing a long rest.

## Spiritual Savant

_10th-level School of Witchcraft feature_

When you trap a creature's ability using **Spectral Learning**, you can spend a number of minutes equal to (CR × 10) to permanently commit that non-spell ability to memory. You can retain one permanently memorized creature ability alongside your daily temporary ability.

## Prodigy of the Arcane

_14th-level School of Witchcraft feature_

After finishing a long rest, you can spend 10 minutes per spell level to prepare **one spell of your choice from any class's spell list** for which you have spell slots. When you cast it, it counts as a wizard spell. It remains prepared until your next long rest.
