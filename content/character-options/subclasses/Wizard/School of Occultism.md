# School of Occultism

You are endlessly fascinated with esoteric texts detailing the darkest corners of ritual and the bloody underbelly of arcana. With your corrupt spellbook and sacrificial dagger in hand, you carve your way through flesh and magic alike.

## Ceremonial Blade & Sacrifices

_2nd-level School of Occultism feature_

You wield a ceremonial dagger using your **Intelligence modifier** for attack and damage rolls.

By conducting a 10-minute rite over an incapacitated creature with 10 or fewer hit points, you sacrifice it to activate one Sacrifice ability (allies witnessing it gain 1 Fear; you know 1 ability, learning a 2nd at 10th and 3rd at 14th level):

- **Bloodlet:** Gain Sanguine Charges equal to Intelligence modifier. Bonus action to expend a charge for **1d10 temporary hit points**.
- **Dark Feast:** Consume the entire sacrifice to gain the benefits of a **full long rest** (witnessing allies gain 2 Fear).
- **Death by a Thousand Cuts:** Score critical hits on rolls of **18–20** with your ceremonial blade, inflicting 2d4 bleeding necrotic damage per turn on hit.
- **Heartburn:** Extract the heart. The next time you drop to 0 hit points, the heart bursts into flames: regain 1 hit point and gain 1 Fear.
- **Psychic Ward:** Consume the sacrifice’s brain. When forced to make a Strength, Dexterity, or Constitution save, make an **Intelligence saving throw** instead.

## Precision Cast

_6th-level School of Occultism feature_

You can deal nonlethal damage with spells that target only one creature.

## Mass Sacrifice

_10th-level School of Occultism feature_

You can maintain a number of active Sacrifice abilities equal to your Intelligence modifier (requiring one sacrificed creature per active ability).

## Carnal Supper

_14th-level School of Occultism feature_

You can bestow active Sacrifice benefits upon willing allies who participate in your rites. Reselect known Sacrifice abilities each long rest.
