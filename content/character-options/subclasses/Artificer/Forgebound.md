# Forgebound

Forgebound artificers bind part of their soul to a companion capable of spontaneously generating weapons. Together, they coordinate attacks and share magic.

## Forgebound Features

| Artificer Level | Feature                                                |
| --------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| 3rd             | Tools of the Trade, Forge Companion, Forgebound Spells |
| 5th             | Extra Attack                                           |
| 10th            | Coordinated Combatants                                 |
| 15th            | Master Forgebound                                      |

## Tools of the Trade

_3rd-level Forgebound feature_

You gain proficiency with smith's tools and martial weapons. If you are already proficient with smith's tools, you gain proficiency with another type of artisan's tools of your choice.

## Forge Companion

_3rd-level Forgebound feature_

You create a faithful Forge Companion. It is friendly to you and obeys your orders. Its game statistics appear below and use your spell save DC, proficiency bonus (PB), and Intelligence modifier.

**Creating a Companion.** During a long rest, you can spend 1 hour using smith's tools to create a Forge Companion. You can have only one companion; creating another destroys the previous one. If your companion dies, you can expend a spell slot and spend 1 minute repairing it with smith's tools, restoring it to life with its maximum hit points.

**Infusions.** When the companion uses Conjure Armament, it can apply one of your infusions to that weapon, provided you meet the infusion's prerequisites.

**Combat.** In combat, your companion acts during your turn. It can move and use its reaction on its own, but it takes only the Dodge action unless you use a bonus action to order it to take an action in its stat block or another action. If you are incapacitated, the companion acts on its own to defend you to the best of its ability.

**Spellcasting Focus.** Weapons created with Conjure Armament can be used as a spellcasting focus for your artificer spells.

_Medium construct, unaligned_

| Hit Points           | 5 + five times your Artificer level           |
| Speed                | 30 ft.                                        |

| Condition Immunities | Charmed, exhaustion, poisoned                 |

| Languages            | Understands the languages you speak           |

**STR** 16 (+3), **DEX** 12 (+1), **CON** 14 (+2), **INT** 4 (−3), **WIS** 10 (+0), **CHA** 6 (−2)

**Hit Dice.** The companion has a number of d8 Hit Dice equal to your Artificer level and gains the normal benefits of short and long rests.

**Might of the Master.** Add your PB to any ability check or saving throw the companion makes.

#### Actions

**Conjure Armament.** The companion instantaneously creates a simple or martial weapon, causing it to appear in its creator's hand. This weapon is magical and deals force damage instead of its normal damage type on a hit. It disappears if dropped or if the companion creates another weapon.

**Slam.** _Melee Weapon Attack:_ your spell attack modifier to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 1d8 + your Intelligence modifier force damage.

## Extra Attack

_5th-level Forgebound feature_

You can attack twice, instead of once, whenever you take the Attack action on your turn. Moreover, when you cast a spell with a casting time of 1 action, you can make one melee attack as a bonus action on that turn.

## Coordinated Combatants

_10th-level Forgebound feature_

When you and your companion are both within 5 feet of the same creature, you both have advantage on attacks against it. In addition, while you are within 60 feet of your companion, you can use a bonus action to switch places with it.

## Master Forgebound

_15th-level Forgebound feature_

Whenever your companion creates a weapon for you with Conjure Armament, it also creates a copy of the weapon for itself with the same infusion, and it is proficient with that weapon. In addition, whenever you cast an artificer spell that targets only you, your companion also gains the spell's benefits if it is within 60 feet of you.
