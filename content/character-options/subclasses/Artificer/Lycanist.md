# Lycanist

Lycanists study the cursed science of werebeasts, transforming into a powerful amalgamation of beasts and choosing new traits with each transformation.

## Lycanist Features

| Artificer Level | Feature                                  |
| --------------- | ---------------------------------------- |
| 3rd             | Tools of the Trade, Lycan Transformation |
| 5th             | Extra Attack                             |
| 10th            | Supernatural Resilience                  |
| 15th            | Master Lycanist                          |

## Tools of the Trade

_3rd-level Lycanist feature_

You gain proficiency with alchemist's supplies. If you are already proficient with them, you instead gain proficiency with another type of artisan's tools of your choice.

## Lycan Transformation

_3rd-level Lycanist feature_

As a bonus action, you can expend a spell slot to transform into your Lycan Form, using its stat block below. While transformed, you retain your Intelligence, Wisdom, and Charisma scores, your personality, alignment, and ability to speak. If both you and the form are proficient in the same skill, use whichever bonus is higher. Choose whether your equipment merges into the form, is worn or wielded by it, or falls to the ground.

When you transform, you gain temporary hit points equal to your Artificer level. You remain in this form for 1 hour, until you use a bonus action to revert, or until you are reduced to 0 hit points.

**Appearance.** You choose what type or combination of beasts your form resembles; this choice doesn't affect its abilities or statistics.

**Spellcasting.** You can't cast spells while in Lycan Form, but you can concentrate on spells you cast before transforming, such as _enlarge/reduce_.

**Bestial Traits.** When you transform, choose a number of Bestial Traits equal to 1 + the level of the spell slot expended. If a trait has a level prerequisite, you can choose it once you meet that prerequisite.

**Infusions.** If you are wearing or wielding armor or a weapon bearing an infusion, the Lycan Form's Armor Class (for armor) and Bite and Maul attacks (for weapons) gain the benefits of the respective infusions.

### Lycan Form

_Large beast (shapechanger)_

| Statistic      | Value                                                 |
| -------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| Armor Class    | 13 + your Intelligence modifier                       |
| Speed          | 40 ft.                                                |
| Senses         | Darkvision 120 ft.                                    |
| Bestial Traits | 1 + the level of the spell slot expended to transform |

**STR** 18 (+4), **DEX** 14 (+2), **CON** 16 (+3)

**Skills.** Athletics +4 + PB; Perception your Wisdom modifier + PB  
**Primal Fury.** When the form makes an ability check or saving throw using Strength, Dexterity, or Constitution, you can add your PB to the roll.

#### Actions

**Bite.** _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +4 + PB to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 1d8 + 4 piercing damage.

**Maul.** _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +4 + PB to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 2d6 + 4 slashing damage.

## Bestial Traits

When a trait requires a saving throw, it uses your artificer spell save DC.

### Traits without a level prerequisite

- **Aggressive.** As a bonus action, your Lycan Form can move up to its speed toward a hostile creature it can see, hear, or smell.
- **Amphibious.** Your Lycan Form gains a swimming speed equal to its walking speed and can breathe air and water.
- **Charge.** When your Lycan Form moves at least 20 feet straight toward a target and hits it with Maul, it deals an extra 1d6 damage. A creature no larger than your form must succeed on a Strength saving throw or fall prone.
- **Keen Senses.** Your Lycan Form has advantage on Wisdom (Perception) or Wisdom (Survival) checks that rely on hearing or smell.
- **Light Step.** Your Lycan Form has advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks while lightly obscured by natural foliage or weather.
- **Mimicry.** Your Lycan Form can mimic simple sounds it has heard, such as a person's voice or animal calls. A creature can identify the sounds as imitations by using its action to succeed on a DC 10 Wisdom (Insight) check.
- **Nimble.** Your Lycan Form doubles its proficiency bonus for Dexterity (Acrobatics) checks.
- **Opportunist.** When your Lycan Form hits a prone target with Bite, it rolls one additional damage die.
- **Opposable Thumbs.** Your Lycan Form can manipulate objects and tools as well as a commoner, but can't use weapons or complex tools.
- **Powerful Build.** Your Lycan Form counts as one size larger when determining the creatures it can grapple and the weight it can move or lift.
- **Prehensile Tail.** Your Lycan Form's tail can hold Tiny objects and grasp surfaces while climbing.
- **Reflexive Fall.** When your Lycan Form falls, it can use its reaction to reduce falling damage by its Dexterity score. If the damage is reduced to 0, it lands on its feet.
- **Savage Grip.** Your Lycan Form gains a climbing speed equal to its walking speed and can climb sheer surfaces and ceilings without an ability check. It has advantage on saving throws to resist being moved against its will, grappled, restrained, or knocked prone.
- **Standing Leap.** Your Lycan Form's long jump is at least 15 feet and its high jump at least 10 feet, even without a running start.
- **Vicious Bite.** When your Lycan Form hits a creature no larger than itself with Bite, it can grapple the target. Until the grapple ends, the target is restrained, and your form can't Bite another target.

### Traits requiring 5th level

- **Thermal Vision.** Your Lycan Form knows the exact location of warm-blooded creatures within 30 feet.
- **Concussive Strike.** Once per turn when your Lycan Form hits a creature with Maul, it can force it to make a Constitution saving throw or be stunned until the start of your next turn. A creature larger than your form has advantage on this save.
- **Echolocation.** Your Lycan Form gains blindsight out to 30 feet. When it makes a Wisdom (Perception) check based on hearing, it can treat a d20 roll of 7 or lower as an 8. It loses this benefit and its blindsight while deafened.
- **Pack Tactics.** Your Lycan Form has advantage on attack rolls against a target if one of its conscious allies is within 5 feet of it.
- **Relentless.** When your Lycan Form would be reduced to 0 hit points but not killed outright, you can expend one of your Hit Dice to fall to 1 hit point instead.
- **Thick Hide.** Whenever your Lycan Form takes damage, reduce it by your Constitution modifier. Force and psychic damage ignore this reduction.
- **Venomous (3/Day).** When your Lycan Form hits a creature with Bite, it can force the target to make a Constitution saving throw. On a failure, the target takes 1d4 poison damage and is poisoned. It repeats the save at the end of each of its turns, ending the condition on a success or taking 1d4 poison damage on a failure.

## Extra Attack

_5th-level Lycanist feature_

You can attack twice, instead of once, whenever you take the Attack action on your turn. Also, when you use your action to cast a spell, you can make a melee attack as a bonus action. Your attacks in Lycan Form count as magical for overcoming resistance and immunity to nonmagical attacks and damage.

## Supernatural Resilience

_10th-level Lycanist feature_

While in Lycan Form, you have resistance to bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing damage from non-silvered weapons.

## Master Lycanist

_15th-level Lycanist feature_

You can cast spells normally while in Lycan Form. Moreover, whenever you take the Attack action in that form, you can make three attacks instead of two.
