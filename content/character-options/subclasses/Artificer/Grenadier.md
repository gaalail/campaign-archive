# Grenadier

Grenadiers use their knowledge of alchemy to craft explosives for demolishing obstacles and overwhelming foes.

## Grenadier Features

| Artificer Level | Feature                                                    |
| --------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| 3rd             | Tools of the Trade, Grenadier Explosives, Grenadier Spells |
| 5th             | Extra Attack, Quick Chemistry                              |
| 10th            | Sabotage                                                   |
| 15th            | Arcane Trigger, Master Grenadier                           |

## Tools of the Trade

_3rd-level Grenadier feature_

You gain proficiency with alchemist's supplies. If you are already proficient with them, you instead gain proficiency with another type of artisan's tools of your choice.

## Grenadier Explosives

_3rd-level Grenadier feature_

You learn to craft two explosives: a Hand Bomb and one other explosive of your choice from the list below. You learn another Grenadier Explosive of your choice at 5th, 11th, and 17th level. Whenever you would learn a new infusion, you can instead learn a Grenadier Explosive.

**Crafting Explosives.** During a long rest, you can spend 1 hour using alchemist's supplies to craft one copy of each explosive you know. These Tiny objects lose their potency at the end of your next long rest. As an action, you can use your alchemist's supplies and expend the spell slot specified in an explosive's description to craft another copy. It also loses its potency at the end of your next long rest.

**Using Explosives.** A creature can take the Use an Object action to throw an explosive at a point it can see within 60 feet. It explodes on impact, producing the effects in its description, centered on the point of impact. Any saving throw against an explosive uses your spell save DC.

### Grenadier Explosives

If an explosive has an Artificer-level prerequisite, you can learn it when you reach that level.

#### 3rd-level explosives

**Glittering Dust** _(1st-level spell slot)._ On impact, reflective particles cover a 20-foot cube. Creatures in the area must make a Dexterity saving throw. On a failure, a creature is covered in particles, and attack rolls against it have advantage. A creature can use its action to scrape off the particles, ending the effect.

**Hand Bomb** _(1st-level spell slot)._ Creatures within 5 feet of the impact must make a Dexterity saving throw, taking 2d6 fire damage on a failure or half as much on a success. Flammable objects in the area ignite. Damage increases to 4d6 at 5th level, 6d6 at 11th level, and 8d6 at 17th level.

**Seismic Charge** _(1st-level spell slot)._ Creatures within 10 feet must make a Strength saving throw. On a failure, a creature takes 2d6 thunder damage and any Large or smaller creature falls prone. On a success, it takes half damage and doesn't fall prone. Nonmagical structures made of wood, earth, or stone take maximum damage. Loose dirt or stone in the area becomes difficult terrain; a creature can use its action to clear a 5-foot square. Damage increases to 4d6 at 5th level, 6d6 at 11th level, and 8d6 at 17th level.

**Tangleroot** _(1st-level spell slot)._ Synthetic vines burst on impact. Creatures within 5 feet must make a Dexterity saving throw or be restrained by the vines for up to 1 minute. A restrained creature can use its action to make a Strength or Dexterity check (its choice) against your spell save DC, ending the restraint on a success.

**Thunder Pulse** _(1st-level spell slot)._ A thunderous wave, audible out to 300 feet, bursts from the impact. Creatures in a 15-foot cube must make a Constitution saving throw. On a failure, a creature takes 2d6 thunder damage and is pushed 10 feet away; on a success, it takes half damage and isn't moved. Unsecured objects in the area are pushed 10 feet away. Damage increases to 4d6 at 5th level, 6d6 at 11th level, and 8d6 at 17th level.

#### 5th-level explosives

**Arcane Explosive** _(2nd-level spell slot)._ You infuse the explosive with a spell you know. The spell must have a casting time of 1 action, can't require concentration, and must force a saving throw.

**Alchemical Webbing** _(2nd-level spell slot)._ Sticky webbing covers a 20-foot cube, creating difficult terrain and lightly obscuring the area. If no vertical surfaces, walls, or ceilings support it, the webbing collapses and dissolves. A creature that starts its turn in the webbing or enters it during its turn must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw or be restrained. A restrained creature can use its action to make a Strength or Dexterity check (its choice) against your spell save DC, ending the restraint on a success. The webbing lasts 1 minute and dissolves if it takes fire damage.

**Noxious Gas** _(2nd-level spell slot)._ A cloud of putrid yellow gas spreads in a 20-foot-radius sphere, spreads around corners, and heavily obscures the area for 1 minute. A creature that starts its turn in the cloud must succeed on a Constitution saving throw or spend its turn gagging and reeling. A creature immune to poison or that doesn't need to breathe automatically succeeds.

#### 11th-level explosives

**Greater Seismic Charge** _(3rd-level spell slot)._ Creatures within 30 feet must make a Strength saving throw. On a failure, a creature takes 6d6 thunder damage and any Huge or smaller creature falls prone. On a success, it takes half damage and doesn't fall prone. Nonmagical structures made of wood, earth, or stone take maximum damage. Loose dirt or stone in the area becomes difficult terrain; a creature can use its action to clear a 5-foot square. At 17th level, damage increases to 8d6.

**Vitriolic Charge** _(3rd-level spell slot)._ Creatures within 30 feet of the impact must make a Dexterity saving throw. On a failure, a creature takes 6d6 acid damage and 3d6 acid damage at the start of its next turn. At 17th level, the initial damage increases to 8d6 and the follow-up damage to 5d6.

#### 17th-level explosive

**Astral Vortex** _(4th-level spell slot)._ A rift to the Astral Plane opens. Creatures within 5 feet must succeed on a Charisma saving throw or be sucked through the rift for 1 minute. At the end of each of its turns, an affected creature can repeat the save, returning to its original space on a success. The rift then disappears.

## Extra Attack

_5th-level Grenadier feature_

You can attack twice, instead of once, whenever you take the Attack action on your turn. Moreover, you can take the Use an Object action in place of one or both of these attacks.

## Quick Chemistry

_5th-level Grenadier feature_

As a bonus action on your turn, you can expend a spell slot to craft a Grenadier Explosive you know.

## Sabotage

_10th-level Grenadier feature_

Any creature that is surprised or hasn't acted during the first round of combat has disadvantage on its initial saving throw against any Grenadier Explosive you throw.

In addition, whenever you roll initiative, you can craft one Hand Bomb without expending a spell slot, or craft one other explosive you know, expending a spell slot as normal.

## Arcane Trigger

_15th-level Grenadier feature_

When you craft an explosive, you can incorporate a rudimentary arcane remote. As an action, you can trigger one explosive you crafted with a remote if you are within 200 feet of it.

## Master Grenadier

_15th-level Grenadier feature_

Your explosive throwing range increases to 120 feet. If you are reduced to 0 hit points, you can throw up to two explosives at two different targets within range or drop them at your feet. Creatures have disadvantage on saving throws against these explosives. Once you use this benefit, you can't do so again until you finish a long rest.
