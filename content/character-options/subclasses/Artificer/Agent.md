# Agent

Agents use their inventions and magic to pursue quarry, gather information, and capture their targets.

## Agent Features

| Artificer Level | Feature                                                            |
| --------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 3rd             | Seeking Sight, Tools of the Trade, Agent Spells, Arcane Restraints |
| 5th             | Enhanced Sight, Extra Attack                                       |
| 9th             | Defensive Maneuvers                                                |
| 15th            | Master Agent                                                       |

## Seeking Sight

_3rd-level Agent feature_

You have crafted a wondrous device to aid you in pursuing your foes. Over the course of 1 hour, which can be during a short or long rest, you can use a set of tools to craft a Tiny object. You can have only one such object.

While wearing the object, you can use a bonus action to place an arcane mark on a creature or object you can see within 60 feet. The mark grants you the following benefits against the marked target:

- Whenever you make a Wisdom (Perception) or Wisdom (Survival) check related to it, you gain a bonus to the roll equal to your Intelligence modifier (minimum +1).
- You can use Intelligence instead of Strength or Dexterity for attack and damage rolls with weapons bearing one of your infusions.
- You always know the direction and distance to it while you are on the same plane of existence.

The mark lasts for 1 hour, but ends early if you mark another target or use a bonus action to end it. If the marked target is slain, you can use your reaction to mark another target you can see within range.

You can use this feature a number of times equal to your Intelligence modifier (minimum once), regaining all expended uses when you finish a long rest. If you have no uses remaining, you can expend a spell slot to use it again.

## Tools of the Trade

_3rd-level Agent feature_

You gain proficiency with martial weapons and smith's tools. If you are already proficient with smith's tools, you instead gain proficiency with one type of artisan's tools of your choice.

## Arcane Restraints

_3rd-level Agent feature_

Once per turn when you hit your marked target with a weapon bearing one of your infusions, you can force it to make a Strength saving throw against your spell save DC. On a failure, it is restrained, and your mark immediately ends. As an action, the target or another creature within 5 feet of it can make a Strength check against your spell save DC, ending the restrained condition on a success. The condition also ends if the target takes any damage.

## Enhanced Sight

_5th-level Agent feature_

When you mark a target with Seeking Sight, you immediately learn one of the following characteristics of your choice:

- Its spellcasting ability modifier and the level of its highest-level spell.
- Its creature type, alignment, and one language it speaks.
- Its civilization of origin and approximate age.

## Extra Attack

_5th-level Agent feature_

You can attack twice, instead of once, whenever you take the Attack action on your turn. Moreover, you can cast one of your artificer cantrips in place of one of these attacks.

## Defensive Maneuvers

_9th-level Agent feature_

You gain the following reactions:

- When your marked target makes an attack against you that you can see, you can use your reaction to add your Intelligence modifier to your AC against that attack.
- When your marked target misses you with a melee attack, you can use your reaction to force it to make a Dexterity saving throw against your spell save DC. On a failure, you knock it prone or grapple it (your choice). You must have a free hand to grapple it this way.

## Master Agent

_15th-level Agent feature_

Your marked target has disadvantage on any attack roll it makes against you and on the first saving throw you force it to make on each of your turns.
