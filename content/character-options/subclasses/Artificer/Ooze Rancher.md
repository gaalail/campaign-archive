# Ooze Rancher

Ooze Ranchers use an Ooze Containment Unit to raise a loyal ooze and harvest creature essences that grant it new abilities.

## Ooze Rancher Features

| Artificer Level | Feature                                                                          |
| --------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 3rd             | Domesticated Ooze, Ooze Containment Unit, Ooze Rancher Spells, Essence Harvester |
| 5th             | Extra Attack                                                                     |
| 9th             | Ooze Adept                                                                       |
| 15th            | Master Rancher                                                                   |

## Domesticated Ooze

_3rd-level Ooze Rancher feature_

You have captured, bred, or created a Domesticated Ooze to serve you. Its stat block appears below and uses your proficiency bonus (PB) and spell save DC. It is friendly to you and your allies and obeys your commands.

In combat, it acts on your turn. It can move and use its reaction on its own, but takes only the Dodge action unless you use a bonus action to order it to take an action from its stat block or another action. Whenever you take the Attack action, you can forgo one of your attacks to order your Ooze to attack. If you are incapacitated, it acts on its own to defend itself and you to the best of its ability.

**Amorphous.** The Ooze can move through a space as narrow as 1 inch wide without squeezing.  
**Might of the Master.** Add your PB to any ability check or saving throw the Ooze makes.

#### Actions

**Slam.** _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +2 + PB to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 1d8 + PB acid damage.

## Ooze Containment Unit

_3rd-level Ooze Rancher feature_

You create a Small item that creates and holds your Domesticated Ooze. It can be worn as a backpack or carried with two hands.

While wearing the Unit and with your Ooze within 10 feet, you can use a bonus action to store it in the Unit or eject it into an unoccupied space you can see within 10 feet. While stored, the Ooze can't take damage or be targeted. The Unit can hold only one Ooze at a time.

If your Ooze is killed, you can use your action and expend a 1st-level or higher spell slot to create another Ooze inside the Unit. At the end of each long rest, you can create a new Ooze in the Unit without expending a spell slot. You can have only one Ooze at a time; creating a new one causes any previous Ooze to dissolve into goo. If your Unit is destroyed, you can craft another from spare parts over the course of a long rest.

## Essence Harvester

_3rd-level Ooze Rancher feature_

As an action, touch your Ooze Containment Unit to a creature that has died within the last minute and harvest its Essence. The Unit can hold a number of Essences equal to your Artificer level. If you harvest one while at your maximum, choose one Essence to replace.

While your Ooze is inside the Unit, you can use an action to infuse it with an Essence, granting the benefits described below. An Essence is expended when infused. Your Ooze can benefit from one Essence at a time. It can benefit from more at once as you gain levels: two at 5th level, three at 9th level, and four at 15th level.

## Extra Attack

_5th-level Ooze Rancher feature_

You can attack twice, rather than once, when you take the Attack action on your turn. Moreover, once per turn when you order your Ooze to attack, it can make two attacks.

## Ooze Adept

_9th-level Ooze Rancher feature_

You gain resistance to acid and poison damage. In addition, when you infuse your Ooze with an Essence, the Essence is no longer expended.

## Master Rancher

_15th-level Ooze Rancher feature_

Whenever you use your Ooze Containment Unit to create an Ooze, choose whether it is Small, Medium, or Large.

## Creature Essences

These features apply to your Domesticated Ooze when you infuse it with the corresponding Essence.

- **Aberrant Essence.** The Ooze gains resistance to psychic damage. When it hits a creature with Slam, that target has disadvantage on the first Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma check or saving throw it makes before the start of your next turn. Its Slam can deal psychic damage instead.
- **Bestial Essence.** The Ooze's speed increases by 10 feet, and it gains a climbing or swimming speed (your choice) equal to its walking speed. It adds your Intelligence modifier (minimum +1) to Wisdom (Perception) checks. Its appearance becomes primal and wild.
- **Celestial Essence.** The Ooze gains resistance to necrotic and radiant damage and a flying speed equal to its walking speed. As an action, it can grant a creature it touches temporary hit points equal to your Intelligence modifier. Its Slam can deal fire or radiant damage instead.
- **Draconic Essence.** The Ooze gains resistance to cold, fire, poison, lightning, or thunder damage (your choice) and a flying speed equal to its walking speed. When you order it to make a Slam attack, it can make one additional Slam attack. Its Slam can deal the type of damage to which it has resistance instead.
- **Elemental Essence.** The Ooze gains resistance to bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing damage and to cold, fire, or lightning damage (choose one of these types). It can replace a Slam attack with a burst of elemental energy. Creatures within 10 feet must make a Dexterity saving throw; on a failure, they take damage of the chosen type equal to the damage of its Slam attack.
- **Faerie Essence.** The Ooze gains resistance to psychic damage and immunity to the charmed condition. After it makes a Slam attack, it can teleport to an unoccupied space it can see within 30 feet. Its Slam can deal poison or psychic damage instead.
- **Fiendish Essence.** The Ooze gains resistance to fire and necrotic damage. When you command it to make a Slam attack, it makes one additional Slam attack. A creature it hits can't regain hit points until the start of your next turn. Its Slam can deal poison or fire damage instead.
- **Monstrous Essence.** The Ooze's size increases by one category and its speed increases by 10 feet. Its Slam can deal slashing damage instead.
- **Titanic Essence.** The Ooze's size increases by one category, it gains bonus hit points equal to twice your Artificer level, and it has advantage on Strength checks and saving throws. Its Slam can deal bludgeoning damage instead.
- **Verdant Essence.** The Ooze takes on a plantlike appearance, is immune to the poisoned condition, and can't be moved against its will. It gains temporary hit points equal to your Intelligence modifier (minimum 1) at the start of each of its turns. Its Slam can deal poison or slashing damage instead.
- **Undying Essence.** The Ooze gains immunity to necrotic and poison damage and to the poisoned condition. Its Slam can deal necrotic damage instead; when it does, the Ooze gains temporary hit points equal to half the necrotic damage dealt.
