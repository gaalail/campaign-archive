# Venomist

Venomists study toxins, venoms, and poisons from monsters and animals, reproducing them for personal use and creating signature poisons.

## Venomist Features

| Artificer Level | Feature                                                              |
| --------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 3rd             | Tools of the Trade, Poisoner Adept, Venomist Spells, Infused Poisons |
| 5th             | Potent Venom                                                         |
| 9th             | Inoculation                                                          |
| 15th            | Deadly Toxins                                                        |

## Tools of the Trade

_3rd-level Venomist feature_

You gain proficiency with poisoner's kits and blowguns. If you are already proficient with poisoner's kits, you instead gain proficiency with another type of artisan's tools of your choice.

Moreover, whenever you make a poisoner's kit check and have at least 10 minutes to complete it, you can treat a roll of 9 or lower on the d20 as a 10.

## Poisoner Adept

_3rd-level Venomist feature_

You learn the _[[Poison Spray]]_ cantrip, which doesn't count against the number of cantrips you know. When you cast _[[Poison Spray]]_ using a poisoner's kit as the spellcasting focus and target a creature you touch, it has disadvantage on its Constitution saving throw.

In addition, you can take the Use an Object action as a bonus action on each of your turns, but you can use it only to use a poisoner's kit or apply a vial of poison. Poisons you craft use your artificer spell save DC instead of their normal DC.

## Infused Poisons

_3rd-level Venomist feature_

Your number of infusions known increases by one, and the additional infusion must be chosen from the Venomist Infusions below. Your infusions known increases by one again at 9th level and at 15th level; each of these additional infusions must also be a Venomist Infusion.

At the end of each long rest, you can use a poisoner's kit to create one vial of each Infused Poison you know without expending a spell slot. These poisons lose their potency at the end of your next long rest. As an action, you can use a poisoner's kit and expend the spell slot listed in an infusion's description to craft another vial of that poison.

### Venomist Infusions

If an infusion has a level prerequisite, you can learn it when you meet that prerequisite.

#### Craft Minor Poison

_Prerequisite: 3rd-level Artificer; item: one empty vial filled with water_

As an action, expend a 1st-level spell slot and use a poisoner's kit to craft a vial of Minor Poison. It retains its potency until the end of your next long rest. A creature can take the Use an Object action to apply it to one weapon or piece of ammunition it is holding. On its next hit, the weapon deals poison damage instead of its normal damage, and the target must succeed on a Constitution saving throw or be poisoned for 1 minute. The creature repeats the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on a success.

#### Replicate Poison

_Prerequisite: 3rd-level Artificer_

Over the course of a long rest, use a poisoner's kit to examine a vial of poison you touch. At the end of the rest, you can replace this infusion with the knowledge of how to create that poison. Once you do, you can craft one vial of it at the end of each long rest. You can learn this infusion multiple times, each time learning to recreate a different poison you discover, buy, or craft.

#### Craft Greater Poison

_Prerequisite: 5th-level Artificer; item: one empty vial filled with water_

As an action, expend a 2nd-level spell slot and use a poisoner's kit to craft a vial of Greater Poison. It retains its potency until the end of your next long rest. A creature can take the Use an Object action to apply it to one weapon or piece of ammunition it is holding. On its next hit, the weapon deals an additional 2d6 poison damage, and the target must succeed on a Constitution saving throw or suffer the following effects for 1 minute:

- It has disadvantage on attack rolls and ability checks.
- Its speed is halved, and it can't take reactions.

The poisoned creature repeats the saving throw at the end of each of its turns. On a success, the effects end. On a failure, it takes an additional 2d6 poison damage.

#### Craft Advanced Poison

_Prerequisite: 9th-level Artificer; item: one empty vial filled with water_

As an action, expend a 3rd-level spell slot and use a poisoner's kit to craft a vial of Advanced Poison. It retains its potency until the end of your next long rest. A creature can take the Use an Object action to apply it to one weapon or piece of ammunition it is holding. On a hit, the weapon deals an additional 6d6 acid damage, and the target must succeed on a Constitution saving throw or have disadvantage on all attack rolls and ability checks for 1 hour. If it fails by 5 or more, it is also blinded for the duration.

As an action, the poisoned creature can repeat the saving throw. On a success, these effects end. On a failure, it takes an additional 2d6 acid damage.

#### Craft Masterwork Poison

_Prerequisite: 15th-level Artificer; item: one empty vial filled with water_

As an action, expend a 4th-level spell slot and use a poisoner's kit to craft a vial of Masterwork Poison. It retains its potency until the end of your next long rest. A creature can take the Use an Object action to apply it to one weapon or piece of ammunition it is holding. On a hit, the weapon deals an additional 10d6 necrotic damage, and the target must succeed on a Constitution saving throw or have disadvantage on all attack rolls and ability checks until a 5th-level or higher spell neutralizes the poison, such as _[[Greater Restoration]]_. If it fails by 5 or more, it is incapacitated and can't move or speak until cured.

## Potent Venom

_5th-level Venomist feature_

Whenever one of your poisons or an artificer spell you cast deals acid, necrotic, or poison damage, you can add your Intelligence modifier (minimum +1) to one damage roll.

## Inoculation

_9th-level Venomist feature_

You are immune to the poisoned condition and have resistance to acid and poison damage.

## Deadly Toxins

_15th-level Venomist feature_

Whenever you, or a poison you created, deals acid, poison, or necrotic damage, you can treat a roll of 1 or 2 on any of its damage dice as a 3.
