# Static Conductor

Static Conductors harness lightning and store its power in a signature suit, using it to protect allies, stun foes, and move with startling speed.

## Static Conductor Features

| Artificer Level | Feature                                           |
| --------------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| 3rd             | Tools of the Trade, Static Suit, Conductor Spells |
| 5th             | Channel Lightning, Extra Attack                   |
| 10th            | Improved Efficiency                               |
| 15th            | Master Lightningrod                               |

## Tools of the Trade

_3rd-level Static Conductor feature_

You gain proficiency with smith's tools. If you are already proficient with them, you instead gain proficiency with another type of artisan's tools of your choice.

## Static Suit

_3rd-level Static Conductor feature_

During a long rest, you can spend 1 hour using smith's tools to modify one set of light or medium armor you touch, transforming it into a Static Suit. You can have only one suit at a time, and only you can use it. Crafting another causes the previous suit to lose its power.

While wearing the suit, you have resistance to lightning damage and can use it as a spellcasting focus for your artificer spells.

**Lightning Rod.** When you take lightning damage while wearing the suit, you can use your reaction to gain temporary hit points equal to the lightning damage you would have taken after resistance, instead of taking the damage. Whenever you expend a 1st-level or higher spell slot to cast a spell that deals lightning damage, you gain temporary hit points equal to twice the slot's level.

**Electrical Jolt.** While wearing the suit, you know _shocking grasp_ and _spare the dying_. They count as artificer spells for you. Your unarmed strikes deal 1d6 lightning damage on a hit, and you can use Strength or Intelligence for their attack and damage rolls. The damage increases to 1d8 at 10th level and 1d10 at 15th level.

**Infusions.** Your Static Suit can bear one infusion meant for a suit of armor, a spellcasting focus, or a metal rod.

## Conductor Spells

_3rd-level Static Conductor feature_

You always have the following spells prepared. They don't count against the number of spells you can prepare.

| Artificer Level | Spells                                  |
| --------------- | --------------------------------------- |
| 3rd             | _Thunderous smite, witch bolt_          |
| 5th             | _[[Misty Step]], [[Shatter]]_           |
| 9th             | _[[Lightning Bolt]], thunder step_      |
| 13th            | _[[Freedom of Movement]], storm sphere_ |
| 17th            | _Far step, steel wind strike_           |

## Channel Lightning

_5th-level Static Conductor feature_

While you have temporary hit points from Lightning Rod, you can expend those temporary hit points to use the following features:

- **Quick Step.** Expend 1 temporary hit point to take the Dash or Disengage action as a bonus action on your turn.
- **Tasing Touch.** When you hit a target with a weapon attack, expend 3 temporary hit points to force it to make a Constitution saving throw against your spell save DC. On a failure, its speed becomes 0 until the start of your next turn.

## Extra Attack

_5th-level Static Conductor feature_

You can attack twice, instead of once, whenever you take the Attack action. Also, whenever you use your action to cast a spell, you can make one weapon attack as a bonus action.

## Improved Efficiency

_10th-level Static Conductor feature_

Your Channel Lightning features improve:

- **Quick Step.** You gain the benefits of both Dash and Disengage whenever you use this feature.
- **Tasing Touch.** On a failed save, the target is also stunned until the start of your next turn.

## Master Lightningrod

_15th-level Static Conductor feature_

While you have temporary hit points from Lightning Rod, your speed increases by 10 feet and you gain one additional reaction each round. You can use only one reaction per turn.
