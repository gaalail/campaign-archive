# Wagoneer

Wagoneers build Wondrous Caravans that carry travelers in comfort and serve as mobile tools in combat.

## Wagoneer Features

| Artificer Level | Feature                                               |
| --------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| 3rd             | Tools of the Trade, Wagoneer Spells, Wondrous Caravan |
| 5th             | Mystical Locomotion, Wondrous Ward                    |
| 10th            | Enhanced Caravan                                      |
| 15th            | Master Wagoneer                                       |

## Tools of the Trade

_3rd-level Wagoneer feature_

You gain proficiency with smith's tools and land vehicles. If you are already proficient with smith's tools or land vehicles, you gain proficiency with one type of artisan's tools of your choice.

## Wondrous Caravan

_3rd-level Wagoneer feature_

During a long rest, you can spend 1 hour using smith's tools to construct a Large Caravan. You choose its appearance, which doesn't affect its statistics. You can have only one Caravan at a time; constructing another causes the first to fall into disrepair and become unusable.

You can use tinker's or smith's tools to repair the Caravan. For every 10 minutes you spend repairing it, it regains hit points equal to your Artificer level.

The Caravan is a vehicle that can be driven by any creature proficient with land vehicles. Its stat block uses your Intelligence modifier, PB, and artificer spell save DC. While riding on it, you can use it as a spellcasting focus for your artificer spells. While riding on it, you use its speed instead of your own, and it acts during your turn. You can use an action to use an action in its stat block.

The Caravan can bear one infusion meant for boots (wheels), armor (chassis), or a melee weapon (ram). When you create it, choose two Upgrades below. It gains one additional upgrade at 5th level (three total), 10th level (four total), and 15th level (five total).

### Wondrous Caravan

_Large vehicle, 600 lb._

| Statistic                 | Value                                                                    |
| ------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Maximum Creature Capacity | 1 Large, 3 Medium, or 6 Small or smaller creatures                       |
| Maximum Cargo Capacity    | 250 lb.                                                                  |
| Armor Class               | 8 + your Intelligence modifier + PB (natural armor)                      |
| Hit Points                | 5 + six times your Artificer level                                       |
| Speed                     | 30 ft.                                                                   |
| Damage Immunities         | Poison, psychic                                                          |
| Condition Immunities      | Blinded, charmed, deafened, frightened, paralyzed, poisoned, unconscious |

**STR** 18 (+4), **DEX** 12 (+1), **CON** 16 (+3)

**Expert Handling.** If you are driving the Caravan and aren't incapacitated, add your PB to any ability check or saving throw the Caravan is forced to make.

**Inanimate.** The Caravan is incapacitated without a driver. When it is forced to make an Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma ability check or saving throw, its driver is considered the target of the effect instead.

**Mechanical Momentum.** If the Caravan moves at least 20 feet straight toward a creature and hits it with a Ram attack on the same turn, that target must succeed on a Strength saving throw or be knocked prone.

**Swerve.** When the upright Caravan is forced to make a Dexterity saving throw, its rider can use their reaction to grant it advantage on the save.

#### Rider Actions

**Ram.** _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +4 + PB to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 1d10 + 4 + PB bludgeoning damage.

## Mystical Locomotion

_5th-level Wagoneer feature_

Your Caravan ignores the effects of magical and nonmagical difficult terrain.

## Wondrous Ward

_5th-level Wagoneer feature_

Creatures riding in your Caravan can use a bonus action to gain temporary hit points equal to your Intelligence modifier.

## Enhanced Caravan

_10th-level Wagoneer feature_

Your Caravan gains a special room leading to an extradimensional space the size of a 40-foot cube. Creatures in the space can't be targeted by effects originating outside it. The Caravan's driver must remain outside the space. If the Caravan is destroyed, creatures and objects in the extradimensional space are ejected into the closest unoccupied space.

## Master Wagoneer

_15th-level Wagoneer feature_

Your Caravan can maneuver without a driver while you concentrate on this effect as if concentrating on a spell. When you do so, it acts during your turn. If it is traveling at a normal pace, the effect doesn't require concentration, and you can take a short or long rest.

## Caravan Upgrades

When an upgrade requires a saving throw, it uses your artificer spell save DC. If an upgrade has an Artificer-level prerequisite, you can choose it once you meet that prerequisite.

- **Defense Systems.** When a creature hits the Caravan with a melee attack, its pilot can use a reaction to force the attacker to make a Dexterity saving throw. On a failure, it takes 1d8 + your Intelligence modifier lightning damage.
- **Harpoon.** As an action, the Caravan's pilot can make a ranged spell attack against a target they can see within 30 feet. On a hit, the target takes 1d12 piercing damage and is impaled by a harpoon connected to the Caravan by a 30-foot rope. A creature can use its action to remove the harpoon with a successful Strength check against your spell save DC. As a bonus action, the pilot can retract the harpoon. If the target is larger than the Caravan, the Caravan is pulled up to 30 feet toward it; if smaller, the target is pulled up to 30 feet toward the Caravan. The harpoon must be reloaded as an action.
- **Protective Plating.** The Caravan gains a +2 bonus to AC, and creatures riding in it have the benefits of half cover.
- **Restful Lodging.** A creature riding in the Caravan that expends Hit Dice to regain hit points or regains hit points from a spell regains additional hit points equal to your Intelligence modifier. Creatures other than the driver can take short and long rests while riding in the Caravan.
- **Aquatic Adaptation (5th-level prerequisite).** The Caravan gains a swimming speed equal to its speed. Underwater, it holds enough air for creatures within its maximum creature capacity to breathe normally for up to 1 hour.
- **Arcane Enhancement (5th-level prerequisite).** Whenever you cast an artificer spell using the Caravan as its spellcasting focus, add 1d8 to one damage roll or to the hit points restored by the spell.
- **Climbing Treads (5th-level prerequisite).** The Caravan gains a climbing speed equal to half its speed, including on sheer surfaces and upside down on ceilings. The surface must be wide enough for it to drive on.
- **Adamantine Plating (10th-level prerequisite).** Creatures riding in the Caravan have three-quarters cover, and it gains resistance to nonmagical bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing damage.
- **Aeronautical Propulsion (15th-level prerequisite).** The Caravan gains a flying speed of 60 feet and has advantage on Dexterity checks and saving throws while flying.
- **Miniature Caravan (15th-level prerequisite).** As an action, the Caravan's pilot can cause it to shrink to Small size. Creatures riding in it shrink to fit. This effect ends when a creature dismounts or after 1 hour.
