# Path of the Reaper

Reapers slip through shadows and the Mortis Veil, delivering swift, precise strikes that leave little warning before death arrives.

## Tool Proficiency

_3rd-level Path of the Reaper feature_

You gain proficiency with alchemist's supplies. If you already have this proficiency, you gain proficiency with one other type of artisan's tools of your choice.

## Chained Strikes

_3rd-level Path of the Reaper feature_

When you use your action to target a creature with a melee weapon attack or melee spell attack and miss, you can use your bonus action to make a melee weapon attack against the same target.

When you use your action to target a creature with a melee weapon attack or melee spell attack and hit, you can make a melee weapon attack against the same target as part of the same action, or expend 1 mortis point to unleash a chained strike. As part of the chained strike, teleport up to 15 feet to an unoccupied space within reach of a different hostile creature you can see, then make one melee weapon attack against it as part of the same action. A creature can't be targeted more than once in a chained strike each turn. You can use this feature only once per turn.

At 10th level, after you hit with a chained strike, you can expend 1 additional mortis point to teleport to a third target and make another melee weapon attack. At 18th level, you can expend 1 additional mortis point to teleport to a fourth target and attack it in the same way.

## Reaper's Weaponry

_3rd-level Path of the Reaper feature_

- You can use your Intelligence modifier instead of Strength or Dexterity for attack and damage rolls with melee weapons you are proficient with.
- The first time you hit a creature with a melee weapon attack on your turn, the attack deals extra necrotic damage equal to your Intelligence modifier (minimum 1).

## Veilpiercer

_6th-level Path of the Reaper feature_

**Necrotic Strikes.** Your necrotic damage from Reaper's Weaponry ignores resistance, and immunity to necrotic damage is treated as resistance instead.

**Severing Rhythm.** When you use your action to cast a cantrip, you can expend 1 mortis point to make a melee weapon attack as a bonus action.

## Mortis Strike

_10th-level Path of the Reaper feature_

When you hit a creature with a melee weapon attack as part of Chained Strikes, you can use a Veil Technique that triggers on a hit with a weapon attack, even if you have already used one. You must still pay the technique's mortis point cost.

## Death's Final Cut

_18th-level Path of the Reaper feature_

You gain the following benefits:

**Soul Conversion.** When you hit a creature with a melee weapon attack or melee spell attack, you can expend a spell slot of 1st to 5th level to regain mortis points equal to twice the spell slot's level. Once you use this feature, you can't use it again until you finish a short or long rest.

**Soul Severance.** When you hit a creature with half its hit points or fewer remaining with a melee weapon attack or melee spell attack, you can expend 5 mortis points to attempt a soul-rending strike. The target must make a Constitution saving throw (DC = 8 + your Intelligence modifier + your proficiency bonus). On a failed save, it takes 6d10 necrotic damage, can't regain hit points until the end of your next turn, and has disadvantage on saving throws against your spells for the same duration. On a successful save, it takes half as much damage and suffers no additional effects. This damage ignores resistance and immunity to necrotic damage.
