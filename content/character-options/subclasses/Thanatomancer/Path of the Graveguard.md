# Path of the Graveguard

Graveguards turn the power of death inward, becoming enduring defenders who drain vitality from those who challenge them.

## Tool Proficiency

_3rd-level Path of the Graveguard feature_

You gain proficiency with smith's tools. If you already have this proficiency, you gain proficiency with one other type of artisan's tools of your choice.

## Death's Bulwark

_3rd-level Path of the Graveguard feature_

You gain proficiency with medium armor, shields, and martial weapons.

While wearing medium armor or wielding a shield, when you make a saving throw against a spell or magical effect, you can expend 1 mortis point to add your Intelligence modifier (minimum +1) to the roll.

**Grave Binding.** The first time on your turn when you deal necrotic damage to a Large or smaller creature with a cantrip or melee weapon attack, you can force it to succeed on a Strength saving throw (DC = 8 + your Intelligence modifier + your proficiency bonus) or have its speed reduced to 0 until the start of your next turn.

## Grim Vitality

_3rd-level Path of the Graveguard feature_

Whenever you deal damage to a creature with a melee weapon attack or deal necrotic damage with a spell or feature that targets only one creature, you gain temporary hit points equal to the damage dealt. These temporary hit points last until the start of your next turn.

## Warden's Intervention

_6th-level Path of the Graveguard feature_

You gain the following benefits:

**Veil of Retaliation.** When a hostile creature hits an ally within 30 feet of you with an attack, you can use your reaction to expend 1 mortis point and immediately use a Veil Technique that uses a reaction, targeting the attacker as if the technique came from the ally. You must still spend the technique's normal mortis point cost.

**Warding Aegis.** As a reaction when an ally within 15 feet of you is targeted by an attack, expend 2 mortis points to teleport to an unoccupied space within 5 feet of the attacker. The attack targets you instead. Until the end of the attacker's turn, you gain a bonus to AC equal to your Intelligence modifier (minimum +1) against that attacker. At 13th level, you can instead expend 3 mortis points to use this feature to assist an ally within 30 feet of you.

## Sentinel of the Veil

_10th-level Path of the Graveguard feature_

As a bonus action, expend 2 mortis points to tether a creature you can see within 10 feet of you with deathly energy for 1 minute. If the tethered creature moves more than 10 feet away from you, it takes necrotic damage equal to twice your Intelligence modifier + your proficiency bonus. You can also force the creature to make a Constitution saving throw (DC = 8 + your Intelligence modifier + your proficiency bonus). On a failed save, it is pulled up to 10 feet toward you and knocked prone.

## Deathless Bastion

_18th-level Path of the Graveguard feature_

You have advantage on ability checks and saving throws against the grappled, paralyzed, prone, and stunned conditions. You can't be forcibly moved against your will.

**Eternal Warden.** As a reaction when an ally within 30 feet of you would drop to 0 hit points, expend 5 mortis points or a 5th-level or higher spell slot to cause the ally to drop to 1 hit point instead. Once you use this reaction, you can't use it again until you finish a long rest.
