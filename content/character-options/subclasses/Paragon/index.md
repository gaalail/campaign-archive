# Paragon Subclasses

**3 subclasses available** | Choose at 1st level | Orders (Corpus Angelus)

The **Paragon** is a Corpus Angelus class (not in standard D&D 5e). These are custom "holy warrior" subclass options.

## How to Choose

1. **Read all orders** — Click the names above
2. **Pick one matching your philosophy** — Roleplay + mechanics
3. **At character creation** — You choose your order
4. **Tell your DM** — Declare your order
