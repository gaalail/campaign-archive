# Spirit Knight (Witchblade)

Some covens of witches practice witchcraft not to sling spells, but to enhance their martial prowess in battle. Warriors who choose this path are known as **Spirit Knights** (or **Witchblades** within witch traditions). Renowned for their supernatural adaptability on the battlefield, Spirit Knights imbue their weapons with the echoes and spirits of legendary warriors who came before them, absorbing their fighting techniques and strategies.

A Spirit Knight functions as a versatile "mimic warrior", channeling the abilities and archetype features of other fighter disciplines to match whatever tactical scenario they face.

### Witchmark

A Spirit Knight's witchmark typically appears as scars across their flesh. Instead of a single scar growing larger, new scars appear along their body as they grow in power, mirroring the mortal strikes dealt to the warrior spirits bound to their weapon.

## Spirit Blade

_3rd-level Spirit Knight feature_

Your weapon becomes an arcane beacon that pierces the veil between the Material and Ethereal Planes. By meditating with any weapon with which you are proficient for 10 minutes, you transform it into your spirit blade.

- Your spirit blade counts as a magical weapon for overcoming resistance and immunity to nonmagical attacks and damage.
- You always know the precise location of your spirit blade as long as it remains on the same plane of existence as you.
- You can only bond with one spirit blade at a time.

## Spirits of Masters

_3rd-level Spirit Knight feature_

You can summon a fallen martial master to your blade, gaining their specialized knowledge in battle:

- Select two martial archetypes available to fighters. After finishing a long rest, you can meditate for 10 minutes to summon a master spirit belonging to one of those archetypes into your spirit blade.
- You gain all features granted at 3rd level by the chosen archetype. For subclasses that grant spellcasting (such as Eldritch Knight) or specific resource pools, you gain the resources normally granted at 3rd level. The spirit departs at the end of your next long rest, allowing you to commune with the same or another spirit.
- You can expand your catalog of master spirits over time: you can train for 7 days with another fighter of a different archetype, or deliver the finishing blow to a fighter possessing that archetype. You cannot learn archetypes from fellow Spirit Knights.

## Spiritual Style

_7th-level Spirit Knight feature_

You can channel spirits to acquire alternative fighting styles. Select a fighting style other than the one you already possess. When you finish a long rest and meditate to summon a master spirit, you also manifest this additional fighting style into your blade.

You can learn new fighting styles over time through the same methods as master spirits (training for 7 days with a practitioner or delivering a killing blow, choosing between the archetype or the fighting style).

## Battlefield Witchcraft

_10th-level Spirit Knight feature_

When you deal the finishing blow to a hostile creature of Challenge Rating 1 or higher (or a creature of significant threat), or when you score a critical hit, you can choose to regain one additional use of **Second Wind**.

You cannot gain more than one additional use of Second Wind per turn. All additional uses gained through this feature expire once combat ends and you leave initiative. You can use this feature a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus, regaining all expended uses upon completing a long rest.

## Exalted Spirit Blade

_15th-level Spirit Knight feature_

The spiritual energy coursing through your weapon allows it to reshape itself on command. As an action, you can transform your spirit blade into any weapon type with which you are proficient.

The blade retains its original material colors, magical bonuses to attack and damage rolls, and special enchantments. If transformed into a ranged weapon requiring ammunition (such as a bow or crossbow), it automatically manifests spectral ammunition when fired (though loading properties and recovering thrown weapons still apply).

## Master of Spirits

_18th-level Spirit Knight feature_

When you select a martial archetype after a long rest using **Spirits of Masters**, you also unlock that archetype's 18th-level feature(s) alongside its 3rd-level features. If the chosen archetype grants spell slots or specific scaling resources, you possess the resources equivalent to an 18th-level fighter in that archetype.
