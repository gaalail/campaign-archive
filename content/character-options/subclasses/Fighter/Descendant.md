# Descendant

Guided by legacy, Descendants are trained from childhood to combat ancient enemies and uphold creeds of honor honed by techniques passed down from their militant ancestors. Some Descendants are fueled by vengeance for families lost, others dedicate themselves to hunting down every vestige of their sworn foes, and some few find themselves locked into battles not of their choosing.

---

## Ancestral Weapon

_3rd-level Descendant feature_

You inherit an heirloom whip from your ancestors. You gain a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls with this weapon, and it counts as magical for the purpose of overcoming resistance and immunity to nonmagical attacks and damage.

## Whip Warrior

_3rd-level Descendant feature_

You have developed specialized prowess fighting with whips. As an action, you can execute one of the following special attacks:

- **Whiplash:** Make a whip attack against each creature in a line whose length equals double your whip's reach (typically 20 feet). Make a single attack roll and compare it against the AC of each target in the line.
- **Whirl:** Make a whip attack against each creature within a radius equal to your whip's reach (typically 10 feet). Make a single attack roll and compare it against the AC of each target in the radius.

## Stinging Strike

_7th-level Descendant feature_

When you hit a creature with your ancestral whip, you can use a bonus action to inflict one of the following conditions on the target:

- **Deafened:** Lasts for 5 rounds.
- **Grappled:** Lasts until the creature breaks the grapple.
- **Prone:** Lasts for 5 rounds, whereupon the creature can stand again.
- **Stunned:** Lasts until the start of your next turn.

You can use this feature once, regaining the ability upon finishing a short or long rest.

## Ancestral Technique

_10th-level Descendant feature_

You have perfected the fighting style of an allied ancestor. Choose one of the following classes:

- **Barbarian:** You gain the barbarian’s **Rage** ability and can use it twice, regaining expended uses on a long rest.
- **Monk:** You gain the monk’s **Martial Arts** feature. Your unarmed damage die is 1d4 (progressing normally if you multiclass into monk).
- **Paladin:** You can innately use **Divine Smite** as though expending a 1st-level spell slot once per short or long rest.
- **Ranger:** You gain the ranger’s **Favored Enemy** feature and can cast [[hunter's-mark]] innately once per short or long rest.
- **Rogue:** You gain the rogue’s **Sneak Attack** feature (1d6 damage, progressing normally if you multiclass into rogue).

## Unending Rivalry

_15th-level Descendant feature_

Choose a creature type other than humanoid. Your AC gains a **+2 bonus** against attacks from creatures of that type, you gain proficiency in any saving throws forced by creatures of that type, deal an extra **2 points of damage** on hits against them, and score critical hits on rolls of **19 or 20**.

## Vengeful Heir

_18th-level Descendant feature_

When a hostile creature survives combat against you, you can declare it your **Mortal Enemy** (you can have only one Mortal Enemy at a time). Regardless of its creature type, your **Unending Rivalry** benefits apply against it in future engagements.

Furthermore, when you target your Mortal Enemy with **Stinging Strike**, you do not expend your use of the feature.
