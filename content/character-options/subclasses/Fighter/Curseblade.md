# Curseblade

Whether inherited or stumbled upon, the Curseblade is a keeper of darkness, perhaps themselves cursed to shepherd powers beyond them. Beholden to the will of a weapon with a strength all its own, these warriors draw upon and control sentient, cursed weapons that threaten to overwhelm them should they stray even an inch from their vigil. Most Curseblades are victims of happenstance drawn into their role by chance, while others belong to orders that bind hateful and vicious spirits to wood and metal in an effort to control their powers for the greater good, or for devilish whims of their own.

---

## Imbued Blade

_3rd-level Curseblade feature_

You infuse any single, mundane melee weapon of your choice with a supernatural spirit, creating your **curseblade** (which does not necessarily have to be a blade). This weapon acts as a spellcasting focus for your spells, satisfies the need for somatic components while wielded, counts as magical, and you cannot be disarmed of it against your will unless you are incapacitated.

Additionally, when you first infuse the blade at 3rd level, and again at 7th, 10th, 15th, and 18th level, your weapon gains a basic property from the following list:

- **Dualistic:** As a bonus action, you can create a copy of your weapon for 1 minute as long as it possesses the light property.
- **Empowered:** Your weapon gains a +1 magic bonus to attack and damage rolls and counts as magical. This stacks with existing magic bonuses, up to a maximum of +3.
- **Featherweight:** Your weapon gains the light property if it did not already possess it. (Cannot be added to a two-handed weapon).
- **Halfhand:** You treat a weapon with the versatile property as if it is always being wielded in two hands.
- **Overbearing:** When you hit a creature for the first time on your turn, you can shove that creature for free (once per turn).
- **Overreach:** Your weapon’s reach extends by 5 feet.
- **Returning:** Your weapon gains the thrown property with a range of 20/60 feet. When thrown, the weapon returns to your hand immediately after the attack is resolved. If it already possessed the thrown property, its range is doubled.

## Spellcasting

_3rd-level Curseblade feature_

Your weapon manifests magical power, instilling you with the ability to cast spells through it. Choose from the available spirit types: **Arcane**, **Primal**, or **Occult**. This choice determines your available spell list, cantrips, and spellcasting ability modifier:

- **Arcane:** Intelligence modifier; Sorcerer spell list.
- **Primal:** Wisdom modifier; Druid spell list.
- **Occult:** Charisma modifier; Warlock spell list.

### Cantrips

You learn two cantrips of your choice from the spell list corresponding to your spirit type. You learn an additional cantrip of your choice at 10th level from the same list.

### Spell Slots

The Curseblade Spellcasting table shows how many spell slots you have to cast your spells of 1st level and higher. You regain all expended spell slots when you finish a long rest.

### Spells Known of 1st Level and Higher

You know three 1st-level spells of your choice from your spirit type's spell list. The Spells Known column shows when you learn more spells. Whenever you gain a level in this class, you can replace one spell you know with another spell of your choice from the same spell list for which you have spell slots.

### Spellcasting Ability

Your spellcasting ability depends on your spirit type:
- **Spell save DC** = 8 + your proficiency bonus + your Intelligence (Arcane), Wisdom (Primal), or Charisma (Occult) modifier.
- **Spell attack modifier** = your proficiency bonus + your Intelligence (Arcane), Wisdom (Primal), or Charisma (Occult) modifier.

## Spirit Blade

_7th-level Curseblade feature_

As a bonus action, you wreathe your curseblade in supernatural energy for 1 minute. Your weapon deals an additional **1d6 damage** of an element you choose and you gain advantage on attack rolls against specific creature types based on your spirit type:

- **Arcane:** Cold or fire damage. Creature types: Aberration, Construct, Dragon, Elemental.
- **Primal:** Acid or lightning damage. Creature types: Beast, Fey, Monstrosity, Undead.
- **Occult:** Necrotic or poison damage. Creature types: Aberration, Fiend, Celestial, Undead.

At 10th level, the bonus damage increases to **1d10**. At 15th level, you can add an additional creature type from any of the other spirit types' lists to your own. You can use this ability a number of times equal to half your proficiency bonus (rounded up), regaining expended uses when you finish a long rest.

## Morphing

_7th-level Curseblade feature_

Over the course of a 1-hour ritual (which counts as light activity), you can alter your curseblade into any other mundane melee weapon of your choice, reselecting the basic properties granted by Imbued Blade to fit the new weapon.

## Advanced Property

_10th-level Curseblade feature_

At 10th level, you can select one of the following **advanced properties** in lieu of a basic property from Imbued Blade. At 18th level, you can take a second advanced property instead of another basic property:

- **Binding:** When you hit a creature with this weapon, you can use your reaction to force it to make a Charisma saving throw against your spell save DC. On a failure, the creature loses its flying speed, if it has one, and cannot cast spells or use abilities that allow teleportation or planar shifting for 1 minute (save ends at end of turn). Proficiency bonus uses per long rest.
- **Draining:** When you hit a creature, you can use your reaction to regain hit points equal to half the damage dealt + your spellcasting ability modifier (or full damage if you are below half your hit point maximum). Proficiency bonus uses per long rest.
- **Impossible Design:** You can wield a weapon with the two-handed property in a single hand without penalty.
- **Quick:** When you take the Attack action on your turn, you can make one additional weapon attack with this weapon. Proficiency bonus uses per long rest.
- **Siphoning:** When you hit a spellcasting creature, you can use your reaction to force a Wisdom saving throw. On a failure, it loses a spell slot equal to or less than your proficiency bonus, and you regain an expended spell slot of the highest level you can cast. Proficiency bonus uses per long rest.

## Advanced Morphing

_10th-level Curseblade feature_

You can enact an 8-hour ritual over the course of a long rest to extract your weapon's spirit and transfer it into any weapon of your choice—including magical weapons (provided the weapon has no sentience of its own). The weapon's maximum rarity depends on your proficiency bonus:
- PB +4: Uncommon
- PB +5: Rare
- PB +6: Very Rare

## Curse Blast

_15th-level Curseblade feature_

You unleash the full devastation of your curseblade. Choose one of the following special attacks, usable once per short or long rest (or by expending a use of Action Surge):

- **Whirling Dervish:** As an action, make a single melee weapon attack against every creature of your choice within a 10-foot-radius sphere centered on you. On a hit, a creature takes 4d10 force damage + your spellcasting ability modifier.
- **Explosive Burst:** As an action, make a single melee weapon attack against every creature of your choice in a 15-foot cone in front of you. On a hit, a creature takes 4d10 force damage and is either knocked 5 feet back or knocked prone (your choice; up to two size categories larger than you).
- **Scything Blow:** As an action, make a single melee weapon attack against a target within 30 feet, as well as every creature of your choice in a straight line between you and the target. On a hit, each creature takes 4d10 force damage + your spellcasting ability modifier, and you teleport to the primary target's space.

## Spirit Keeper

_18th-level Curseblade feature_

Your soul fuses irrevocably with your weapon's spirit. You no longer need to sleep (though you still require light activity for a long rest), and you are immune to surprise. Furthermore, your creature type permanently shifts based on your spirit type, granting immunity to spells that target humanoids:

- **Arcane:** Your creature type becomes Aberration (resistance to psychic damage) or Fiend (resistance to fire damage).
- **Primal:** Your creature type becomes Elemental (resistance to acid, cold, fire, or lightning) or Fey (immunity to being charmed).
- **Occult:** Your creature type becomes Fiend (resistance to fire damage) or Undead (no longer need to breathe, eat, drink, or age; healed by spells as normal).
