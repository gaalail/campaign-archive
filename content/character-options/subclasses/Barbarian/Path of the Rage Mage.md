# Path of the Rage Mage

*An arcanist who channels explosive surges of magic through brute ferocity.*

Most mages require calm, discipline, and meticulous study to shape the weave of magic. The Rage Mage scoffs at such delicate reserve. Born with volatile arcane energy surging through their veins or infused with primal ley-line power, these barbarians ignite their magical potential through blood-pumping fury.

When a rage mage screams into the fray, crackling lightning arcs from their blades, raw kinetic force detonates around their footsteps, and spellcraft explodes in sync with their martial fury.

### Spellcasting
*3rd-level Path of the Rage Mage feature*

When you reach 3rd level, you augment your combat prowess with the ability to cast spells from the sorcerer spell list.

#### Cantrips
You learn two cantrips of your choice from the sorcerer spell list. You learn an additional sorcerer cantrip of your choice at 10th level.

#### Spell Slots
The Rage Mage Spellcasting table shows how many spell slots you have to cast your spells of 1st level and higher. To cast one of these spells, you must expend a slot of the spell's level or higher. You regain all expended spell slots when you finish a long rest.

#### Spells Known of 1st-Level and Higher
You know three 1st-level sorcerer spells of your choice, two of which you must choose from the **evocation** and **transmutation** spells on the sorcerer spell list.

The Spells Known column of the Rage Mage Spellcasting table shows when you learn more sorcerer spells of 1st level or higher. Each of these spells must be an evocation or transmutation spell of your choice, and must be of a level for which you have spell slots. For instance, when you reach 7th level in this class, you can learn one new spell of 1st or 2nd level.

The spells you learn at 8th, 14th, and 20th level can come from any school of magic on the sorcerer spell list.

Whenever you gain a level in this class, you can replace one of the sorcerer spells you know with another spell of your choice from the sorcerer spell list. The new spell must be of a level for which you have spell slots, and it must be an evocation or transmutation spell, unless you're replacing the spell you gained at 3rd, 8th, 14th, or 20th level from any school of magic.

#### Spellcasting Ability
Charisma is your spellcasting ability for your sorcerer spells, since your magic erupts from your inner willpower and fiery emotions. You use your Charisma whenever a spell refers to your spellcasting ability. In addition, you use your Charisma modifier when setting the saving throw DC for a sorcerer spell you cast and when making an attack roll with one.

- **Spell save DC** = 8 + your proficiency bonus + your Charisma modifier
- **Spell attack modifier** = your proficiency bonus + your Charisma modifier

### Arcane Intuition
*3rd-level Path of the Rage Mage feature*

Your innate connection to raw magical energy sharpens your physical instincts. You can use your Constitution modifier instead of your Intelligence modifier when making Intelligence (Arcana) checks. 

In addition, you can use a melee weapon you are proficient with as a spellcasting focus for your sorcerer spells.

---

### Raging Sorcery
*3rd-level Path of the Rage Mage feature*

Your primal rage fuels your magical reserves rather than stifling them. While raging, you can cast and concentrate on spells from the evocation and transmutation schools of magic from your Rage Mage spell list. 

Casting a spell or maintaining concentration on a spell counts as attacking a hostile creature for the purpose of sustaining your rage.

---

### Arcane Rampage
*6th-level Path of the Rage Mage feature*

Starting at 6th level, you fluidly weave spells into your furious strikes. Whenever you use your Reckless Attack feature on your turn, you can use a bonus action to cast a sorcerer spell with a casting time of 1 action.

Alternatively, when you take the Attack action on your turn, you can forgo one of your attacks to cast a sorcerer cantrip that targets a single creature within your melee weapon's reach.

---

### Supernatural Exploits
*10th-level Path of the Rage Mage feature*

At 10th level, ambient magical energies infuse your everyday physical feats. You can cast the following spells on yourself at will, requiring no spell slots or material components:
- *expeditious retreat*
- *feather fall*
- *jump*
- *see invisibility*

While raging, casting any of these spells requires only a bonus action or reaction as normal, and maintaining them does not disrupt your focus.

---

### Eldritch Explosions
*14th-level Path of the Rage Mage feature*

At 14th level, you can discharge concentrated bursts of volatile arcane power upon impact.

When you hit a creature with a melee weapon attack while raging, you can expend one spell slot to detonate an eldritch shockwave. The target takes extra force damage equal to **2d6 per level of the expended spell slot**. Additionally, each creature of your choice within a 10-foot radius of the target must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw against your spell save DC or take the same force damage and be pushed up to 10 feet directly away from the target.
