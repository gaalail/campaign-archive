# Path of the Witch

Barbarians fill a unique role within witch societies. Instead of seeking structured urban covens, the first witch barbarians ventured into primeval wilderness in search of untamed witchcraft. Taught by ancient forest spirits, barrow hags, or the whispering earth itself, they channel wild explosive arcana through their battle rages.

Designed by Logan Reese (*Runesmith*).

---

## Ancient Mentor

_3rd-level Path of the Witch feature_

While raging, you subconsciously recite incantations learned from your primal mentor. Any spell attack or saving throw granted by this subclass has a save DC equal to 8 + your proficiency bonus + your Strength modifier.

Choose your mentor archetype:
- **Hermit:** While raging, you can cast a cantrip as a bonus action (Strength modifier uses per long rest). Choose aid (*guidance, resistance, spare the dying, true strike*), harm (*acid splash, chill touch, poison spray, shocking grasp*), or change (*light, mage hand, minor illusion, prestidigitation*) and roll a d4 to determine the cantrip cast.
- **Wandering Jinx:** While raging, at the start of your turn, choose a creature within 30 feet. The target's next skill check or saving throw is reduced by 1d4.
- **Forgotten Voice:** While raging and unarmored, you add your proficiency bonus to your Armor Class.

## Traveler’s Witchmark

_6th-level Path of the Witch feature_

- **Hermit:** You gain resistance to poison damage, immunity to the poisoned condition, and can consume 2 pounds of organic matter as daily rations.
- **Wandering Jinx:** When attacked, you can use your reaction to impose disadvantage on the attack; if it misses, you teleport 5 feet to an unoccupied space (uses equal to half barbarian level per rest). You also gain proficiency in Deception.
- **Forgotten Voice:** As a bonus action, touch a weapon to imbue it with force: it deals an extra 1d6 force damage until the start of your next turn.

## Arcane Offering

_10th-level Path of the Witch feature_

As an action, choose a creature within 30 feet: it gains either **Unarmored Defense**, **Reckless Attack**, or your **Traveler’s Witchmark** feature. When you transfer or revoke this boon, you can spend 1 hour cursing the recipient: it suffers 3d8 psychic damage, 1 level of exhaustion, and remains cursed for 1d6 days (removable via *remove curse*).

## Final Teachings

_14th-level Path of the Witch feature_

- **Hermit:** While raging, a spectral night hag appears within 5 feet (immune to all damage except force). Bonus action to command her claw attack; otherwise, she casts *magic missile* at random hostile targets.
- **Wandering Jinx:** When struck in melee, reaction to inflict blinded, charmed, frightened, deafened, or prone until the end of your next turn.
- **Forgotten Voice:** While raging, incantations boom with a thunderous roar heard only by foes: chosen creatures within 15 feet must make a Constitution saving throw or take 2d8 thunder damage.
