# Path of the Colossus

The colossus, sometimes known as a war-hulk, is a barbarian of the frontlines hefting massive weapons and sweeping away smaller combatants in their fury. To a war-hulk, strength is the only attribute that matters. Cunning is for the weak; faith is for the weak; speed is for the weak. Victory is for the strong alone, and rage is the purest path to strength. This path is a favorite of goliaths and giants, who step easily into crushing smaller opponents.

---

## Larger Than Life

_3rd-level Path of the Colossus feature_

You become a titan in your anger. When you begin a rage, your size doubles in all dimensions and your weight is multiplied by eight. This growth increases your size category by one (for example, from Medium to Large). This size increase doesn't stack with similar size increases, such as from the [[enlarge-reduce]] spell. If there isn't enough room for you to double your size, you attain the maximum possible size in the space available.

Your weapons also grow to match your new size. While these weapons are enlarged, your attacks with them deal **1d4 extra damage**.

While raging, you can use your action to make a massive sweeping strike against all creatures in a 15-foot line within your reach. Roll a separate attack roll for each target in the line.

## Imposing Presence

_6th-level Path of the Colossus feature_

Your immense stature can block line of sight. Ranged effects drawing line of sight through your position treat targets behind you as if they have **three-quarters cover**. When you are raging, this becomes **total cover**.

## Boulder Throw

_10th-level Path of the Colossus feature_

You can throw massive boulders or other suitably large objects as weapons. Such weapons are considered two-handed, heavy, thrown weapons with a range of 30/60 feet. You have proficiency in such weapons.

On a hit, a boulder deals **2d8 bludgeoning damage**. You can throw one boulder per turn.

## Colossal Strength

_14th-level Path of the Colossus feature_

Your Strength score increases by 2 and your maximum for this score becomes 22. At 20th level, your Strength maximum becomes 26, rather than 24.

When you make a melee weapon attack while raging, the attack has **reach** if it did not have it before.
