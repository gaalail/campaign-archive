# Path of Tranquility

*A serene, reactionary warrior who enters a zen-like state in conflict.*

While many barbarians scream, gnash their teeth, and thrash wildly across the battlefield, those who follow the Path of Tranquility discover power in stillness. To these warriors, rage is not a blinding red haze of chaos, but an icy, crystal-clear focus—a state of undisturbed mental quiet where time seems to slow to a crawl.

In battle, a barbarian of tranquility stands unmovable in the eye of the storm, dodging strikes with fluid economy of motion and retaliating with swift, clinical precision.

---

## Primal Path Features

| Barbarian Level | Features |
| :---: | :--- |
| **3rd** | Serene Fury, Mantra, Anticipate |
| **6th** | Battle Trance |
| **10th** | Aura of Tranquility |
| **14th** | Zen State |

---

### Serene Fury
*3rd-level Path of Tranquility feature*

When you enter a rage, your demeanor becomes eerily calm, silent, and measured. You gain the benefits of rage as normal, but your fury manifests as absolute focus rather than outward rage. 

You gain proficiency in either **Insight** or **Perception** (your choice). Additionally, while raging, you cannot be frightened or charmed, and you have advantage on Wisdom saving throws.

---

### Mantra
*3rd-level Path of Tranquility feature*

Starting at 3rd level, stillness sustains your focus. Taking the **Dodge** action on your turn counts as making an attack for the purpose of extending the duration of your Rage feature. 

When you take the Dodge action while raging, your walking speed also increases by 10 feet until the end of your turn, allowing you to glide effortlessly across shifting battle lines.

---

### Anticipate
*3rd-level Path of Tranquility feature*

Also at 3rd level, your patient focus turns an opponent's aggression against them. If you do not make an attack roll during your turn, you can use your reaction when a creature within your reach targets you with an attack or spell to make one melee weapon attack against that creature. 

This counter-attack resolves immediately before the triggering attack or spell roll is made. If your strike hits, the triggering creature also suffers disadvantage on its attack roll against you.

---

### Battle Trance
*6th-level Path of Tranquility feature*

At 6th level, your reactionary precision reaches sublime heights. When you use your **Anticipate** reaction to strike back at a foe, you can now make two melee weapon attacks instead of one.

Additionally, whenever you successfully hit a creature with your Anticipate counter-attack, you can immediately move up to 10 feet without provoking opportunity attacks from that creature.

---

### Aura of Tranquility
*10th-level Path of Tranquility feature*

At 10th level, the serene stillness of your spirit ripples outward, damping hostile bloodlust around you. 

While raging, you can cast the following spells once each, without requiring spell slots or material components:
- *calm emotions*
- *enthrall*
- *sanctuary*

Constitution is your spellcasting ability for these spells (spell save DC = 8 + your proficiency bonus + your Constitution modifier). Casting or concentrating on these spells does not end your rage, and taking an action to cast one counts as an attack for the purpose of maintaining your rage. 

You regain the ability to cast each of these spells when you finish a long rest.

---

### Zen State
*14th-level Path of Tranquility feature*

At 14th level, you attain absolute harmony between body, weapon, and instinct. 

While raging, whenever a hostile creature makes a melee attack roll against you and misses, your defensive momentum increases: you gain a **+2 bonus to your AC**. 

This bonus can stack up to a maximum of **+6 to your AC**. The accumulated AC bonus lasts until you are successfully hit by an attack, until your rage ends, or until the start of your next turn.

Additionally, if a creature misses you with a melee attack while your Zen State bonus is at its maximum (+6), you can immediately use your reaction to knock that creature prone and make a single melee weapon attack with advantage against it.
