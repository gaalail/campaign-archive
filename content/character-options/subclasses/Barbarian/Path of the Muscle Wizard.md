# Path of the Muscle Wizard

*Definitely a "wizard." A buff, angry "wizard."*

While traditional mages spend decades in dusty libraries memorizing incantations and measuring somatic gestures, barbarians who walk the Path of the Muscle Wizard discover a much more direct magical tradition: flexing their biceps until reality bends. 

Adherents of this path wear academic robes (usually torn open at the chest), carry hefty tomes they use as bludgeoning implements, and insist loudly and aggressively that their devastating physical feats are high-tier arcana. Few scholars dare to dispute them to their faces.

### Unarguable Wizardry
*3rd-level Path of the Muscle Wizard feature*

Starting when you choose this path at 3rd level, your conviction in your own magical prowess is overwhelming. You gain proficiency in the **Intimidation** skill if you do not already have it, and you add double your proficiency bonus to checks made to convince others that you are a genuine, scholarly wizard.

Furthermore, if a creature openly questions your arcane credentials within earshot, you can enter a rage as a reaction without expending a use of your Rage feature. This reactionary rage lasts until the end of your next turn.

---

### "Cantrips"
*3rd-level Path of the Muscle Wizard feature*

At 3rd level, you learn three primary "cantrips." While raging, you can cast these cantrips at will. When not raging, you can use these cantrips a total number of times equal to your Strength modifier (minimum of once), regaining all expended uses when you finish a short or long rest.

- **"Mage Hand":** When you take the Attack action on your turn, you can replace one attack with an extended, overwhelming shove against a creature within 10 feet of you. The target must succeed on a Strength saving throw (DC = 8 + your proficiency bonus + your Strength modifier) or be knocked prone and pushed 5 feet away.
- **"Shocking Grasp":** When you hit a creature with an unarmed strike or melee weapon attack, you can channel sheer concussive force through the blow. The target cannot take reactions until the start of its next turn.
- **"True Strike":** As a bonus action on your turn, you lock eyes with a target within 15 feet of you, flexing menacingly. The next melee weapon attack you hit against that creature before the end of your current turn deals an additional 1d8 bludgeoning damage.

---

### "Spells"
*3rd-level Path of the Muscle Wizard feature*

Also at 3rd level, you master three higher-level "spells." You can cast each of these spells once, regaining the ability to cast it when you finish a long rest.

- **"Burning Hands":** As an action while raging, you clap your hands together with blistering concussive friction. Each creature in a 15-foot cone originating from you must make a Dexterity saving throw (DC = 8 + your proficiency bonus + your Strength modifier). A creature takes bludgeoning damage equal to your unarmed strike damage + your Strength modifier on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.
- **"Magic Missile":** As a bonus action, you grab a handful of rocks, daggers, or nearby battlefield debris and fling them with superhuman force at a creature you can see within 60 feet. Make a ranged improvised weapon attack with advantage. On a hit, the target takes 3d4 + your Strength modifier piercing and bludgeoning damage. This attack counts as magical.
- **"Shield":** When you are hit by an attack or targeted by the *magic missile* spell, you can use your reaction to interpose your bulging forearms or a hefty weapon. You gain a +5 bonus to AC against the triggering attack, potentially causing it to miss. If the attack still hits, you reduce the damage taken by 1d12 + your Constitution modifier (minimum of 0).

---

### Arcane Flex
*6th-level Path of the Muscle Wizard feature*

Starting at 6th level, your strikes pulse with pseudo-magical momentum. Your unarmed strikes and attacks with improvised weapons count as magical for the purpose of overcoming resistance and immunity to nonmagical attacks and damage.

Additionally, whenever you score a critical hit with a melee weapon or unarmed strike while raging, shockwaves reverberate outward: all hostile creatures within 5 feet of the target must succeed on a Constitution saving throw (DC = 8 + your proficiency bonus + your Strength modifier) or be deafened until the end of your next turn.

---

### Magic Resistance
*10th-level Path of the Muscle Wizard feature*

At 10th level, your thick musculature and indomitable stubbornness physically deflect genuine sorcery. While raging, you have resistance to all damage dealt by spells and magical effects.

In addition, you have advantage on saving throws against spells and magical effects that would cause you to be charmed, paralyzed, or stunned.

---

### I Cast Fist
*14th-level Path of the Muscle Wizard feature*

At 14th level, you unveil the pinnacle of your "9th-level arcane spellcasting": the ultimate punch.

As an action on your turn while raging, you can expend both your action and bonus action to wind up and unleash a cataclysmic punch against an adjacent target. Make an unarmed strike with advantage. On a hit, the target takes **8d8 + your Strength modifier bludgeoning damage**, plus an additional **4d8 force damage**, and must succeed on a Strength saving throw (DC = 8 + your proficiency bonus + your Strength modifier) or be launched up to 30 feet directly away from you and knocked prone. If the target collides with a solid barrier or another creature, both take 1d6 bludgeoning damage for every 10 feet traveled.

Once you use this feature, you cannot use it again until you enter a new rage or finish a short or long rest.
