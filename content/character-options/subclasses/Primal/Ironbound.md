# Ironbound

*Ironbound Primals assert that all that comes of earth is born of primal power, embracing forged metal to become invincible edifices of iron.*

Often drawn from mining bastions, dwarven delves, or forge shrines, Ironbound Primals represent the union of primal geomancy and metallurgical mastery. Clad in impenetrable heavy armor and commanding animated chains that erupt from their gauntlets and the earth, they anchor themselves as immovable bulwarks.

### Ironmonger
*1st-level Ironbound feature*

Starting at 1st level, you gain proficiency in **heavy armor** and **martial melee weapons**. 

Your **Mystic Bulwark** functions even when wearing heavy armor (though if you gain additional sources of passive damage reduction, additional sources beyond the highest value increase your reduction by only 1 each).

---

### Armor Bond
*1st-level Ironbound feature*

At 1st level, as an action, you can touch a set of metal armor worn by a creature of your choice (including yourself) and fortify it. The creature gains temporary hit points equal to **1d8 + your Primal level**. 

Once you use this feature, you cannot use it again until you complete a short or long rest. Any temporary hit points remaining from this feature fade when you use it again.

---

### Innate Magic
*1st-level Ironbound feature*

You gain access to several innate magical abilities by channeling your primal connection. You gain the following spells at the listed Primal levels, and can cast each spell once without material components or spell slots (regained on a long rest). 

Wisdom is your spellcasting ability for these spells. You can cast a spell again before completing a long rest by spending **1 + the level of the spell** Endurance Dice.

| Primal Level | Innate Spells (1/Long Rest Each) |
| :---: | :--- |
| **1st** | *unburden* |
| **3rd** | *arcane ablation* |
| **5th** | *clay touch* |
| **9th** | *protection from energy* |
| **13th** | *iron garden* |
| **17th** | *repair* |

### Unbreakable Endurance
*3rd-level Ironbound feature*

At 3rd level, when you roll an Endurance Die to reduce incoming damage from an attack, if the die does not completely block all damage taken, you gain **resistance to the remaining damage**.

---

### Binding Interdiction
*3rd-level Ironbound feature*

Additionally at 3rd level, you gain the ability to manifest heavy iron chains within the range of your Primal Interdiction.

As a reaction to a creature attempting to leave your Primal Interdiction, or as an attack made as part of the Attack action, you can manifest a chain and attempt to bind a Large or smaller creature with it, making a grapple check against the target. On a success, the target is grappled by the chain until it escapes (contesting your grapple check), the chain is destroyed, or it is no longer within your Primal Interdiction.

The chain can be attacked: it has an **AC of 18** and hit points equal to your **Primal level** (you take no damage when a chain is destroyed). If a target escapes, the chain crumbles to rust.

You can maintain one active chain at a time. The number of chains you can manifest increases by one each time you gain an effect that increases the damage die of your Primal natural weapons (such as *Evolving Power* or *Savage Weapons*). Once you can manifest more than one chain, you can target the same creature with multiple chains; a creature is **restrained** while grappled by more than one chain.

---

### Iron Carapace
*7th-level Ironbound feature*

Starting at 7th level, you can morph and adapt heavy armor you are wearing. As an action, you can reconfigure your armor, selecting one of the following configurations:
- **Reinforced Carapace:** Your AC while wearing heavy armor increases by 1.
- **Regenerating Carapace:** At the end of each of your turns, your armor repairs any damage it has taken, restoring its original AC value. If you are wearing no armor, you can summon a suit of mundane heavy armor of your choice (automatically equipped), which rusts to nothing when removed.
- **Spiked Carapace:** When a creature hits you with a melee attack while within 5 feet of you or attempts to grapple you, it takes **1d4 piercing damage** (increasing to **2d4** at 14th level).

The selected carapace lasts until you choose a different option or end it as a bonus action.

---

### Iron Incarceration
*14th-level Ironbound feature*

Starting at 14th level, as a bonus action, you can force all Large or smaller creatures of your choice within your Primal Interdiction to make a Dexterity saving throw (DC = 8 + your Strength or Wisdom modifier + your proficiency bonus). 

On a failure, each target is grappled by chains as per your Binding Interdiction feature (these chains do not count against your maximum limit). You can choose to pull creatures that fail their save to the closest available spaces adjacent to you. 

Once you use this feature, you cannot use it again until you complete a short or long rest.

---

### Metallic Monolith
*17th-level Ironbound feature*

Starting at 17th level, as an action, you empower yourself into an invincible metal colossus for 1 minute:
- **Enlarged Weapons:** Metal weapons you wield grow heavier and larger, increasing their damage by **1d8**.
- **Unlimited Chains:** You can generate an unlimited number of chains with Binding Interdiction, and they have no creature size restrictions.
- **Implacable Monolith:** You are completely immune to the prone condition and any effect that would move you against your will.
- **Damage Resistance:** You gain resistance to bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing damage.

Once you assume this form, you cannot do so again until you complete a long rest.
