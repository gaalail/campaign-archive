# Stoneblood

*The enduring blood of the world flows through you, empowering you with inhuman durability, stone-rending fists, and footsteps that cause the earth to tremble.*

Stoneblood Primals draw directly upon the continental weight of tectonic plates and deep subterranean strata. Their limbs harden into solid rock, enabling them to crush armor with their bare hands, pull flying foes crashing to the earth, and shake the ground with devastating seismic tremors.

### Stone Fists
*1st-level Stoneblood feature*

Starting at 1st level, when you roll initiative or as a bonus action at any time, you can transform one or both arms into durable limbs of living stone. You cannot wield weapons or shields in hands turned to stone, but they can be used to grapple creatures. 

While grappling a creature with your stone fists, that fist can only attack that creature. The stone fists last until you dismiss them as a bonus action:

- **Stone Fist:** A hardened limb of dense stone. This becomes a natural one-handed melee weapon that deals **1d6 bludgeoning damage** on a hit, and has the *Light* and *Special* properties.
  - *Special:* You gain a **+1 bonus to your AC** while wielding one of these weapons in each hand (you cannot benefit from this bonus and the bonus AC of a shield simultaneously).

---

### Empowered Earth
*1st-level Stoneblood feature*

At 1st level, you learn the *manipulate earth* and *stone forming* cantrips. Wisdom is your spellcasting ability for them.

When you use *stone forming*, you can meld any one-handed weapon created by it with your Stone Fists, gaining proficiency in that weapon and replacing the Stone Fist's properties (including its Special property) with the created weapon's damage dice, damage type, and properties (except thrown).

---

### Innate Magic
*1st-level Stoneblood feature*

You gain access to several innate magical abilities by channeling your primal connection. You gain the following spells at the listed Primal levels, and can cast each spell once without material components or spell slots (regained on a long rest). 

Wisdom is your spellcasting ability for these spells. You can cast a spell again before completing a long rest by spending **1 + the level of the spell** Endurance Dice.

| Primal Level | Innate Spells (1/Long Rest Each) |
| :---: | :--- |
| **1st** | *create pit* |
| **3rd** | *earth ripple* |
| **5th** | *become stone* |
| **9th** | *quicksand* |
| **13th** | *stone coffin* |
| **17th** | *orbital stones* |

### Seismic Backlash
*3rd-level Stoneblood feature*

Starting at 3rd level, each time you expend an Endurance Die, you store that die in a special pool of charged tectonic energy for 1 minute. You can store a number of dice equal to your proficiency bonus. 

As a bonus action on your turn, you can unleash this stored energy in an explosive tremor, rolling all stored dice and expending them from the pool. All creatures of your choice within the radius of your Primal Interdiction must make a Strength saving throw (DC = 8 + your Strength or Wisdom modifier + your proficiency bonus). On a failed save, a creature takes bludgeoning damage equal to the total rolled and falls prone. On a successful save, it takes half as much damage and does not fall prone.

---

### Gravitational Pull
*3rd-level Stoneblood feature*

Additionally at 3rd level, when you take the Attack action on your turn, you can replace one of your attacks with a surge of localized gravity. 

A Large or smaller creature of your choice within 60 feet must make a Strength saving throw (DC = 8 + your Strength or Wisdom modifier + your proficiency bonus). On a failure, it is pulled up to 15 feet toward you or knocked prone (your choice). The pull distance is doubled if the target creature is flying.

---

### Resilient Bulwark
*7th-level Stoneblood feature*

Starting at 7th level, the damage reduction value of your **Mystic Bulwark** increases by 1. 

Additionally, you have resistance to damage from effects that cause ongoing bleeding.

---

### World Breaker
*14th-level Stoneblood feature*

Starting at 14th level, you can cast *fissure* once without expending a spell slot (regained on a long rest). 

You can also cast it early by expending 5 or more charges from your Seismic Backlash pool in place of unleashing the tremor. Expending additional charges beyond 5 increases the spell slot level by 1 per additional charge spent.

---

### Stone Sense
*14th-level Stoneblood feature*

Additionally at 14th level, you gain tremorsense out to a range equal to the radius of your Primal Interdiction.

---

### Primordial Avatar
*17th-level Stoneblood feature*

Starting at 17th level, as an action, you draw upon the bedrock beneath your feet to become a living embodiment of the earth for 1 minute. If you start your turn in contact with the ground, you gain the following benefits:
- **Strength Advantage:** You have advantage on Strength checks and Strength saving throws.
- **Endless Vitality:** You regain hit points equal to half your Primal level (rounded down) at the start of each of your turns.
- **Call of the Earth:** Flying creatures lose their flying speed while inside your Primal Interdiction.
- **Reflective Carapace:** If you are targeted by *magic missile*, a line spell, or a spell that requires a ranged attack roll, roll a d6. On a 4–5, you are unaffected. On a 6, you are unaffected and the spell reflects back at the caster as if it originated from you.
- **Empowered Backlash:** You add one bonus die to your Seismic Backlash pool whenever you roll damage for it.

Once you assume this avatar form, you cannot do so again until you complete a long rest.
