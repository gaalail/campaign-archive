# Primal Bonds

**10 Primal Bonds available** | Choose at 1st level (with core progression at 1st, 3rd, 7th, 14th, and 17th level)

At 1st level, a [[character-options/classes/Primal|Primal]] forms a bond with a fundamental current of the universe—manifesting natural weapons, altering their body, and projecting an aura of primal control across the battlefield.

---

## All Primal Bonds

- [[character-options/subclasses/Primal/Elemental Soul|Elemental Soul]] — Harnesses primordial fire, ice, and lightning armaments to incinerate and freeze foes.
- [[character-options/subclasses/Primal/Beasthide|Beasthide]] — Awakens apex predator traits with bestial claws, savage resilience, and colossal gigantification.
- [[character-options/subclasses/Primal/Elderheart|Elderheart]] — Commands primeval flora with grasping lashing vines, thorned barriers, and treant transformations.
- [[character-options/subclasses/Primal/Stoneblood|Stoneblood]] — Channels tectonic mass into stone fists, seismic shockwaves, and gravitational disruption.
- [[character-options/subclasses/Primal/Sunwatcher|Sunwatcher]] — Channels radiant solar power into fiery blasts, sunlit cleansing auras, and blinding flares.
- [[character-options/subclasses/Primal/Ironbound|Ironbound]] — Master of forged earth and heavy armor, summoning crushing chains to incarcerate enemy ranks.
- [[character-options/subclasses/Primal/Dreadwing|Dreadwing]] — Awakens the primordial dread and elemental majesty of dragonkind with wings, breath, and true dragon forms.
- [[character-options/subclasses/Primal/Timetwister|Timetwister]] — Distorts the flow of moments with time dilation, reactionary timeline rewinds, and localized time locks.
- [[character-options/subclasses/Primal/Astral Guardian|Astral Guardian]] — Manipulates extraplanar space with ethereal astral arms, dimension-locking barriers, and spatial projection.
- [[character-options/subclasses/Primal/Bone Binder|Bone Binder]] — Recycles fallen remains into macabre bone armor, grasping skeletal graves, and a companion bone construct.
