# Beasthide

*Tapping into the primal ferocity of beasts, Beasthide Primals fight with savage predatory instinct, unleashing wrath on any who dare threaten their pack.*

Beasthide Primals blur the boundary between mortal and monster. Their skin thickens into leathery hide, their fingernails sharpen into beast-rending talons, and their bodies swell in size to wrestle giants and throw monsters across the battlefield.

### Bestial Claws
*1st-level Beasthide feature*

Starting at 1st level, when you roll initiative or as a bonus action at any time, you can morph one or both of your arms into clawed natural weapons. You cannot wield weapons or shields in hands morphed into claws, but they can be used to grapple creatures. 

While you are grappling a creature with a claw, that claw can only attack that creature. The claws last until you dismiss them as a bonus action.

- **Bestial Claws:** A vicious set of claws extending from your hand. This becomes a natural one-handed melee weapon that deals **1d8 slashing damage** on a hit, and has the *Light* and *Special* properties.
  - *Special:* This weapon counts as a free hand for the purposes of grappling. When you successfully initiate a grapple with it, the target takes **1d6 slashing damage**.

---

### Bond of the Wild
*1st-level Beasthide feature*

At 1st level, you gain proficiency in the **Animal Handling** skill. If you already have proficiency in Animal Handling, you gain expertise, adding double your proficiency bonus to checks made with it.

---

### Innate Magic
*1st-level Beasthide feature*

You gain access to several innate magical abilities by channeling your primal connection. You gain the following spells at the listed Primal levels, and can cast each spell once without material components or spell slots (regained on a long rest). 

Wisdom is your spellcasting ability for these spells. You can cast a spell again before completing a long rest by spending **1 + the level of the spell** Endurance Dice.

| Primal Level | Innate Spells (1/Long Rest Each) |
| :---: | :--- |
| **1st** | *speak with animals* |
| **3rd** | *animal messenger* |
| **5th** | *alter self* |
| **9th** | *enlarge/reduce* |
| **13th** | *mutate* |
| **17th** | *polymorph* |

---

### Ruthless Interdiction
*3rd-level Beasthide feature*

Starting at 3rd level, you have advantage on the first attack roll or grapple check you make against a creature that attacked anyone inside the range of your Primal Interdiction (including yourself) since the end of your last turn.

---

### Enduring Ferocity
*3rd-level Beasthide feature*

Additionally at 3rd level, when you roll an Endurance Die, you can also add the die result to the next damage roll or Strength (Athletics) check you make before the end of your next turn.

---

### Path of Survival
*7th-level Beasthide feature*

Starting at 7th level, you gain proficiency in your choice of **Constitution** or **Dexterity** saving throws.

---

### Vicious Endurance
*14th-level Beasthide feature*

Starting at 14th level, your attacks with natural weapons score a critical hit on a roll of **19 or 20**. 

When you score a critical hit with your natural weapons, you immediately regain **1 expended Endurance Die**.

---

### Focused Transformation
*14th-level Beasthide feature*

Additionally at 14th level, while you are concentrating on an innate spell granted by this subclass, your concentration cannot be broken as a result of taking damage.

---

### Gigantification
*17th-level Beasthide feature*

Starting at 17th level, as a bonus action or when you manifest your bestial claws, you can unleash the titanic beast within, swelling to massive proportions. You become **Large or Huge** (your choice) for 1 minute.

Upon transforming, you gain temporary hit points equal to half of your maximum hit points. For the duration of your transformation, you gain the following benefits:
- **Bonus Damage:** Your natural weapons deal an additional die of damage.
- **Grappling Advantage:** You have advantage on all ability checks to grapple creatures smaller than you.
- **Reach:** Your reach increases by 5 feet with all weapons.
- **Faster Movement:** Your walking speed increases by 20 feet.
- **Jumping Distance:** The distance you can long or high jump with a running start is doubled.
- **Saving Throws:** You have advantage on Strength and Constitution saving throws.
- **Throwing Things:** While in this form, when you make an attack, you can throw Medium or Small objects or creatures you are holding with a range of 50/100 feet. You have proficiency with this attack. On a hit, both the target and the thrown creature or object take **4d6 + your Strength modifier damage**. On a miss, only the thrown creature or object takes the damage.

Once you use this feature, you cannot use it again until you complete a long rest.
