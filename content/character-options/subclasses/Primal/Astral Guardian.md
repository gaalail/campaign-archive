# Astral Guardian

*Embarking on a path less traveled, the Astral Guardian Primal transcends the earthly realm, drawing power from the infinite planar sea to manipulate space and force.*

Astral Guardians manifest spectral, translucent arms that reach across space to drag foes into their grasp. They shut down enemy teleportation, bend spatial vectors to relocate companions, open tears in reality, and project their spirit onto higher planes of existence.

### Astral Arms
*1st-level Astral Guardian feature*

Starting at 1st level, you can form up to two astral arms as a bonus action. 

These arms are natural melee weapons with the *Light* property that deal **1d4 force damage** on a hit. You can use your **Wisdom modifier** in place of Strength or Dexterity for attack and damage rolls made with them. You can also use them to make unarmed strikes and grapple creatures.

These arms last until you fall unconscious or dismiss them as a bonus action. The maximum number of arms you can form increases to **4 arms at 9th level**, and **6 arms at 17th level** (additional arms do not grant extra attacks, but can each grapple separate creatures).

When you take the Attack action on your turn, you can make a single unarmed strike or grapple check with one of your Astral Arms as a bonus action.

---

### Sixth Sense
*1st-level Astral Guardian feature*

Additionally at 1st level, you sense extraplanar anomalies. You can detect if you are being observed through divination magic, or by creatures within 30 feet of you in the Ethereal Plane, if your passive Wisdom (Perception) score equals or exceeds 10 + their CR (or spell save DC).

---

### Innate Magic
*1st-level Astral Guardian feature*

You gain access to several innate magical abilities by channeling your primal connection. You gain the following spells at the listed Primal levels, and can cast each spell once without material components or spell slots (regained on a long rest). 

Wisdom is your spellcasting ability for these spells. You can cast a spell again before completing a long rest by spending **1 + the level of the spell** Endurance Dice.

| Primal Level | Innate Spells (1/Long Rest Each) |
| :---: | :--- |
| **1st** | *detect magic* |
| **3rd** | *arcane conduit* |
| **5th** | *misty step* |
| **9th** | *ghost step* |
| **13th** | *dimension cutter* |
| **17th** | *dimension door* |

### Dimensional Interdiction
*3rd-level Astral Guardian feature*

Starting at 3rd level, the difficult terrain of your Primal Interdiction impedes flying creatures as well. 

Additionally, any creature attempting to teleport into or out of your Primal Interdiction must succeed on a Charisma saving throw (DC = 8 + your Wisdom modifier + your proficiency bonus). On a failed save, the teleportation fails and the spell or action is wasted. You can choose to allow specific creatures to teleport freely without rolling.

---

### Astral Strike
*3rd-level Astral Guardian feature*

Additionally at 3rd level, when you expend an Endurance Die, you can empower your next attack with your Astral Arms before the end of your next turn:
- The attack gains a reach equal to the radius of your Primal Interdiction.
- On a hit, it deals extra damage equal to one roll of your Endurance Die.
- You can force the target to make a Charisma saving throw (DC = 8 + your Wisdom modifier + your proficiency bonus). On a failure, you teleport the creature to an unoccupied space within your Primal Interdiction.

---

### Astral Beacon
*7th-level Astral Guardian feature*

Starting at 7th level, as a bonus action, you can teleport a willing creature within 60 feet into an unoccupied space within your Primal Interdiction. You can do this a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus per long rest.

Additionally, your Primal Interdiction is always considered within range of allied teleportation abilities, even if it would normally exceed their range, provided the ally is within 300 feet of you.

---

### Dimensional Guardian
*7th-level Astral Guardian feature*

Additionally at 7th level, you gain resistance to **force damage**, and you have advantage on saving throws against being teleported or banished between planes against your will.

---

### Astral Rift
*14th-level Astral Guardian feature*

Starting at 14th level, as an action, you can open an Astral Rift tearing open space between two locations. One portal appears within 5 feet of you, and the other appears at a familiar location within a number of miles equal to your **Primal level**. 

The rift forms a swirling 5-foot-radius circular aperture. Willing creatures passing through are instantly transported to the other side. The rift remains open for 1 minute or until you close it as an action. Usable once per long rest.

---

### Astral Projection
*17th-level Astral Guardian feature*

Starting at 17th level, as an action, you can project your spirit out of your physical body:
- Your physical body becomes stunned and incapacitated.
- Your astral form gains resistance to bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing damage.
- You gain a flying speed equal to your walking speed and can hover.
- You can move through physical creatures and solid objects as difficult terrain (taking 1d10 force damage and shunting if ending your turn inside).
- You can shift between your current plane and the Ethereal Plane as an action.
- Your **Primal Interdiction** radiates from your astral form rather than your physical body.

If your astral form is reduced to 0 hit points, your spirit immediately snaps back to your body and you regain 1 hit point. You can end the projection at any time as a bonus action.
