# Elemental Soul

*Tapping into the endless power of the elemental planes, these Primals shroud themselves in harsh primal forces, destroying their foes with their very presence.*

Primals of the Elemental Soul are conduits for the untamed energies of fire, frost, and storm. Rather than wielding manufactured steel, they draw forth raw elemental fury, shaping blades of flame, shields of permafrost, and whips of crackling lightning that scorch and electrify everything in their path.

### Elemental Armaments
*1st-level Elemental Soul feature*

Starting at 1st level, when you roll initiative, or as a bonus action at any time, you can manifest elemental weapons into your hands, causing them to burst into flames, form icy claws, lashes of lightning, or frozen shields. 

Select one or two of the following weapons to manifest. You can manifest the same weapon in each hand, or different weapons in each hand. The weapons last until you drop them, summon new weapons, or dismiss them as a bonus action:

- **Primal Flames:** You conjure flames around one of your hands. These become a natural one-handed melee weapon that deals **1d6 fire damage** on a hit, and has the *Light*, *Thrown (range 30/90)*, and *Special* properties. It sheds bright light for 20 feet and dim light for an additional 20 feet while manifested. When you throw it, the flames reignite in your hand immediately.
  - *Special:* This weapon counts as a free hand for the purposes of grappling. When you successfully initiate a grapple with it, the target takes **1d6 fire damage**.
- **Frost Shield:** You conjure a slab of solid ice. This becomes a shield you wield in that hand. Summoning the shield in this way equips it. Wielding a shield increases your Armor Class by 2. You can benefit from only one shield at a time.
- **Icy Claws:** You conjure icy claws that grow forth from one of your hands. This becomes a one-handed natural melee weapon that deals **1d6 slashing or cold damage** on a hit (your choice), and has the *Light* and *Special* properties. If the damage die is increased to a d8, it instead deals 1d4 slashing and 1d4 cold damage (you choose which damage type adds your ability modifier).
  - *Special:* This weapon counts as a free hand for the purposes of grappling. When you successfully initiate a grapple with it, the target takes **1d6 cold damage**.
- **Lightning Lash:** You conjure a whip of lightning tethered to one of your hands. This becomes a one-handed natural melee weapon that deals **1d6 lightning damage** on a hit, and has the *Light*, *Reach*, and *Special* properties. It sheds bright light for 10 feet and dim light for an additional 10 feet while manifested.
  - *Special:* If you hit a creature with this weapon, you can pull the creature up to 5 feet toward you.

### Elemental Manipulation
*1st-level Elemental Soul feature*

At 1st level, you learn the *manipulate fire* and *manipulate water* cantrips. Wisdom is your spellcasting ability for them.

---

### Innate Magic
*1st-level Elemental Soul feature*

You gain access to several innate magical abilities by channeling the power of your primal connection. You gain the following spells at the listed Primal levels, and can cast each spell once without material components or spell slots. Once you cast a spell in this way, you cannot cast it again until you complete a long rest. 

Wisdom is your spellcasting ability for these spells. You can cast a spell again before completing a long rest by spending **1 + the level of the spell** Endurance Dice.

| Primal Level | Innate Spells (1/Long Rest Each) |
| :---: | :--- |
| **1st** | *Aldricor's elemental rebuke* |
| **3rd** | *entomb* |
| **5th** | *cold snap* |
| **9th** | *sleet storm* |
| **13th** | *fire cyclone* |
| **17th** | *fire shield* |

### Elemental Interdiction
*3rd-level Elemental Soul feature*

Starting at 3rd level, your Primal Interdiction becomes a buffeting maelstrom of elemental power whenever you manifest an Elemental Armament. The difficult terrain of your Primal Interdiction affects flying creatures within range, and you can select one of the following effects (choosing each time you manifest an Elemental Armament):

- **Creeping Frost:** Creatures that start their turn within the area of your Primal Interdiction have their movement speed reduced by 10 feet until the start of their next turn.
- **Blazing Pyre:** Fire burns fiercely around you. Creatures of your choice that end their turn within your Primal Interdiction take **1d6 fire damage** (increases to **2d6** at 11th level).
- **Static Field:** The first time a hostile creature moves 5 feet or more within your Primal Interdiction on a turn, it takes **1d8 lightning damage** (increases to **2d8** at 11th level). Once this damage occurs, it cannot occur again until the start of your next turn.

---

### Elemental Reflection
*3rd-level Elemental Soul feature*

Additionally at 3rd level, when you expend an Endurance Die to reduce damage taken from a melee attack, the attacker takes cold, fire, or lightning damage (your choice) equal to the amount of damage blocked.

---

### Elemental Body
*7th-level Elemental Soul feature*

Starting at 7th level, you gain resistance to your choice of cold, fire, or lightning damage. Whenever you finish a long rest, you can change which damage type you resist.

---

### Primal State
*7th-level Elemental Soul feature*

Also at 7th level, you can expend one Endurance Die to cast *become fire*, *become water*, or *become wind* without expending a spell slot.

---

### Elemental Form
*14th-level Elemental Soul feature*

Starting at 14th level, you can cast *form of fire*, *form of ice*, or *form of wind* without expending a spell slot. 

When you cast the spell this way, you can cast it as a bonus action, and it does not require concentration. Once per turn during the transformation, you can use the action it grants in place of an attack made as part of the Attack action.

Once you cast a spell in this way, you cannot do so again until you complete a long rest. You can use it again early by expending 6 Endurance Dice.

---

### Paraelemental Interdiction
*14th-level Elemental Soul feature*

Additionally at 14th level, when you activate your Elemental Interdiction, you can select two options simultaneously, activating both effects.

---

### Cataclysm Unleashed
*17th-level Elemental Soul feature*

Starting at 17th level, when you use your Elemental Form ability, you can instead cast *form of the elements*, and you are no longer limited to using the action it grants in place of an attack only once per turn, provided you use two different actions granted by the different forms. 

When you cast *form of the elements* this way, you can choose to become Large for the duration of the spell. While in this form, the damage dice of your Elemental Armaments and Elemental Interdiction effects are doubled.

Once you use this feature, you cannot do so again until you complete a long rest.
