# Timetwister

*The Timetwister draws upon the enigmatic forces of time—its unstoppable march, and the ebb and flow of past, present, and future.*

Timetwister Primals see combat not as a static clash, but as a fluid web of branching moments. They speed allies into blurring flurries, decelerate foes into helpless slow-motion, stop incoming arrows mid-flight, and freeze entire rooms in temporal stasis.

### Time Dilation
*1st-level Timetwister feature*

Starting at 1st level, you can manipulate the flow of time for a creature within 60 feet as an action:
- **Accelerate:** You accelerate time for the creature until the start of your next turn. Its movement speed increases by 10 feet, and when it takes the Attack action, it can make a single additional weapon attack as part of that same action.
- **Decelerate:** You decelerate time for the creature until the start of your next turn. Its movement speed is reduced by 10 feet, and it subtracts a **1d4** from its attack rolls and Dexterity saving throws. When you gain a feature that increases the size of your natural weapon damage dice, the size of this penalty die increases by one step (to d6, d8, etc.).

When you reach 5th level in this class, you can choose to accelerate or decelerate a creature as an attack made as part of the Attack action (though you can perform each option only once per turn).

---

### Foreshadowing
*1st-level Timetwister feature*

Additionally at 1st level, when you roll initiative, record the result rolled on the d20. Within the next minute, you can replace one of your own attack rolls, ability checks, or saving throws with that recorded roll.

Alternatively, you can replace one attack roll or contested ability check (such as a grapple check) made by an enemy within 5 feet of you (or within your Primal Interdiction once gained) with that recorded result.

---

### Innate Magic
*1st-level Timetwister feature*

You gain access to several innate magical abilities by channeling your primal connection. You gain the following spells at the listed Primal levels, and can cast each spell once without material components or spell slots (regained on a long rest). 

Wisdom is your spellcasting ability for these spells. You can cast a spell again before completing a long rest by spending **1 + the level of the spell** Endurance Dice.

| Primal Level | Innate Spells (1/Long Rest Each) |
| :---: | :--- |
| **1st** | *longstrider* |
| **3rd** | *hold person* |
| **5th** | *alacrity* |
| **9th** | *slow* |
| **13th** | *haste* |
| **17th** | *hold monster* |

### Time Distortion
*3rd-level Timetwister feature*

Starting at 3rd level, your Primal Interdiction bends temporal currents. The difficult terrain created by your Primal Interdiction affects the movement of flying creatures, and when you recklessly defend your allies with your Primal Interdiction, you grant them advantage on **Dexterity saving throws** as well.

---

### Timely Intervention
*3rd-level Timetwister feature*

Additionally at 3rd level, you can expend Endurance Dice to manipulate moments within your Primal Interdiction:
- **Missed Beat:** As a reaction when an enemy within your Primal Interdiction is attacked, you expend an Endurance Die to slow its reactions, granting the triggering attack roll against it advantage.
- **Minor Adjustment:** As a reaction when a creature within your Primal Interdiction is targeted by an area-of-effect ability, you freeze time just long enough for it to move up to 5 feet before the effect resolves, potentially escaping the area.
- **Arrow Time:** As a reaction when a creature within your Primal Interdiction is targeted by a ranged projectile attack, you freeze the missile in the air: roll an Endurance Die and subtract the result from the attacker's attack roll.

---

### Time Zone
*7th-level Timetwister feature*

Starting at 7th level, as a bonus action, you can project your Primal Interdiction to be centered on a point within 60 feet instead of on yourself. The difficult terrain and abilities tied to your Primal Interdiction apply to that area. 

The aura returns to surrounding you if you move more than 60 feet from the point, or if you call it back as a bonus action. You cannot recklessly defend allies while your interdiction is projected away from your position.

---

### Chrono Controller
*14th-level Timetwister feature*

Starting at 14th level, your mastery of time becomes second nature. Your concentration on spells granted by your Innate Magic feature cannot be broken as a result of taking damage. 

Furthermore, whenever you cast an innate spell from this subclass, you can cast it as though it were upcast by **one spell slot level** above its base level.

---

### Time Lock
*17th-level Timetwister feature*

Starting at 17th level, you can cast *time stop* once without expending a spell slot. 

When you cast *time stop* in this way, you automatically gain the **maximum duration** (5 turns) rather than rolling a d4 + 1. Once you use this feature, you cannot use it again until you complete a long rest.
