# Sunwatcher

*Drawing power from the highest primal authority, you embody the brilliance of the sun, burning away shadows and infusing allies with daylight.*

Sunwatcher Primals act as beacons in dark and desolate lands. They channel the searing heat and blinding illumination of high noon, transforming simple weapons into glowing solar instruments, projecting piercing rays of light, and leading allies through the fiercest planar darkness.

### Solar Fragment
*1st-level Sunwatcher feature*

Starting at 1st level, you can cast the *light* spell at will (Wisdom is your spellcasting ability for it). 

If you cast *light* on a simple melee weapon you are wielding, the spell lasts as long as the weapon remains in your possession, and the weapon deals **fire or radiant damage** (your choice) instead of its normal damage type. The damage of a weapon under this effect is increased by Primal features that increase the damage of your natural weapons.

---

### Radiant Blast
*1st-level Sunwatcher feature*

At 1st level, you can gather and release fiery radiant power from your hands or a wielded weapon. 

Radiant Blasts are a ranged natural weapon attack with a normal range of 60 feet and a long range of 120 feet, dealing **1d6 fire or radiant damage** (your choice). If the damage die increases to 1d8 (through Primal features that enlarge natural weapons), the damage becomes **1d4 fire damage and 1d4 radiant damage** (you choose which damage type adds your modifier). 

You can use your **Strength or Wisdom modifier** (your choice) in place of Dexterity for the attack and damage rolls of this feature.

---

### Innate Magic
*1st-level Sunwatcher feature*

You gain access to several innate magical abilities by channeling your primal connection. You gain the following spells at the listed Primal levels, and can cast each spell once without material components or spell slots (regained on a long rest). 

Wisdom is your spellcasting ability for these spells. You can cast a spell again before completing a long rest by spending **1 + the level of the spell** Endurance Dice.

| Primal Level | Innate Spells (1/Long Rest Each) |
| :---: | :--- |
| **1st** | *heroism* |
| **3rd** | *guiding bolt* |
| **5th** | *blazing beacon* |
| **9th** | *daylight* |
| **13th** | *brilliant blade* |
| **17th** | *heavenly ray* |

### Solar Flare
*3rd-level Sunwatcher feature*

At 3rd level, when you expend an Endurance Die to reduce damage from an attack, you can release a brilliant flare of blinding light. 

The attacker must make a Constitution saving throw (DC = 8 + your Constitution modifier + your proficiency bonus) or become blinded until the start of their next turn. Regardless of whether the attacker succeeds or fails, if it is invisible, it is outlined in light and revealed until the start of its next turn.

---

### Radiant Aura
*3rd-level Sunwatcher feature*

Additionally at 3rd level, your Primal Interdiction is bathed in bright light, and casts dim light for an additional radius equal to your interdiction radius. This light counts as a spell of a level equal to half your Primal level (rounded down) for the purpose of dispelling or countering magical darkness. You can dim, extinguish, or relight this aura at will (no action required).

Creatures inside your Primal Interdiction remain comfortably warm, ignoring the effects of extreme cold weather or solar heat. Furthermore, you and friendly creatures within your Primal Interdiction apply the damage reduction of your **Mystic Bulwark** to **cold and necrotic damage**.

---

### Empowering Presence
*7th-level Sunwatcher feature*

Starting at 7th level, when you expend an Endurance Die to boost a saving throw against a spell, all allies within your Primal Interdiction that make a saving throw against the same spell before the start of your next turn can add that same Endurance Die result to their saves.

---

### Transcendent Passage
*14th-level Sunwatcher feature*

Starting at 14th level, as a bonus action, you can transform into a swirling streak of pure sunlight, traveling up to 60 feet in a straight line. You can pass through creatures and objects, though you are blocked by opaque barriers that completely obstruct light. This movement does not provoke opportunity attacks.

Hostile creatures of your choice in your path must make a Dexterity saving throw (DC = 8 + your Strength or Wisdom modifier + your proficiency bonus). On a failure, they take **2d6 fire damage** and **2d6 radiant damage**, and are blinded until the start of your next turn. On a success, they take half damage and are not blinded. 

Allied creatures in your path gain **4d6 temporary hit points**. Once you use this feature, you cannot use it again until you complete a short or long rest.

---

### Solar Avatar
*17th-level Sunwatcher feature*

Starting at 17th level, as a bonus action, you embody the full blazing radiance of the sun for 1 minute:
- **Sunlight:** You shed bright light for 60 feet and dim light for an additional 60 feet. The bright light is authentic sunlight.
- **Harsh Light:** Hostile creatures in your bright light have disadvantage on saving throws against your Primal abilities and spells.
- **Empowered Radiance:** When you use Radiant Blast, you can fire a piercing solar beam instead of an attack roll, forcing all creatures in a 60-foot line to make a Dexterity saving throw (DC = 8 + your Strength or Wisdom modifier + your proficiency bonus), taking full Radiant Blast damage on a failure or half as much on a success.
- **Lingering Light:** Whenever you deal radiant damage to a target, you illuminate it, granting advantage to the next attack made against that target before the start of your next turn.

Once you assume this form, you cannot do so again until you complete a long rest.
