# Dreadwing

*The Dreadwing Primal utilizes an ancient bond tapping into the deep primal powers that flow within dragonkind, embodying strength, elemental fury, and terror.*

Dreadwing Primals align their spirit with the apex rulers of the primordial world: dragons. They manifest razor talons and expansive wings, exhale torrential blasts of elemental fire or frost, and terrify foes with a glance before assuming the full majesty of an ancient dragon.

### Draconic Talons
*1st-level Dreadwing feature*

Starting at 1st level, when you roll initiative or as a bonus action at any time, you can manifest one or both hands into draconic talons. You cannot wield weapons or shields in hands morphed into talons, but they can be used to grapple creatures. 

While grappling a creature with a talon, that talon can only attack that creature. The talons last until you dismiss them as a bonus action:

- **Draconic Talons:** A vicious set of talons extending from your hand. This becomes a natural one-handed melee weapon that deals **1d6 slashing damage** on a hit, and has the *Light* and *Special* properties.
  - *Special:* This weapon counts as a free hand for the purposes of grappling. When you successfully initiate a grapple with it, the target takes **1d6 slashing damage**.

---

### Dragon's Presence
*1st-level Dreadwing feature*

At 1st level, you learn the **Draconic** language, and gain proficiency in the **Intimidation** skill as you channel a fragment of draconic majesty.

---

### Innate Magic
*1st-level Dreadwing feature*

You gain access to several innate magical abilities by channeling your primal connection. You gain the following spells at the listed Primal levels, and can cast each spell once without material components or spell slots (regained on a long rest). 

Wisdom is your spellcasting ability for these spells. You can cast a spell again before completing a long rest by spending **1 + the level of the spell** Endurance Dice.

| Primal Level | Innate Spells (1/Long Rest Each) |
| :---: | :--- |
| **1st** | *elemental orb* |
| **3rd** | *dragon surge* |
| **5th** | *elemental exhalation* |
| **9th** | *fly* |
| **13th** | *fear* |
| **17th** | *secret chest* |

### Intimidating Interdiction
*3rd-level Dreadwing feature*

Starting at 3rd level, your Primal Interdiction sparks primordial fear. As a bonus action, you can unleash a wave of supernatural dread, forcing all creatures of your choice within your Primal Interdiction to make a Wisdom saving throw (DC = 8 + your Strength or Wisdom modifier + your proficiency bonus).

On a failure, a creature is **frightened** of you until the start of your next turn. On a success, the creature is not frightened, but cannot gain advantage on attack rolls against you until the start of your next turn. Once a creature passes this saving throw, it automatically passes all saving throws against this feature for the next minute.

---

### Dragon Breath
*3rd-level Dreadwing feature*

Starting at 3rd level, each time you expend an Endurance Die, you store elemental energy, tracking the expended die in a pool of charged power for 1 minute. You can store a number of dice equal to your proficiency bonus. When you gain this feature, choose your damage type: **acid, cold, fire, lightning, or poison**.

As a bonus action on your turn, you can exhale this stored power in a blast of elemental breath, expending all stored dice. All creatures of your choice within a **15-foot cone** must make a Dexterity saving throw (DC = 8 + your Strength or Wisdom modifier + your proficiency bonus). On a failed save, a creature takes elemental damage of the chosen type equal to the total rolled on the stored dice, or half as much on a successful one. 

The size of the cone increases by 15 feet each time your Primal Interdiction range increases by 5 feet.

---

### Dragon Wings
*7th-level Dreadwing feature*

Starting at 7th level, when you manifest your Draconic Talons, you can manifest a pair of draconic wings. While they do not grant true flight yet, they provide powerful benefits:
- **Enhanced Jump:** You can jump twice as far and change direction mid-jump by beating your wings.
- **Feather Fall:** You can slow your descent when falling (no action required), gliding down at 60 feet per round and taking zero falling damage.
- **Wing Shield:** As a reaction, you can expend an Endurance Die to reduce damage against an allied creature within 5 feet of you as if you were blocking damage against yourself.

---

### Empowered Wings
*14th-level Dreadwing feature*

Starting at 14th level, your wings grow strong enough for sustained aerial combat: you gain a **flying speed of 30 feet**.

---

### Dragon Form
*17th-level Dreadwing feature*

Starting at 17th level, when you manifest your Draconic Talons, you can choose to transform completely into an adult dragon for up to 10 minutes:
- **Large Size:** Your size becomes Large, and you have advantage on Strength (Athletics) checks to grapple creatures smaller than you.
- **Empowered Dragon Breath:** When you unleash your Dragon Breath, you roll twice the number of stored dice for damage.
- **Extra Natural Weapons:** You gain a Bite and Tail attack. The **Bite** deals 1d8 piercing damage + 1d8 elemental damage matching your breath weapon. The **Tail** deals 1d8 bludgeoning damage and has the Reach property.
- **Empowered Weapons:** All of your natural weapons deal one additional die of damage while in this form.
- **Dragon Dread:** Creatures frightened by your Intimidating Interdiction remain frightened for 1 minute (repeating the save at the end of each turn).

You return to your normal form early if you fall unconscious or end it as an action. Once you assume this form, you cannot do so again until you complete a long rest.
