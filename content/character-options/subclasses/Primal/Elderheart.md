# Elderheart

*Rooted in the ancient growth of the world, Elderheart Primals are linked to the primordial forest. Their vines lash and bind, dragging enemies to their inevitable place in the natural cycle.*

Elderheart Primals embody the creeping, inexorable might of root, vine, and ancient wood. They entwine foes in thorny undergrowth, punish attackers with venomous thorns, and transform into primeval treant guardians that cannot be uprooted.

### Lashing Vines
*1st-level Elderheart feature*

Starting at 1st level, when you roll initiative or as a bonus action at any time, you can manifest lashing vines to wield as natural weapons in one or both hands. The vines last until you dismiss them as a bonus action:

- **Lashing Vine:** A natural one-handed melee weapon that deals **1d6 slashing damage** on a hit, and has the *Light*, *Reach*, and *Special* properties.
  - *Special:* This weapon grants the following benefits:
    - **Grappling Weapon:** When you hit a creature with this weapon, you can attempt to initiate a grapple against that creature as a bonus action. While grappling a creature with it, you can only attack that creature with that vine.
    - **Restraining Vines:** You can make a grapple check with this weapon targeting a Large or smaller creature you are currently grappling with it. On a success, the creature is restrained while grappled in this manner.
    - **Reaching Grapples:** You can grapple creatures with this weapon at a reach equal to the weapon's reach. If a creature more than 5 feet away from you is grappled or restrained by you with this weapon, its movement is not reduced to zero, but it can only move toward you. Once within 5 feet of you, its movement speed becomes zero.
    - **Grappling Larger Creatures:** You can attempt to grapple creatures up to two sizes larger than you with this weapon. However, creatures two sizes larger have their movement speed halved rather than reduced to zero. If they move out of reach, you can choose to release them or be pulled along with their movement.

### Eyes of Nature
*1st-level Elderheart feature*

At 1st level, you gain advantage on Intelligence (Nature) and Wisdom (Survival) checks that involve plants (identifying them, determining if they are edible, or tracking creatures passing through vegetation).

---

### Innate Magic
*1st-level Elderheart feature*

You gain access to several innate magical abilities by channeling your primal connection. You gain the following spells at the listed Primal levels, and can cast each spell once without material components or spell slots (regained on a long rest). 

Wisdom is your spellcasting ability for these spells. You can cast a spell again before completing a long rest by spending **1 + the level of the spell** Endurance Dice.

| Primal Level | Innate Spells (1/Long Rest Each) |
| :---: | :--- |
| **1st** | *grasping roots* |
| **3rd** | *entangle* |
| **5th** | *speak with plants* |
| **9th** | *plant growth* |
| **13th** | *summon plant* |
| **17th** | *carnivorous garden* |

### Body of Thorns
*3rd-level Elderheart feature*

Starting at 3rd level, when you are hit by a melee attack, the attacker takes piercing damage equal to the damage reduction value of your **Mystic Bulwark** feature.

---

### Spiked Interdiction
*3rd-level Elderheart feature*

Additionally at 3rd level, the difficult terrain caused by your Primal Interdiction becomes overgrown with sharp, tearing thorns. Creatures of your choice take **1d4 piercing damage** for each 5 feet of travel within your Primal Interdiction (increasing to **2d4 piercing damage** at 11th level). When you move, this damage effect is suspended until the end of your turn.

---

### Hardened Core
*7th-level Elderheart feature*

Starting at 7th level, your bark-like skin thickens: you gain a **+1 bonus to your Armor Class**, and any critical hit against you becomes a normal hit.

---

### Primeval Guardian
*14th-level Elderheart feature*

Starting at 14th level, as a bonus action, you can sink roots deep into the earth, transforming into a wrathful treant. 

While rooted, your movement speed is reduced to zero. When you transform, you can choose to grow one size larger (up to a maximum of Large), your reach increases by 5 feet, you are immune to the prone condition and any effect that would move you against your will, and you have advantage on Strength checks and saving throws.

Creatures two sizes larger than you that you are grappling can no longer drag you, meaning they cannot leave your reach without breaking the grapple. You can end your tree form at any time as an action.

---

### Arboreal Shelter
*14th-level Elderheart feature*

Additionally at 14th level, you no longer need to be in range of an incoming attack to impose disadvantage on it with your Primal Interdiction, provided the target of the attack is within range of your Primal Interdiction.

---

### New Growth
*17th-level Elderheart feature*

Starting at 17th level, as an action, you can cause a tree to sprout from the ground at a point within 30 feet of you that morphs into a tree-like clone of you. 

The clone copies all of your statistics and abilities, except features that recharge on a long rest (which it cannot access). It acts immediately after you in initiative and is under your mental command. It has hit points equal to half your hit point maximum and enters the battlefield in your Primeval Guardian form. It lasts for 1 minute or until reduced to 0 hit points. 

If you die while the clone exists, your soul instantly transfers into the clone, after which you can end the Primeval Guardian form as normal. Once you use this feature, you cannot use it again until you complete a long rest.
