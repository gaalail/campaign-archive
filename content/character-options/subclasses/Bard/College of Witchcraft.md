# College of Witchcraft (Intoner)

Bards of the College of Witchcraft are called **Intoners**. They specialize in calling spirits through the use of songs. Intoners do not use musical instruments other than their own voices to cast their magic, and as a result must train their vocal cords rigorously for them to withstand the arcane energy required to summon spirits. The training takes years, as mastering any branch of witchcraft often does.

The Intoners of the College of Witchcraft keep the secrets of their arcane songs to themselves. These songs are charged with magical energy so that their sounds can pierce through the veil between the planes, reaching the astral home of spirits where the living cannot tread. The songs are then used as invitations — or lures — for specific spirits to cross the veil and manifest themselves in a specific location. With these spirits, the Intoner dominates the flow of battle, invoking specific spirits to change the tempo of the battlefield to their whim.

### Witchmark

Witchmarks for Intoners are usually placed on the neck or chest to signify how their voices have been altered and enhanced with arcane energy through witchcraft. Some Intoners have thin vertical lines extending from the bottom of their chin to the center of their chest to simulate the strings of a violin, moving when they sing as if they were being plucked. Other Intoners use the musical sheet of an emotionally significant song, f-shaped violin holes, or evocative lyrical stanzas as their witchmarks.

## Spiritual Knowledge

_3rd-level College of Witchcraft feature_

You can use your voice to commune with the spirits that dwell beyond the veil and ask for their wisdom. At the end of a short or long rest, you can spend 10 minutes singing a song that draws forth a specific spirit to grant you additional proficiencies:

- **Farmer:** Proficiency with Land Vehicles
- **Healer:** Proficiency with Healer's Kit
- **Pirate:** Proficiency with Navigator's Tools and Water Vehicles
- **Scholar:** Proficiency in an additional language of your choice
- **Thespian:** Proficiency with Disguise Kit
- **Thief:** Proficiency with Thieves' Tools

This feature does not provide physical tools, only proficiency in using them. You lose any proficiency or language acquired through this ability when your hit points are reduced to 0, or after your next long rest.

## Arcane Voice

_3rd-level College of Witchcraft feature_

You have sufficiently trained your voice to withstand the arcane power channeled through it. You can make your voice your spellcasting focus when casting a spell. If you do, all spells you cast have a verbal component, even if the spell doesn't normally specify one. When using your voice as your focus, you cannot lose your spellcasting focus unless you are silenced or otherwise unable to perform verbal components.

## Song of the Spirits

_3rd-level College of Witchcraft feature_

You are able to use songs to summon spirits through the veil into the Material Plane. When you finish a long rest, you prepare a number of songs equal to your Charisma modifier (minimum of one). Preparing a song requires warming up your voice: 2 minutes per song prepared.

As an action, you can spend up to three uses of your Bardic Inspiration to sing a specific prepared song, creating an aura centered on you. The radius of the aura depends on the number of Bardic Inspiration uses expended:
- **1 use:** 15-foot radius aura
- **2 uses:** 25-foot radius aura
- **3 uses:** 35-foot radius aura

Once you've started singing, you can use a bonus action on each of your turns to continue your current song without spending further uses of Bardic Inspiration. If you don't use your bonus action to continue singing, the song ends. A song automatically ends if you reach 0 hit points. When casting a spell requiring verbal components while singing, the verbal components are replaced with singing. While singing, your walking speed is reduced by half.

You cannot sing two different songs at the same time; starting a new song immediately ends the previous one.

- **Song of the Nurses:** Unconscious creatures within the aura, friendly or hostile, are immediately stabilized upon entering the aura.
- **Song of the Lovers:** Friendly creatures within the aura make Charisma (Performance) checks and Charisma saving throws with advantage.
- **Song of the Pious:** Friendly creatures within the aura have advantage on saving throws against being charmed and checks to see through illusions.
- **Song of the Wildlings:** The aura is considered magical difficult terrain for hostile creatures.
- **Song of the Courtiers:** Hostile creatures within the aura have disadvantage on saving throws against being charmed.
- **Song of the Inquisitors:** Creatures within the aura have advantage on Wisdom (Perception) and Intelligence (Investigation) checks.
- **Song of the Warriors:** Friendly creatures within the aura cannot be frightened, and gain an extra 5 feet of movement that can only be used to move directly toward an enemy.
- **Song of the Champions:** Friendly creatures within the aura can reroll one damage die per attack, taking the new roll.

## Hymn of the Spirits

_6th-level College of Witchcraft feature_

You can summon spirits from beyond the Ethereal Plane for brief, explosive manifestations. As an action, you can spend either one use of your Bardic Inspiration or a 3rd-level or higher spell slot to sing a hymn, affecting creatures in a 15-foot-radius sphere centered on you until the end of your next turn.

You can maintain both a Song of the Spirits and a Hymn of the Spirits simultaneously, giving your voice an ethereal polyphonic resonance:

- **Hymn of the Elementals:** Roll an inspiration die and choose fire, cold, or lightning. Friendly creatures within the area add damage of that type equal to the roll to their spell attacks until the end of your next turn.
- **Hymn of the Shadows:** Friendly creatures within the area don't provoke opportunity attacks and gain an additional 10 feet of movement until the end of your next turn.
- **Hymn of the Fey:** Friendly creatures within the area can't be charmed, and add your inspiration die roll to their spell save DC when attempting to charm another creature until the end of your next turn.
- **Hymn of the Celestials:** Roll an inspiration die. Friendly creatures within the area gain temporary hit points equal to the number rolled.
- **Hymn of the Devils:** Hostile creatures within the area must make a Charisma saving throw against your spell save DC or suffer disadvantage on Wisdom saving throws until the end of your next turn.

## Spiritual Choir

_14th-level College of Witchcraft feature_

Whenever a creature with a Challenge Rating of 1 or higher drops to 0 hit points inside your Song of the Spirits aura, you immediately regain one expended use of your Bardic Inspiration.
