**Avatar Form Only.** Choose two of the following spells: Holy Verdict: Retribution (p. 174), Holy Verdict: Vengeance (p. 174), Holy Verdict: Wrath (p. 174). You can cast the chosen spells requiring no material components. Once you cast one of the chosen spells in this way, you can't cast it again until you finish a long rest.

**Prerequisite:** Echo of the Divine  
**Minimum level:** 17
