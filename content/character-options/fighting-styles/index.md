# Fighting Styles

**Martial techniques available through features from multiple classes and other character options.**

These styles offer different ways to specialize in combat. Some classes have their own Fighting Style feature and specific choice lists; this catalogue collects styles referenced by the Alternate Fighter and related character options. Check each style's prerequisites and the feature that grants it to see when you can choose it.

Some of the supplemental styles are uncommon. Learning one may require training from a dedicated master and DM approval.
