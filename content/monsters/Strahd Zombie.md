# Strahd Zombie

*Medium undead, unaligned*

- **Armor Class:** 8
- **Hit Points:** 30 (4d8 + 12)
- **Speed:** 20 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| 13 (+1) | 6 (-2) | 16 (+3) | 3 (-4) | 6 (-2) | 5 (-3) |

- **Saving Throws:** Wis +0
- **Damage Immunities:** Poison
- **Condition Immunities:** Poisoned
- **Senses:** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 8
- **Languages:** Understands Common but can't speak
- **Challenge:** 1 (200 XP) | **Proficiency Bonus:** +2

### Special Traits

- **Loathsome Limbs:** Whenever the zombie takes 5 or more bludgeoning or slashing damage at one time, roll a d20 to see what happens:
  - 1–8: One leg is severed. The zombie falls prone and its walking speed is halved.
  - 9–16: One arm is severed. The severed arm has AC 8, 10 hit points, speed 10 ft., and can attack with Slam (+3 to hit, 3 bludgeoning damage).
  - 17–20: The zombie is decapitated. Its head dies, but its body continues to fight blindly (blinded).

### Actions

- **Multiattack:** The zombie makes two slam attacks if it has both arms, or one slam attack if it has only one arm.
- **Slam:** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +3 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 4 (1d6 + 1) bludgeoning damage.
- **Bite:** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +3 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 3 (1d4 + 1) piercing damage.
