# Weather Fish

*Tiny construct, unaligned*

- **Armor Class:** 13 (natural armor)
- **Hit Points:** 1 (1d4 - 1)
- **Speed:** 0 ft., fly 20 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| 4 (-3) | 15 (+2) | 8 (-1) | 12 (+1) | 13 (+1) | 12 (+1) |

- **Skills:** Perception +3, Persuasion +3
- **Senses:** Passive Perception 13
- **Languages:** Aquan, and understands the languages its master speaks
- **Challenge:** 0 (10 XP) | **Proficiency Bonus:** +2

### Special Traits

- **Weather Shifting Scales:** The colored patches across the weather fish's scales shift color to forecast the weather of the following day: bright sky-blue for sunny skies, dark grey with flashing yellow streaks for approaching thunderstorms.
- **Perpetual Bubble:** The weather fish is permanently encased in a protective spherical bubble of water that provides its natural armor and allows it to swim through the air.

### Actions

- **Help:** The weather fish aids a friendly creature in attacking a target within 5 feet. The next attack roll against that target is made with advantage.
