# One-Eyed Raven

*Tiny construct, unaligned*

- **Armor Class:** 11
- **Hit Points:** 1 (1d4 - 1)
- **Speed:** 5 ft., fly 40 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| 4 (-3) | 13 (+1) | 8 (-1) | 15 (+2) | 16 (+3) | 10 (+0) |

- **Skills:** Perception +5, Investigation +4
- **Senses:** Passive Perception 15
- **Languages:** Understands the languages its master speaks
- **Challenge:** 0 (10 XP) | **Proficiency Bonus:** +2

### Special Traits

- **Mimicry:** The one-eyed raven can mimic simple sounds it has heard (whispers, infant crying, animal noises). A creature hearing the sounds can discern they are imitations with a successful DC 10 Wisdom (Insight) check.

### Actions

- **Help:** The one-eyed raven aids a friendly creature in attacking a target within 5 feet. The next attack roll against that target is made with advantage.

### Bonus Actions

- **Gold Sight:** The one-eyed raven's eye glints with golden radiance. For 1 minute, it perceives the exact quantity of gold pieces a creature within sight carries, or the precise amount of gold coins contained inside a closed chest, safe, or container (1/long rest).
