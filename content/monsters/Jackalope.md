# Jackalope

*Tiny beast, unaligned*

- **Armor Class:** 12
- **Hit Points:** 2 (1d4)
- **Speed:** 20 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| 7 (-2) | 14 (+2) | 10 (+0) | 4 (-3) | 13 (+1) | 9 (-1) |

- **Skills:** Stealth +4, Perception +3
- **Senses:** Passive Perception 13
- **Languages:** Understands the languages its master speaks
- **Challenge:** 0 (10 XP) | **Proficiency Bonus:** +2

### Special Traits

- **Legendary Stomach:** The jackalope can eat and permanently destroy any object—edible or non-edible, magical or non-magical—that fits inside its mouth. The consumed object cannot be restored by any known magic or mundane method.

### Actions

- **Help:** The jackalope aids a friendly creature in attacking a target within 5 feet. The next attack roll against that target is made with advantage.
