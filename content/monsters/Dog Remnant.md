# Dog Remnant

*Medium construct, unaligned*

- **Armor Class:** 11
- **Hit Points:** 5 (1d8 + 1)
- **Speed:** 40 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| 14 (+2) | 12 (+1) | 12 (+1) | 6 (-2) | 14 (+2) | 10 (+0) |

- **Skills:** Perception +4
- **Senses:** Passive Perception 14
- **Languages:** Understands the languages its master speaks
- **Challenge:** 1/8 (25 XP) | **Proficiency Bonus:** +2

### Special Traits

- **Keen Hearing and Smell:** The dog remnant has advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on hearing or smell.
- **Ghostly Tracker:** The dog remnant always knows the general cardinal direction of any creature with which it has spent at least 1 hour in close quarters, as long as that creature is within 500 feet. The dog remnant also senses if the creature is on another plane of existence.

### Actions

- **Help:** The dog remnant aids a friendly creature in attacking a target within 5 feet. The next attack roll against that target is made with advantage.
