# Barovian Witch

*Medium humanoid (human), chaotic evil*

- **Armor Class:** 10
- **Hit Points:** 16 (3d8 + 3)
- **Speed:** 30 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| 7 (-2) | 11 (+0) | 12 (+1) | 14 (+2) | 11 (+0) | 12 (+1) |

- **Skills:** Arcana +4, Perception +2
- **Senses:** Passive Perception 12
- **Languages:** Common
- **Challenge:** 1/2 (100 XP) | **Proficiency Bonus:** +2

### Special Traits

- **Spellcasting:** The witch is a 3rd-level spellcaster (spell save DC 12, +4 to hit with spell attacks). She has the following wizard spells prepared:

  - 1st level (4 slots): *comprehend languages*, *ray of sickness*, *sleep*
  - 2nd level (2 slots): *alter self*, *blindness/deafness*

### Actions

- **Dagger:** *Melee or Ranged Weapon Attack:* +2 to hit, reach 5 ft. or range 20/60 ft., one target. *Hit:* 2 (1d4) piercing damage.
- **Claws:** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +2 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 3 (1d6) slashing damage.
