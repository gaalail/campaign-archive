# Etherfire Cat

*Tiny construct, unaligned*

- **Armor Class:** 13
- **Hit Points:** 1 (1d4)
- **Speed:** 40 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| 4 (-3) | 16 (+3) | 10 (+0) | 8 (-1) | 16 (+3) | 12 (+1) |

- **Skills:** Perception +5
- **Senses:** Passive Perception 15
- **Languages:** Understands the languages its master speaks
- **Challenge:** 0 (10 XP) | **Proficiency Bonus:** +2

### Special Traits

- **Keen Sight:** The etherfire cat has advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on sight.
- **Undead Sense:** The etherfire cat senses any undead creature within a 60-foot radius centered on itself. It detects whether each undead is corporeal (such as a zombie, skeleton, or mummy) or ethereal/incorporeal (such as a ghost, wraith, or specter). This sense ignores walls and ordinary obstacles, but is blocked by any thickness of lead.

### Actions

- **Help:** The etherfire cat aids a friendly creature in attacking a target within 5 feet. The next attack roll against that target is made with advantage.
