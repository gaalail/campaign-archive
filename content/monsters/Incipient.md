# Incipient

*Small construct, unaligned*

- **Armor Class:** 9
- **Hit Points:** 5 (1d6 + 2)
- **Speed:** 0 ft., fly 20 ft. (hover)

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| 6 (-2) | 14 (+2) | 14 (+2) | 13 (+1) | 14 (+2) | 11 (+0) |

- **Skills:** Perception +3, Insight +3
- **Senses:** Passive Perception 13
- **Languages:** Understands the languages its master speaks
- **Challenge:** 0 (10 XP) | **Proficiency Bonus:** +2

### Special Traits

- **Prophet:** Once a day, the incipient whispers a prophecy to its master. Roll d100: there is a 20% chance of the prophecy pertaining directly to the master's impending actions or fate. Otherwise, it foretells a broader worldly event at the GM's discretion.

### Actions

- **Help:** The incipient aids a friendly creature in attacking a target within 5 feet. The next attack roll against that target is made with advantage.
