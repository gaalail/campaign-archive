# How to Build a D&D Character

New to D&D? This guide walks you through creating your character for play.

## The Five Steps

```
Pick a RACE
    ↓
Pick a CLASS
    ↓
Pick a SUBCLASS (optional, powerful)
    ↓
Pick FEATS (customize your abilities)
    ↓
You're Ready to Play!
```

---

## Step 1: Choose a Race

Your **race** defines your ancestry and background. It gives you special abilities and traits.

**Browse races:** [[Races]]

Each race can have **subraces** (variants). For example, there are different types of elves. Check inside the race page for details.

**What races give you:**
- Ability score bonuses
- Special abilities (darkvision, flying, etc.)
- Cultural traits and background
- Size and appearance

---

## Step 2: Choose a Class

Your **class** is your role in the party. It determines what you're good at: fighting, casting spells, sneaking, healing, etc.

**Browse classes:** [[Classes]]

Popular classes:
- **Fighter** — Master of weapons and armor
- **Wizard** — Spellcaster with arcane magic
- **Rogue** — Sneaky, dexterous, skilled
- **Cleric** — Holy spellcaster and healer
- **Barbarian** — Powerful warrior driven by rage

**What your class gives you:**
- Your main abilities and features
- Hit die (how much health you get per level)
- Spell list (if you're a spellcaster)
- Proficiencies (what you're trained in)

---

## Step 3: Choose a Subclass (Optional)

At a certain level (usually 3rd), you pick a **subclass**. This specializes your class into a specific flavor.

**Examples:**
- Fighter → [[Champion]] (master of weapons)
- Fighter → [[Holy Knight]] (faithful warrior)
- Wizard → [[Evocation]] (master of explosive spells)
- Wizard → [[School of Sealing]] (abjuration and binding)
- Cleric → [[Life]] (powerful healer)
- Cleric → [[Dawn Domain]] (light and dawn)

**Browse subclasses:** [[Subclasses]]

Subclasses don't change how your class works fundamentally—they just add specialized abilities. You can play without one, but they make characters much more interesting.

---

## Step 4: Choose Feats (Optional)

**Feats** are optional bonuses that customize your character. They represent special training or talents.

You get to choose a feat at certain levels (usually 4th, 8th, 12th, etc., depending on your class).

**Example feats:**
- **[[Grappler]]** — Better at holding enemies
- **[[Courageous]]** — Stand firm against fear
- **[[Consecrated Spell]]** — Infuse spells with holy power
- **[[Celestial Blood]]** — Angelic heritage in your veins
- **Athlete** — Stronger, faster, better at physical tasks

**Browse feats:** [[Feats]]

**Note:** Some feats have **prerequisites**. For example, "Great Weapon Master" requires you to have a certain fighting style. Check the prerequisite before choosing.
